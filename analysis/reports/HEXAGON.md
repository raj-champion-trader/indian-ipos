# Hexagon Nutrition Ltd (HEXAGON)

## What it does
Hexagon Nutrition makes nutrition products — micronutrient premixes, clinical and therapeutic nutrition, ready-to-use foods and micronutrient powders. It sells to food companies, governments and health programmes that fortify staples and treat malnutrition. Operating since 1993.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 114 crore (FY23), Rs 132 crore (FY24), Rs 319 crore (FY26) — standalone, per screener.in (FY25 column not shown; Rs 319 crore figure appears under FY26 head)
- Net profit: Rs -3 crore (FY23), Rs 10 crore (FY24), Rs 38 crore (FY26)
- Operating margin: -0.8% (FY23), 10% (FY24), 14% (FY26)
- Other income Rs 16 crore included in FY26 earnings — a chunk of profit
- Borrowings Rs 25 crore (Mar 2026) — low debt; reserves Rs 220 crore
- 5-year sales CAGR 30%, profit CAGR 52%
- Market cap Rs 840 crore; P/E 20.1

## Lynch lens
Turnaround that worked — losses in FY23 turned into double-digit margins by FY26. Boring business (vitamin premixes nobody talks about) with 30% five-year sales CAGR fits Lynch's quiet-growth checklist. P/E 20 against that growth reads cheap, but strip out Rs 16 crore other income and core earnings shrink meaningfully.

## TAM
Global micronutrient premix / food fortification market — large, driven by government nutrition programmes; exact TAM figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Micronutrient premix sales to food and FMCG companies — core product line
2. Ready-to-use foods and micronutrient powders for institutional/government nutrition programmes
3. Margin recovery — OPM from -0.8% (FY23) to 14% (FY26) drove the profit swing

## Red flags
- Rs 15.6 crore other income props up FY26 earnings
- Working capital days ballooned from 102 to 204 — cash stuck in receivables/inventory
- FY25 standalone figures missing from screener view

## Verdict
Quiet turnaround at 20x — promising, but verify earnings quality after other income.
