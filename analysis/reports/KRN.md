# KRN Heat Exchanger and Refrigeration Limited (KRN)

## What it does
KRN makes fin-and-tube and microchannel heat exchangers — the coils that sit inside air conditioners and refrigerators. It sells these parts to big AC and refrigeration brands such as Daikin, Blue Star, Lloyd and Emerson, and grows a fast export business.

## The numbers
- FY25: net profit rose ~42% to Rs 71.31 crore; EBITDA up 19.41% to Rs 84.79 crore (full-year basis per results coverage).
- Q3 FY25: revenue Rs 116.36 crore (+73.7% YoY), PAT Rs 13.73 crore (+74.4%).
- Q4 FY25 standalone total income: Rs 689.95 crore figure appeared in one report (57.4% YoY) — treat with caution, quarterly basis unclear.
- FY25 export split: UAE 51.7% (Rs 34.9 crore), Canada 20.3% (Rs 13.7 crore), Italy 14.6% (Rs 9.8 crore); overseas revenue later scaled to ~21% of sales (Rs 52.4 crore in a recent quarter).
- Management targets 5-6x revenue growth over three years from FY25 on the new microchannel facility; analysts model ~49% revenue / ~50% PAT CAGR FY25-28E.
- Expense heads detail: data not found; aluminium and copper (tube/fin stock) dominate raw-material costs.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — a tiny, boring component maker (AC coils) compounding profits at 40-70% with a capacity kicker in microchannel condensers and rising exports. P/E vs growth: data not found here (DCF-based target Rs 820 per one analyst note). Watch execution — such growth rates attract competition and require flawless capex.

## TAM
Global heat exchanger market ~USD 330 billion (per company/industry report cited in Scribd coverage); India HVAC-R component demand grows with AC penetration.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. AC and refrigeration OEM demand (Daikin, Blue Star, Lloyd, Emerson) for fin-and-tube coils.
2. New microchannel condenser facility ramp — basis of the 5-6x three-year guidance.
3. Export mix (UAE, Canada, Italy) crossing ~20% of sales, improving volumes and margins.

## Red flags
- Customer concentration among a few AC OEMs.
- Guidance (5-6x in 3 years) sets high expectations; any slip punishes the multiple.
- Aluminium/copper price swings squeeze margins.

## Verdict
A genuine small-cap fast grower in an unglamorous coil business — hold while capacity ramps, exit when guidance wobbles.
