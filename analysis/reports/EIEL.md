# Enviro Infra Engineers Ltd (EIEL)

## What it does
Enviro Infra designs, builds and runs water and wastewater treatment plants for government bodies, plus water supply schemes. It has expanded into renewable-energy EPC — solar, battery storage and wind projects.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 334 cr (FY23), Rs 726 cr (FY24), Rs 1,046 cr (FY25), Rs 1,015 cr (FY26, standalone)
- Net profit: Rs 56 cr (FY23), Rs 114 cr (FY24), Rs 173 cr (FY25), Rs 171 cr (FY26)
- OPM steady 23-24%; 5-year profit CAGR ~83%
- FY26 expenses Rs 782 cr (interest Rs 21 cr, depreciation Rs 16 cr); P/E ~20.7; ROE 16.4%

## Lynch lens
A fast grower in a boring, unglamorous niche — sewage and water infrastructure — exactly where Lynch likes to hunt. Government-driven demand, 23-24% operating margins held across years, and a P/E of ~21 against a multi-year 80%+ profit CAGR gives an attractive PEG on historical numbers. Revenue paused in FY26 (flat YoY), so the question becomes whether the renewables push sustains growth. Insider activity data not found.

## TAM
Indian water/wastewater treatment capex market — Jal Jeevan Mission and urban sewage programmes drive lakhs of crores of planned spend; exact figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Execution of water/wastewater EPC contracts (OPM ~23-24%)
2. O&M revenue on completed plants (recurring, higher-margin)
3. New renewable EPC segment (solar/BESS/wind) ramp-up

## Red flags
- FY26 revenue flat YoY (1,046 to 1,015 cr) — growth stall
- Government receivables risk common to the sector
- Diversersion into renewables brings new competitors and execution risk

## Verdict
Best value profile of a boring-infra compounder in this batch — watch order inflow to confirm FY26 marks a pause, not a peak.
