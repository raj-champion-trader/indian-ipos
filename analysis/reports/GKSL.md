# Gujarat Kidney & Super Speciality Ltd (GKSL)

## What it does
Gujarat Kidney & Super Speciality runs a chain of mid-sized multi-speciality hospitals in central Gujarat, focused on kidney care plus other secondary and tertiary specialities. It earns from surgeries, dialysis, in-patient beds, diagnostics and pharmacy sales.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 4.77 crore (FY24, first full year), Rs 35.17 crore (FY25) — standalone, per screener.in
- Net profit: Rs 1.45 crore (FY24), Rs 9.11 crore (FY25)
- Operating margin: 33% (FY24), 44% (FY25)
- Finance cost: Rs 0.53 crore (FY25); borrowings Rs 5.48 crore — minimal debt
- Market cap Rs 1,267 crore; P/E 158 — extreme for a regional hospital

## Lynch lens
Boring regional healthcare operator — the kind of business Lynch liked — but the price tag ruins it. At P/E 158 on FY25 EPS, investors pay many decades of earnings upfront. Growth from Rs 5 crore to Rs 35 crore revenue looks spectacular only because the base sits near zero. Not a fast grower you can bank on yet; the classification fits "story stock priced as a sure thing".

## TAM
Indian hospital and dialysis/kidney-care market — large and under-served; exact TAM figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. In-patient surgeries and procedures (kidney + super-speciality)
2. Dialysis and repeat outpatient revenues
3. Bed occupancy ramp at hospitals — FY24 to FY25 revenue jump drove margin from 33% to 44%

## Red flags
- P/E 158 on a tiny Rs 35 crore revenue base
- Only two years of operating history as a listed entity
- Small regional footprint — one bad quarter moves everything

## Verdict
Decent little hospital business trapped inside a silly valuation.
