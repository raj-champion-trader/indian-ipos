# Tolins Tyres Ltd (TOLINS)

## What it does
Company makes precured tread rubber (PCTR) for tyre retreading and sells tyres for light commercial vehicles, tractors, and two/three-wheelers under the Tolins brand. It also makes bonding gum, vulcanizing solutions, tyre flaps, and tubes in-house. Kerala base with sizeable Gulf export business.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 118 cr (FY23), Rs 157 cr (FY24), Rs 178 cr (FY25), TTM Rs 204 cr — standalone.
- PAT: Rs 5 cr (FY23), Rs 14 cr (FY24), Rs 21 cr (FY25); TTM dropped to Rs 13 cr.
- OPM: 10% (FY23), 18% (FY24), 17% (FY25), 10% (TTM).
- Nearly debt free now — borrowings cut from Rs 62 cr (FY24) to ~Rs 4 cr; 5-yr sales CAGR 16%.

## Lynch lens
Boring-business candidate — retreading rubber earns little analyst attention and serves truck/bus fleet cost-cutting. Sales growing steadily and debt repaid after the 2024 SME IPO. TTM margin collapse (18% to 10%) hints at rubber-price cyclicality, so treat as a niche small-cap cyclical. Exact segment split: data not found.

## TAM
India tyre retreading market estimated around Rs 12,000-15,000 cr annually (industry estimates, FY24); Tolins holds a small single-digit share.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Precured tread rubber sales — core product and margin driver.
2. Natural rubber / raw material prices — swings OPM between 10% and 18% (FY23-FY25).
3. Tyre sales (LCV, agri, 2W/3W) and Gulf exports — growth leg.

## Red flags
- TTM PAT nearly halved vs FY25 (Rs 13 cr vs Rs 21 cr).
- Small absolute profit base; SME-listed with thin liquidity.
- Customer demand tied to commercial vehicle cycles.

## Verdict
Nice niche retreader with clean books, but rubber-cost cyclicality and shrinking TTM profit demand a cheap entry price.
