# DEE Development Engineers Limited (DEEDEV)

## What it does
DEE fabricates pipes, pipe fittings and heavy process piping systems for refineries, power plants and chemical projects in India and abroad. Prefabricates the piping in modules so customers bolt them together on site. Listed via June 2024 IPO.

## The numbers
- Revenue FY25: ~Rs 1,213 crore (consolidated). Q4 FY25: record ~Rs 289 crore income, PAT ~Rs 32 crore (+165.5% YoY).
- Order book: Rs 1,275 crore (April 2025); crossed ~Rs 2,428 crore by mid-2026.
- Piping division Q4 FY25: revenue ~Rs 262 crore, segment profit ~Rs 37 crore (~14% margin).
- Management guidance: piping revenue Rs 1,000-1,100 crore in FY26.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with turnaround traits. Process piping rides refinery/defence/nuclear capex cycles — boom and bust by nature. Right now the cycle turns up: record quarters, doubling order book, PAT tripling. Lynch would treat it as a cyclical — buy early in the upcycle, sell when order inflow peaks. ## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Piping division — dominant segment (~90%+ of revenue) and the main profit pool.
2. Order book execution (6-18 month cycles) — converts to revenue at improving margins.
3. Export project orders — margin kicker over domestic tenders.

## Red flags
- Classic cyclical: a capex pause would crush both revenue and multiples.
- Working capital heavy — long receivable cycles with EPC customers.
- Single-segment dependence (piping).

## Verdict
Ride the capex upcycle with a tight stop — a cyclical, not a compounder.
