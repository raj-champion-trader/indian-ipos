# Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BHARATCOAL)

## What it does
BCCL, a Coal India subsidiary, digs coking coal, non-coking coal, and washed coal from 34 mines in Jharia and Raniganj coalfields (Jharkhand, West Bengal). It sells mostly to steel plants and power companies. It produced nearly 58.5% of India's domestic coking coal in FY25.

## The numbers
- Total income: Rs 14,653 cr (FY24), Rs 14,402 cr (FY25); H1 FY26 (Sep 2025) Rs 6,312 cr.
- PAT: Rs 1,564 cr (FY24), Rs 1,240 cr (FY25, 8.6% margin); H1 FY26 PAT only Rs 124 cr — sharp deterioration (screener FY26 annualized: sales Rs 13,645 cr, PAT Rs 128 cr, negative operating profit).
- EBITDA margin: 17.0% (FY24), 16.4% (FY25), 7.3% (H1 FY26).
- Borrowings Rs 1,559 cr (IPO review) / screener Rs 233 cr (Mar 2025); net worth Rs 5,831 cr; RoNW 20.8%, ROCE 30.1% (FY25).
- Expenses (FY25, screener): total Rs 14,159 cr; finance cost Rs 72 cr; depreciation Rs 581 cr. Employee and store/spares costs dominate this PSU cost base (detailed split: data not found).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical, plain and simple. Coal prices and notified prices swing, costs stay sticky, and a government owner caps pricing freedom. Not a fast grower, not a turnaround story worth chasing while H1 FY26 runs at near-zero profit. IPO (Jan 2026) came purely as an OFS — sellers took all money out.

## TAM
India coking coal demand projected to grow from 67 million tonnes (FY25) to 138 million tonnes by FY35 (IPO DRHP coverage). BCCL holds 58.5% of domestic production — a dominant but slowly shrinking share as imports fill the gap.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Coking coal production volumes and grade mix from its 34 mines.
2. Notified coal prices vs rising wage, store, and explosives costs — the H1 FY26 profit crash.
3. Washed coal premium and mine-reopening programs (discontinued underground mines).

## Red flags
- PAT collapsed ~90% in H1 FY26 vs FY25 run-rate.
- 87% of revenue from top 10 customers (SAIL-type steel PSUs).
- Contingent liabilities Rs 4,930 cr; rising debtor days (48.2 vs 39.1); Rs 598 cr non-core income propping results.

## Verdict
A cyclical PSU at a cyclical trough in earnings — watch, don't chase.
