# Fabtech Technologies Limited (FABTECH)

## What it does
Fabtech Technologies builds cleanrooms and turnkey pharma plants — it designs, engineers, procures, installs and validates factories where drugmakers make medicines. Clients also hire it for standalone equipment supply. Listed on NSE (recent SME-to-main migration).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 225 crore (FY24); Rs 236 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 287 crore.
- PAT: Rs 26 crore (FY24); Rs 38 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 34 crore.
- Warning inside FY25: PAT includes other income of Rs 25-30 crore — core operating profit actually fell to Rs 27 crore (FY25) from Rs 35 crore (FY24).
- Interest Rs 3-4 crore; depreciation ~Rs 2 crore; total expenses Rs 210 crore (FY25).
- Market cap ~Rs 656 crore; P/E ~19; ROE ~8.6%; ROCE ~11%.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical riding a structural theme. Pharma capex (US FDA-compliant plants, PLI schemes, China+1) feeds its order book — that tailwind gives growth-cycle upside. But FY25 profit quality looks weak once you strip the other income. Small-cap, P/E 19 moderate. Lynch would call it a cyclical tied to the pharma-capex cycle, needing evidence of operating-margin recovery before commitment.

## TAM
India pharma capex/cleanroom engineering market — growing with PLI and US-FDA capacity additions; precise TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Order book and execution of turnkey pharma/cleanroom projects.
2. Operating margin on projects (fell in FY25) vs other-income support.
3. Pharma capex cycle — client expansion for regulated-market (US/EU) supply.

## Red flags
- FY25 profit heavily dependent on other income (Rs 25 crore of Rs 38 crore PAT).
- Operating profit declined FY24→FY25 despite higher sales.
- Small size (Rs 656 crore market cap) and client concentration risk in pharma capex.

## Verdict
Pharma-capex cyclical with a profit-quality question mark — watch operating margins, not headline PAT.
