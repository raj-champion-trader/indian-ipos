# Sunshine Pictures Limited (SUNSHINE)

## What it does
Sunshine Pictures makes films, web series and TV serials, and sells them to studios, streamers and TV channels. The company owns the intellectual property behind its content and earns money again when those rights get re-licensed. Hits include The Kerala Story, Holiday and the Commando and Force franchises.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 139 crore FY24 (consolidated), Rs 106 crore FY25 (consolidated), Rs 76 crore FY26 (standalone) — lumpy, hit-driven.
- PAT: Rs 52 crore FY24, Rs 34 crore FY25, Rs 40 crore FY26 (standalone); PAT margin ~34% FY25, ~54% FY26 (standalone).
- Debt near zero (D/E ~0.06 FY26); ROE ~32% FY26.
- IPO listed Aug 2026 (Rs 282 crore issue; Rs 173 crore fresh + OFS); object: working capital and general corporate purposes.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical/asset play hybrid — a hit-driven content house with valuable film IP library (The Kerala Story franchise). High margins but revenue swings with release slates, so this is no fast grower. Promoters held 100% pre-IPO; IPO itself dilutes. P/E ~34x on FY25 earnings (screener, post-listing). The IP library and near-zero debt give the asset-play angle.

## TAM
Data not found (Indian film & OTT content market size not stated in sources reviewed).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Box-office performance of new releases (e.g. The Kerala Story drove FY24 spike).
2. Satellite/digital rights monetisation of owned IP library.
3. Production cost control per film (low cost base — only ~28 permanent employees).

## Red flags
- Revenue depends on a handful of films; a dry slate year craters earnings.
- FY25 and FY26 revenue declined vs FY24.
- Promoter-heavy, small permanent team; working-capital object of IPO while needing Rs 940 crore working capital per RHP.

## Verdict
A hit-driven IP library at 34x earnings — only for investors who believe the next Kerala Story comes soon.
