# Shringar House of Mangalsutra Ltd (SHRINGARMS)

## What it does
Company designs and makes mangalsutras in 18k, 22k and 24k gold, studded with American diamonds, cubic zirconia and semi-precious stones. It sells mostly B2B to jewellers — the largest organized B2B mangalsutra maker in India with ~6% of the organized market.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,102 cr (FY24), Rs 1,430 cr (FY25), Rs 2,246 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 2,462 cr.
- PAT: Rs 31 cr (FY24), Rs 61 cr (FY25), Rs 115 cr (FY26).
- OPM: 4.5% (FY24) to 7% (FY26). 5-year sales CAGR 34%, profit CAGR 54%.
- ROE 26.3% (FY26). P/E ~17.6x; market cap ~Rs 2,123 cr.
- Borrowings rose to Rs 189 cr (Mar 2026) from Rs 23 cr (FY21).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a wedding-essential niche — mangalsutras sell regardless of fashion cycles. Dominance angle: largest organized B2B player, riding the shift from unorganized to organized jewellery making. 17.6x P/E against 30%+ growth = attractive PEG. Recent IPO (2025).

## TAM
Indian organized mangalsutra/wedding jewellery market — company holds ~6% of organized mangalsutra segment; absolute TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. B2B mangalsutra volume growth (gold-makes margin on making charges).
2. Gold price levels — revenue inflates with gold rates, making charges drive real profit.
3. Shift of jewellers from in-house to outsourced organized manufacturing.

## Red flags
- Razor-thin margins (4-7%) — gold price volatility can swing results.
- Borrowings grew 8x since FY21 (gold metal loans/working capital).
- Unorganized competition; dependence on wedding demand seasonality.

## Verdict
Fast-growing wedding niche leader at fair price — cyclical gold risk stays in mind.
