# GSP Crop Science Ltd (GSPCROP)

## What it does
GSP Crop Science makes crop protection chemicals — fungicides and insecticides — selling both formulations (ready-to-use products) and technicals (active ingredients), for 40+ years since 1985. It sells to Indian farmers through distributors and also exports.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,309 crore (FY23), Rs 1,226 crore (FY24), Rs 1,409 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 1,630 crore — standalone, per screener.in
- Net profit: Rs 8 crore (FY23), Rs 46 crore (FY24), Rs 76 crore (FY25), Rs 97 crore TTM
- Operating margin: 4.6% (FY23), 8% (FY24), 9% (FY25) — thin, typical agrochemicals
- Finance cost: Rs 30 crore (FY25); borrowings Rs 247 crore
- ROE 16%, ROCE 19% (FY25); 3-year profit growth 125%
- Market cap Rs 2,915 crore; P/E 28.9

## Lynch lens
Cyclical — agrochemicals swing with monsoon, crop prices and inventory cycles. But profit has compounded fast off a low base (FY23 PAT just Rs 8 crore). Mix of cyclical with a turnaround-in-margins flavour: OPM nearly doubled from FY23 to FY25. P/E 28.9 against TTM profit growth ~28% gives PEG near 1 — reasonable if margins hold.

## TAM
Indian crop protection / agrochemical market (a few billion dollars, exports included) — exact TAM figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Formulations business — branded products to farmers, higher margin than technicals
2. Export sales of technicals/formulations — volume lever
3. Raw material cost cycles — OP margins doubled FY23→FY25 on favourable input prices; reversal hurts

## Red flags
- Thin 9% operating margin leaves little cushion
- Rs 247 crore borrowings plus Rs 30 crore interest against Rs 76 crore FY25 PAT
- Working-capital-heavy business; monsoon/pest-cycle dependence

## Verdict
Margin-recovery cyclical at fair price — profits could shrink as fast as they grew.
