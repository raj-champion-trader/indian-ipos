# Orkla India Ltd (ORKLAINDIA)

## What it does
Orkla India sells Indian food products — breakfast to dinner, snacks, beverages and desserts — under heritage brands MTR, Eastern and Rasoi Magic. Consumers pick its spice mixes, masalas and ready-to-eat packets off grocery shelves. Norwegian group Orkla owns it; it listed in 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,172 crore (FY23), Rs 2,356 crore (FY24), Rs 2,395 crore (FY25); FY26 TTM Rs 2,571 crore — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 339 crore (FY23), Rs 226 crore (FY24), Rs 256 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 294 crore
- Operating margin: 14% (FY23, FY24), 17% (FY25)
- Almost debt free; ROCE 14.9%, ROE 11.2%; 5-year sales CAGR 21%, profit CAGR 20%
- Expenses (FY25): total Rs 1,998 crore, interest Rs 7 crore, depreciation Rs 62 crore; raw material (spices/packaging) dominates; head-wise %: data not found.

## Lynch lens
A stalwart — a branded packaged-food business with pricing power, near-zero debt and trusted legacy brands (MTR in the South, Eastern in Kerala). Lynch loved boring consumer brands with repeat purchase. Growth has slowed (FY25 revenue up just ~2%), making it a steady compounder rather than a fast grower. ## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. MTR brand (ready-to-eat, mixes) — flagship profit pool
2. Eastern masalas — Kerala-strong regional moat
3. Gross-margin recovery — OPM jump from 14% (FY24) to 17% (FY25) drove the profit bounce

## Red flags
- Revenue growth stalled: Rs 2,356 crore (FY24) to Rs 2,395 crore (FY25), ~2%
- PAT still below FY23's Rs 339 crore
- Regional concentration (South India-heavy brands)
- Parent-group ownership may cap minority upside

## Verdict
A dependable food stalwart at stalwart prices — buy for steady compounding, not fireworks.
