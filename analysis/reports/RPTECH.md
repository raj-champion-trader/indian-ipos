# Rashi Peripherals Limited (RPTECH)

## What it does
Rashi Peripherals (RP Tech India) distributes computers, phones, parts and IT gear from 80+ global brands — including Apple, Intel and Dell — to shops and businesses across India. Adds value with credit, logistics and technical support, plus cloud and enterprise solutions. Founded 1989; listed 2024.

## The numbers
- Revenue FY25: ~Rs 13,773 crore (+24.1% YoY). PAT FY25: ~Rs 223 crore (+~46% YoY); EBITDA +17.5% YoY.
- Net margin ~1.6% — classic distribution economics.
- FY25 operating cash flow negative (~-Rs 299 crore), turned positive in FY26.
- Q1 FY27: revenue ~Rs 5,102 crore (+62% YoY), PAT Rs 104.6 crore (+69% YoY).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical-ish stalwart in a slow-growth body with fast-growth recent numbers. Distribution looks dull — boxes from A to B — and that suits Lynch fine. But 1.6% net margins mean one inventory write-down or credit slip wipes a year of profit growth. Margins on Applepremium products hold up better than commodity parts. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: ~25x on Rs 223 crore PAT at ~Rs 5,500 crore market cap; with 40-60% profit growth, PEG looks under 1 — but distribution growth rarely sustains that pace.

## TAM
India ICT distribution / consumer tech market: very large, grows with digitisation and PC penetration; exact TAM figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Consumer technology distribution (laptops, components) — volume base of profit.
2. Apple and premium brand lines — better margin per unit than commodity gear.
3. Enterprise solutions and cloud services — services layer growing faster than hardware.

## Red flags
- Razor-thin ~1.6% margins; no room for error.
- Working capital swings: negative Rs 299 crore operating cash flow in FY25.
- Vendor dependence: losing a key brand (e.g., Apple) would gut the model.

## Verdict
A decent, cheap-ish distributor riding the India device boom — fine at low multiples, dangerous when priced as a tech stock.
