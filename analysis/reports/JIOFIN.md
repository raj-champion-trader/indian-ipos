# Jio Financial Services (JIOFIN)

## What it does
Jio Financial lends money to consumers, shops and homebuyers through its app and Reliance's network, and runs insurance broking, digital payments and an asset-management joint venture with BlackRock. It sits inside the Reliance group.

## The numbers
- Revenue (total income): FY24 ~Rs 1,854 cr; FY25 ~Rs 2,043 cr; FY26 ~Rs 3,513 cr (quarter sums, screener.in) — strong growth.
- PAT: FY24 ~Rs 1,604 cr; FY25 ~Rs 1,613 cr; FY26 ~Rs 1,561 cr — flat, because interest costs jumped from ~zero to ~Rs 900 cr FY26 as lending books scaled and Sep-quarter investment one-offs normalized.
- OPM ~60-90%; minimal equity capital stress; promoter Reliance holds ~majority.
- Note: Sep quarters carry large treasury/other income (Rs 226-238 cr spikes).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart-in-the-making with fast-grower ambitions — a balance-sheet-heavy lender, so watch ROE (~9% per screener flag), not just growth. Niche: cheapest distribution cost in Indian lending via Relatio's retail reach. Insider buying: promoter buying data not found. P/E: data not found.

## TAM
India retail credit — tens of lakh crores of addressable loans; digital lending growing 20%+ annually (RBI/industry estimates). Exact figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Net interest income — loan book growth minus rising borrowing costs (the FY26 margin squeeze).
2. Treasury/investment income on the huge equity base (Sep-quarter one-offs).
3. Scaling of new verticals — insurance distribution, payments, AMC (BlackRock JV).

## Red flags
Growth without PAT growth; ROE ~9% vs banks' cost of equity; execution across too many verticals at once; dependence on Reliance ecosystem; credit-quality record still short.

## Verdict
Well-capitalised fintech platform, but currently a "show-me" stock — profits must catch up to revenue.
