# Omnitech Engineering Ltd (OMNI)

## What it does
Omnitech makes high-precision engineered components and assemblies for safety-critical industrial uses, and ships them worldwide. It also builds turnkey industrial automation systems and custom mechanical solutions for global customers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 345 crore FY25 vs Rs 188 crore FY24 (+83%); TTM Rs 575 crore.
- PAT: Rs 44 crore FY25 vs Rs 22 crore FY24 (+100%); TTM Rs 101 crore. OPM 33% FY25.
- Borrowings: Rs 355 crore Mar 2025; interest Rs 29 crore, depreciation Rs 37 crore FY25.
- Working capital stress: debtor days 172, WC days 71 to 192 FY25; CFO Rs -71 crore, FCF Rs -146 crore FY25.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — revenue nearly doubling with 33% operating margins, a rare mix. But the cash story tells a different tale: profits sit in receivables, not the bank. Lynch always said check the cash. Strong candidate if receivables unwind; dangerous if they never do. No P/E noted — check before acting.

## TAM
Global precision-engineered components and industrial automation market: large multi-billion-dollar global opportunity; segment-specific TAM data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Export demand for precision machined components (safety-critical applications).
2. Turnkey automation project execution margins.
3. Working-capital cycle — 172 debtor days decide whether profits convert to cash.

## Red flags
- Negative operating cash flow (Rs -71 crore FY25) despite record profits.
- Debt rising to Rs 355 crore FY25 to fund working capital.
- Customer concentration risk typical of export precision players (mix data not found).

## Verdict
Blazing growth, questionable cash conversion — a fast grower that must prove its receivables.
