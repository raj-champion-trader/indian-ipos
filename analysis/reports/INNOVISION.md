# Innovision Ltd (INNOVISION)

## What it does
Innovision provides manpower services, toll plaza management and skill-development training. It started in private security and grew into facility management, staffing and running toll collection operations for road operators. Thin-margin, people-intensive services business.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 510 crore (FY24), Rs 893 crore (FY25), Rs 979 crore (FY26) — standalone, per screener.in
- Net profit: Rs 11 crore (FY24), Rs 31 crore (FY25), Rs 38 crore (FY26)
- Operating margin: 3.5% (FY24), 6% (FY25), 6% (FY26) — very thin
- Finance cost: Rs 7 crore (FY24), Rs 10 crore (FY25), Rs 13 crore (FY26)
- Borrowings: Rs 125 crore (Mar 2026); promoter holding 74.17%
- ROE 3-year average 26%; ROCE 20.5%
- Market cap Rs 589 crore; P/E 32.6

## Lynch lens
Boring business done at scale — staffing and toll management never make headlines, and 75% sales growth in FY25 shows real momentum. But 6% operating margins mean tiny errors wipe out profit. PEG roughly 32/20 ≈ 1.6 on recent profit growth — not cheap for this margin profile. Q1 FY27 reported negative operating profit — early warning worth checking.

## TAM
Indian staffing/facility management and toll operations market — very large, labour-intensive; exact TAM figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Manpower/staffing contracts — volume engine at very low margin
2. Toll plaza management fees — steadier, annuity-like income
3. Wage cost and statutory compliance management — the entire profit hinge at 6% OPM

## Red flags
- GST notice of Rs 10.70 crore covering FY20-25 — over a quarter of market-cap-adjacent risk against FY26 PAT of Rs 38 crore
- 6% operating margins; Q1 FY27 operating profit negative
- Rising finance cost (Rs 13 crore FY26) on Rs 125 crore borrowings

## Verdict
Fast-growing but razor-thin services business with a live tax dispute — speculatives only.
