# Vibhor Steel Tubes (VSTL)

## What it does
Vibhor makes steel pipes and tubes — ERW black pipes, galvanised pipes, hollow sections and primer-painted pipes — at plants in Maharashtra and Andhra. It sells largely to pipe brands and infrastructure users, with Jindal Pipes as a key customer.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 1,073 cr; FY25 Rs 996 cr; FY26 Rs 1,151 cr (quarter sums, screener.in).
- PAT: FY24 ~Rs 18 cr; FY25 ~Rs 12 cr; FY26 ~Rs 9 cr — thin and shrinking.
- OPM: only 3-5% every quarter; interest ~Rs 15 cr/yr and depreciation ~Rs 17 cr FY26 eat most of the operating profit.
- IPO-listed February 2024.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical, commodity converter with no pricing power. Steel tube making converts HR coil into pipe for a small conversion fee — high steel prices help inventory gains, crashes hurt. Lynch rule: in a 4%-margin business, a 1% margin slip halves profit; that showed up FY25-FY26. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: data not found.

## TAM
India steel tubes/pipes market — tens of thousands of crores, growing with water, gas, housing and infra pipelines (industry estimates). Exact sourced TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Conversion spread — pipe price minus HR coil cost.
2. Customer concentration with Jindal Pipes — volumes hinge on that relationship (DRHP flagged; exact FY share data not found).
3. Interest and freight costs on the thin margin.

## Red flags
Sub-5% OPM; falling PAT despite rising FY26 sales; heavy customer concentration; commodity price exposure; working-capital intensity.

## Verdict
Thin-margin commodity cyclical — pass unless bought very cheap at a steel-cycle trough.
