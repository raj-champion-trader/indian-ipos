# J Kumar Infraprojects Limited (JKIPL)

## What it does
Company builds heavy civil infrastructure — metro rail (elevated and underground), roads, flyovers, bridges, and water/irrigation works — mainly as government contracts in and around Maharashtra. It earns money by executing these projects over several years.

## The numbers
- FY25 consolidated revenue ~Rs 5,650+ crore (FY26 revenue Rs 5,723 crore, +1% YoY, implies strong FY25 base).
- Q4 FY25 PAT Rs 114 crore, up 14.3% YoY; FY26 PAT Rs 387 crore (-1% YoY), so FY25 PAT ~Rs 390 crore.
- Order book: Rs 22,238 crore as of March 31, 2025; Rs 20,946 crore as of June 30, 2025 (~4x annual revenue — multi-year visibility).
- Q1 FY26 PAT grew 19% YoY to Rs 103 crore.
- Expense heads: construction materials (cement, steel, aggregates), subcontracting, equipment and employee costs — exact percentages data not found.

## Lynch lens
A cyclical contractor, not a fast grower — earnings depend on government capex cycles and execution. The metro-rail niche across multiple Indian cities gives it a steady order pipeline, which adds a stalwart flavour. Government receivables and working capital remain the classic drags. P/E: data not found; PEG not computable.

## TAM
India's National Infrastructure Pipeline envisages ~Rs 100+ lakh crore of capex through 2025, with metro rail expansion in 20+ cities — civil EPC demand stays deep for years (source: government/industry estimates).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Metro rail projects (elevated + underground) — largest chunk of the Rs 22,238 crore order book (FY25).
2. Roads, flyovers and bridges — steady execution margin base.
3. Project execution pace and commodity (cement/steel) costs — swings margin either way.

## Red flags
- Government payment cycles strain working capital.
- Order book declined from Rs 26,320 crore (Q1 FY25) to Rs 20,946 crore (Jun 2025) — check bidding wins.
- Zero growth in FY26 revenue/PAT — cyclical flattening.

## Verdict
Solid, boring metro-rail contractor with big order cover — a cyclical to buy on weakness, not chase.
