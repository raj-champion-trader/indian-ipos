# Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Ltd (NIVABUPA)

## What it does
Niva Bupa sells health insurance policies to families and companies across India. It collects premiums, invests them, and pays hospital claims. It runs the business mostly digitally — apps, cashless hospital network — which keeps servicing cheap.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 4,115 crore (FY24) → Rs 5,374 crore (FY25); ~30% growth. TTM Rs 7,907 crore (FY26).
- PAT: Rs 82 crore (FY24) → Rs 214 crore (FY25); Q1 FY26 PAT Rs 138 crore.
- Market share: ~5.31% of health insurance, 17.59% among standalone private health insurers (FY25).
- Debt: Rs 250 crore (FY25). ROE ~3.5%. Market cap ~Rs 15,127 crore; P/E ~87 (Aug 2026).
- Expense structure: total expenses Rs 5,292 crore (FY25), dominated by insurance claims payout; finance cost near zero. Employee/claims split per head: data not found on screener view.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring-but-growing industry. Health insurance in India grows ~10-15%+ a year as hospital costs rise and people buy cover. Niva Bupa grows faster than the market via its digital-first model. But P/E ~87 against ~30% earnings growth gives a rich PEG (~2.9) — you pay tomorrow's price today. Insiders/promoters at 55.3%; no notable insider buying signal found.

## TAM
Indian health insurance industry premium pool: Niva Bupa holds ~5.3% of it (FY25); absolute industry size data not found in fetched sources — health GWP runs in the Rs 1 lakh crore+ range per IRDAI industry reports (verify before relying).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gross written premium growth (~30% FY25) — more policies, more float.
2. Claims/combined ratio discipline — small improvement swings profit hugely (OPM only 1.5% FY25).
3. Investment income on the premium float — supports net profit.

## Red flags
- Thin margins: a bad claims year wipes out profit.
- P/E ~87 leaves no room for error.
- Competition from big general insurers (Star, HDFC Ergo, ICICI Lombard) and new digital players.

## Verdict
Quality fast grower at a fat price — Lynch would admire the business but wait for a cheaper entry.
