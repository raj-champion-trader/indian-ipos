# Unimech Aerospace and Manufacturing Limited (UNIMECH)

## What it does
Unimech Aerospace makes high-precision tooling and components — engine and airframe tools, ground-support equipment and machined parts — that airlines and MRO shops need to build and repair aircraft. It sells to Airbus, Boeing, GE, Rolls-Royce and Dassault across 2,500+ SKUs. Listed via Rs 500 crore IPO in December 2024.

## The numbers
- Standalone (screener): sales Rs 28 crore FY23; Rs 31 crore FY24; Rs 42 crore FY25; net profit Rs 2 / 2 / 19 crore.
- Consolidated IPO-basis: FY25 revenue ~Rs 241–260 crore with PAT ~Rs 77 crore; FY24 ~Rs 214 crore revenue, ~Rs 58 crore PAT.
- Almost debt-free; 5-year profit CAGR ~59%; market cap ~Rs 7,651 crore; P/E ~492 (standalone basis — distorted; consolidated P/E materially lower but still very high).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in an elite niche. Certification-heavy aerospace tooling keeps competitors out — a Lynch-style "boring business with a toll-bridge position". ROE ~3-4% post-IPO equity dilution looks weak but should recover. Even on consolidated earnings (~Rs 77 crore FY25), ~Rs 7,650 crore market cap prices years of flawless ramp; PEG poor unless profit triples. Post-IPO order wins (e.g., long-term FACC order) support growth.

## TAM
Global aerospace tooling/MRO precision parts market: multi-billion USD annually (industry estimates); India MRO market projected ~USD 4–6 billion by 2030 (industry estimates). Exact sourced figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Aero tooling and precision components for global OEMs/MRO (core profit engine).
2. New verticals: defence, power-generation turbines, semiconductor tooling.
3. Operating leverage from Bengaluru capacity expansion funded by IPO.

## Red flags
Extreme valuation on any earnings basis; standalone-vs-consolidated reporting gap; customer concentration among a handful of global aerospace names; ROE depressed post-listing.

## Verdict
Wonderful niche, absurd price — a watchlist stock, not a buy, until growth catches up with the multiple.
