# Studds Accessories Limited (STUDDS)

## What it does
Company makes two-wheeler helmets and motorcycle riding accessories. Sells under two brands: Studds (mass market) and SMK (premium). Ranks as the world's second-largest helmet maker by volume.

## The numbers
- Revenue Rs 630 crore, net profit Rs 84 crore (FY26, latest available).
- Operating margin ~20%, ROE 17.2%, ROCE 23.3% (FY26).
- Nearly debt-free: borrowings only ~Rs 4 crore.
- 5-year sales CAGR ~6%, profit CAGR ~2%; TTM sales growth ~10% — growth has slowed.
- Screener lists FY24/FY25 detail lines as data not found in fetched summary; use FY26 as anchor.
- Market cap ~Rs 1,771 crore; P/E ~23 (Aug 2026).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with asset-play hints. Boring product, dominant niche (world #2 by volume), strong balance sheet, ~20% operating margins. But growth of ~6% over five years makes it a slow grower, not a fast grower. PEG roughly 23/6 ≈ 3.8 on 5-yr growth — expensive for the growth on offer; on TTM growth of ~10% PEG ≈ 2.3. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
Indian two-wheeler helmet market — estimated at roughly Rs 8,000-12,000 crore and growing with enforcement of helmet laws and premiumisation (exact figure: data not found in fetched sources). Company also exports globally.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Helmet volumes and mix shift toward premium SMK brand (drives margin).
2. Raw material costs (ABS plastic, polycarbonate, EPS) — biggest expense head.
3. Domestic two-wheeler demand plus export demand.

## Red flags
- Poor 5-year sales growth (5.6%).
- Profit growth (2% CAGR) lags revenue.
- Small recent-IPO float; valuation at 23x for single-digit growth.

## Verdict
World-class niche player at a fair-ish price — a stalwart to hold, not a fast grower to chase.
