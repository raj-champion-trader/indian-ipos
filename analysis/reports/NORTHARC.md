# Northern Arc Capital (NORTHARC)

## What it does
Northern Arc lends money and buys loan portfolios serving small borrowers in India — microfinance, small business, consumer and vehicle loans. It also runs a platform that connects other lenders with these borrowers. It has enabled over Rs 2.3 lakh crore of financing reaching 130 million-plus lives.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,870 cr TTM (Mar 2026), +21% YoY — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 439 cr TTM, +50% YoY
- Financing margin ~20%; ROE 11.1%; ROCE 9.7%
- 5-yr sales CAGR 32%; 5-yr profit CAGR 43%
- Total assets Rs 16,745 cr; borrowings Rs 12,258 cr; debt/equity ~3.1x (normal for an NBFC's leverage, funded largely by debt capital-market instruments)
- P/E ~10.5x; book value Rs 241/share; no dividend

## Lynch lens
Cyclical-ish financial at a value price. A niche player in the "lending to lenders" and financial inclusion space — unglamorous, which Lynch liked. 10.5x P/E against 50% TTM profit growth looks cheap, but NBFC earnings swing with credit cycles; the low P/E prices that risk. ROE of 11% limits the compounding engine.

## TAM
Indian financial-inclusion / retail credit to underserved households and businesses — a market running into lakhs of crores of annual disbursements (precise TAM figure not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Microfinance portfolio and debt-platform flows — the core spread business
2. Small business and consumer/vehicle finance direct lending
3. Credit costs (provisions) — the main swing factor between profit and loss, especially after MFI stress in FY25 industry-wide

## Red flags
- 3.1x leverage; low interest coverage flagged by screener
- 11% ROE — thin for the risk taken
- Exposure to microfinance repayment cycles (Assam-style regulatory/credit events hit the sector)
- No dividends

## Verdict
Cheap niche lender — fine as a cyclical bet, not a compounder.
