# EMS Limited (EMSLIMITED)

## What it does
EMS builds complete water and wastewater projects for government clients — from design through construction — under turnkey EPC contracts. It also runs sewage treatment plants on operation contracts.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 484 cr (FY23), Rs 719 cr (FY24), Rs 941 cr (FY25), Rs 608 cr (FY26, standalone)
- Net profit: Rs 103 cr (FY23), Rs 150 cr (FY24), Rs 182 cr (FY25), Rs 88 cr (FY26) — profit fell ~52%
- OPM: 29% (FY23) declining to 21% (FY26), TTM ~19%
- Almost debt-free; but debtor days ~223 and working-capital days ~400
- ROE ~9% (FY26)

## Lynch lens
A former fast grower hitting a cyclical air-pocket: three years of strong growth reversed sharply in FY26 with both revenue (-35%) and profit (-52%) down. The asset-light, debt-free balance sheet gives it turnaround potential if order execution revives, but 223 debtor days means the government holds the cash. Too early to call the bottom; wait for order-book evidence. P/E not retrieved.

## TAM
Indian water/wastewater EPC market — large government programme pipeline (data not found for exact size).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Order book and execution pace of STP/water contracts (FY26 slowdown drove the profit fall)
2. Operating margins on new bids (OPM slid 29% to 21%)
3. Receivables collection — 223 debtor days convert profit into IOUs

## Red flags
- FY26 profit halved; TTM still falling (Rs 66 cr)
- Working capital days ~400 — severe cash strain
- Margin erosion across four straight years

## Verdict
Deep-value turnaround candidate, but the falling knife still falls — demand proof of order revival first.
