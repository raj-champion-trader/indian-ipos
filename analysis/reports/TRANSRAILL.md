# Transrail Lighting Ltd (TRANSRAILL)

## What it does
Company builds power transmission lines and substations on turnkey EPC contracts, and makes transmission poles and towers. Wires the grid — poles, lines, and everything between power plant and plug.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 4,077 cr (FY24), Rs 5,308 cr (FY25), Rs 6,879 cr (FY26) — 5-yr CAGR 26%
- PAT: Rs 235 cr (FY24), Rs 334 cr (FY25), Rs 412 cr (FY26) — 5-yr CAGR 33%
- OPM: 14% (FY24), 15% (FY25), 12% (FY26)
- Finance cost: Rs 246 cr (FY24), Rs 300 cr (FY25), Rs 219 cr (FY26); borrowings Rs 661-678 cr
- ROE ~20%; negative working capital days — customers effectively fund the business

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a favoured sector — India's grid build-out gives multi-year order visibility, and 33% profit CAGR with 20% ROE backs it. Negative working capital is the classic Lynch "boring business, beautiful balance sheet" trait. Debt exists but finance cost fell in FY26 and it earns its keep easily. ## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. T&D EPC order book execution — revenue engine riding grid capex
2. Poles/towers & monopole manufacturing — in-house supply supports margin vs pure EPC peers
3. Finance cost (Rs 219 cr FY26, down from Rs 300 cr FY25) — deleveraging adds straight to PAT

## Red flags
- OPM slipped to 12% (FY26) from 15% (FY25)
- Working-capital swings and Rs 661 cr+ debt typical of EPC
- Government/utility receivable risk

## Verdict
Grid-capex fast grower with self-funding working capital — the strongest story in this batch; buy-the-dip candidate.
