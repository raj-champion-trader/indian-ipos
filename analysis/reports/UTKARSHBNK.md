# Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Ltd (UTKARSHBNK)

## What it does
Utkarsh Small Finance Bank started as a microfinance institution and now runs a full small finance bank focused on rural and semi-urban financial inclusion, mainly in Bihar, UP, and eastern India. It raises deposits (Rs 21,654 cr, Mar 2026) and lends small-ticket microfinance loans plus MSE and other secured products. Listed July 2023.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 3,178 cr (FY24), Rs 3,765 cr (FY25).
- PAT: Rs 498 cr (FY24) collapsed to Rs 24 cr (FY25); TTM profit growth -168%.
- FY25 financing margin swung negative (-13%) per screener data; interest expenses Rs 1,742 cr of Rs 2,518 cr total expenses (FY25) — ~69% of expenses.
- GNPA 6.09%, NNPA 2.86% (Mar 2026); total assets Rs 28,869 cr; ROE crashed from 20% (FY24) to 1% (FY25).

## Lynch lens
A textbook turnaround candidate — profitable micro-lender (20% ROE in FY24) hit by the sector-wide microfinance stress of FY25: credit-cost blowout crushed PAT to near zero and the stock has compounded -29% over 3 years. If asset quality peaks and normalises, earnings could rebound sharply; if not, dilution or capital strain follows. Classic high-risk turnaround, not for widows and orphans.

## TAM
India microfinance industry gross loan portfolio stands around Rs 4-5 lakh cr (approximate, FY25 era); SFBs also compete for rural deposits. Exact figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Microfinance loan book performance — credit costs/npas decide profit (the FY25 loss driver).
2. Cost of deposits vs lending yield spread (interest expense ate 69% of FY25 expenses).
3. Branch-led deposit growth reducing reliance on costly borrowings.

## Red flags
- PAT fell 95% (Rs 498 cr to Rs 24 cr, FY24 to FY25).
- GNPA 6.09% (Mar 2026); collections in eastern-India microfinance remain fragile.
- Negative financing margin in FY25 per screener data.

## Verdict
Deep turnaround bet on microfinance cycle recovery — big upside if npas peak, big pain if they don't.
