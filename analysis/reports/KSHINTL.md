# KSH International Limited (KSHINTL)

## What it does
KSH International makes magnet winding wires — the copper wires wound inside transformers, motors, generators, and EV traction motors. It ranks as India's third-largest maker and the largest exporter of these wires.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 1,049 cr; FY24 Rs 1,383 cr; FY25 Rs 1,928 cr; FY26 Rs 3,107 cr (Screener).
- PAT: FY23 Rs 27 cr; FY24 Rs 37 cr; FY25 Rs 68 cr; FY26 Rs 110 cr.
- OPM thin at ~5-6% all years; net margin ~3.5% (FY26).
- 5-year sales CAGR 46%, profit CAGR 50%.
- Debt Rs 321 cr (Mar 2026); ROE 20.1%, ROCE 21.5%.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with a growth costume. Revenue nearly tripled FY24→FY26, riding the copper-price surge plus EV-motor and capital-goods demand — but 6% operating margins mean the business earns a thin spread over copper, and copper swings both ways. The exporter-plus-EV angle gives it a niche, yet margin fragility keeps it out of true fast-grower territory.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Copper prices (revenue and margin pass-through dominate the P&L).
2. EV traction motor and industrial motor demand.
3. Export volumes — largest Indian exporter of magnet wire.

## Red flags
5-6% OPM leaves no cushion in a copper downturn; working-capital-heavy (80-day cash cycle); profit quality tied to commodity prices; segment mix data not found.

## Verdict
A copper-price bet dressed as an EV story — fine while copper runs, painful when it reverses.
