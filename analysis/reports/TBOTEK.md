# TBO Tek Ltd. (TBOTEK)

## What it does
TBO Tek runs Travelboutiqueonline.com (TBO), a B2B travel booking platform. Travel agents in 100+ countries use it to book flights and hotels for their customers. The company earns a commission on each booking.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 476 cr (FY23), Rs 539 cr (FY24), Rs 576 cr (FY25, standalone); TTM ~Rs 592 cr
- Net profit: Rs 52 cr (FY23), Rs 47 cr (FY24), Rs 57 cr (FY25); TTM ~Rs 60 cr
- OPM: 18% (FY23) falling to ~11% (FY25) — margin compression visible
- 5-year CAGR: sales ~42%, profit ~44% (older base years were small)
- P/E ~300 at Rs 1,683 (market cap ~Rs 18,272 cr); debtor days ~310

## Lynch lens
A fast grower in niche B2B travel distribution — a classic "boring middleman with a toll booth" angle. But the market already prices perfection at P/E ~300 against single-digit near-term profit growth, so PEG looks terrible. Post-IPO lock-in expiries and promoter/PE (General Atlantic) stake overhang remain a watch item; insider selling data not found.

## TAM
Global travel distribution / B2B travel booking market — exact India/global figure data not found; structural driver comes from agents in emerging markets shifting bookings online.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Hotel bookings through the platform (largest chunk of take-rate revenue)
2. Air ticketing volumes (thin margin, high volume)
3. Overseas agent base expansion (US, Europe, Middle East) vs India business

## Red flags
- P/E ~300 vs modest profit growth
- OPM slide from 18% (FY23) to ~11% (FY25)
- Debtors at 310 days — cash conversion lag
- No dividend

## Verdict
Great toll-booth business, priced like it will never stumble — wait for a margin or valuation reset.
