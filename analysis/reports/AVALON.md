# Avalon Technologies Limited (AVALON)

## What it does
Avalon assembles electronic circuit boards and builds finished boxes — the guts inside industrial machines, clean-energy gear, vehicles and defence equipment. Takes designs from OEMs and manufactures them at scale across the whole product life. IPO in 2024.

## The numbers
- Revenue FY25: ~Rs 1,098 crore (consolidated, +26.6% YoY). FY25 consolidated net profit surged ~78% to ~Rs 112.9 crore; standalone PAT ~Rs 63 crore (+126.7% YoY) — basis differences across sources.
- Q4 FY25 PAT: ~Rs 41 crore (+69% YoY).
- Order book: ~Rs 3,200 crore (Dec 2025), ~3x FY25 sales, executable over ~3 years.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower riding the India EMS wave. Defence and aerospace share of the order book gives a sticky, high-margin kicker. Box-build integration (not just board assembly) earns deeper customer lock-in — a Lynch-style "boring but sticky" angle. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: data not found; PEG N/A.

## TAM
India electronics manufacturing / EMS market: large and growing with PLI push; exact figure data not found. Defence electronics TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Industrial and clean energy PCB/box-build volumes — the base profit pool.
2. Defence & aerospace orders — higher margin, long-cycle contracts.
3. Operating leverage — profit grew ~3x revenue growth pace in FY25.

## Red flags
- Customer concentration typical of EMS; margin swings on component costs.
- Working capital intensity of long-cycle defence orders.
- Q1 FY27 PAT margin ~7.2% — thin buffer if orders slow.

## Verdict
Order-book-backed fast grower at the right end of the China+1/defence cycle — watch receivables, not just headlines.
