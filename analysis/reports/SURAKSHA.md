# Suraksha Diagnostic Ltd (SURAKSHA)

## What it does
Runs a diagnostics chain in Eastern India offering 1,000+ pathology tests and 450+ radiology tests through a hub-and-spoke model (central lab plus collection centres). Sells mostly to individual patients (B2C) plus some hospital contracts (B2B).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 219 cr (FY24) → Rs 252 cr (FY25) → Rs 310 cr (FY26)
- PAT: Rs 23 cr (FY24) → Rs 31 cr (FY25) → Rs 31 cr (FY26) — flat FY26
- OPM: 33% (FY24), 33% (FY25), 31% (FY26)
- Borrowings: Rs 92 cr (FY24) → Rs 89 cr (FY25) → Rs 130 cr (FY26, rising)
- ROCE 17%, ROE 14.1%; P/E ~49.5
- Market cap ~Rs 1,772 cr

## Lynch lens
Regional niche player — Dr Lal-style diagnostics economics confined to Eastern India. High ~33% operating margins show pricing power versus mom-and-pop labs. But PAT flat in FY26 and rising debt suggest growth costs money (new centres). P/E 49.5 against flat profits gives an absurd PEG — market pays for a story, not current numbers. Recently listed (late 2024 IPO); no insider-buying data found.

## TAM
Indian diagnostics market estimated at Rs 80,000+ cr, largely unorganised; organised chains hold a small share and gain ground (industry estimates; precise figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Test volumes from hub-and-spoke network expansion in East India
2. Radiology high-value tests (better realisation than routine pathology)
3. Operating leverage vs depreciation on new centres — PAT stalled (FY26) as expansion costs bit

## Red flags
- PAT flat FY26 despite 23% revenue growth
- Borrowings rising (Rs 130 cr FY26)
- P/E ~49.5 vs zero profit growth
- Regional concentration risk (Eastern India)

## Verdict
Decent regional diagnostics niche — but 49x earnings for flat profits makes this a story stock, not a Lynch bargain.
