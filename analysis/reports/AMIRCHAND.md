# Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Ltd (AMIRCHAND)

Note: NSE symbol AMIRCHAND (SME IPO March 2026, listed on both NSE and BSE) refers to the same company that screener.in carries under the slug "AEROPLANE", after its Aeroplane basmati brand. See also AEROPLANE.md in this folder.

## What it does
Company processes and exports basmati and other rice (kolam, sona masuri, idli, ponni) plus FMCG staples like aata, maida, sooji, besan, salt and sugar. Runs the full chain from procurement and storage to processing, packaging and distribution, exporting to 37+ countries with 100 trademarks.

## The numbers
- FY26 revenue Rs 2,030 crore vs Rs 1,709 crore FY25 vs Rs 1,341 crore FY24; PAT Rs 76 crore (FY26) vs Rs 40 crore (FY25) vs Rs 22 crore (FY24) (screener).
- IPO RHP figures: FY23 revenue Rs 1,317.9 crore / PAT Rs 17.5 crore; FY25 revenue Rs 2,004 crore / PAT Rs 60.8 crore.
- OPM 8-9%; interest Rs 86 crore (FY26); borrowings Rs 758 crore; ROCE 13.8%.
- IPO price band Rs 201-212; listed ~5.7% below issue price on NSE. P/E ~20.4; market cap ~Rs 1,810 crore.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring staple — ranked 3rd among listed basmati peers, profit compounding ~40% over 5 years at P/E ~20 (PEG ~0.5 if growth holds). Cyclical basmati prices and heavy working capital temper the story.

## TAM
India basmati exports roughly USD 5-6 billion a year in recent crop years (approximate trade figures); global demand from Middle East, US, EU, Iran.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Basmati export volumes and price realization spreads — dominant.
2. Paddy procurement cost and inventory positions.
3. Interest expense (Rs 86 crore FY26) on working-capital debt.

## Red flags
- Thin margins, commodity cycles, Rs 758 crore borrowings.
- SME-platform liquidity; weak listing.

## Verdict
Fast-compounding basmati exporter at a fair price — cyclical and leveraged, so size the position accordingly.
