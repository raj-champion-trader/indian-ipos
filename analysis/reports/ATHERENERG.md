# Ather Energy Limited (ATHERENERG)

## What it does
Ather designs and sells premium electric scooters in India, plus charging networks and software services around them. Makes smart, connected two-wheelers and earns extra from accessory sales, service and charger installations. Listed in 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue FY25: ~Rs 4,244 crore. Net loss FY25: ~Rs 651 crore (consolidated, per company disclosures; screener shows -Rs 390 crore on a different basis).
- Revenue per scooter FY25: Rs 1,28,295 — stays premium, avoids price wars.
- Volumes: ~2.63 lakh scooters sold in FY26 (+69% YoY). Q1 FY27 net loss narrowed 71% YoY to Rs 51.1 crore on revenue of Rs 1,217 crore (+~90% YoY).

## Lynch lens
Turnaround / early fast grower. Losses today, but the loss shrinks fast as volumes double and fixed costs spread. Lynch would want to see the loss narrowing quarter after quarter — exactly what Q1 FY27 shows. No niche moat yet: competes with Ola, TVS iQube, Bajaj. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: not meaningful (loss-making); PEG N/A.

## TAM
India electric two-wheeler market: rapid penetration phase; EV share of two-wheeler sales rising toward double digits. Exact TAM figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Scooter volumes — every extra unit spreads R&D and factory cost; the single biggest loss-narrowing lever.
2. Gross margin per scooter — battery cell costs and premium pricing mix.
3. Non-vehicle revenue (accessories, charging, service subscriptions) — small but higher margin.

## Red flags
- Still loss-making at PAT level (FY25).
- Cash burn and past funding dependence; competition from deep-pocketed incumbents.
- FAME/PLI subsidy policy changes swing economics.

## Verdict
A real turnaround-in-progress bet — volumes compound, but hold only while losses keep shrinking.
