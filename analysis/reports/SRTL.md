# Shree Ram Twistex Ltd (SRTL)

## What it does
B2B cotton yarn maker producing compact ring-spun and carded yarns (combed and carded) for knitting and weaving. Sells to textile manufacturers, garment exporters, fabric processors and bulk buyers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 213 Cr; FY24 Rs 232 Cr; FY25 Rs 255 Cr; FY26 (TTM) Rs 302 Cr (screener).
- PAT: FY23 Rs 2 Cr; FY24 Rs 7 Cr; FY25 Rs 8 Cr; FY26 (TTM) Rs 14 Cr. TTM PAT growth ~78%.
- OPM: 8-9% (FY23-FY25), 12% (FY26 TTM); net margin 0.9% → 4.6%.
- Interest Rs 9-10 Cr/yr; depreciation ~Rs 9 Cr. Borrowings cut Rs 62 Cr (FY25) to Rs 30 Cr (FY26) post-IPO.
- Promoter holding 34.59%; no dividend.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical in a genuinely boring business — spinning cotton yarn, exactly the kind of dusty operation Lynch liked at the right point in the cycle. Cotton textiles run 3-4 year cycles; FY26 margin pop (12% OPM) plus post-IPO debt cut (interest burden halves) pushed PAT up sharply. But 34.59% promoter stake stays modest and micro-cap size means liquidity and governance risks.

## TAM
Indian technical/textile yarn spinning sector — India textile industry ~US$ 150-200 bn overall; spinning segment commodity-grade (industry estimates). Company-specific TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Cotton vs yarn spread — raw cotton cost dominates expenses; margin cycle drives everything.
2. Debt reduction post-IPO (Rs 62→30 Cr, FY25→FY26) cutting interest from ~Rs 10 Cr.
3. Volume/capacity utilisation in compact yarn niche (higher realisation than carded).

## Red flags
- Hairline margins historically (net margin <1% in FY23) — cotton spike wipes profit.
- Low promoter stake (34.59%) for a small company.
- No dividends; interest coverage still tight.

## Verdict
Small cyclical at a favourable point — trade the cycle, don't marry it.
