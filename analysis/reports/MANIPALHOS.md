# Manipal Health Enterprises Limited (MANIPALHOS)

## What it does
Manipal runs one of India's largest private hospital chains, with multi-specialty hospitals plus clinics and diagnostic centres across the country. Patients pay for surgery, treatment, and tests; insurers and government schemes cover much of the bill.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 6,172 cr; FY25 Rs 8,242 cr; FY26 Rs 10,336 cr (consolidated, Screener).
- PAT: FY24 Rs 533 cr; FY25 Rs 1,082 cr; FY26 Rs 917 cr.
- Revenue up ~67% over two years; PAT margin ~9-13% range.
- Standalone FY25: revenue Rs 3,104 cr, PAT Rs 535 cr, OPM 27%.
- Borrowings Rs 2,516 cr (FY25, standalone); 5-year sales CAGR 21%, profit CAGR 26%.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with growth spurts. Hospitals throw off steady cash, enjoy pricing power (healthcare demand rises with incomes and insurance coverage), and Manipal ranks among the top few national chains — an oligopoly niche. FY25 PAT doubled (likely aided by one-offs and occupancy), then FY26 dipped ~15%: expect lumpier earnings than a classic stalwart, but the bed-additions story compounds patiently. Watch bed count growth and average revenue per occupied bed rather than any single year's PAT.

## TAM
India private-hospital market size: data not found in sources fetched (healthcare demand driven by low beds-per-1,000 population and rising insurance penetration).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. In-patient occupancy and average revenue per occupied bed (ARPOB).
2. New hospital additions and ramp-up of recently opened units.
3. Payor mix — insured and cashless cases versus discounted government-scheme pricing.

## Red flags
FY26 consolidated PAT fell ~15% from FY25's peak; Rs 2,516 cr borrowings; doctor-dependence and attrition risk; capex-heavy expansion; medical-regulation and pricing-control risk.

## Verdict
A steady compounder in a grow-forever industry — a hold-forever stalwart candidate bought at the right price.
