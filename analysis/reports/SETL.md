# Standard Engineering Technology Limited (SETL)

## What it does
SETL (formerly Standard Glass Lining Technology) builds glass-lined reactors, dryers, heat exchangers, storage tanks and piping for pharma and chemical factories. Designs, manufactures, installs and services the equipment end-to-end. Listed via IPO; renamed in December 2025.

## The numbers
- Trailing revenue around Rs 849 crore with profit ~Rs 88.7 crore (recent screener snapshot); quarterly revenue Rs 188 crore (up 34% QoQ) with ~11% net margin.
- Virtually debt-free with healthy interest coverage.
- Order book over Rs 1,000 crore, ~30% from exports.
- Exact FY25 full-year split: data not found. Aim: India's largest glass-lined equipment maker by FY27 (company statement).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower / niche stalwart hybrid. A genuinely dull business — glass-lined vessels for drug factories — with pricing power because switching costs run high and few Indian rivals exist at scale. Growth of 30-40% plus exports and a clean balance sheet fits Lynch's "boring niche" playbook. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: ~9x book multiple noted but earnings P/E: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Glass-lined reactors and vessels sold to pharma/chemical capex — core profit pool.
2. Exports (~30% of order book) — margin accretive.
3. Lifecycle services and spares — recurring, high-margin layer.

## Red flags
- Trades at ~9x book — rich for a capital-goods maker.
- Lumpy order book; any pharma capex slowdown hits revenue.
- Renaming/repositioning adds story risk versus substance.

## Verdict
Quality niche compounder at a full price — buy the dips, not the story peaks.
