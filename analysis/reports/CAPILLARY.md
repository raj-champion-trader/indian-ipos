# Capillary Technologies India Limited (CAPILLARY)

## What it does
Capillary Technologies sells AI-powered loyalty and customer-engagement software to large enterprises on multi-year cloud subscriptions. Brands use it to run reward points, offers and shopper analytics. Recently listed (2025); serves 415+ brands including 20 Fortune 500 clients across 49 countries.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 174 crore (FY24); Rs 212 crore (FY25) — ~22% growth.
- Net result: loss of Rs 52 crore (FY24); profit of Rs 3 crore (FY25) — first turnaround year.
- Expenses: Rs 162 crore (FY24) → Rs 141 crore (FY25); employee/other split data not found.
- Global reach: 1.9 bn+ consumers on platform (as of Mar 2026).

## Lynch lens
Turnaround-to-fast-grower candidate. SaaS loyalty management runs a defensible niche — sticky multi-year contracts, switching costs, global client base — the kind of recurring-revenue business Lynch would call a grower hiding in a boring category ("frequent-flyer points software"). First profit year (FY25) marks the inflection. Key unknown: post-listing P/E and margin trajectory.

## TAM
Global customer loyalty management market estimated in the US$5-10 bn range and growing double-digits (industry analyst estimates); exact sourced figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Recurring subscription revenue from enterprise loyalty contracts.
2. Employee/operating cost leverage as revenue scales (expenses fell while sales grew).
3. Cross-sell of AI analytics and newer products into existing brands.

## Red flags
- Barely profitable (Rs 3 crore FY25) — one bad quarter flips it to loss.
- SaaS sales costs (stock-based comp) often hit reported profit; detail data not found.
- Competition from Salesforce/Adobe loyalty modules and point solutions.

## Verdict
Sticky niche SaaS just crossing into profit — worth tracking for consistent margin expansion before buying hard.
