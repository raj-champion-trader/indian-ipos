# Kross Ltd (KROSS)

## What it does
Company forges and machines heavy metal parts for trucks and tractors — trailer axles and suspensions, axle shafts, gears, and transmission components. It makes safety-critical parts, integrated from forging through machining. Based in Jamshedpur, operating since 1991; listed September 2024.

## The numbers
- Revenue: ~Rs 620 cr (FY25); Rs 673 cr (FY26, +8.5%) — TTM ~Rs 718 cr (+17%).
- PAT: ~Rs 48 cr (FY25); Rs 55 cr (FY26, +15%); TTM Rs 58 cr.
- P/E ~24 (current, at Rs 215); market cap ~Rs 1,380 cr; 543 employees.

## Lynch lens
Boring-business candidate — unglamorous forged truck axles, exactly the "metal-bender nobody notices" Lynch liked. Growth runs mid-teens off commercial-vehicle and tractor cycles, so it carries cyclical torque. Exports and trailer-axle localisation (import substitution) give a niche angle, though exact shares: data not found. P/E 24 vs ~15% growth puts PEG near 1.5 — not cheap.

## TAM
Indian trailer axle/suspension and forged auto components market — grows with M&HV truck and trailer demand plus import substitution; specific sourced figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Trailer axle and suspension sales — the flagship line, riding commercial vehicle demand.
2. Tractor components (axle shafts, PTO shafts, ring gears) — farm-cycle diversification.
3. Export volumes and steel (raw material) prices — forgings margins swing with input cost pass-through.

## Red flags
- Cyclical end markets (trucks, tractors) — FY26 growth of just 8.5% already shows moderation.
- High customer concentration (top-5 share flagged in filings; exact % data not found).
- Trading at ~24x earnings for a mid-teens grower — full valuation.

## Verdict
Quality niche auto-component cyclical growing steadily — Lynch would like the business but want the stock cheaper.
