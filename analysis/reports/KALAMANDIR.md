# Sai Silks (Kalamandir) Ltd (KALAMANDIR)

## What it does
Company runs ethnic-wear stores — sarees and festive clothing — under four formats: Kalamandir (premium), VaraMahalakshmi Silks (ultra-premium), Mandir and KLM Fashion Mall (value). It sells to shoppers in South India through 86+ stores (Aug 2026) across Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu, plus online. Wedding and festive seasons drive most of the business.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 1,374 cr (FY24), Rs 1,462 cr (FY25), Rs 1,654 cr (FY26). Growth ~13% in FY26.
- Net profit: Rs 101 cr (FY24), Rs 85 cr (FY25, hit by one-off tax), Rs 141 cr (FY26).
- OPM ~15-16% (FY24-FY26). Borrowings fell from Rs 526 cr (FY23) to Rs 361 cr (FY26).
- ROCE 14.4%, stock P/E ~9.8, dividend yield ~1.7%. Inventory days ~311 (FY26) — sarees sell slowly.

## Revenue streams
Sarees dominate revenue (exact FY split: data not found on free screener). Four store formats span premium, ultra-premium and value segments; revenue per format split — data not found.

## Expenses
Cost of goods (silk and garments purchase) forms the largest head; other heads — data not found in detail. Interest Rs 38 cr, depreciation Rs 59 cr (FY26).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical/asset-heavy retailer with a South-India ethnic niche — closer to a slow-grower-stalwart mix. Boring business, family-controlled (promoter 61%), at ~1x book and ~10x earnings. PEG near 1 (10 P/E vs ~13-16% growth) — not a classic fast grower. FIIs/DIIs cut stakes from ~24% (FY24) to ~7% (Jun 2026); price fell ~51% in one year.

## TAM
Indian ethnic-wear market roughly USD 15-20 bn, growing high-single-digits (industry estimates, FY24); South India bridal saree niche a large slice of it.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Wedding/festive season saree sales (Q3 Dec quarter strongest — Rs 449 cr sales, Dec 2024).
2. New store openings (79 stores FY24 to 86 by Aug 2026) and same-store growth.
3. Falling interest cost (Rs 60 cr FY24 to Rs 38 cr FY26) as IPO proceeds repaid debt.

## Red flags
High inventory (311 days) and long cash cycle (~249 days FY26). One-off tax spike crushed FY25 PAT. Institutional investors exiting. Consumer discretionary slowdown risk.

## Verdict
Cheap, family-run ethnic-wear retailer recovering from an inventory-heavy cycle — a borderline turnaround at 1x book; watch same-store sales and inventory.
