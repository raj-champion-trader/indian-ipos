# Saatvik Green Energy Ltd (SAATVIKGL)

## What it does
Saatvik Green Energy makes solar PV modules (4.86 GW capacity, over 5.5 GW delivered since inception), and also runs solar EPC projects and operations & maintenance services. It listed via IPO in September 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,090 cr (FY24), Rs 1,974 cr (FY25) — 81% growth. 5-yr sales CAGR 71%.
- PAT: Rs 100 cr (FY24), Rs 156 cr (FY25); but TTM PAT has collapsed to Rs 61 cr (-53%) with TTM operating profit near zero (OPM 0.1%).
- Debt modest: Rs 161 cr (FY25); reserves jumped to Rs 979 cr post-IPO.
- FY25 OPM 10%; 3-yr ROE average 35.2% (pre-IPO equity base).

## Lynch lens
A solar-module maker in a brutal commodity cycle — Chinese oversupply crushed module prices, and TTM operating profit near zero shows it. Historically a fast grower, today it trades as a turnaround/cyclical bet on the ALMM (domestic content) protection regime. High risk: profits have already halved post-listing. P/E data not found.

## TAM
India solar module demand — the country targets 500 GW renewables by 2030 with 40+ GW annual module demand; domestic module market estimated at tens of billions of dollars (approximate; exact figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Solar module sales volumes and prices — module price collapse drove TTM profit down 53%.
2. EPC order backlog and O&M annuity income.
3. ALMM/benefits of domestic manufacturing protection policy (BCD on imports).

## Red flags
- TTM operating margin ~0.1% — the core business stopped making money at the operating line.
- Other income (Rs 120 cr annually) props up reported profit.
- Sector-wide oversupply; margin visibility poor despite 71% revenue CAGR.

## Verdict
High-growth revenue story with collapsing margins — wait for the module-price cycle to turn before buying.
