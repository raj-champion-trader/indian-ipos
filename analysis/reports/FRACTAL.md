# Fractal Analytics Ltd (FRACTAL)

## What it does
Fractal builds artificial intelligence and advanced analytics solutions for large global enterprises. It sells AI engineering, data science consulting, and GenAI products (Vaidya, Eugenie, Kazoku) mostly to Fortune 500 clients. People-heavy services business, similar in shape to IT services.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,159 crore (FY24), Rs 1,437 crore (FY25), Rs 1,950 crore (TTM) — standalone, per screener.in
- Net profit: Rs -31 crore (FY23), Rs 122 crore (FY24), Rs 132 crore (FY25), Rs 221 crore (TTM)
- Operating margin: 1.3% (FY23) to 14% (FY25), 17% TTM — sharp turnaround in profitability
- Finance cost: Rs 12 crore (FY25); borrowings Rs 112 crore — almost debt free
- Market cap Rs 14,519 crore; P/E 61.2 — rich valuation for ~15% net margin

## Lynch lens
Fast grower candidate — sales growing ~24% (FY25) and ~36% TTM, with margin expansion driving faster profit growth. Boring? No — AI hype means the stock rarely goes unnoticed. PEG roughly 61/30 ≈ 2 on TTM profit growth — expensive even for a fast grower. Lynch would want to check how much profit comes from GenAI hype clients vs sticky annuity contracts.

## TAM
Global AI/analytics services market — very large and growing fast; exact TAM figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. AI/analytics consulting engagements with large enterprise clients (main revenue engine)
2. GenAI product platforms (Vaidya, Eugenie, Kazoku) — margin kicker
3. Employee cost leverage — operating margin jumped from 1.3% (FY23) to 14% (FY25) as utilisation/billing improved

## Red flags
- P/E 61 with net margin still ~11-13% — priced for perfection
- Employee cost dominates (typical 60%+ for such firms; exact %: data not found)
- GenAI spending could re-prioritise or in-house at big clients

## Verdict
Quality AI fast grower, but at 61x earnings the price already assumes years of flawless execution.
