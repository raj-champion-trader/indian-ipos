# Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Ltd (YATHARTH)

## What it does
Yatharth runs a chain of 9 multi-specialty and super-specialty hospitals with 2,800+ beds and 12 Centres of Excellence, mostly around Delhi-NCR (Noida, Greater Noida) in Uttar Pradesh. Patients pay for tertiary care, trauma treatment, transplants, and advanced surgeries.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 635 crore (FY23), Rs 860 crore (FY24), Rs 1,207 crore (FY25), Rs 1,334 crore (FY26) — 5-year profit CAGR 57%.
- Net profit: Rs 114 crore (FY24), Rs 131 crore (FY25), Rs 170 crore (FY26).
- OPM ~23-24% (FY26); ROE ~10.4% — low for hospitals. Market cap ~Rs 9,321 crore; P/E ~51 (Aug 2026).
- Depreciation heavy (Rs 101 crore FY26) from acquired hospitals; interest small (Rs 13 crore FY26).
- Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026) sales Rs 393 crore, net profit Rs 45 crore — steady.

## Lynch lens
A growth-by-acquisition hospital chain — fast grower on the top line (sales up ~2x in three years), but ROE of ~10% shows the acquisitions earn less than they cost so far. Lynch liked growth, but only when it converts to shareholder return. At 51x earnings with 15-20% profit growth, PEG ~2.7 — priced for perfection.

## TAM
Data not found (NCR private tertiary-care market size not reliably published). Demand driver: healthcare penetration in UP/NCR.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. In-patient volume and ARPOB growth across the NCR hospitals (core earnings engine).
2. Acquired/added hospitals ramping occupancy — depreciation (Rs 101 crore FY26) drags reported profit while beds fill.
3. Other income (Rs 26-29 crore FY25/FY26) and low interest costs boosting PBT.

## Red flags
- Low ROE ~10% over 3 years despite fast growth — acquisitions diluting returns.
- OPM sliding: 29% (FY23) to 23% (FY26).
- P/E ~51 vs decelerating profit growth (FY26 PAT +24%).
- Integration/lease costs of newly bought hospitals keep biting.

## Verdict
Decent regional hospital compounder wearing a very expensive P/E — the stock needs years of clean execution to grow into its price.
