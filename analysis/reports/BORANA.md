# Borana Weaves Ltd (BORANA)

## What it does
Company weaves unbleached synthetic grey fabric in Surat — the raw polyester cloth others dye and print for fashion and home decor. Also makes polyester textured yarn, mostly for its own looms. A classic upstream textile commodity play.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 199 cr (FY24), Rs 290 cr (FY25) — 46% growth
- PAT: Rs 24 cr (FY24), Rs 40 cr (FY25) — 67% growth
- OPM: 21% (FY24), 22% (FY25)
- Borrowings: Rs 71 cr (FY24), Rs 61 cr (FY25); interest 1.7% of sales (FY25); depreciation 4.5%
- ROE 35% (FY24), ROCE 33% (FY25)

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — revenue up 46% and PAT up 67% in FY25, with 22% OPM and 33% ROCE. Grey fabric sounds boring (good, in Lynch terms), but margins this fat in commodity weaving invite competition; polyester fabric cycles can turn fast. Recent IPO, so track post-listing quarters for margin hold-up. PEG needs current P/E — data not found.

1. Polyester yarn cost vs grey fabric realisation — the spread behind the 22% OPM
2. Capacity/capex — new looms drive the 46% FY25 revenue jump
3. Low interest burden (1.7% of sales) — modest debt keeps profit intact
