# Molbio Diagnostics Ltd (MOLBIO)

## What it does
Molbio builds rapid point-of-care molecular diagnostic devices and tests — best known for Truenat, a chip-based portable platform used to detect TB and other infections at clinics rather than big labs. It sells instruments plus a recurring stream of test chips and consumables worldwide.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 971 crore FY25 vs Rs 756 crore FY24 (+28%).
- PAT: Rs 140 crore FY25 vs Rs 111 crore FY24. OPM 23% FY25 (27% FY24).
- Almost debt free (borrowings Rs 26 crore FY25); interest cost Rs 9 crore FY25.

## Lynch lens
A niche technology player with a genuine razor-and-blade model — install Truenat machines, sell chips forever. Lynch likes niche dominators; Truenat holds a strong position in TB screening under national health programmes. Growth of ~28% with ~14% PAT margin gives a plausible fast-grower profile; check current P/E against that growth for PEG.

## TAM
Global point-of-care molecular diagnostics market: multi-billion dollar and growing double digits (industry estimates); India TB-elimination programme plus 160+ country exports form the addressable base. Exact split data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Consumables/test chips recurring revenue on installed Truenat base.
2. Instrument sales to government health programmes (India TB programme) and exports.
3. Product mix shift toward high-margin consumables vs one-time instrument sales.

## Red flags
- Government tender dependency — order timing lumpy.
- Margin dipped from 27% (FY24) to 23% (FY25).
- Competition from larger diagnostics players entering POC molecular.

## Verdict
Fast-growing niche diagnostics play — a genuine Lynch-style candidate if valuation stays sane.
