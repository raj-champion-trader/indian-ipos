# Vishnu Prakash R Punglia Ltd (VPRPL)

## What it does
VPRPL builds water-supply infrastructure — pipelines, water tanks, reservoirs, treatment plants and irrigation works — for central and state governments across 9 states. It also does railway and road projects. Water-supply projects supplied 76% of revenue in H1 FY25.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 1,474 cr (FY24), Rs 1,237 cr (FY25, -16%), Rs 851 cr (FY26, -31%).
- Net profit: Rs 122 cr (FY24), Rs 59 cr (FY25), Rs -150 cr (FY26 loss).
- OPM: 14% (FY24) to -10% (FY26) — huge write-offs/provisions hit Q3 and Q4 FY26.
- Borrowings Rs 650 cr (FY26); receivables 247 days; contingent liabilities Rs 601 cr.

## Revenue streams
Water-supply projects 76% (H1 FY25), railway and road/civil works the rest. Sales by vertical for FY26 — data not found (business collapsed during the year).

## Expenses
Construction materials (pipes, cement, steel) and subcontractors dominate (detail: data not found). FY26: interest Rs 74 cr, depreciation Rs 22 cr.

## Lynch lens
Failed growth story turned distressed turnaround. Promoters pledged 95.2% of their holding and cut stake from 67.8% to 38% (Jun 2026) — insiders running, the loudest red flag Lynch cites. FY26 loss of Rs 150 cr with negative OPM, 247-day receivables, and a BB+ credit rating say the business model broke on payment delays and aggressive bidding. Book value Rs 50/share vs price Rs 32, but book quality looks doubtful.

## TAM
Indian water infrastructure — Jal Jeevan Mission and allied programmes spent lakhs of crores through FY24-FY26; plenty of TAM, capital-intensive clients.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Government receivable collection — the FY26 collapse came from payment delays and provisions.
2. Project execution margin (OPM 14% FY24 to -10% FY26).
3. Interest burden Rs 74 cr (FY26) on Rs 650 cr debt against vanishing operating profit.

## Red flags
95.2% promoter pledge. Promoter stake nearly halved in a year. FY26 loss Rs 150 cr. Negative operating cash flow FY24-FY25 (-316 cr, -207 cr). Rating downgraded to BB+/Stable. Contingent liabilities Rs 601 cr.

## Verdict
Distressed EPC with insiders exiting — classic value trap; touch only after pledges clear and cash flow turns positive.
