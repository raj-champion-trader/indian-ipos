# Glottis Ltd (GLOTTIS)

## What it does
Glottis moves goods for customers by road, sea and air — freight forwarding, customs clearance, cargo handling, warehousing and third-party logistics under one roof. It serves exporters, importers and industrial clients across India.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 723 cr (FY26, Mar 2026); net profit Rs 38 cr (FY26).
- FY24 and FY25 full-year figures: data not found on public screens.
- OPM ~7% (FY26); ROE ~13.4%.
- Borrowings Rs 53 cr (Mar 2026) — small debt; interest Rs 3 cr; depreciation Rs 3 cr.
- P/E ~15.8x; market cap ~Rs 594 cr (2026).
- Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026): revenue Rs 235 cr, PAT Rs 11 cr.

## RevenueStreams
Freight forwarding (ocean/air/road) forms the core; customs clearance, cargo handling, warehousing and 3PL add the rest. Exact split: data not found.

## Lynch lens
Boring business in the best Lynch sense — trucks, ships and paperwork nobody talks about. A small-cap logistics player at ~15.8x earnings with modest debt and 13% ROE. Thin 7% margins cap the upside; scale and route density decide the winner. Insider buying: data not found. PEG: data not found.

## TAM
Indian logistics market estimated at ~US$ 150-200 billion and growing (industry estimates, mid-2020s). Precise period figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Freight volumes and rates on ocean/air routes (freight-cycle sensitive).
2. Margin on customs clearance and value-added services.
3. Operating leverage from the asset-light forwarding model — low interest and depreciation keep PAT close to operating profit.

## Expenses
Transportation/freight purchase costs dominate (total expenses Rs 673 cr of Rs 723 cr revenue, FY26); interest Rs 3 cr; depreciation Rs 3 cr.

## Red flags
- Thin ~7% operating margins; profit swings with freight rates.
- Small scale versus giants like Delhivery and Blue Dart.
- Full FY24/FY25 comparatives: data not found.

## Verdict
A dull-but-decent small-cap logistics operator — cheap-ish and lightly leveraged, but it needs scale before it earns a portfolio slot.
