# Unicommerce eSolutions Ltd (UNIECOM)

## What it does
Company runs a SaaS platform (Uniware) that runs e-commerce operations for brands, sellers, and logistics companies — managing orders, inventory, warehouses, and omnichannel fulfilment. It serves 7,596+ clients across India, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 90 cr (FY23), Rs 104 cr (FY24), Rs 114 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 123 cr.
- PAT: Rs 6 cr (FY23), Rs 13 cr (FY24), Rs 22 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 29 cr.
- OPM ~31% (TTM); ROE 18.1%, ROCE 24.1%; P/E ~33 at ~Rs 980 cr market cap.
- Almost debt free — borrowings ~Rs 1 cr (Mar FY26). Expenses ~Rs 85 cr TTM, mainly employees and technology.

## Lynch lens
The ideal Lynch niche: boring back-office plumbing for online sellers, asset-light, debt-free, 30%+ margins, and profits compounding faster than sales. Small revenue base caps the story's size, but every new e-commerce warehouse adds subscription revenue. Growth rate (revenue ~10%/yr recently) looks modest against the 33 P/E — the PEG question matters here.

## TAM
India e-commerce enablement/SaaS market — India e-commerce GMV projected well above $100 bn (FY24 industry estimates), with warehouse and order-management software a growing multi-hundred-million-dollar slice.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Uniware SaaS subscriptions from brands and D2C sellers — recurring, high-margin core.
2. Employee/technology cost discipline — converts 31% OPM into 20%+ net margin.
3. New market expansion (Middle East, Southeast Asia) and logistics clients — growth leg.

## Red flags
- Revenue growth (~10% FY25) slower than typical SaaS Compounders; margin story doing the heavy lifting.
- Client concentration among large e-commerce players; platform dependence on marketplace ecosystems.
- Small absolute base (Rs 114 cr FY25 revenue) — one lost client hurts.

## Verdict
Clean, boring, profitable e-commerce plumber at a fair-but-not-cheap price — accumulate on dips.
