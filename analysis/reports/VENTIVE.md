# Ventive Hospitality Ltd (VENTIVE)

## What it does
Company owns and runs luxury hotels and resorts — 13 hotels, 2,178 keys across India, Maldives and Sri Lanka, ~80% luxury focus. Operates them under Marriott, Hilton, Minor Hotels and Atmosphere brands (asset owner, not the brand).

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 431 crore (FY23) → Rs 478 crore (FY24) → Rs 561 crore (FY25) → Rs 638 crore (FY26); TTM Rs 650 crore.
- Net profit: Rs 131 crore (FY23) → Rs 166 crore (FY24) → Rs 134 crore (FY25) → Rs 229 crore (FY26).
- OPM ~49-59% range across years; FY26 profit includes Rs 106 crore other income.
- Borrowings Rs 862 crore (FY26); ROE ~5%; ROCE ~7.1%.
- Market cap ~Rs 13,711 crore; P/E ~57 (Aug 2026); stock -17% over 1 year; no dividend.

## Lynch lens
Asset play in luxury hotels. The real story: prime land and luxury properties (Ritz-Carlton, etc.) bought cheap, plus Maldives resorts printing dollars. But reported numbers show low ROE (~5%) and heavy pledge — promoters pledged 36.4% of their holdings, a genuine Lynch red flag. P/E 57 prices the asset story fully.

## TAM
Indian tourism and hospitality market estimated at roughly USD 100+ billion trajectory; luxury hotel segment growing faster than mid-market; Maldives premium travel a dollar-earner (exact sourced figures: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Room revenue and occupancy at Maldives luxury resorts (high ADR, dollar earnings).
2. Indian luxury city hotels (Marriott/Hilton-flagged) banquet and F&B income.
3. Other income Rs 106 crore (FY26) and finance costs on Rs 862 crore debt.

## Red flags
- Promoters pledged 36.4% of holdings.
- Other income (Rs 106 crore) props up FY26 profit.
- 57x P/E with ~5% ROE and zero dividend.

## Verdict
Beautiful assets, ugly price and a pledged-shares red flag — admire the hotels, skip the stock for now.
