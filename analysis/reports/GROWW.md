# Billionbrains Garage Ventures / Groww (GROWW)

## What it does
Groww runs a phone app where Indians buy and sell shares, mutual funds, futures and options, and gold. Customers do everything themselves — no brokers or phone calls. It counts as India's largest digital investment platform by active users on the NSE.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY25 Rs 4,061 cr → FY26 Rs 4,645 cr; TTM Rs 5,242 cr — screener.in
- PAT: FY25 Rs 1,824 cr → FY26 Rs 2,083 cr; TTM Rs 2,440 cr
- Operating margin 59% (FY26); Q1 FY27 revenue Rs 1,501 cr, PAT Rs 735 cr (65% margin)
- ROE 28.8%; ROCE 37.3%; 3-yr sales CAGR 60%, profit CAGR 66%
- Nearly debt-free (Rs 292 cr borrowings vs Rs 8,404 cr reserves)
- P/E ~49x; market cap ~Rs 1.2 lakh cr

## Lynch lens
Fast grower riding a secular wave — financialisation of Indian savings. Scalping-style economics: each new user adds revenue at near-zero extra cost, hence 59-65% margins. Regulatory risk (SEBI's F&O curbs hit the whole industry's most lucrative stream) acts as the cycle. At 49x P/E with ~14-15% current-year profit growth, PEG looks stretched; the market prices years of future user growth.

## TAM
Indian retail investing — demat accounts have grown into the crores (well past 10 crore accounts in recent years) with low mutual-fund penetration; precise current TAM figure not found in fetched sources.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Derivatives (F&O) trading income — highest-margin revenue line and biggest profit contributor
2. Equity delivery brokerage and margin trading (MTF) interest income
3. Mutual fund distribution and other platform fees (order-book/subscription income)

## Red flags
- Heavy dependence on F&O income, which SEBI keeps tightening
- Competition from Zerodha, Angel One, Upstox caps pricing power
- 49x P/E leaves no room for a regulatory stumble
- Key-manager (founder-CEO) transition risk noted in the past

## Verdict
Brilliant fast grower priced for perfection — small position or wait for a drawdown.
