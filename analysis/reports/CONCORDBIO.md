# Concord Biotech Ltd (CONCORDBIO)

## What it does
Concord Biotech makes fermentation-based APIs — the active chemicals inside medicines — with a leading global position in immunosuppressants (transplant drugs) plus oncology and anti-infective APIs. It also sells finished-dosage formulations from Ahmedabad, Gujarat to buyers worldwide.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 1,017 crore (FY24), Rs 1,200 crore (FY25), Rs 1,055 crore (FY26) — FY26 sales fell ~12%.
- Net profit: Rs 308 crore (FY24), Rs 372 crore (FY25), Rs 259 crore (FY26). OPM fell from 42% (FY25) to 35% (FY26).
- Market cap ~Rs 15,495 crore; P/E ~55.8 (Aug 2026). ROCE ~17%, ROE ~12.5%.
- Nearly debt free (borrowings Rs 2 crore FY26); healthy dividend payout ~25-30%.
- Debtors 159 days, inventory 485 days — long but typical for fermentation cycles.

## Lynch lens
A niche pharma with genuine dominance (global leader in immunosuppressant fermentation APIs) — the moat Lynch loved. But the numbers now describe a stalwart in a rough patch: sales shrank in FY26 while the stock carries a 56 P/E. PEG is meaningless with negative growth; the market pays for a recovery. Waiting for the turnaround to show in numbers costs little.

## TAM
Data not found (global fermentation API / immunosuppressant market size not reliably published).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Immunosuppressant API sales (tacrolimus, mycophenolate mofetil — company's flagship franchise; largest profit pool).
2. Oncology and other fermentation APIs plus growing formulations business.
3. Margin compression — FY26 OPM fell 7 points to 35%, the main driver of the profit drop; other income (Rs 52 crore FY26) cushions the fall.

## Red flags
- FY26 sales -7% TTM and profit -22% TTM; 5-year sales growth only ~11%.
- P/E 55.8 against falling earnings — expensive for a shrinking year.
- Debtors 159 days and inventory 485 days pile up working capital.
- Promoter holding static at 44.08% (no fresh buying).

## Verdict
Great niche, wrong price — a quality franchise priced for a recovery that has not started; wait for growth to return before paying 56x.
