# Prostarm Info Systems Ltd (PROSTARM)

## What it does
Company makes power back-up and energy equipment — UPS systems, inverters, solar hybrid inverters, lithium-ion battery packs and transformers — under its own brand. It also builds rooftop solar plants and runs an IT solutions arm.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 256 cr (FY24), Rs 346 cr (FY25), Rs 378 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 403 cr.
- PAT: Rs 24 cr (FY24), Rs 31 cr (FY25), Rs 35 cr (FY26).
- OPM: 14% (FY25), 13% (FY26). 5-year sales CAGR 24%, profit CAGR 34%.
- P/E ~21x; market cap ~Rs 797 cr. ROE 16.6% (FY26). Debt Rs 93 cr (Mar 2026).
- Order book ~Rs 1,090 cr (recent announcements).

## Lynch lens
Small fast grower in a boring-but-necessary niche — nobody gets excited about UPS and inverters, yet data centers, factories and homes all need clean power. Lithium battery packs and solar hybrids ride the energy-transition wave. 21x against ~25% growth gives PEG near 1. Order book of Rs 1,090 cr (nearly 3x revenue) underwrites FY27 growth.

## TAM
Indian UPS/power-conditioning and energy-storage market plus PM-Kusum/rooftop solar schemes — growing double digits; exact TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. UPS/inverter hardware sales (core margin engine, ~14% OPM).
2. Lithium-ion battery packs and solar hybrid inverters (fastest-growing line).
3. Rooftop solar EPC + order-book execution (Rs 1,090 cr book).

## Red flags
- Debtor days at 246 — customers (EPC/government) pay very slowly.
- Working capital days ballooned from 112 to 192; growth consumes cash.
- Small scale vs competitors (Luminous, Su-Kam, Exide) — price war risk.

## Verdict
Niche fast grower with fat order book — the receivables pile keeps me sizing small.
