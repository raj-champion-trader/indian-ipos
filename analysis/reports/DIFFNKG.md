# Diffusion Engineers Limited (DIFFNKG)

## What it does
Company makes welding consumables (electrodes, wires, fluxes), wear plates and wear parts, and heavy engineering equipment. It sells to core-sector industries like cement, steel, power and mining, and also repairs and reconditions kilns and machinery on site. Founded 1982; listed via IPO in October 2024.

## The numbers
- FY24 revenue ~Rs 276-280 crore; FY25 revenue ~Rs 377 crore (up ~38.5% YoY; consolidated ~Rs 436 crore per Screener).
- FY25 consolidated profit ~Rs 55 crore (Screener); Q4 FY25 net profit ~Rs 16 crore, up 23% YoY.
- ROE ~13% (3-year average). Market cap ~Rs 1,538 crore (Screener). Negative free cash flow (~Rs 18 crore) in the last year despite profit — working capital and capex eat cash.
- Expense heads: data not found (typical for welding consumables makers: raw materials dominate, plus employee costs for services).

## Lynch lens
Boring business in a Lynch-friendly way — nobody gets excited about welding electrodes and kiln reconditioning, yet the parts wear out constantly, so demand repeats. It fits "niche player in an unglamorous industry serving core sectors" — closest to a small fast grower / niche stalwart. Cyclical exposure to cement/steel capex exists. ## TAM
Indian welding consumables market estimated at roughly Rs 8,000-10,000 crore, growing with core-sector capex (period/source: industry estimates — exact figure data not found). Wear-resistant solutions and reconditioning add a large services layer on top.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Wear plates and parts (~33% of revenue) — high-margin replacement demand.
2. Welding consumables (~33% of revenue) — recurring, volume-driven.
3. Heavy engineering equipment and repair/reconditioning services (balance) — kiln maintenance contracts with cement and steel plants.

## Red flags
- Core-sector (cement/steel) cyclicality; order flow tied to capex cycles.
- Negative free cash flow despite profits.
- Low ROE (~13%) for a manufacturer of this size.

## Verdict
Boring, repeat-purchase niche with strong FY25 growth — worth tracking cash conversion before buying.
