# Lenskart Solutions Ltd (LENSKART)

## What it does
Lenskart designs, makes, and sells eyeglasses, sunglasses, contact lenses, and accessories through a mix of 2,000+ stores, apps, and websites in India and abroad. Owning its factories and brands lets it price specs cheaper than traditional opticians.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 5,428 crore (FY24), Rs 6,653 crore (FY25), Rs 8,814 crore (FY26) — 58% 5-year sales CAGR.
- Net profit: Rs -10 crore (FY24), Rs 297 crore (FY25), Rs 501 crore (FY26) — first big profits after years of losses (Rs -102 crore FY22).
- OPM improved from 7% (FY23) to 20% (FY26). Market cap ~Rs 1,10,646 crore; P/E ~168 (Aug 2026).
- ROE just 6.3%; depreciation heavy at Rs 1,048 crore (FY26); interest Rs 178 crore (FY26). No dividend.

## Lynch lens
A fast grower, freshly profitable — the classic growth-story stage. The moat looks real: vertical integration plus omnichannel scale in an underpenetrated eyewear market. But a 168 P/E on a 6% ROE business screams Lynch's warning: "no price is too high for the best growth stocks — until the growth stops." At this valuation, execution must stay flawless for a decade. Promoter holding only 17.5%, typical founder-VC structure.

## TAM
Data not found (exact market size not cited here; company and industry reports peg Indian eyewear at roughly USD 6-10 bn growing double-digits — treat as unverified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. India eyewear sales — glasses/contacts through omnichannel network (bulk of Rs 8,814 crore FY26 revenue).
2. Operating leverage — OPM doubled from 10% (FY23) to 20% (FY26) as stores matured and losses flipped to Rs 501 crore profit.
3. Depreciation and interest (Rs 1,048 crore + Rs 178 crore FY26) — the biggest drag keeping reported profit far below operating profit of Rs 1,754 crore.

## Red flags
- P/E ~168; even bulls call it priced-for-perfection.
- ROE 6.3% — capital-hungry model; heavy ongoing capex.
- Promoter stake just 17.5%; no dividend.
- Quarterly profit lumpy (Q3 FY25 PAT Rs 2 crore vs Q3 FY26 Rs 204 crore) — tax and one-offs distort.
- Competition from Tata's Titan Eye+ and online rivals.

## Verdict
Great business, nosebleed price — Lynch would admire the model and refuse the valuation; wait for a growth stumble to buy.
