# Senores Pharmaceuticals Ltd (SENORES)

## What it does
Company develops and sells medicines, focusing on niche generic and specialty drugs for regulated markets (mainly the US) plus emerging markets. It sells through its US arm (including B2C brand Zoraya Pharma and the Amerisyn federal-procurement route) and through partners across ~60 countries. Listed on NSE/BSE via IPO in December 2024.

## The numbers
- FY25: total income up ~91% YoY; revenue growth ~62%; PAT ~Rs 327 crore (consolidated) — note this includes a ~Rs 78 crore tax credit, so core PBT ran ~Rs 249 crore.
- Regulated markets (mainly US): ~63% of FY25 revenue.
- FY26: regulated-market revenue ~Rs 427 crore; Q4 FY26 PAT +105% YoY; management targeted 50%+ top-line growth and PAT doubling vs FY25.
- Long-term target: Rs 3,000 crore revenue in 3-5 years (management guidance).
- Exact FY25 revenue figure: data not found in searched sources (only growth rates confirmed).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — serial acquirer model (buys US assets/files, scales niche molecules) compounding revenue 60%+. Pharma niche-play fits Lynch's "boring industry, focused operator" pattern, but acquisition-led growth carries integration risk. P/E and insider data: data not found.

## TAM
US generics market ~US$ 90-100 billion+; US federal/government drug procurement a niche within it (exact sourced figures: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. US regulated-market generic sales (~63% of revenue, FY25).
2. Margin expansion from niche/specialty mix and US federal procurement entry (Amerisyn).
3. Emerging-market branded sales (~37%) and new capacity in India and the US.

## Red flags
- PAT flattered by tax credit in FY25 — check core earnings power.
- Acquisition-driven growth depends on finding fairly priced US assets.
- US price erosion in generics; FDA compliance risk.
- Rich market expectations after the post-IPO re-rating.

## Verdict
Aggressive pharma fast grower with a credible US niche playbook — hold while growth compounds, but discount the tax-credit-inflated PAT.
