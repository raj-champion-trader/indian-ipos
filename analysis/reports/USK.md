# Udayshivakumar Infra Ltd (USK)

## What it does
USK does civil-construction work: high-altitude roads, bridges, canals, irrigation and building projects, mostly for Karnataka government bodies. It also makes ready-mix concrete (RMC). The company runs from Karnataka and listed via IPO in 2023.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 577 cr (FY24), Rs 289 cr (FY25, halved), Rs 211 cr (FY26, down another 27%).
- Net profit: Rs 30 cr (FY24), Rs -7 cr (FY25 loss), Rs 2 cr (FY26, barely positive).
- OPM: 7% (FY24) to 1% (FY25) to 7% (FY26). ROE ~1%. Borrowings rose to Rs 77 cr (FY26).
- Stock P/E ~10, price 0.7x book; contingent liabilities Rs 157 cr.

## Revenue streams
Road and irrigation/canal projects for state government the core; RMC manufacturing secondary. Exact FY split — data not found on free screener.

## Expenses
Bitumen, cement, steel and subcontracting dominate (detail: data not found). FY26: interest Rs 8 cr, depreciation Rs 9 cr — together they wiped out most operating profit.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround attempt, but with weak tools. Revenue halved then fell again — order flow dried up post-IPO. Tiny ROE (1%) and negative-to-breakeven profit mean the business barely earns its keep. At 0.7x book it trades as an asset play on equipment and receivables, but Lynch demands a catalyst, and none shows. Avoid until order book revives.

## TAM
Karnataka/India road and irrigation capex — national infrastructure pipeline ~Rs 10+ lakh crore over FY25-30; plenty of TAM, too little competitive strength.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Order-book inflow from government tenders — the FY25 collapse caused the loss.
2. Execution margin (OPM swung 1% to 7% FY25-FY26 on job mix).
3. Interest + depreciation Rs 17 cr (FY26) against tiny operating profit — fixed-cost leverage cuts both ways.

## Red flags
Sales -63% over two years. FY25 loss, FY26 near-zero PAT. Fined for delayed results filing. Contingent liabilities Rs 157 cr vs Rs 119 cr market cap. No dividend.

## Verdict
Micro-cap contractor in decline — the cheapness reflects real deterioration; skip unless a big order book rebuild shows up.
