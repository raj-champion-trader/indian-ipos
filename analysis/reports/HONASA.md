# Honasa Consumer Limited (HONASA)

## What it does
Company makes and sells beauty and personal-care brands — Mamaearth, The Derma Co, Bblunt, Aqualogica, Dr. Sheth's and Foxtale. It sells mostly through online channels and is shifting its distribution toward super-stockists for general trade. Listed in November 2023.

## The numbers
- FY24 revenue ~Rs 1,750+ crore; PAT Rs 110.6 crore.
- FY25 PAT ~Rs 72.7 crore, down ~34% YoY; FY25 revenue growth slowed (roughly Rs 1,800-1,900 crore range reported — exact audited figure data not found).
- Q1 FY25 PAT Rs 40.2 crore (up 62.9% YoY); Q4 FY25 PAT fell 18% YoY to Rs 25 crore.
- FY25 hit by one-off costs from restructuring distribution to a super-stockist model.
- Expense heads: advertising & marketing (historically ~30%+ of revenue), cost of materials, employee costs and freight — exact FY25 percentages data not found.

## Lynch lens
A consumer-brand play — Lynch liked companies building brands consumers repeat-buy. But growth decelerated (Mamaearth in single digits) and profits fell, so it trades as a former fast grower working through a distribution transition; a possible turnaround if new brands keep scaling. ## TAM
Indian beauty and personal care market estimated at ~$25-30 billion, growing high single/low double digits (period/source: industry estimates — exact figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Mamaearth — still the largest brand; single-digit growth drags the total.
2. The Derma Co and Aqualogica — newer brands scaling fast, now major profit contributors.
3. Distribution shift to super-stockists — one-off FY25 costs, but should cut channel friction and improve general-trade reach going forward.

## Red flags
- PAT fell ~34% in FY25; growth story wobbling.
- Heavy ad spend keeps margins thin for a consumer company.
- Founder-led brand companies can lose pricing power fast when growth stalls.

## Verdict
Wait for proof the distribution reset and new brands restore earnings momentum before buying the turnaround.
