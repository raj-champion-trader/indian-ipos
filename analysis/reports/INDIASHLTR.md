# India Shelter Finance Corporation Ltd (INDIASHLTR)

## What it does
India Shelter gives small home loans to self-employed borrowers in tier-2 and tier-3 towns, using property as security and its own credit model. It borrows from banks and market bonds, then lends at a spread. Listed December 2023.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Revenue: FY23 830; FY24 1,166; FY25 1,529 (about 30% growth each year)
- Net profit (per Screener): FY23 248; FY24 378; FY25 503 — note: screener PAT for finance cos can include other income; company-reported PAT runs lower — verify against annual report before acting
- Interest cost: FY25 446 (the single biggest expense); operating expenses FY25 417
- Borrowings FY25 6,246 (leverage ~4x+; growth funded by debt and equity)
- ROE 17%, P/E ~14 at price Rs 650; market cap Rs 7,081 cr; AUM roughly Rs 6,000+ cr (FY25, company statements)

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring niche — small-ticket home loans for shopkeepers in small towns that big banks skip. ~30% revenue growth, NIM around 9%+ (company disclosures). P/E ~14 with that growth gives an attractive PEG (~0.5). The catch: it sits one funding squeeze or credit-slide away from trouble; 2024-25 unsecured/small-ticket lending stress across the sector already de-rated the stock from Rs 948 to ~Rs 650.

## TAM
Self-employed affordable housing finance in tier-2/3 India — an underpenetrated market variously estimated at Rs 10+ lakh crore of addressable housing credit (industry/NHB estimates), with low penetration outside metros.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Net interest income — loan book growth (~25-30%) at wide spreads
2. Cost of funds (borrowings Rs 6,246 cr FY25) — rates and bank-line pricing set the margin
3. Credit costs — GNPA/loan losses on small-town self-employed borrowers

## Red flags
- Operating cash flow deeply negative every year (growth consumes cash via loan origination)
- Sector-wide asset-quality stress in small-ticket lending during FY25
- High leverage inherent to the HFC model; PAT figure above needs annual-report confirmation

## Verdict
Small-town home-loan fast grower at half its peak price — attractive PEG, but only for investors who trust the credit book.
