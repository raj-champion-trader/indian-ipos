# Gem Aromatics Ltd (GEMAROMA)

## What it does
Company makes speciality aroma ingredients — essential oils, aroma chemicals and value-added derivatives (70 products, four categories). Its ingredients go into toothpastes, cosmetics, pharma and personal-care products.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 447 cr (FY24), Rs 497 cr (FY25), Rs 371 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 378 cr (-22%).
- PAT: Rs 49 cr (FY24), Rs 57 cr (FY25), Rs 27 cr (FY26) — TTM down 44%.
- OPM fell from 17% (FY25) to 11% (FY26).
- P/E ~32x; market cap ~Rs 882 cr. ROE 7%; ROCE 9.1%.
- Borrowings reduced to Rs 90 cr (Mar 2026) from Rs 147 cr (FY25).

## Lynch lens
Boring-ingredient maker that turned sour — flavours and fragrances feed steady consumer staples, but this business just posted -22% sales and -44% profit in FY26. At 32x P/E with shrinking earnings, it trades like a growth stock while results scream commodity chemical. Only the asset base and low debt give comfort.

## TAM
Global flavours & fragrances / aroma chemicals ingredients market — multibillion-dollar industry served by giants (Givaudan, Firmenich); India-specific TAM data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Menthol/aroma-chemical prices and spreads — commodity cycle drives swings.
2. Export demand from oral-care and personal-care customers.
3. Raw material (crude-derivative) input costs compressing FY26 margins.

## Red flags
- FY26 revenue -22%, PAT -44%, OPM down 600 bps — sharp deterioration.
- No dividends despite years of profits.
- 32x P/E on falling earnings; one-time/other income (Rs 13.8 cr) pads results.

## Verdict
Falling knife at growth valuation — avoid until numbers stabilise.
