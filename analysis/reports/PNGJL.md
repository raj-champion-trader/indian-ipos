# P N Gadgil Jewellers Ltd (PNGJL)

## What it does
PNG Jewellers (founded 1832) sells gold, silver, and diamond jewellery through stores across Maharashtra, with strong presence in Pune and Mumbai. It also sells online. The brand, not the product, drives repeat customers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 5,982 crore (FY24) → Rs 7,523 crore (FY25) → TTM Rs 11,319 crore (FY26, screener).
- PAT: Rs 154 crore (FY24) → Rs 215 crore (FY25) → TTM Rs 436 crore (FY26).
- 5-year sales CAGR 43%; 5-year profit CAGR 122%.
- Borrowings: Rs 903 crore (Mar 2025). ROE ~22% (5-yr avg). Market cap Rs 8,803 crore; P/E ~20 (Aug 2026).
- Expense split: gold purchases dominate ~85-90% of cost (exact % data not found).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with a brand moat in a boring-old trade. Forty-three percent sales CAGR plus doubling profits — expansion stores plus gold-price tailwind did the work. P/E 20 against that growth looks fair (PEG well under 1 if growth holds), but growth partly reflects rising gold prices, which can reverse.

## TAM
Indian jewellery market ~USD 80-100 billion; organised players like PNG keep taking share from family jewellers (exact figure data not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gold price moves — inventory gains and higher ticket sizes.
2. New store additions across Maharashtra and franchise (OMO) expansion.
3. Studded/diamond share shift — higher margin than plain gold.

## Red flags
- Thin net margin (~3%) — one bad gold cycle squeezes profit.
- Working-capital debt Rs 903 crore.
- Regional concentration (Maharashtra); competition from national chains.

## Verdict
Strong regional brand compounding fast at a reasonable price — a gold-cycle fast grower worth owning with eyes open.
