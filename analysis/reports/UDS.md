# Updater Services Ltd (UDS)

## What it does
Company runs facility management crews — cleaning, security support, pantry, payroll and other business-support services — for offices, factories, hospitals and airports. India's 2nd largest outsourced integrated facilities management player, with 76,000-plus employees.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 1,453 Cr (FY24), Rs 1,592 Cr (FY25), Rs 1,762 Cr (FY26).
- Net profit: Rs 37 Cr (FY24), Rs 79 Cr (FY25), Rs 53 Cr (FY26).
- OPM 3.6% (FY26) — razor-thin; depreciation Rs 12 Cr, interest Rs 2 Cr.
- ROE 6.2%; ROCE 7.2%; nearly debt free; market cap Rs 1,490 Cr; P/E 26.
- 5-yr sales growth just 11.9%.

## Lynch lens
Perfectly boring — Lynch's favourite kind. Facilities management pays recurring monthly contracts nobody thinks about; switching costs exist once crews embed in a client's sites. But the economics show why it stays dull: 3.6% operating margins, 6% ROE, labour-intensive scale game. At 26x earnings for a 6% ROE business, valuation already assumes a turnaround in margins that has not arrived.

## TAM
India integrated facilities management market — data not found (IFM market fragmented, large; exact size not verified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Manpower billing rates vs wage inflation (core margin lever).
2. Client contract wins/retention in FMCG, BFSI, IT, airports.
3. Business Support Services (BSS) segment margin mix.

## Red flags
- 3.6% OPM and 6.2% ROE — structurally thin.
- FY26 PAT fell 33% from FY25 peak.
- Labour law / minimum-wage compliance risk on 76,000 staff.

## Verdict
Boring, resilient, but low-return — a fair business at a full price.
