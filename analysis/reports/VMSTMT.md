# VMS TMT Limited (VSMSTMT)

## What it does
VMS TMT makes Thermo Mechanically Treated (TMT) steel bars used to build houses, bridges and factories. It also sells allied products like billets, binding wire and scrap. Buyers include builders, contractors and steel traders.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 873 crore (FY24); Rs 770 crore (FY25) — sales fell ~12% in FY25.
- PAT: Rs 13 crore (FY24); Rs 15 crore (FY25).
- Margins thin: net margin ~2%. ROE 14%, ROCE 13% (screener).
- Market cap ~Rs 229 crore; P/E ~13.5; trades near 1x book value.
- Expense breakdown (raw material %, employee %): data not found — steel/scrap input cost dominates typical TMT economics.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical. Steel prices swing, and profit moves with the cycle — 5-year profit CAGR of 95% and sales CAGR of 30% come off a tiny base and reflect the up-cycle, not steady growth. Cheap on P/E (13.5) and book (1.0x), but small-cap commodity player with no visible moat. Not the durable "boring niche dominator" Lynch favors.

## TAM
India TMT steel bars market — multi-lakh-crore industry; company-specific share data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Steel/scrap input prices (spreads between raw material and TMT bar prices).
2. Construction demand cycle in its region.
3. Capacity utilisation of its melting/rolling plant.

## Red flags
- Revenue declined in FY25.
- Razor-thin net margin (~2%) — small price swings wipe out profit.
- Very small market cap (Rs 229 cr) — liquidity and survival risk.
- No dividends.

## Verdict
Cheap cyclical, not a compounder — track only when steel cycle turns up.
