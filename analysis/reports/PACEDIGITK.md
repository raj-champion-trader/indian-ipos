# Pace Digitek (PACEDIGITK)

## What it does
The company builds and installs equipment that keeps telecom towers and solar plants running — DC power systems, lithium batteries, monitoring systems and inverters. It serves telecom, energy and ICT customers with turnkey installation and commissioning work. It listed on the NSE SME platform via IPO in 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 181 crore (FY23); Rs 2,321 crore (FY24) — a 13x jump, likely a mix of ramp-up and contract scaling; Rs 2,271 crore (FY25)
- PAT: Rs 7 crore (FY23); Rs 170 crore (FY24); Rs 233 crore (FY25)
- OPM: 10% (FY23) → 14% (FY24) → 17% (FY25); TTM 19% OPM, sales Rs 1,635 crore, PAT Rs 238 crore
- Expenses: Rs 164 crore (FY23); Rs 1,995 crore (FY24); Rs 1,876 crore (FY25)

## RevenueStreams
- Telecom infrastructure — DC power systems and turnkey installation/commissioning, dominant revenue source (FY24-FY25)
- Lithium batteries and energy storage (FY25)
- Solar inverters and solar-EPC related solutions (FY25)

## Lynch lens
A puzzling fast grower — revenue exploded from Rs 181 crore to Rs 2,321 crore (FY23-FY24) and PAT from Rs 7 crore to Rs 170 crore, with margins still rising (17% OPM FY25). Riding India's 5G rollout and green-energy capex. But such step-changes usually mean a few large contracts (telecom PSU orders), which makes the base lumpy and the TTM revenue decline (Rs 2,271 → Rs 1,635 crore) confirms it. High risk, high reward — needs verification of order book and receivables.

## TAM
India telecom capex ran ~US$ 10-12 billion annually during 5G rollout (FY23-FY25, industry estimates); energy-storage and solar-inverter markets each worth thousands of crore rupees and growing 20%+ a year.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Large telecom-infrastructure orders (DC power, batteries) — lumpy contract wins
2. Input costs — lithium cells and power electronics pricing
3. Working capital/receivables from PSU-style customers — interest and cash-flow impact

## Red flags
- Extreme revenue volatility (FY23 Rs 181 crore → FY24 Rs 2,321 crore → TTM falling again)
- Likely customer concentration in a few telecom/energy buyers
- SME-listed — thin disclosure and liquidity
- Receivables risk on turnkey government-adjacent contracts

## Verdict
A rocket-ride fast grower with lumpy orders — speculative; verify the order book before buying.
