# Capital Small Finance Bank Ltd (CAPITALSFB)

## What it does
Capital Small Finance Bank — India's first small finance bank — takes deposits and lends to middle-income households in smaller cities: farm loans, MSME and trading loans, mortgages, and consumption loans. It makes money on the gap between deposit rates and lending rates.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 794 crore (FY24) → Rs 908 crore (FY25). Interest income Rs 449 crore (FY24) → Rs 498 crore (FY25), rest from investments/treasury.
- PAT: Rs 112 crore (FY24) → Rs 132 crore (FY25). 5-year profit CAGR 28%.
- Deposits: Rs 10,018 crore (FY26). Gross NPA 2.47%, Net NPA 1.14% (Jun 2026).
- ROE ~10%; market cap Rs 1,281 crore; P/E ~8.5 (Aug 2026). Borrowings (deposits fund the book; statutory bank).
- Expense split (employee/branches): data not found in fetched sources.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart/boring-bank play. Lending to salaried and farm borrowers in Punjab and nearby states — slow, dull, profitable. P/E 8.5 and book-value trading at a discount to peers, with 5-year profit CAGR of 28%, makes it a classic overlooked small bank. Risk: small SFBs get acquired or stay small; NIM pressure from competition.

## TAM
Indian small finance / retail lending market — SFB industry collectively holds several lakh crore in assets (exact size data not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Net interest margin on agriculture/MSME mortgages.
2. Deposit growth (CASA) funding cheap lending.
3. Credit costs — GNPA 2.47% (Jun 2026); provisioning swings profit.

## Red flags
- Modest ROE ~10% versus best-in-class banks at 15%+.
- Small scale limits technology and branch economics.
- Loan book concentration in one region/crop-linked borrowers.

## Verdict
Cheap, dull, decent — a hold-forever candidate unless growth or ROE improves.
