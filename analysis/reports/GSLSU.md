# Global Surfaces Ltd (GSLSU)

## What it does
Company mines, processes and exports natural stones (granite, marble, soft quartzite) and manufactures engineered quartz slabs and countertops. Sells slabs and customized countertop applications, mostly to export markets. Listed via IPO in 2023.

## The numbers
- FY26 revenue Rs 74.84 crore, down 46% YoY; PAT Rs 7.61 crore; net margin 10.2% (screener).
- FY26 operating margin showed as negative (-17%) with PAT propped up by other income of Rs 36.8 crore.
- ROE ~2%; ROCE ~5%; P/E ~12.5; market cap ~Rs 115 crore; price-to-book 0.33x.
- 5-year sales growth -15.6% CAGR; contingent liabilities Rs 200 crore; debtor days 222; working capital days up from 209 to 308.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical, currently in a down-leg — export-driven stone demand collapsed. Trades below book (0.33x), which gives it an asset-play angle, but earnings quality poor (other income-driven PAT). No insider buying data found.

## TAM
Global natural stone and engineered quartz surfaces market for countertops/flooring; exact size data not found. Demand tied to US/EU housing and renovation cycles.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Export volumes/prices of engineered quartz (largest swing factor).
2. Raw material and mining costs (quartz/granite input economics).
3. Other income (Rs 36.8 crore, FY26) — currently the difference between profit and loss.

## Red flags
- Revenue nearly halved in FY26; negative operating margin.
- Rs 200 crore contingent liabilities vs Rs 115 crore market cap.
- 222 debtor days and ballooning working capital; no dividends.

## Verdict
Deep-cyclical bet below book value — only for buyers who believe the export cycle turns.
