# Excelsoft Technologies Ltd (EXCELSOFT)

## What it does
Company builds education-technology software — learning management systems, online assessment engines, and cloud platforms for digital learning. Sells to universities, test providers, publishers and enterprises.

## The numbers
- Revenue (standalone): Rs 196 crore (FY23) → Rs 193 crore (FY24) → Rs 233 crore (FY25) → Rs 273 crore (FY26); TTM Rs 298 crore.
- Net profit: Rs 23 crore (FY23) → Rs 12 crore (FY24) → Rs 34 crore (FY25) → Rs 42 crore (FY26).
- OPM ~26% (FY26), down from ~34% (FY23).
- Nearly debt-free (Rs 7 crore borrowings); interest cost fell from Rs 13 crore (FY20) to Rs 4 crore (FY25).
- ROE ~9.7%; market cap ~Rs 817 crore; P/E ~16.8 (Aug 2026).
- TTM sales growth ~27%; 3-year profit growth ~26%.

## Lynch lens
Boring-software stalwart at a reasonable price. Nobody gets excited about exam-and-LMS platforms, yet recurring education spend supports it. Recovery story inside: FY24 profit dip to Rs 12 crore, then re-rating to Rs 42 crore by FY26. P/E ~17 against ~25% profit growth gives PEG near 0.7 — attractive if growth holds. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
Global ed-tech and digital assessment market estimated in the tens of billions of USD; Indian online education segment estimated at several billion USD (exact sourced figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Assessment/LMS platform licence and subscription revenues (main revenue engine).
2. Employee costs — the dominant expense for a software firm (exact % data not found).
3. Margin recovery from FY24 trough (FY26 OPM 26% vs FY23 34% still leaves upside).

## Red flags
- Margin erosion: OPM 37% (FY20) → 26% (FY26).
- FY24 showed both falling sales and profits — execution wobbles.
- Small-cap liquidity; modest ROE ~10%.

## Verdict
Dull-but-profitable edu-software house at ~17x with PEG under 1 — the kind of boring stock Lynch liked.
