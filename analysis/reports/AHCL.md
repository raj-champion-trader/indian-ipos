# Anlon Healthcare Ltd (AHCL)

## What it does
Anlon Healthcare makes pharma intermediates (building-block chemicals for drugs) and APIs in Gujarat. It sells to pharma formulators in India and abroad, and runs a small CDMO arm making custom molecules for two global innovator companies.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 120 crore (FY25), Rs 172 crore (FY26) — up ~43% YoY.
- Net profit: Rs 21 crore (FY25), Rs 29 crore (FY26). OPM 28% (FY26).
- ROCE ~21%, ROE ~18% (FY26). Market cap ~Rs 846 crore; P/E ~26.7 (Aug 2026).
- Debt reduced: borrowings Rs 67 crore (FY25) to Rs 44 crore (FY26).
- Red flag on cash: operating cash flow minus Rs 59 crore (FY26) vs reported profit of Rs 29 crore; debtor days 220, inventory days 393.

## Lynch lens
Small-cap chemical maker — a potential fast grower at a reasonable multiple (P/E ~27 with 40%+ TTM profit growth). The niche angle: high-purity intermediates plus CDMO work for global innovators. But the cash conversion stinks; Lynch always said profits you cannot bank count for little.

## TAM
Data not found (niche pharma intermediates/API market; no reliable public size).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. API and pharma intermediates sales (bulk of Rs 172 crore FY26 revenue).
2. New capacity commissioned (fixed assets grew Rs 23 crore to Rs 58 crore, FY26).
3. CDMO molecules — 3 molecules for 2 global innovators; commercial supplies targeted from Q3 FY27 (not yet contributing).

## Red flags
- Operating cash flow deeply negative (FY25 Rs -22 crore, FY26 Rs -59 crore) while profits rise.
- Debtors 220 days, inventory ~393 days — money stuck in working capital.
- No dividend despite profits.
- Tiny track record as a listed company (recent IPO).

## Verdict
Cheap-looking grower with ugly cash flow — watch the CDMO ramp, but do not confuse accounting profit with cash.
