# TruAlt Bioenergy Ltd (TRUALT)

## What it does
TruAlt Bioenergy produces ethanol and other biofuels from molasses and grain. With 1,800 KLPD operational capacity (2,000 KLPD installed), it ranks among India's largest ethanol producers (about 3.6% of national capacity) and sells mainly to government oil marketing companies for fuel blending. Listed June 2024 (NSE SME).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,217 cr (FY24), Rs 1,880 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 2,027 cr. 3-yr sales CAGR 31%.
- Operating profit: Rs 188 cr / 15% (FY24), Rs 297 cr / 16% (FY25); TTM 19%.
- PAT: Rs 35 cr (FY24), Rs 141 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 143 cr but TTM profit growth -14%; FY26 (full year) PAT Rs 80 cr — profit halved after the FY25 spike.
- Debt Rs 1,586 cr (Mar 2026); interest Rs 164 cr and depreciation Rs 88 cr (TTM). ROE 7% last year; P/E ~26.

## Lynch lens
A cyclical disguised as a grower — ethanol prices get set/revised by government policy, and molasses/feedstock costs swing, so profits whipsaw (PAT Rs 35 to 141 to 80 cr in three years). Heavy debt on top. Not a Lynch favourite unless bought near a cycle trough. PEG looks poor: high P/E with falling earnings.

## TAM
India's Ethanol Blending Programme targets 20% blending (E20); national ethanol demand of roughly 1,000+ crore litres for blending supports a market of Rs 50,000+ cr (approximate; exact size data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Government-set ethanol prices paid by OMCs (main revenue source).
2. Feedstock cost (molasses/grain) swings — the biggest margin driver.
3. Interest burden: Rs 164 cr interest (TTM) against Rs 143 cr PAT caps the bottom line.

## Red flags
- Promoters pledged 36.8% of holdings.
- Profit fell ~43% in FY26 (Rs 141 cr to Rs 80 cr) despite flat revenue.
- Rs 1,586 cr debt, ROE 7%, no dividends, rising working-capital days, GST demand of Rs 6.6 cr.

## Verdict
Policy-dependent cyclical with heavy debt and pledging — a trader's stock, not an investor's.
