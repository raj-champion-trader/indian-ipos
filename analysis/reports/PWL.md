# Physicswallah Ltd (PWL)

## What it does
Physicswallah (PW) coaches students for tough exams — JEE, NEET, UPSC — through online and offline classes, plus upskilling courses in data, banking and software. Students pay course fees, mostly at prices far below premium rivals.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 735 cr (FY23), Rs 1,536 cr (FY24), Rs 2,334 cr (FY25), Rs 3,245 cr (FY26, standalone)
- Net profit: Rs -77 cr (FY23), Rs -1,071 cr (FY24, includes large one-time/esop items), Rs -136 cr (FY25), Rs -40 cr (FY26); TTM +Rs 31 cr
- OPM improved from -57% (FY24) to +8% (FY26)
- FY26 expenses Rs 2,994 cr, including interest Rs 93 cr and depreciation Rs 337 cr; 3-year sales CAGR ~64%

## Lynch lens
A fast grower trying to become a turnaround at the net level: revenue quadrupled in three years, but acquisitions, ESOP costs and depreciation kept the bottom line red until a thin TTM profit appeared. The value-price niche (mass-market test prep) gives it a genuine moat against premium online players. No meaningful P/E/PEG yet on TTM profit of Rs 31 cr.

## TAM
Indian test-preparation market — one of the world's largest, tens of millions of exam takers annually; exact figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Test-prep course fees (JEE/NEET verticals form the core)
2. ESOP and employee costs (the biggest swing factor between loss and profit)
3. Acquired businesses (offline centres, PW schools/vidyapeeth integration)

## Red flags
- History of large one-time charges (FY24 loss Rs 1,071 cr)
- Still barely profitable despite Rs 3,200 cr revenue
- Acquisition integration and ESOP dilution
- Competitive intensity in online test prep

## Verdict
Spectacular growth, paper-thin profits — a show-me story for one more year.
