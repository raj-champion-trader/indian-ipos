# Amanta Healthcare Ltd (AMANTA)

## What it does
Company makes sterile liquid medicines in Ahmedabad: eye drops (ophthalmics), IV fluids, irrigation solutions, respiratory solutions and small medical devices. It sells to hospitals, doctors and pharmacy brands, including contract manufacturing for other pharma companies. Listed on NSE/BSE in September 2026 via a ~Rs 126 crore IPO.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: ~Rs 275 crore (Rs 274.7-276.1 crore per IPO documents/Finology).
- FY25 PAT: ~Rs 10.5 crore; EBITDA margin ~22%.
- TTM (post-listing): revenue ~Rs 291 crore (+7.1%); PAT slightly negative growth (-8.3%).
- Q3 FY26/9M FY26: profit rose on margin expansion and deleveraging (AlphaStreet).
- Net margin ~3.8% (FY25) — EBITDA strong, bottom line thin.

## Lynch lens
Boring stalwart niche — sterile ophthalmic/IV manufacturing has high regulatory barriers (sterile plant approvals) and steady hospital demand, but thin net margins and modest growth (~7%) cap the story. New capacity in niche molecules (anti-infectives, respiratory, ophthalmics) gives mild optionality. P/E, insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
India ophthalmic and IV fluid market — large, growing with healthcare access expansion; exact TAM: data not found in searched sources.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Eye drops/ophthalmic portfolio (specialty margin).
2. IV fluids and irrigation solutions (volume business).
3. Contract manufacturing orders and margin expansion/deleveraging post-IPO.

## Red flags
- Thin net margin (~4%) — sensitive to raw-material (API, packaging) costs.
- PAT fell even as revenue grew (TTM) — margin pressure.
- Small scale vs pharma giants; customer concentration risk in CMO orders.

## Verdict
Boring sterile-liquids niche with decent EBITDA but thin profit — wait for margin proof post-IPO before buying.

Sources: Finology, Screener.in, AlphaStreet, IPO coverage of NSE listing.
