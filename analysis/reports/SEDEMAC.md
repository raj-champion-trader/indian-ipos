# SEDEMAC Mechatronics Ltd (SEDEMAC)

## What it does
Pune company (est. 2007) designs and supplies control electronics — ECUs and control-intensive embedded systems — to vehicle makers and industrial customers. Tier-I supplier whose boxes sit deep inside OEM platforms: engine management, motor controllers, off-highway electronics.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 423 Cr; FY24 Rs 531 Cr; FY25 Rs 658 Cr; FY26 Rs 1,058 Cr (screener). 5-yr CAGR ~35%.
- PAT: FY23 Rs 9 Cr; FY24 Rs 6 Cr; FY25 Rs 47 Cr; FY26 Rs 104 Cr. 5-yr profit CAGR ~69%.
- OPM: 11% → 15% → 18% → 20% (FY23→FY26).
- Interest down from Rs 16 Cr (FY24) to Rs 9 Cr (FY26); depreciation Rs 63 Cr (FY26). Borrowings Rs 72 Cr (FY26).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with a tech moat — control software/hardware that OEMs certify once and rarely replace. Boring-sounding "ECU supplier" but rides EV powertrain, off-highway automation and drivetrain electrification waves. Profit jumped 7x FY24→FY26 on 2x revenue — textbook operating leverage. Revenue doubled in FY26 (EV controllers ramp likely); watch sustainability of that step-change.

## TAM
India automotive ECU/electronics content per vehicle rising sharply; global automotive electronics market estimated US$ 250+ bn and EV power electronics among fastest pockets (industry estimates). Company-specific TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Mobility/automotive ECU volumes — FY26 revenue jump to Rs 1,058 Cr drives profit.
2. Operating leverage — OPM 20% (FY26) vs 11% (FY23).
3. Falling interest burden (Rs 16→9 Cr, FY24→FY26) post deleveraging.

## Red flags
- FY26 doubling needs validation — one large EV program can also walk away.
- Customer concentration typical for Tier-I electronics; segment mix undisclosed publicly.
- Depreciation heavy (Rs 63 Cr) — capex-intensive.

## Verdict
High-quality fast grower in vehicle electrification — hold core position, recheck FY27 for repeat growth.
