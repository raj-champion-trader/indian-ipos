# Ardee Industries Ltd (ARDEE)

## What it does
Company recycles end-of-life lead-acid batteries and non-ferrous metal scrap, producing high-purity refined lead and custom lead alloys. Serves energy storage, automotive, e-mobility, cable, chemical and telecom buyers. Incorporated 1993.

## The numbers
- FY26 revenue Rs 1,168 crore vs Rs 743 crore FY25 (+57%); PAT Rs 85 crore vs Rs 33 crore (+158%).
- OPM 13% (FY26) vs 9% (FY25).
- Borrowings Rs 184 crore; ROE 80.9%; ROCE 49%.
- Market cap ~Rs 1,928 crore; P/E ~22.4; promoters 67.6%.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a dirty, boring, badly-needed niche — battery recycling rides lead demand from backups, telecom and the battery chain. ROE of 81% and P/E 22 give PEG well under 1 if the 3-year 115% profit CAGR persists even partially. Cyclical lead prices are the catch. No insider buying data found.

## TAM
Global recycled lead / lead-acid battery recycling market; exact size data not found. India generates a large and growing volume of end-of-life batteries (inverters, autos, telecom towers, e-rickshaws).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Spread between scrap battery input cost and refined lead/alloy selling prices — core driver.
2. Recycling volumes / capacity utilization.
3. Lead price cycle (LME-linked realizations).

## Red flags
- ROE of 81% partly a thin-equity artifact (reserves Rs 96 crore) — returns normalize as capital builds.
- Commodity spread business; profits swing with lead prices.
- Environmental/regulatory compliance risk in lead recycling; contingent-liability details not found.

## Verdict
Boring-but-necessary recycler compounding fast at a fair multiple — a genuine cyclical-growth hybrid worth tracking.
