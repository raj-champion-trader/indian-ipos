# Corona Remedies Limited (CORONA)

## What it does
Corona Remedies makes and sells branded medicines in India — pills and treatments for women's health, heart disease, pain, and urology (urinary problems). Doctors prescribe them, chemists stock them, and repeat prescriptions drive sales. It ranks #5 in women's healthcare and pain management in India, #14 overall in the Indian pharma market.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,014-1,021 cr (FY24), Rs 1,196-1,202 cr (FY25), Rs 1,403 cr (FY26 screener standalone) — ~18% growth in FY26.
- PAT: Rs 90 cr (FY24, ~9%), Rs 149 cr (FY25, ~12%), Rs 185 cr (FY26, ~13%) — 5-year PAT CAGR 20.4%.
- OPM: 15% (FY24), 20% (FY25), 21% (FY26).
- ROE 29.5%, ROCE 33.3%; debt/equity only 0.10; borrowings Rs 165 cr (FY26).
- Expenses (FY26): total Rs 1,109 cr, interest Rs 7 cr, depreciation Rs 38 cr — marketing and raw material costs dominate (split %: data not found).
- Stock P/E ~60 after listing at 38% premium (2025 IPO).

## Lynch lens
A classic fast grower: 20% compound profit growth, rising margins, 29% ROE, near-zero debt, and a boring, doctor-driven prescription model. The #5 rank in women's health gives the niche dominance angle. Only problem: price. At 60x earnings for ~20% growth, PEG near 3 — Lynch would love the company, not the stock.

## TAM
Indian Pharmaceutical Market (IPM): Corona ranks #14 overall; absolute IPM size figure: data not found in fetched sources (industry estimates place it near Rs 2 lakh crore, unverified). Women's health and urology niches grow faster than the market.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Women's healthcare brands — #5 ranked, the flagship franchise.
2. Pain management portfolio — #5 ranked, high-repeat prescriptions.
3. Brand-building (marketing spend) and margin-accretive new launches — OPM climbed from 15% to 21% (FY24-FY26).

## Red flags
- Valuation: P/E 60, P/BV 10.7.
- India-only revenue — exposed to NPPA drug price control.
- Source IPO notes carried conflicting business descriptions; verify DRHP directly.

## Verdict
High-quality fast grower bought expensively — wait for a pullback before boarding.
