# Medi Assist Healthcare Services Limited (MEDIASSIST)

## What it does
Company works as a third-party administrator (TPA) between insurers and hospitals. Processes health-insurance claims, runs hospital networks, and manages policies for insurance companies. Makes money largely on fee/income streams tied to claims volume.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 64 crore (FY23) → Rs 108 crore (FY24) → Rs 151 crore (FY25) → Rs 225 crore (FY26).
- Net profit: Rs 17 crore (FY23) → Rs 19 crore (FY24) → Rs 33 crore (FY25) → Rs 52 crore (FY26).
- OPM scaled from ~5% (FY23) to ~36% (FY26); net margin ~27%.
- Minimal debt (Rs 5 crore, FY26); ROE ~14.5%; ROCE ~20.4%.
- Market cap ~Rs 2,494 crore; P/E ~43.6 (Aug 2026); stock down ~34% over 1 year.
- Promoter stake just 4.6%; FIIs 25%, DIIs 47% (Jun 2026).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower hiding in a boring business. Nobody talks about claims processing, yet revenue and profit compound near 50% (3-year CAGRs of 52% and 48%). High margins plus asset-light model and almost no debt. P/E ~44 against ~48-58% recent profit growth puts PEG near or under 1 — decent value after a 34% one-year price drop. Note the near-absent promoter stake (professionally-run, PE-origin company) — no insider-ownership alignment, but strong institutional sponsorship.

## TAM
Indian health insurance premium pool roughly Rs 1.2+ lakh crore and growing ~15-20% annually; TPA-serviced claims market runs into tens of thousands of crore (exact sourced figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Claims volume growth from health-insurance penetration (TPA fee income).
2. Margin leverage from automated claims processing — OPM jumped 5% → 36% (FY23→FY26).
3. Network/hospital management and value-added service fees.

## Red flags
- Promoter holding only 4.6% — governance rests on institutions, not owners.
- 43x P/E leaves little room for a growth stumble.
- Regulatory/pricing risk: TPA fees sit under IRDAI oversight.

## Verdict
Boring, profitable, fast-growing claims engine at a plausible price — a Lynch-style fast grower worth owning on dips.
