# International Gemological Institute Ltd (IGIL)

## What it does
IGI certifies and grades diamonds, gemstones and jewellery — it works as the world's largest independent certification and accreditation provider in the field. Founded in Belgium 50 years ago, it grades stones so buyers and sellers can trust quality. Customers: diamond manufacturers, retailers, lab-grown diamond producers and consumers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 898 crore (FY23), Rs 1,053 crore (FY24), Rs 1,363 crore (FY25/TTM per screener) — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 331 crore (FY23), Rs 427 crore (FY24), Rs 610 crore (FY25 TTM)
- Operating margin: 55% (FY23) → 61% (TTM); 3-year sales CAGR 48%, profit CAGR 43%
- ROE 56%, ROCE 69%; P/E ~24; market cap ~Rs 14,648 crore
- Expenses (FY25 TTM): total Rs 536 crore, interest Rs 10 crore, depreciation Rs 50 crore; tax rate 27%. People-cost-heavy lab business; detailed head split: data not found.

## Lynch lens
A near-monopoly toll booth on trust — every certified stone pays IGI a fee, and its 55-61% margins prove pricing power. Lynch would call this a fast grower with a niche dominance angle: lab-grown diamond certification turbocharged growth. PEG ≈ 24 P/E vs ~40% growth ≈ 0.6 — attractive if growth holds. - Indian Diamond industry export softness
- High P/E multiple depends on sustained 30%+ growth
