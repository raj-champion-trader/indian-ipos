# Arkade Developers Ltd (ARKADE)

## What it does
Arkade builds and sells residential apartment projects in Mumbai — mid and premium housing. It buys land, constructs buildings, and sells flats, booking revenue as homes complete. A family-run developer with a Mumbai-only focus.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 635 crore (FY24) → Rs 683 crore (FY25).
- PAT: Rs 123 crore (FY24) → Rs 157 crore (FY25) — net margin above 20%.
- ROE 20.8% (3-year average ~26%). Borrowings just Rs 99 crore (Mar 2026) — nearly debt-free model.
- Market cap Rs 2,365 crore; P/E ~13.3 (Aug 2026).
- Expense split: construction cost dominates; exact % data not found.

## Lynch lens
Small stalwart of Mumbai housing. High ROE, low debt, 20%+ net margins — rare discipline for a real-estate developer. Mumbai redevelopment demand gives a long runway. P/E 13 for 20%+ ROE reads cheap; catch: lumpy project revenue means one slow launch year dents the numbers.

## TAM
Mumbai residential + redevelopment market: tens of thousands of ageing buildings need redevelopment; exact market size data not found in fetched sources.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Project completions — revenue recognition timing swings annual profit.
2. Mumbai property prices and stamp-duty/rate environment.
3. Land acquisition cost — asset-light joint development keeps margins high.

## Red flags
- Revenue lumpiness; FY25 sales grew only 7%.
- Small project pipeline concentration — few projects dominate.
- Mumbai real-estate regulatory and approval risks.

## Verdict
Clean, cheap, high-ROE Mumbai developer — a boring-business Lynch buy if project pipeline stays busy.
