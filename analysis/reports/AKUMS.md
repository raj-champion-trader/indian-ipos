# Akums Drugs and Pharmaceuticals Limited (AKUMS)

## What it does
Akums makes medicines for other pharma brands — India's largest contract drug manufacturer (CDMO) by volume. It produces tablets, capsules, injections and gummies at large plants and sells manufacturing services to branded pharma companies.

## The numbers
- Revenue: ~Rs 4,120–4,170 crore FY25 consolidated (roughly flat, ~-1% YoY); standalone sales Rs 1,312 crore FY25 (screener).
- PAT: ~Rs 234 crore FY25 consolidated (vs ~Rs 220 crore FY24); standalone Rs 161 crore FY25 after a Rs 165 crore FY24 standalone loss (one-offs).
- Net margin ~8% (FY25 consolidated); net debt Rs 339 crore (Q3 FY25); ROCE ~7%; P/E ~104 (Mar 2026).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart-tending-cyclical. Boring business (pills for someone else's brand), steady demand, but pricing pressure capped FY25 growth at ~-1% against a 15% target. Customer concentration: top ~10% of customers ~47% of revenue. At P/E ~104, the market already pays for a re-rating that ~flat revenue has not delivered — poor PEG.

## TAM
Indian pharma contract manufacturing market estimated around USD 15–20 billion and growing high single digits to low teens CAGR (industry estimates; exact FY-basis figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Contract manufacturing volumes and price negotiations with top customers (~47% of revenue from top ~10% of customers).
2. Capacity utilisation of its large formulation plants.
3. Raw material (API/excipient) costs and working-capital intensity (101 inventory days, 59 debtor days).

## Red flags
P/E ~104 on ~flat revenue; FY24 standalone loss shows one-off/adjustment risk; customer concentration; thin ROCE (~7%).

## Verdict
Decent underlying franchise, absurd price — Lynch would pass until the multiple shrinks or growth returns.
