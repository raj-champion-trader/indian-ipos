# Senco Gold Limited (SENCO)

## What it does
Senco Gold sells gold, diamond, and silver jewellery through a large store network across Eastern India — the biggest organised jeweller there by store count. It also sells online and offers lifestyle products. Customers pay cash; the company makes money on making charges and gold price spreads.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 5,230 crore (FY24) → Rs 6,259 crore (FY25) → ~Rs 8,374 crore (FY26 TTM, screener).
- PAT: Rs 189 crore (FY24) → Rs 165 crore (FY25, margin squeeze) → ~Rs 581 crore (FY26 TTM, includes strong Q1 FY26 PAT of Rs 267 crore — check for one-offs).
- OPM: 8% (FY24) → 6% (FY25) → 12% (FY26 TTM).
- Borrowings: Rs 2,611 crore (Mar 2026) — working-capital heavy (gold inventory). ROE 25.7%. Market cap Rs 5,816 crore; P/E ~9.8 (Aug 2026).
- Expense split: raw material (gold) dominates ~85-90% of cost (exact % data not found); finance cost on gold loans material.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical-with-growth. Jewellery retail rides gold prices and wedding demand; Senco's regional dominance in Eastern India gives it a niche Lynch likes. P/E under 10 with 26% 5-year sales CAGR and 25.7% ROE reads like a bargain — but gold-price swings and high debt make earnings jumpy. FY26 profit jump partly reflects scale and possibly one-time gains; verify before trusting the run-rate.

## TAM
Indian organised jewellery market: one of the world's largest, ~USD 80-100 billion overall jewellery market with organised share rising (exact figure and source data not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gold price and making-charge margins — inventory gains when gold rises.
2. Store expansion and same-store growth in Eastern India.
3. Finance cost on gold loans/working capital — a big profit swing factor.

## Red flags
- Rs 2,611 crore borrowings; gold loans reprice fast.
- FY25 PAT fell despite higher sales — thin margins.
- Heavy competition from Titan, Kalyan, PNG and local players.

## Verdict
Cheap regional jeweller with real scale — a gold-cycle bet, not a steady compounder.
