# Garuda Construction and Engineering Limited (GARUDA)

## What it does
Garuda Construction and Engineering builds civil structures — residential towers, commercial buildings, industrial and infrastructure projects — handling everything from planning to finishing. It also earns O&M and mechanical-electrical-plumbing (MEP) service income. Listed Oct 2024 IPO.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 154 crore (FY24); Rs 225 crore (FY25) — ~46% growth.
- PAT: Rs 36 crore (FY24); Rs 50 crore (FY25); ~Rs 123 crore on Rs 531 crore revenue (FY26) — explosive jump.
- OPM steady at 30-32% — unusually rich for construction.
- Total expenses Rs 159 crore (FY25), ~71% of revenue.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring, needed business. Construction execution sounds dull but this one prints 30%+ operating margins — far above typical EPC peers — suggesting niche pricing (finishing/MEP specialisation or favourable client mix). FY26 doubling raises the classic Lynch question: repeatable or one-off project lumpiness? Construction lumpy orders make earnings volatile; verify order book and receivables before trusting the FY26 spike.

## TAM
India construction industry — multi-lakh-crore market growing with infra and real estate capex; precise TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Large project execution pace (revenue recognition on completion milestones).
2. Rich OPM on finishing/MEP and O&M work vs plain civil construction.
3. Client/segment mix — commercial and government projects vs residential.

## Red flags
- FY26 doubling could reflect one or two lumpy projects, not durable growth.
- High margins invite competition; receivables and working capital typical industry risks.
- Small-cap, short listed history (IPO Oct 2024); promoter-concentration details data not found.

## Verdict
Fast-growing small construction firm with standout margins — promising, but verify the order book before calling it a compounder.
