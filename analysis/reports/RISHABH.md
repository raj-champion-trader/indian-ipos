# Rishabh Instruments Limited (RISHABH)

## What it does
Rishabh Instruments makes test-and-measurement gear — analog panel meters, digital multimeters, transmitters — plus industrial control products and aluminium die-cast parts (through its Lumel units in India and Poland). Roughly two-thirds of sales come from exports. Listed August 2023.

## The numbers
- Standalone (screener): sales Rs 196 crore FY23; Rs 225 crore FY24; Rs 239 crore FY25; net profit Rs 19 / 32 / 23 crore; FY26: Rs 268 crore sales, Rs 42 crore profit. Consolidated FY23 revenue ~Rs 570 crore (IPO documents), so consolidated scale runs ~2.5x standalone.
- 5-year sales CAGR ~16%, profit CAGR ~25%; almost debt-free; promoter holding ~69%; P/E ~61 at Rs 692 (market cap ~Rs 2,676 crore).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with niche pockets. Analog meters sound antiquarian, but factories worldwide still need panel meters and measuring instruments — a durable, unglamorous replacement market. Poland (Lumel) gives European manufacturing reach, a real differentiator. FY25 profit dipped ~28% before an FY26 rebound — mild cyclicality, not distress. P/E ~61 against mid-teens growth = poor PEG; the Lynch buy point would sit far below the current quote.

## TAM
Global test & measurement instruments market ~USD 25–30 billion (industry estimates, mid-2020s); India electrical measurement/instrumentation TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Test & measuring instruments and industrial control products (core, export-led).
2. Aluminium high-pressure die-castings (HPDC) segment — auto/industrial demand in Europe.
3. INR-EUR/USD currency movements on the ~66% overseas revenue.

## Red flags
Rich P/E (~61) for ~15% growth; FY25 profit drop; Europe demand exposure; HPDC margin volatility.

## Verdict
Solid, boring, export-quality instrument maker — good company, wrong price; revisit closer to 25–30x earnings.
