# Vikran Engineering Limited (VIKRAN)

## What it does
Company builds infrastructure on a turnkey basis — power transmission lines, power distribution, water infrastructure and railway electrification projects. Handles design, supply, installation, testing and commissioning from start to finish.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 524 crore (FY23) → Rs 786 crore (FY24) → Rs 916 crore (FY25) → Rs 1,249 crore (FY26).
- Net profit: Rs 43 crore (FY23) → Rs 75 crore (FY24) → Rs 78 crore (FY25) → Rs 92 crore (FY26).
- OPM ~14-18% range; FY26 ~14%.
- Borrowings Rs 301 crore (Mar 2026); ROE ~10.8%; ROCE ~16.8%.
- Market cap ~Rs 1,532 crore; P/E ~14.7 (Aug 2026).
- 3-year sales CAGR ~34%, profit CAGR ~30%.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical growth stock riding an infrastructure supercycle. Lynch would call EPC a cyclical — orders flow when government capex flows. India's transmission and railway electrification pipeline keeps the cycle running, so it behaves partly like a fast grower (34% revenue CAGR). P/E 14.7 against ~30% recent profit growth gives PEG near 0.5 — cheap if the order book holds. Promoters hold 56%.

## TAM
India power transmission capex planned at several lakh crore over the next decade (National Electricity Plan trajectory); railway electrification and Jal Jeevan Mission water spend add crores more (exact sourced figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Execution pace on power transmission/DISCOM orders (revenue recognition).
2. Raw material costs — steel, conductors, cables (commodity price sensitive; exact % data not found).
3. Working capital: 296 debtor days — interest on Rs 301 crore borrowings swings the bottom line.

## Red flags
- Debtor days 296; working capital days up from 169 to 282 — government receivables drag.
- High cost of borrowing flagged by screener.
- Order-book concentration risk typical for EPC players (customer detail: data not found).

## Verdict
Cheap cyclical (PEG ~0.5) on India's grid-build boom — buy the cycle, but mind the receivables.
