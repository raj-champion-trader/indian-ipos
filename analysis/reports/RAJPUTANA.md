# Rajputana Biodiesel Ltd (RAJPUTANA)

## What it does
Rajputana Biodiesel manufactures and supplies biofuels and by-products — biodiesel, glycerine, and fatty acids — mainly to oil marketing companies for diesel blending. The NSE SME listing (IPO January 2025) also funded capacity expansion; it acquired Arvant Bioenergy as a wholly owned subsidiary in August 2026.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 53.8 cr (FY24), Rs 47 cr (FY25), Rs 59.1 cr (FY26). 5-yr sales CAGR 44% (small base).
- Operating profit: Rs 7.8 cr / 14.4% (FY24), Rs 6.5 cr / 13.7% (FY25), Rs 6.4 cr / 10.8% (FY26) — margins shrinking.
- PAT: Rs 4.6 cr (FY24), Rs 3.8 cr (FY25), Rs 4.9 cr (FY26).
- Borrowings Rs 22.9 cr (Mar 2026); ROE 11.8%; P/E ~33 at Rs 231/share (market cap Rs 163 cr).

## Lynch lens
A tiny micro-cap in a policy-driven commodity business — biodiesel pricing depends on OMC blending mandates and used-cooking-oil/feedstock costs. At 33x earnings with single-digit crore profits and 10.8% OPM, this trades as a story stock, not on numbers. Lynch would pass: speculative, sub-Rs 60 cr revenue.

## TAM
India biodiesel market — the government targets 5% biodiesel blending; national demand potential in thousands of crores (approximate; exact size data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Biodiesel sales to OMCs under blending programmes (main revenue channel).
2. By-product sales: glycerine and fatty acids add margin on top of fuel.
3. Feedstock (used cooking oil / acid oil) cost — the key margin swing factor.

## Red flags
- OPM compressed from 14.4% (FY24) to 10.8% (FY26); FY25 revenue actually fell.
- P/E ~33 for a Rs 59 cr-revenue micro-cap.
- SME-listed, tiny float, acquisition integration risk (Arvant).

## Verdict
Micro-cap policy punt — too small and too expensive for a Lynch-style buy.
