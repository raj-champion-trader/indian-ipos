# Gala Precision Engineering Ltd (GALAPREC)

## What it does
Gala Precision makes precision components: technical springs (disc, strip, coil, spiral) and high-tensile fasteners (studs, anchor bolts, wedge-lock washers). It sells 750+ SKUs to automakers, railways, and industrial customers in India and abroad.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 203 crore (FY24), Rs 238 crore (FY25), Rs 314 crore (FY26) — 5-year sales CAGR 24%, profit CAGR 73%.
- Net profit: Rs 22 crore (FY24), Rs 27 crore (FY25), Rs 35 crore (FY26). OPM ~17%.
- Market cap ~Rs 1,325 crore; P/E ~34.8 (Aug 2026). ROCE 15.6%, ROE 13.2%.
- Interest cost small (Rs 3 crore FY26); borrowings modest and reduced.
- Quarterly momentum strong: Q4 FY26 sales Rs 94.6 crore, net profit Rs 12.2 crore — best quarter yet.

## Lynch lens
A classic Lynch small-cap stalwart-in-training: a boring, niche precision-parts maker growing 24%+ a year. Boring businesses with long SKU lists and switching costs (customers qualify these safety parts) fit the "low-tech in a high-tech world" Lynch favorite. P/E 35 vs ~30% TTM profit growth gives PEG near 1 — not cheap, not silly. Promoter holding trimmed slightly last quarter (minor negative).

## TAM
Data not found (Indian precision springs/fasteners market size not reliably published). Growth rides EV content, railways, and import substitution.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Disc & strip springs and coil springs for auto/EV and industrial demand (volume growth lifting FY26 sales 32%).
2. High-tensile fasteners (SFS) including wedge-lock washers.
3. Operating leverage — profit growing faster than sales as the new capacity fills.

## Red flags
- No dividend despite consistent profits.
- Promoter holding decreased over last quarter (-0.66%).
- Customer concentration risks typical of auto ancillary (data not found on exact split).
- OPM dipped from 19% (FY24) to 17% (FY26).

## Verdict
Boring precision-parts compounder growing ~25% a year at PEG ~1 — the kind of stock Lynch loved holding while it stays dull.
