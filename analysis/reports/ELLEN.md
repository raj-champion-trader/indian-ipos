# Ellenbarrie Industrial Gases Ltd (ELLEN)

## What it does
Ellenbarrie makes and supplies industrial gases — oxygen, nitrogen, argon, helium, hydrogen, acetylene, CO2, and medical gases — in eastern and southern India, both in bulk tankers and packed cylinders. It sells to steel plants, hospitals, pharma, chemicals, construction, defense and energy customers; largest 100% Indian-owned industrial gases company by installed capacity.

## The numbers
- Sales (standalone): Rs 205 crore (FY23), Rs 269 crore (FY24), Rs 312 crore (FY25), Rs 342 crore (FY26).
- Net profit: Rs 28 crore (FY23), Rs 45 crore (FY24), Rs 83 crore (FY25), Rs 104 crore (FY26). OPM improved to ~34-35%.
- Market cap ~Rs 4,507 crore; P/E ~37.4 (Aug 2026). 5-year profit CAGR 34%.
- IPO in 2025 cut debt: interest cost fell to Rs 9 crore (FY26) from Rs 17 crore (FY25); promoters hold 77.15% post-IPO.
- Other income sizable: Rs 50-60 crore (FY25/FY26) — nearly half of PBT.

## Lynch lens
A bona fide Lynch pick on paper: a boring, 50-year-old gas company nobody talks about, local oligopoly economics, sticky bulk contracts with on-site tanks, and profits compounding at 34% a year. Turnaround-plus-stalwart: margins jumped from 16% (FY23) to 35% (FY25) after restructuring and IPO deleveraging. P/E 37 vs mid-30s growth gives PEG near 1. Promoters hold 77% — skin in the game.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Oxygen and nitrogen sales to steel, pharma/chemicals, and hospitals (core gas revenue; oxygen named a leading share of gas revenue).
2. Margin expansion and deleveraging — OPM up ~19 points since FY23; interest cost nearly eliminated post-IPO.
3. Other income (Rs 50 crore FY26, flagged by screener as a large share of earnings) — quality caveat on headline PAT.

## Red flags
- Other income of ~Rs 60 crore props up earnings — core business earns less than the PBT line suggests.
- 37x P/E for a commodity-linked gas supplier; margins may mean-revert.
- Regional concentration (eastern/southern India) and steel-cycle exposure.
- No dividend; short listed history.

## Verdict
Classic boring-niche compounder at a fair-ish price — Lynch material, provided gas margins hold and other income keeps fading in importance.
