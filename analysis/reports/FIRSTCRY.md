# Brainbees Solutions Ltd (FIRSTCRY)

## What it does
Company runs FirstCry, India's largest mother-baby-kids products platform, selling online plus through a large store network (multi-channel). Also runs GlobalBees (brands) and international baby-care sites.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 2,542 crore (FY23) → Rs 2,163 crore (FY24) → Rs 2,471 crore (FY25) → Rs 2,732 crore (FY26); TTM Rs 2,820 crore.
- Net profit: -Rs 156 crore (FY23) → -Rs 62 crore (FY24) → +Rs 60 crore (FY25) → +Rs 109 crore (FY26).
- Operating margin ~3.6% (FY26); GMV grew 16% to Rs 10,585 crore (FY25).
- Borrowings Rs 475 crore; other income Rs 211 crore (TTM) — larger than reported profit, so core business still earns less than the P&L shows.
- ROE ~1.2%; market cap ~Rs 9,684 crore; P/E ~99.6 (Aug 2026). No dividends.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround that just turned — and the market already threw a party. Losses to profit swing (FY23 -156 to FY26 +109 crore) proves the model works, but a 100x P/E on thin, other-income-flattered earnings prices in years of flawless execution. Lynch rule: a fast grower can deserve a high P/E, but PEG here (99.6 vs ~15% profit growth trajectory) sits far above 1.

## TAM
India baby-care products market estimated at roughly USD 10+ billion and growing ~10-15% annually with organised players gaining share (exact sourced figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Product gross margin — cost of goods dominates expenses for a retailer (exact % data not found).
2. Fulfilment and logistics costs (stores + warehouses network).
3. Advertising/discount spend controlling customer acquisition cost; plus Rs 211 crore other income (TTM) boosting the bottom line.

## Red flags
- P/E ~100 on ~1% ROE.
- Other income (Rs 211 crore TTM) exceeds net profit — core profitability unproven.
- Borrowings Rs 475 crore; zero dividend.

## Verdict
Genuine turnaround with a great franchise, but at 100x you pay 2028 prices today — wait for a pullback.
