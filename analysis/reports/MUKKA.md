# Mukka Proteins Ltd (MUKKA)

## What it does
Company turns fish into fish meal, fish oil, and fish soluble paste. Aquaculture and poultry feed makers buy these as protein ingredients; fish oil also goes into Omega-3 pills, soaps, and paints. Based in Mangalore; listed March 2024.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,380 cr (FY24); Rs 1,006 cr (FY25) — down ~27%.
- PAT: Rs 74 cr (FY24); Rs 48 cr (FY25) — down ~35%.
- OPM: 8% (FY24); 9% (FY25).
- Interest Rs 37 cr (FY25, up from Rs 25 cr FY24); depreciation Rs 14 cr (FY25); borrowings Rs 453 cr (FY25).
- P/E 10.9; ROE 11.1%; ROCE 11.6%; working capital days improved from 63 to 45.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical in the purest sense — earnings swing with fish catch (raw material) and aqua-feed demand. The 27% revenue drop in FY25 shows the cycle at work. Cheap at P/E ~11, which cyclical stocks typically are at peak-ish earnings — Lynch's classic trap. Not a fast grower, not a stalwart; a commodity processor.

## TAM
Indian aqua feed and fish meal / fish oil market — tied to shrimp and fish aquaculture output; specific sourced figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Raw fish (trash fish) availability and cost — the dominant input, drives both volume and margin.
2. Aquaculture/poultry feed demand — shrimp-cycle health sets volumes.
3. Fish oil export prices (Omega-3/pharma demand) and interest cost (Rs 37 cr FY25 vs Rs 48 cr PAT).

## Red flags
- Revenue fell 27% and PAT 35% in FY25 — deep cyclical downturn.
- Rs 453 cr borrowings against Rs 48 cr PAT; interest rising.
- Raw material depends on wild catch — monsoon fishing bans and catch volatility hit production directly.

## Verdict
Cheap-looking fish-meal cyclical near a downcycle — Lynch would wait for the catch cycle to turn before biting.
