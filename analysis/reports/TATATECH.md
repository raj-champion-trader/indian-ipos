# Tata Technologies Ltd (TATATECH)

## What it does
Company does engineering work for vehicle and aircraft makers — designing products, embedded software, and running digital factory systems (ERP, analytics). Engineers sit inside auto and aerospace clients and bill by project; Tata group parent, with Tata Motors and JLR among key customers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,732 Cr (FY24), Rs 3,024 Cr (FY25), Rs 3,126 Cr (FY26) — growth stalled (FY26 +3.4%).
- Net profit: Rs 687 Cr (FY24), Rs 849 Cr (FY25), Rs 570 Cr (FY26) — FY26 fell 33%.
- Services 77% / Technology Solutions 23% of revenue.
- Nearly debt free (Rs 73 Cr); ROE 36.3%; expenses Rs 2,550 Cr (FY26), mostly employee costs.
- Market cap Rs 33,879 Cr; P/E ~53 at Rs 834.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart in a niche — vehicle ER&D outsourcing. 36% ROE, debt-free, cash-rich, sticky Tata/JLR relationship. But FY26 earnings fell 33% (client spending cuts, pass-through software reselling margin mix), and a 53x P/E on a shrinking-year base gives a terrible PEG. Lynch would admire the franchise (boring engineering work, high switching costs) and wait for the price to deflate.

## TAM
Global ER&D outsourcing market — data not found (multi-hundred-billion-dollar engineering spend; TAM figure not verified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Services segment (77% of revenue) — automotive OEM project volumes.
2. Tata Motors / JLR workloads (related-party concentration).
3. Employee cost control (largest expense; utilisation rates).

## Red flags
- PAT -33% in FY26 on flat sales.
- Related-party revenue dependence.
- 53x P/E for a business currently shrinking.

## Verdict
Quality niche compounder at wrong price — wait for de-rating or earnings revival.
