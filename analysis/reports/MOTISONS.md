# Motisons Jewellers Ltd (MOTISONS)

## What it does
Motisons Jewellers sells gold, diamond and kundan jewellery from showrooms in Jaipur, running since 1997. It offers traditional, modern and combination designs — diamond, polki, temple, Italian lines — plus silver, platinum, coins and utensils across 3,00,000+ designs. Customers buy mainly at the retail counter.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 366 crore (FY23), Rs 417 crore (FY24), Rs 462 crore (FY25) — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 22 crore (FY23), Rs 32 crore (FY24), Rs 43 crore (FY25)
- Operating margin: 13% (FY23) to 15% (FY25); net margin ~9% (FY25)
- Total expenses Rs 394 crore (FY25); jewellery retail runs on gold/diamond purchases, so raw material (bullion and stones) dominates the cost base. Detailed head-wise split: data not found.
- Listed December 2024 via ~Rs 151-crore IPO.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gold jewellery sales volume and gold price movement (dominant revenue line)
2. Diamond/kundan premium product mix lifting margins
3. Showroom footfall in Jaipur — single-city dependence cuts both ways

## Red flags
- All showrooms concentrated in one city (Jaipur) — local demand risk
- Thin net margins (~9% FY25) typical of gold-heavy retail
- Small base; promoter-heavy, post-IPO float limited
- Segment mix and expense split not publicly visible

## Verdict
A tiny one-city jeweller with decent growth but no visible moat — watch, don't chase.
