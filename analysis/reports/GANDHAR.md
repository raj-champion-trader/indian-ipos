# Gandhar Oil Refinery (India) Ltd (GANDHAR)

## What it does
Company refines crude oil into white oils — highly purified mineral oils used in baby oils, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals and lubricants — plus waxes and jellies under the "Divyol" brand. Customers include Procter & Gamble, Unilever and pharma companies, in India and 100+ countries. Listed on NSE/BSE in November 2023.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: ~Rs 4,250 crore (+11.5% YoY); PAT ~Rs 291 crore; PAT margin ~12% (per ICICI/Screener data).
- Q2 FY26 revenue: ~Rs 106 crore per quarter run-rate coverage (Rs 1,060 million quarterly release) — growth continuing.
- Q1 FY27: consolidated revenue Rs 1,732 crore (+92% YoY); record PAT Rs 206 crore; gross margin spread expanded 3.4x YoY.
- Market cap ~Rs 2,510 crore at ~Rs 256/share (mid-2026); stock +75% over the prior year.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with a consumer tilt — refiners live on crude-to-product spreads, so profits swing with crude prices and GRMs; the Q1 FY27 spread blowout (+3.4x) proves the cyclicality. But the mix shift toward personal-care and pharma white oils (stable, specification-heavy, sticky customers) softens the cycle versus fuel refiners. Cheap on book/P&E basis typical of Lynch's disliked-oil plays. P/E: data not found.

## TAM
Global white oil market ~US$ 2-3 billion+ and growing with personal care and pharma demand (exact sourced figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Crude refining spread (gross margin per kilolitre) — the dominant profit lever.
2. Personal care/Consumer (baby oil, cosmetics grade) white oil volumes to FMCG majors.
3. Pharma/healthcare-grade oils and specialty (Divyol) lubricants margin mix.

## Red flags
- Earnings swing with crude prices — Q1 FY27's +92% revenue and record PAT may mark a cycle peak.
- Customer concentration (large FMCG buyers with bargaining power).
- Q1 FY27 revenue jump (+92%) suggests inventory/spread timing, verify sustainability.

## Verdict
Cyclical consumer-oil refiner — cheap and cash-generating; buy when spreads look depressed, take profits when spreads blow out like FY27.
