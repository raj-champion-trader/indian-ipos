# DOMS Industries Limited (DOMS)

## What it does
DOMS makes and sells stationery and art products — pencils, mathematical instrument boxes, pens, erasers, sketch pens and more — under the DOMS brand. India's 2nd-largest branded stationery player; sells in 28 states and exports to 55+ countries. Listed Dec 2023 IPO.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,388 crore (FY24); Rs 1,709 crore (FY25); ~Rs 2,050 crore (FY26) — ~20%+ CAGR.
- PAT: Rs 145 crore (FY24); Rs 190 crore (FY25); ~Rs 220-226 crore (FY26/TTM).
- OPM 17-19%; ROE ~21%, ROCE ~24-25%.
- Market share: 29% pencils, 30% mathematical instrument boxes (FY23, India).
- P/E ~61-65; market cap ~Rs 13,300 crore.
- Expense heads: raw materials (wood, polymer, graphite) dominate cost of an ~18% OPM business; exact split data not found.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring business. Pencils and geometry boxes — the perfect Lynch dull product with repeat purchase every school year. 5-year sales CAGR ~41% and profit CAGR ~81-91%, high ROCE, 4,600+ SKUs, exports growing. Catch: P/E ~62 against ~20-25% growth gives PEG well above 1 — Lynch would wait for the multiple to compress.

## TAM
India branded stationery and art products market — company ranks #2 in it; market size estimated in the Rs 12,000-15,000 crore range (industry estimates); exact sourced figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Pencils/writing instruments and instrument-box volumes (core 29-30% share categories).
2. New categories (art material, scholastic pens) and export growth to 55+ countries.
3. Gross margin on wood/polymer inputs and operating leverage at its plants.

## Red flags
- Valuation: P/E ~62 leaves little room for a growth miss.
- Fila (Italian promoter group) cross-holdings and related-party dealings.
- Raw material (timber, polymer) price sensitivity.

## Verdict
Genuinely great boring business growing fast — but Lynch rule says the price, not the company, decides; wait for a pullback.
