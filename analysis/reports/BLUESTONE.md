# Bluestone Jewellery & Lifestyle Ltd (BLUESTONE)

## What it does
Company designs and sells gold, diamond, platinum and gemstone jewellery through an omnichannel model — a website/app plus 250+ physical stores across India. Customers browse online, buy in stores or home-delivered; plain gold and lightweight designs form a large chunk of sales. Listed on NSE in August 2025 (Rs 1,541 crore IPO at Rs 517/share).

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue from operations: ~Rs 1,770 crore (+40% YoY).
- FY25 net loss: Rs 222 crore (widened 56% from Rs 142 crore in FY24) on store expansion and marketing.
- EBITDA margin ~4.3% (FY25).
- Online channel: only ~7-8% of revenue — the model behaves like a store retailer.
- FY26: turned profitable — PAT ~Rs 26 crore (per annual report coverage); Q1 FY26 net profit ~Rs 7 crore on Rs 737 crore revenue; Q2 FY26 loss narrowed 38% to Rs 52 crore (quarterly figure, seasonality-driven).

## Lynch lens
Turnaround story inside a hot sector — jewellers earn thin margins on gold, so profit hinges on studded/share and store maturity. Store-opening S-curve: heavy losses while new stores ramp, profits as cohorts mature. Not a classic Lynch favorite (glamorous consumer brand, low margin, no dividend), but the loss-to-profit swing in FY26 rewards patience. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
India jewellery market ~US$ 85-100 billion, among the world's largest; organised/omnichannel players gaining share from family jewellers (per IPO coverage; exact figure varies by source).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Plain gold jewellery volume (largest revenue share, thin margin).
2. Studded/diamond and gemstone share (higher gross margin).
3. Store count ramp and marketing efficiency (loss-to-profit swing).

## Red flags
- Gold price volatility squeezes working capital and margins.
- Competition from Titan (Tanishq), Senco, Kalyan and hundreds of local jewellers.
- Marketing and lease costs keep profitability fragile.
- Weighted EBITDA margin ~4% leaves little room for error.

## Verdict
Turnaround bet on store maturity — the FY26 profit inflection looks real, but the stock needs gold prices and studded mix to cooperate.
