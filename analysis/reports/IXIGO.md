# Le Travenues Technology Ltd (IXIGO)

## What it does
Ixigo runs a travel app that lets users book train, flight and bus tickets and track trains. It earns fees and commissions from bookings plus advertising, and owns ConfirmTatkal and AbhiBus. Listed June 2024.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Revenue: FY23 330; FY24 653; FY25 911; FY26 1,208 (+33% YoY)
- Net profit: FY24 54; FY25 63; FY26 96 (profitable three years straight after years of losses)
- Net margin only ~8% (FY26); operating margin ~7%; ROE 7.4%
- P/E ~65 at price Rs 163; market cap Rs 7,206 cr; negligible debt; other income (interest on cash) contributed Rs 66 cr FY26 — significant versus Rs 96 cr PAT

## Lynch lens
Would-be fast grower priced like one already. Revenue compounds 30%+ but profits stay thin; the P/E of 65 against ~30-40% profit growth gives a PEG near 2 — expensive. The business rides on Indian Railways' vast, underserved traveller base (a Lynch-style dull-but-massive niche), yet competition from IRCTC, MakeMyTrip and Google squeezes take rates. Asset-light and cash-rich — the good part.

## TAM
Indian online travel market estimated at US$40 bn+ and growing double-digits (industry/IMARC-type estimates); rail travellers number in the tens of millions daily — ixigo's core funnel.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Train-ticket volumes and convenience-fee/ancillary attach (its differentiated niche)
2. Flight and bus (AbhiBus) bookings plus advertising revenue
3. Employee and marketing spend — the two big cost heads; interest income on IPO cash pads PAT

## Red flags
- P/E 65 with 8% net margin — valuation leaves no room for error
- PAT quality: a big chunk comes from treasury/other income, not operations
- Stock down from Rs 339 to ~Rs 163 post-IPO; IRCTC/OTT platform dependence for inventory

## Verdict
A real, profitable niche travel platform — but the price already assumes flawless execution; wait for a better entry.
