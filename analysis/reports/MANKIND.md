# Mankind Pharma (MANKIND)

## What it does
Mankind makes medicines — tablets, capsules, syrups — and sells them cheaply through chemists across India. Doctors prescribe its brands for infections, heart, stomach and skin problems. It also sells consumer products like condoms (Manforce) and pregnancy kits (Prega News).

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 8,749 cr; FY24 Rs 10,260 cr; FY25 Rs 12,207 cr; FY26 Rs 14,278 cr (17% growth) — consolidated, screener.in
- PAT: FY24 Rs 1,942 cr; FY25 Rs 2,011 cr; FY26 Rs 1,938 cr (flat/-4%; BSV acquisition costs and finance charges weigh)
- OPM steady at 25-26% (FY24-FY26); total expenses ~75% of sales
- Borrowings Rs 6,312 cr (Mar 2026, TTM basis) — debt taken on for the Bharat Serums & Vaccines (BSV) acquisition
- P/E ~46x, market cap ~Rs 98,000 cr; ROE ~16% (3-yr avg); #4 in Indian pharma by value, #1 in prescriptions

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with a stalwart core. The domestic branded business grows steadily at mid-teens; the BSV acquisition (women's health, critical care) and US generics add new legs. Boring angle: pharma detail-force work in tier-2/3 India — unsung but hugely cash-generative. FY26 PAT dipped while sales grew 17% — watch whether integration costs fade. P/E 46x against ~15% earnings growth gives a PEG near 3 — expensive unless BSV delivers.

## TAM
Indian pharmaceutical market — one of the largest globally, domestic formulations market roughly Rs 4+ lakh cr and growing high single/low double digits (approximate; precise FY figure not found in fetched sources). Also exports to the US and emerging markets.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Domestic branded formulations (~75%+ of revenue; ~97% of FY25 operating revenue from India) — the profit engine
2. BSV acquisition (women's health/serious-care hospitals business) — currently dilutes margins via interest and amortisation
3. Consumer healthcare (Manforce, Prega News) — high-margin OTC kicker

## Red flags
- PAT flat despite 17% revenue growth (FY26)
- Debt of Rs 6,300 cr post-BSV; ROCE down from 37% (FY15) to ~14%
- Expensive valuation (P/E ~46x)
- Price control (NPPA) risk on acute portfolio

## Verdict
Quality fast grower at a rich price — worth buying only on big corrections.
