# Fedbank Financial Services Ltd (FEDFINA)

## What it does
Retail lending arm of Federal Bank. Lends against gold jewellery, gives home loans, loans against property and business loans to small businesses and salaried families. Focuses on borrowers in tier-2/tier-3 towns the big banks underserve.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,577 cr (FY24) → Rs 2,037 cr (FY25), +29%
- PAT: Rs 245 cr (FY24) → Rs 225 cr (FY25), -8%
- ROE: 14% (FY24) → 9% (FY25)
- Gross NPA: 1.87% (Mar 2025)
- P/E ~15.6; book value Rs 78.2
- Claims 2nd lowest cost of borrowing among MSME/gold-loan peers
- Board approved borrowing limit of Rs 23,000 cr and NCD raise up to Rs 2,500 cr (Aug 2026)
- Exact AUM mix (gold vs LAP vs home loans) data not found

## Lynch lens
Cyclical-ish financial in turnaround-question territory. Revenue grows 29% but PAT fell 8% (FY25) — credit costs or margins squeezed. Gold loans dominate; that book carries low credit risk but rate sensitivity. ROE drop from 14% to 9% hurts the thesis. Parent Federal Bank provides funding edge (low borrowing cost = the moat here). At P/E 15.6 and P/B near 1.3x, valuation leaves room if asset quality holds.

## TAM
India gold-loan market estimated at Rs 4-6 lakh cr (largely informal moneylenders) and MSME credit gap estimated at Rs 20+ lakh cr (industry estimates; precise figures data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Credit costs / GNPA trajectory (gold vs LAP vs business-loan mix)
2. Gold-loan book growth — low NPA, high-yield
3. Cost of funds — Federal Bank parentage keeps borrowing cheap

## Red flags
- PAT fell (FY25) while revenue grew 29% — margin or provisioning pressure
- ROE halved-ish to 9% (FY25)
- Aggressive NCD/borrowing plans (Rs 2,500 cr) fund fast growth
- Gold-loan competition from banks and fintechs

## Verdict
Cheap-ish gold-loan lender with a funding moat, but falling ROE and PAT make it a "show-me" turnaround before buying.
