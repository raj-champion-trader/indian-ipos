# Cyient DLM Limited (CYIENTDLM)

## What it does
Cyient DLM builds electronic assemblies and complete systems for aerospace, defence, medical and industrial customers — the factory arm spun off from IT firm Cyient. Makes printed circuit board assemblies and does full system integration plus testing. Listed in 2023.

## The numbers
- Revenue FY25: ~Rs 1,520 crore (+27.5% YoY). Adjusted EBITDA FY25: ~Rs 145 crore (+30.8%), ~9.6% margin.
- Net profit margin slipped from 5.1% (FY24) to ~4.5% (FY25) — PAT up ~11% YoY (~Rs 68 crore implied).
- Q4 FY25: revenue Rs 362 crore, PAT Rs 22.7 crore (+~81% YoY).
- Segment momentum (Q1 FY26): Industrial +396.6% YoY to Rs 64 crore; Med-Tech +137.5% to Rs 61 crore.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a strategic niche. High-mix, low-volume electronics for defence and medical kit carries qualification lock-ins — once approved, customers rarely switch. Lynch's "boring business, sticky customers" angle applies. Insider/parent (Cyient) buying: data not found. P/E: data not found; PEG N/A.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Aerospace & defence assemblies — long qualification cycles protect margins.
2. Industrial segment — explosive growth (Q1 FY26 nearly 5x YoY).
3. Operating leverage — margin recovery as new capacities (Karnataka/Andhra plants) fill.

## Red flags
- Net margin ~4.5% (FY25) — thin cushion; margin pressure from mix and interest costs.
- Interest cost burden post capacity expansion.
- Customer concentration in a few large OEMs.

## Verdict
Decent EMS niche player with defence tailwinds — needs margin proof before it earns a big multiple.
