# Net Avenue Technologies Ltd (CBAZAAR)

## What it does
Company sells Indian ethnic wear — sarees, lehengas, salwars — online through its websites cbazaar.com and ethnovog.com. Ships to customers in 100-plus countries; US, UK, Australia and Canada generate most sales.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 26.7 Cr (FY24), Rs 27.1 Cr (FY25), Rs 20.7 Cr (FY26) — shrinking.
- Net loss: -Rs 1.7 Cr (FY24), -Rs 3.5 Cr (FY25), -Rs 1.5 Cr (FY26).
- Market cap Rs 10.5 Cr (micro-cap); price Rs 4.9; book value Rs 2.28/share; borrowings just Rs 0.55 Cr.
- Total expenses Rs 23.5 Cr (FY26) against Rs 20.7 Cr revenue.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround — of the tiny kind. A sub-Rs 30 Cr online ethnic-wear seller losing money every year. The niche (NRI ethnic wear) has loyalty, but the business loses money at Rs 20 Cr scale with no growth. Micro-cap with Rs 10.5 Cr market cap trades below book — possible asset-play/turnaround angle if a new team or channel (quick commerce, marketplaces) revives it. No insider-buying data found.

## TAM
Global online Indian ethnic wear market — data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Order volumes and average order value from US/UK NRI customers.
2. Digital advertising costs (traffic buying for cbazaar.com/ethnovog.com).
3. Product sourcing / garment costs and returns.

## Red flags
- Revenue declined 24% in FY26.
- Losses in all three recent years; negative ROE (-32% FY25).
- Tiny, illiquid micro-cap — business-quality risk extreme.

## Verdict
Falling micro-cap — punt money only, not investment.
