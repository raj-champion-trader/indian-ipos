# Flair Writing Industries Ltd (FLAIR)

## What it does
Company manufactures pens and writing instruments — top-3 player in India — and sells in 115 countries. Also makes stationery. Makes the humble pens and markers sitting in every school bag.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 919 cr (FY24), Rs 949 cr (FY25), TTM Rs 1,029 cr
- PAT: Rs 118 cr (FY24), Rs 112 cr (FY25), TTM Rs 116 cr
- OPM: 19.1% (FY24), 16.1% (FY25), 14.9% (TTM) — mild slide
- Interest Rs 4 cr (0.4% of sales, FY25); borrowings Rs 39 cr vs reserves Rs 1,002 cr — effectively debt-free
- Depreciation Rs 33 cr (3.2% of sales, FY25)

## Lynch lens
Textbook Lynch stalwart: pens — a boring, repeat-purchase product — near-zero debt, double-digit PAT for years. Growth is modest (single-digit revenue), margins easing with input costs, but the balance sheet gives staying power. PEG needs current P/E — data not found; buy when P/E sits in mid-teens.

## TAM
Data not found (India writing instruments market size not confirmed in fetched sources; company states top-3 position, presence in 115 countries).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Pen volumes across mass and premium ranges — core profit engine
2. Plastic/raw material (polymer, ink, tips) costs — the 19.1% to 14.9% OPM slide
3. Export sales across 115 countries — second growth leg

## Red flags
- OPM eroding: 19.1% (FY24) to 14.9% (TTM)
- Flat PAT: Rs 118 cr to Rs 112 cr despite rising sales
- Competition from unorganised sector and Cello/Linc

## Verdict
Quality debt-free pen maker at steady margins — a hold/accumulate on dips, not a grower to chase.
