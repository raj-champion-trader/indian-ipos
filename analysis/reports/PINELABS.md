# Pine Labs Ltd (PINELABS)

## What it does
Company runs a merchant commerce platform in India — point-of-sale card machines, digital payment processing, gift card and prepaid issuance (Qwikcilver), and merchant lending/BNPL through its Fave unit. Merchants, brands, and banks use its payment and commerce software.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 1,281 cr (FY23), Rs 1,333 cr (FY24), Rs 1,597 cr (FY25).
- PAT: Rs -56 cr (FY23), Rs -187 cr (FY24), Rs +28 cr (FY25) — turned profitable.
- OPM: 12% (FY23), 8% (FY24), 11% (FY25).
- FY25 expenses Rs 1,421 cr, incl. interest Rs 70 cr, depreciation Rs 213 cr.
- Borrowings Rs 923 cr (Mar FY25); P/E ~114 at ~Rs 19,000 cr market cap; ROE ~3%.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround trying to become a fast grower — losses in FY23/FY24 gave way to modest profit in FY25, but the market already prices it at P/E ~114. Payment processing earns thin margins and faces brutal competition (Paytm, Razorpay, banks' apps). Gift cards (Qwikcilver) remain the tastier niche. Segment split detail: data not found.

## TAM
India digital payments market: UPI plus card payments processed several trillion dollars annually; merchant payments and POS market estimated at Rs 30,000+ cr industry revenue (FY24 RBI/industry reports).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Payment services/POS transaction volumes — core revenue engine.
2. Gift card and prepaid issuance (Qwikcilver) — higher-margin business.
3. Depreciation (Rs 213 cr FY25) and interest (Rs 70 cr FY25) — the drag that keeps net profit thin.

## Red flags
- P/E ~114 for a company earning Rs 28 cr standalone — valuation far ahead of profit.
- FY24 standalone loss of Rs 187 cr; profit history short.
- High borrowing cost flagged; competitive pressure on payment margins.

## Verdict
Interesting payments franchise, turnaround real but tiny profits versus a giant price tag — Lynch would pass.
