# Ajax Engineering (AJAXENGG)

## What it does
Ajax builds machines that make, carry and pour concrete — self-loading mixers, batching plants, boom pumps and 3D concrete printers. Construction firms across India buy these orange machines instead of setting up manual mixing. Second-largest concrete equipment maker in India by volume.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,111 cr TTM (Mar 2026), only +2% YoY — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 228 cr TTM, -6% YoY
- OPM ~12%; ROE 18%; ROCE 24%
- 5-yr sales CAGR 23%; 5-yr profit CAGR 19%
- Virtually debt-free (borrowings Rs 2 cr); P/E ~28x; market cap ~Rs 6,500 cr; no dividend
- 32,900+ machines sold over the last 10 years

## Lynch lens
Cyclical in a boring machine. Construction capex drives demand — roads, ports, real estate — so earnings follow the infra cycle. Debt-free and 24% ROCE show a disciplined, well-run niche leader (Lynch's favourite shape: dull product, dominant share). But TTM revenue growth of 2% says the cycle has cooled; 28x P/E for a flat-earnings cyclical looks full.

## TAM
India concrete equipment market — tied to national infrastructure spend (roads, housing, irrigation); exact market size not found in fetched sources. Longer runway: mechanisation replacing manual concrete handling.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Self-loading concrete mixer sales — flagship product and main profit pool
2. Government infrastructure/housing capex cycle — drives volumes and pricing
3. Working capital swings — receivable days ballooned from 69 to 161, hurting cash conversion

## Red flags
- Revenue growth stalled (+2%) and PAT fell 6%
- Working capital days more than doubled (69 → 161)
- Stock down 21% in a year despite profit
- No dividend despite cash generation

## Verdict
Quality debt-free cyclical — buy on infra-cycle troughs, not at 28x flat earnings.
