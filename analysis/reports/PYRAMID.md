# Pyramid Technoplast Ltd (PYRAMID)

## What it does
Pyramid Technoplast makes polymer-based industrial packaging — HM-HDPE drums, barrels, jerry cans, and IBC (intermediate bulk container) tanks. It sells to agrochemical, specialty chemical, chemical, lubricant, and pharma companies across India.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 532 cr (FY24), Rs 591 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 740 cr. 3-yr growth 12% CAGR.
- Operating profit: Rs 44 cr / 8% OPM (FY24), Rs 43 cr / 7% OPM (FY25). OPM has slid from 10% (FY23).
- PAT: Rs 29 cr (FY24), Rs 27 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 31 cr. 3-yr profit growth -3%.
- ROE 11%, ROCE 12.3%; borrowings Rs 184 cr with interest expense Rs 10 cr (TTM).

## Lynch lens
A boring, commodity-packaging business — Lynch would call this a cyclical/stalwart hybrid: steady demand, but profits swing with polymer prices. No niche moat beyond scale in IBCs; returns (11-12%) sit barely above cost of capital. P/E data not found.

## TAM
India rigid industrial plastic packaging market serves the fast-growing agrochemical and specialty chemical sectors; exact size data not found. Demand tracks chemical sector output growth (high single digit).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. IBC container sales to agrochemical/specialty chemical customers (core product line; exact mix % data not found).
2. Polymer (HDPE) raw material prices — the main margin swing factor.
3. Capacity utilisation and freight costs on pan-India deliveries.

## Red flags
- Margin compression: OPM 10% (FY23) to 7% (FY25).
- Profit declined even as sales grew — raw material pass-through lags.
- Rising debt (Rs 184 cr) against a shrinking profit pool.

## Verdict
A middling cyclical in plastic drums — cheap-looking only because growth has stalled.
