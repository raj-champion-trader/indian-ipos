# Vraj Iron & Steel (VRAJ)

## What it does
The company makes iron and steel products — sponge iron, steel billets and TMT bars sold under the "Vraj" brand. It runs plants in Chhattisgarh and sells mainly to construction buyers. Gopal Sponge and Power owns a controlling stake.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 516 crore (FY23); Rs 420 crore (FY24); Rs 575 crore per screener FY25 (Rs 475 crore shown in one snapshot; TTM Rs 643 crore)
- PAT: Rs 51 crore (FY23); Rs 54 crore (FY24); Rs 42 crore (FY25) — declining
- OPM: 15% (FY23) → 18% (FY24) → 13% (FY25) — cyclical squeeze
- Total expenses Rs 412 crore (FY25); depreciation Rs 8 crore (FY25); interest Rs 2-3 crore (FY25) — low debt
- TTM sales Rs 643 crore (+29%) but PAT Rs 32 crore (-7%) — volume up, margin down

## RevenueStreams
- TMT bars — main product for construction (FY25)
- Sponge iron and MS billets — sold externally and captively consumed (FY25)

## Expenses

- Power and fuel: data not found
- Depreciation Rs 8 crore (FY25); interest Rs 2-3 crore (FY25)

## Lynch lens
Classic cyclical — profits track steel prices and coal/ore costs, not management effort. OPM swinging 13-18% over three years (FY23-FY25) tells the story. Small-cap with an integrated sponge-iron-to-TMT chain in Chhattisgarh gives a modest cost moat. Lynch rule for cyclicals: buy at peak pessimism and low P/E, sell when margins look invincible. No dividend despite profits keeps the score unchanged.

## TAM
India steel consumption ~145-150 million tonnes (FY24-FY25, worldsteel/JSW data); long-products demand rides infrastructure and housing spend.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Spread between TMT prices and iron ore/coal costs — the whole game
2. Volume growth from the Raipur expansion (TTM sales +29%)
3. Captive power and integration margin vs. bought-in raw material

## Red flags
- PAT falling (Rs 54 → Rs 42 crore, FY24-FY25) while sales grow — margin compression
- Commodity price taker, no pricing power
- No dividend despite profits; small scale vs JSW/Tata/SAIL
- Iron ore availability and pricing in Chhattisgarh a recurring risk

## Verdict
A small cyclical at the wrong part of the cycle — revisit when steel spreads bottom out.
