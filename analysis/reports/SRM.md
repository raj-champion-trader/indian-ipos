# SRM Contractors Ltd (SRM)

## What it does
SRM Contractors builds roads, bridges, tunnels, and slope-stabilisation works in tough terrain — Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Arunachal Pradesh, Gujarat, and Odisha. It takes EPC and HAM contracts, mostly from governments. Tough-hill construction gives it a specialist edge few rivals want.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 342 crore (FY24) → Rs 527 crore (FY25) → Rs 850 crore (FY26, standalone).
- PAT: Rs 22 crore (FY24) → Rs 50 crore (FY25) → Rs 86 crore (FY26). OPM improved 12% → 15% → 16%.
- Interest: Rs 9 crore (FY25); depreciation Rs 16 crore (FY25).
- Borrowings: Rs 41 crore (Mar 2025) → Rs 131 crore (Mar 2026, funding growth). ROE ~28%; ROCE 32%. Market cap Rs 1,054 crore; P/E ~11.9 (Aug 2026).
- Order book: exact value hidden on screener (premium) — data not found.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring, difficult niche. Building tunnels in Ladakh when others avoid it = pricing power from specialization. 3-year sales CAGR 41%, profit CAGR 65%, ROE 28%, P/E ~12 — a rare growth-at-value price. Small size (Rs 1,054 crore cap) means government payment delays or one bad project can hurt badly.

## TAM
Indian national roads/tunnels pipeline: government capex on roads runs at Rs 2-3 lakh crore per year (Bharatmala etc.; exact figure data not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Execution speed on EPC/HAM road orders (revenue recognition).
2. Order book wins in hill states where competition stays thin.
3. Working capital — receivables from government; interest cost on rising borrowings.

## Red flags
- Government receivable risk; cash conversion weak in some years (FY25 CFO only Rs 1 crore).
- Borrowings tripled in FY26 to fund working capital.
- Zero dividend despite profits; concentrated regional exposure.

## Verdict
Small specialist growing fast at a single-digit-forward P/E — classic Lynch fast grower, sized accordingly small.
