# SBFC Finance Ltd (SBFC)

## What it does
SBFC Finance lends money to small businesses (secured MSME loans) and against gold. A systemically important NBFC, it focuses on entrepreneurs whom big banks underserve. Borrowers pledge property or gold; SBFC disburses fast.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 734 crore (FY23), Rs 1,019 crore (FY24), Rs 1,306 crore (FY25) — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 150 crore (FY23), Rs 237 crore (FY24), Rs 345 crore (FY25); net margin 26.4% (FY25)
- Interest expense: Rs 278 crore (FY23), Rs 352 crore (FY24), Rs 421 crore (FY25) — largest cost head
- Operating expenses: Rs 247 crore (FY23), Rs 338 crore (FY24), Rs 410 crore (FY25)
- AUM CAGR 33% FY18-Q3FY26; 3-year profit growth 75%; ROE 12% (FY25); P/E ~31.7

## Lynch lens
A fast grower riding the MSME credit wave — AUM compounding at 33% with 26% net margins and improving ROE. Lynch liked niche lenders serving ignored customers; SBFC's secured-first model (collateral + gold) cushions the downside. PEG ≈ 31.7 P/E vs ~45% profit growth ≈ 0.7 — reasonable. ## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Secured MSME loan book growth — main interest-income engine
2. Gold loan vertical — quick, low-risk ticket growth
3. Spread management: borrowing cost (Rs 421 crore FY25 interest) vs lending yield decides net profit

## Red flags
- Unsecured-adjacent MSME customers default more in downturns
- Rate cycles squeeze margins — borrowing costs rose every year FY23-FY25
- P/E ~32 prices in flawless execution
- Promoter stake pledged/institutional mix: data not found

## Verdict
A well-oiled MSME lender growing 30%+ — hold while asset quality stays clean.
