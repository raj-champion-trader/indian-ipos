# Travel Food Services Ltd (TRAVELFOOD)

## What it does
Travel Food Services runs airport restaurants (travel QSR) and airport lounges across India, and also operates abroad. It holds roughly 26% of India's airport travel-QSR market and about 45% of the airport lounge market including JVs (FY25), making it the dominant player in airport food.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,142 cr (FY24), Rs 1,214 cr (FY25) standalone; TTM Rs 1,337 cr.
- Operating profit: Rs 373 cr / 33% OPM (FY24) rising to Rs 471 cr / 39% OPM (FY25); TTM OPM 45%.
- PAT: Rs 216 cr (FY24), Rs 300 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 398 cr, profit growth 27% TTM.
- Debt cut from Rs 364 cr (FY24) to Rs 320 cr (FY25); finance cost Rs 44 cr, depreciation Rs 118 cr (FY25).
- ROE 38.5%, ROCE 45.1%; dividend payout 37% (FY25). Listed July 2025 (demerger/IPO of the travel business).

## Lynch lens
A fast grower with a genuine niche monopoly — 45% lounge share gives pricing power and scale no rival matches. Operating leverage is showing beautifully (OPM 33% to 45%). Boring on the surface (sandwiches at airports), exactly what Lynch liked. P/E data not found; PEG not computable.

## TAM
Indian air passenger traffic has crossed pre-COVID peaks (300 mn+ passengers annually, FY24-FY25 era), with new airports and premium credit-card lounge programmes expanding the lounge and travel-dining market. Exact market size data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Airport lounge business (~45% market share) — the highest-margin engine.
2. Recovery and growth in air passenger traffic driving QSR sales.
3. Operating leverage: OPM expansion from 33% (FY24) to 45% (TTM).

## Red flags
- Concession/rent agreements with airports can be repriced against it.
- Capex-heavy model (depreciation Rs 118 cr FY25) and Rs 320 cr debt.
- Consumer slowdown in air travel would hit both segments at once.

## Verdict
Dominant niche fast grower with expanding margins — the best-quality story in this batch.
