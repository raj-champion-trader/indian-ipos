# Ratnaveer Precision Steel Limited (RATNAVEER)

## What it does
Ratnaveer makes stainless-steel washers, precision fasteners, sheets and solar-panel mounting frames across five factories in Gujarat. It sells to industries in India and exports to many countries, ranking as India's leading exporter of stainless-steel washers.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: Rs 892 crore, up ~49.8% YoY; Q4 FY25 revenue Rs 203 crore (+43% YoY).
- FY25 PAT: Rs 46.8 crore, up 50.8% YoY (FY24: Rs 31.1 crore); PAT margin ~5.2-5.3%.
- FY26 (context): revenue ~Rs 1,069 crore (+20%), PAT ~Rs 64 crore (+37%).
- Growth driven by volumes, better realisations, value-added products and exports.
- Expense heads detail: data not found; stainless-steel scrap/skelp raw material dominates cost base (typical 60-70% for such processors).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring business — washers and fasteners, the ultimate nobody-wants-it niche, growing revenue ~50% in FY25 with a diversification into copper clad laminates (CCL, base material for printed circuit boards, production targeted Nov 2026). Management targets ~Rs 1,500 crore FY27 revenue. Thin ~5% net margins and heavy working capital temper the story. P/E data not found here.

## TAM
India stainless-steel fastener/washer market — exact size data not found. CCL market: global multi-billion-dollar PCB materials market (company diversification target); India imports most CCL today.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Volume growth and improved realisations in stainless-steel washers/fasteners and sheets.
2. Value-added solar mounting frames and export mix expansion.
3. Nickel/SS scrap raw-material price swings squeezing or aiding the ~5% margin.

## Red flags
- Thin net margin (~5%) leaves little room for error.
- Big diversification bet (CCL) outside core competence — execution risk.
- Working-capital-intensive business; debtor days can balloon with growth.

## Verdict
A gritty niche fast grower — the core business works, but the CCL gamble decides whether it becomes a tenbagger or a stumble.
