# Platinum Industries Ltd (PLATIND)

## What it does
Platinum Industries makes PVC stabilisers, CPVC additives and lubricants — chemicals that keep pipes, fittings and profiles from degrading. It ranks third in India's PVC additives with ~13% market share. Customers: PVC pipe and profile manufacturers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 231 crore (FY23), Rs 264 crore (FY24), Rs 392 crore (FY25) — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 38 crore (FY23), Rs 44 crore (FY24), Rs 50 crore (FY25)
- Operating margin: 23% (FY23, FY24), 15% (FY25); FY26 quarters slipped to ~13% — screener
- ROCE: 27% (FY23) → 16% (FY25); 5-year sales CAGR 38%, profit CAGR 61%
- Expenses (FY25): total Rs 334 crore, mainly raw materials (tin, calcium, chemicals) and manufacturing overheads; head-wise %: data not found.

## Lynch lens
A niche chemicals player in a boring input business — Lynch liked exactly this. But the pattern reads growth-then-squeeze: revenue jumped 48% in FY25 while margins fell from 23% to 15%, meaning growth came at thin prices (new CPVC capacity ramp). Classification: fast grower with cyclical margin risk. Segment split (CPVC vs PVC vs lubricants %): data not found. P/E data not confirmed.

## TAM
India PVC additives market: Platinum holds ~13% share as No. 3 (screener/company); absolute market size data not found from verified sources. Driven by India pipe/plumbing and infrastructure demand.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. CPVC additive volumes — fastest-growing line, funded the FY25 revenue jump
2. PVC stabiliser base business — legacy profit pool
3. Raw material (tin/metal salts) prices — the 8-point margin crash in FY25 shows who really sets the P&L

## Red flags
- Margin collapse: OPM 23% (FY24) → 15% (FY25) → ~13% FY26 quarters
- ROCE halved from 27% (FY23) to 16% (FY25)
- Growth via new capacity carries ramp-up and working-capital risk
- Small cap, limited public segment disclosure

## Verdict
Good niche, but margins tell the truth — wait for pricing power to return before buying.
