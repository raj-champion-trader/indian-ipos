# CMR Green Technologies Limited (CMRGREEN)

## What it does
CMR Green recycles scrap metal. It melts aluminium scrap into alloy ingots and liquid aluminium for car and bike makers, plus zinc alloys, billets, and furnace-ready scrap of copper, brass, lead, and steel. It runs India's largest secondary aluminium capacity — about 4x its nearest competitor — and leads the market with 42-45% volume share in cast alloys (FY25).

## The numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 5,968 cr (FY24), Rs 6,697 cr (FY25), 9M FY26 Rs 6,291 cr (annualizing above Rs 8,300 cr).
- PAT: loss Rs 838.56 cr (FY24, after exceptional items), profit Rs 155.04 cr (FY25), 9M FY26 Rs 162.39 cr.
- OPM thin: 2.4% (FY24 standalone) to 3.9% (FY26); ROCE 10.5%, ROE 8.5%.
- Borrowings Rs 737 cr (Mar 2026, screener standalone); FY26 interest Rs 49 cr, depreciation Rs 26 cr.
- IPO (2026) fully OFS — zero money to the company; priced ~19.4x annualized FY26 P/E.

## Lynch lens
A turnaround with a cyclical spine. FY24's huge loss (exceptional items) turned to profit by FY25, and the niche dominance (4x nearest rival, 42-45% cast alloy share) gives real moat. But 2-3% operating margins mean the business earns its living on volume and scrap-spread, not pricing power. Scrap recycling qualifies as a Lynch-approved boring business.

## TAM
India recycled aluminium market: 2.16 million MT (FY25) — cast alloy 1.01 mn MT (46.7%), rolled 0.59 mn MT (27.5%), extrusion 0.34 mn MT (15.6%). Rising EV penetration boosts per-vehicle aluminium use.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Aluminium alloy ingots/liquid — ~82% of revenue; scrap purchase price vs LME-linked selling spread drives almost all profit.
2. New extrusion and rolled alloy value-added segments — margin expansion path.
3. FY24 exceptional write-offs — the loss year; absence of repeats keeps PAT positive.

## Red flags
- Razor-thin OPM (2.4-3.9%) — one bad scrap-spread year wipes profit.
- Entire IPO served as promoter exit (OFS).
- Top 10 customers supply just over half of revenue; commodity cycle and LME price swings rule results.

## Verdict
Dominant scrap recycler mid-turnaround with wafer margins — track the spread, not the story.
