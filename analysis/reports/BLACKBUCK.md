# BlackBuck Limited / Zinka Logistics Solutions Ltd (BLACKBUCK)

## What it does
BlackBuck runs a digital platform for India's truck operators. It connects shippers with truckers for freight, and sells toll payments, fuel payments, vehicle tracking, vehicle finance and fleet services. Customers pay fees and subscriptions for using the platform.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 296 crore (FY24); Rs 422-427 crore (FY25) — ~43% growth.
- Net result: loss of Rs 194 crore (FY24); near-breakeven loss of Rs 9 crore (FY25); TTM profit ~Rs 171 crore (post-IPO, includes other income effects).
- OPM: -53% (FY24) improved to ~22% (FY25).
- Expenses FY25: total Rs 331-333 crore; detailed head-wise split (employee %, advertising %) data not found.
- Market cap ~Rs 10,700 crore; P/E ~62.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround trying to become a fast grower. Losses collapsed in FY25 and the platform crossed into operating profit — classic loss-to-profit inflection. Network-effect niche (largest digital trucking platform in India) gives it a moat if truckers and shippers stay locked in. But P/E ~62 prices in a lot of already-delivered growth; margin of safety looks thin.

## TAM
Indian trucking/freight market estimated at US$150 bn+ per IPO coverage; exact FY and source data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Take-rate/fees on freight matching and payments (toll, fuel) as trucker base scales.
2. Subscription income from fleet services (telematics, finance facilitation).
3. Employee and marketing cost discipline vs revenue growth.

## Red flags
- TTM profit flattered by non-operating items; core net margin still fragile.
- Heavy historical cash burn; competition from other logistics-tech players.
- Valuation rich (P/E ~62) for a business just exiting losses.

## Verdict
Quality network-effect story, but the easy turnaround money already got made — wait for a better price.
