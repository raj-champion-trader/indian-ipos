# Divgi TorqTransfer Systems Limited (DIVGIITTS)

## What it does
Divgi TorqTransfer makes transmission parts for vehicles — torque converters, transfer cases for 4WD SUVs, synchronizers, and EV transmission units. It also assembles turbochargers with BorgWarner. Customers include Tata Motors and Mahindra. Listed March 2023.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 271 crore FY23; Rs 253 crore FY24; Rs 219 crore FY25 (screener). FY26 rebounded to ~Rs 375 crore (+56%), with PAT up ~90%.
- Net profit: Rs 51 crore FY23; Rs 40 crore FY24; Rs 24 crore FY25.
- OPM: 28% → 21% → 17% (FY23→FY25); ROE ~7.6%; P/E ~56.8; dividend payout ~25%; nearly debt-free.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical in turnaround. FY25 collapsed because transfer-case demand fell ~32% (weak SUV/UV volumes plus EV substitution fear for ICE drivetrain parts). The recovery already started in FY26 with record revenue. Niche angle: India's only independent maker of torque converters and transfer cases — a genuine specialist. The EV transition threatens ICE parts but opens its EV transmission line (it brands itself India's largest EV transmission maker). P/E ~57 on recovering earnings leaves little margin for error.

## TAM
Indian auto components industry ~USD 70–80 billion FY24 (ACMA estimates); torque-converter/transfer-case specific TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Transfer-case and torque-converter volumes for Tata/Mahindra SUV programmes.
2. Turbocharger assembly ramp with BorgWarner (localisation tailwind).
3. EV transmission system orders (new, small but strategically vital).

## Red flags
Customer concentration (Tata/Mahindra); ICE-to-EV substitution overhang; FY25 showed how fast profits halve; high P/E against cyclically depressed-then-recovering earnings.

## Verdict
A capable niche auto-parts specialist mid-recovery — interesting on the EV-transmission option, overpriced for the cyclical core.
