# Yatra Online Ltd (YATRA)

## What it does
Yatra runs an online travel platform that books flights, hotels, holidays and buses for consumers (B2C) and manages travel for companies (B2B). It counts as India's largest managed corporate-travel provider, serving 1,300+ corporates and ~58,000 SME clients. It earns commissions and service fees on each booking.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 297 cr (FY24), Rs 607 cr (FY25), Rs 636 cr (FY26) — revenue recognition changes inflate the jump.
- Net profit: Rs -19 cr (FY24), Rs 28 cr (FY25), Rs 38 cr (FY26). First sustained profits after years of losses.
- OPM ~8% (FY25-FY26). Almost debt free (borrowings Rs 51 cr FY26 vs Rs 106 cr FY23).
- Stock P/E ~50, ROE ~5%, no dividend. 223 debtor days (corporate clients pay slowly).

## Revenue streams
B2B corporate travel & SME bookings (largest chunk), B2C online travel agency (air tickets, hotels, holidays); exact FY split — data not found on free screener. ~81% of traffic comes direct/organic.

## Expenses
Employees and technology the main heads (exact split: data not found). FY26: interest Rs 9 cr, depreciation Rs 34 cr. Depreciation runs high after a big write-down/amortisation cycle.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround — years of losses (-373 cr in FY18, -155 cr in FY21) turning into steady small profits since FY25. Niche angle: #1 in managed corporate travel, a boring, sticky B2B business competitors ignore. P/E of 50 looks rich until profits compound; if FY26 PAT of 38 cr grows fast, multiple compresses. Promoter holds 62.7%.

## TAM
Indian online travel market estimated at USD 35-40 bn gross bookings (FY25 industry estimates); corporate travel management a USD 8-10 bn slice.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Corporate travel (B2B) bookings growth and take-rate expansion.
2. Hotel packages and cross-sell margins on the B2C app.
3. Operating leverage — fixed tech cost spread over rising bookings (OPM 5% FY25 to 8% FY26).

## Red flags
Near-zero tax rate flatters PAT (carried-forward losses). High debtor days (223). Stock -27% in a year despite profit growth. Competition from MakeMyTrip, EaseMyTrip, ixigo.

## Verdict
Genuine turnaround with a dull-but-durable corporate-travel niche — buy the B2B story only if profit growth keeps outrunning the 50 P/E.
