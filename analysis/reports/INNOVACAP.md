# Innova Captab Limited (INNOVACAP)

## What it does
Company makes medicines — it runs a CDMO business (contract manufacturing drugs for other pharma companies) plus its own branded generics sold in India. It manufactures tablets, capsules and other formulations at its own plants. Listed on NSE/BSE in 2023.

## The numbers
- FY23 revenue Rs 867 crore, PAT Rs 58 crore; FY24 (restated) revenue ~Rs 876-933 crore, PAT ~Rs 68-70 crore (sources differ).
- FY26 consolidated revenue Rs 1,630 crore with EBITDA Rs 250.3 crore (Tickertape); trailing revenue ~Rs 1,749 crore, profit ~Rs 154 crore (Screener).
- Latest quarter (Jun-2026): revenue Rs 403 crore, up 38.4% YoY — growth accelerating.
- Market cap ~Rs 6,038 crore (Screener); trailing P/E ~38, price/sales ~3.4.
- Expense heads: raw materials (APIs/excipients) dominate, then employee and power costs — exact percentages data not found.

## Lynch lens
A pharma stalwart growing into a fast grower — consistent 20%+ growth recently with 38% YoY in the latest quarter. Boring-business angle: contract drug manufacturing and branded generics nobody talks about. At P/E ~38 with ~30-40% growth, PEG looks near or below 1 — reasonable for the growth. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
Indian pharmaceutical market ~$50 billion, growing ~10% annually; global pharma CDMO market ~$100+ billion (period/source: industry estimates — exact figures data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. CDMO/contract manufacturing — capacity utilisation and new client molecules drive volume growth.
2. Branded generics domestic portfolio — prescription-driven, higher margin.
3. New capacity ramp — capex coming online lifting FY26 revenue to Rs 1,630 crore.

## Red flags
- Growth relies on new plant ramp-ups; execution slippage hurts.
- Interest cost flag (Screener notes possible interest capitalisation).
- Valuation near 38x trailing leaves little room for stumbles.

## Verdict
Quietly compounding pharma name — attractive if growth sustains, but pay attention to the P/E.
