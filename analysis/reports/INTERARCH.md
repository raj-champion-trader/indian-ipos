# Interarch Building Solutions Ltd (INTERARCH)

## What it does
Company builds pre-engineered steel buildings (PEB) — factory sheds, warehouses and industrial structures — as turnkey projects under the TRAC and TRACDEK brands. It designs, fabricates and erects steel structures for industrial clients.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,293 cr (FY24), Rs 1,454 cr (FY25), Rs 1,898 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 1,977 cr.
- PAT: Rs 86 cr (FY24), Rs 108 cr (FY25), Rs 135 cr (FY26).
- OPM ~10%. Almost debt free (borrowings Rs 18 cr, Mar 2026).
- ROE 16.8%; ROCE 23.7%. P/E ~21x; market cap ~Rs 2,861 cr.
- 5-year CAGR: sales 27%, profit 84%.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring business — steel sheds no one talks about, but every factory and warehouse needs one. #2 among integrated PEB players in India on 221k MT installed capacity (FY26). ROCE above 20% shows a genuine franchise. 21x P/E against ~25-30% growth gives a reasonable PEG. IPO Aug 2024.

## TAM
Indian pre-engineered buildings market — industry estimates around Rs 40,000-50,000 cr and growing double digits; exact sourced figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Turnkey PEB project execution volume (core revenue engine).
2. Steel input cost vs contract pricing spread — margins swing with steel prices.
3. Order wins from manufacturing/warehousing capex cycle; capacity utilization of 221k MT.

## Red flags
- Thin ~10% OPM; steel price spikes can compress it.
- Working capital heavy: debtor days 55, inventory days 69.
- Cyclical — tied to industrial construction capex.

## Verdict
Boring-but-growing niche leader at fair price — worth owning on dips.
