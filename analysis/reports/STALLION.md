# Stallion India Fluorochemicals Limited (STALLION)

## What it does
Stallion India blends, fills and processes refrigerant and industrial gases — the cooling gases inside air-conditioners and fridges — plus specialty gases for fire protection, pharma, defence and electronics. It buys gases in bulk, blends them, and sells in cylinders to factories and HVAC customers. Listed January 2025.

## The numbers
- Sales (screener, standalone): Rs 226 crore FY23; Rs 233 crore FY24; Rs 377 crore FY25.
- Net profit: Rs 10 crore FY23; Rs 15 crore FY24; Rs 32 crore FY25.
- OPM 8% → 10% → 13%; ROE ~9%; almost debt-free; P/E ~47.6 at Rs 214 (market cap ~Rs 2,479 crore).
- Next-year trajectory: revenue ~Rs 434 crore and net income ~Rs 43.8 crore (simplywall.st, FY26).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with a boring niche. Gas blending sounds dull — until you note India's AC and refrigeration boom plus semiconductor/pharma demand. Five-year sales CAGR ~30%, profit CAGR ~35%. Management guides 30–35% revenue CAGR. P/E ~47 against ~35% profit growth gives a PEG near 1.3 — not cheap, not absurd. Margins (13% OPM, ROE ~9%) remain the weak link; gas re-pressurisation capacity from IPO money should help.

## TAM
Indian fluorochemicals/specialty gases market projected at USD 675–725 million by 2029, growing 16–18% CAGR from 2024 (Value Research IPO analysis).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Refrigerant gas volumes tied to HVAC/AC and auto AC demand (core revenue).
2. Purchase price of imported bulk gases vs selling spread (blending margin).
3. New high-margin lines: fire-protection, electronics/semiconductor and medical gases.

## Red flags
Commodity-spread business with modest ROE; no dividend; import-price dependence; new capacity execution risk.

## Verdict
A dull-but-compounding gas blender with a credible niche tailwind — reasonably priced for its growth, worth tracking.
