# Signatureglobal (India) Ltd (SIGNATURE)

## What it does
Company builds and sells affordable and mid-segment apartments in Delhi NCR, mainly Gurugram. It claims 27% market share in Gurugram and 13% in NCR in affordable and lower mid-segment housing (company disclosure).

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 862 cr (FY23), Rs 848 cr (FY24), Rs 1,825 cr (FY25), Rs 1,381 cr (FY26).
- PAT: Rs -113 cr (FY23), Rs +22 cr (FY24), Rs +63 cr (FY25), Rs 16 cr (FY26); TTM Rs -22 cr.
- Interest cost Rs 267 cr (TTM FY26) — the big drag.
- Borrowings Rs 3,275 cr (Mar FY26); CFO -Rs 246 cr (FY26).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with turnaround hopes. FY25 showed the promise — revenue more than doubled and PAT hit Rs 63 cr on the NCR housing boom — but FY26 sagged again and cash flow turned negative. Real estate plus Rs 3,275 cr debt equals a boom-bust ride, not a compounder.

## TAM
Indian residential real estate ~Rs 3-4 lakh cr annual sales (FY24, ANAROCK/industry estimates); affordable housing segment a large government-priority slice of it.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gurugram/NCR residential sales and price rises — drove the FY25 doubling.
2. Interest cost of Rs 267 cr (FY26 TTM) — single biggest profit destroyer.
3. Land bank monetization and project completions — lumpy revenue recognition.

## Red flags
- Debt Rs 3,275 cr (Mar FY26) with low interest coverage.
- Negative operating cash flow (FY26) and TTM loss (Rs -22 cr).
- TTM OPM -8% — execution costs running above revenue.

## Verdict
Leveraged NCR housing cyclical — profitable only when Gurugram booms; too hot for a Lynch-style buy.
