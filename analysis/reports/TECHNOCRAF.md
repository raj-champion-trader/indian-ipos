# Technocraft Ventures Ltd (TECHNOCRAF)

## What it does
Technocraft Ventures builds public infrastructure — water and wastewater plants, roads, urban infrastructure, power distribution and micro-tunnelling — under EPC contracts for state governments and government agencies in northern India (UP, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan, Delhi). It started with housing and roads in Uttar Pradesh and now works on central schemes like AMRUT, Namami Gange, JJM and PMGSY.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 181 crore FY23, Rs 227 crore FY24, Rs 281 crore FY25, Rs 347 crore FY26 (RHP figures).
- PAT: Rs 11 crore FY23, Rs 19 crore FY24, Rs 28 crore FY25, Rs 43 crore FY26; PAT margin 12.6% FY26.
- ROE 26.5%, ROCE 27.7% FY26; debt-to-equity 0.55 FY26.
- Borrowings Rs 111 crore Mar 2024; debtor days ~162 (standalone screener data).
- IPO Aug 2026: Rs 252 crore issue, Rs 150 crore earmarked for working capital.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical, government-dependent EPC play — order-book driven, working-capital hungry, and payments depend on state budgets. Solid growth (revenue roughly doubling FY23-FY26) and healthy ROCE, but 77x P/E post listing (screener) prices in a lot. Classic small-cap infra cycle stock: great in a capex upcycle, painful when government spending slows.

## TAM
Data not found (India water infrastructure / EPC market size not stated in sources reviewed).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Execution and margin on water & wastewater EPC orders (core segment).
2. Order inflow from central schemes (JJM, Namami Gange, AMRUT).
3. Working-capital cycle — receivable days from government clients swing finance costs.

## Red flags
- Customer concentration: government clients, slow payments (162 debtor days).
- Rising debt (Rs 67 crore to Rs 111 crore in FY24); IPO funds mostly for working capital, not growth assets.
- Very high P/E (~78x) for a cyclical EPC business.
- No dividend in 5 years.

## Verdict
Decent small EPC operator, but the valuation already assumes several flawless years of government orders.
