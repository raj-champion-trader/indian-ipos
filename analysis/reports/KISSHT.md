# Onemi Technology Solutions Limited (KISSHT)

## What it does
Onemi runs Kissht, a phone app that lends money for shopping and business needs. It checks customers with its own software and hands out small loans quickly. Recently listed on the NSE.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 264 cr; FY24 Rs 429 cr; FY25 Rs 321 cr (standalone, Screener.in).
- PAT: FY23 Rs 25 cr; FY24 Rs 82 cr; FY25 Rs 51 cr.
- OPM: FY24 30%; FY25 26%.
- Nearly debt-free: borrowings Rs 32 cr in FY25, Rs 18 cr in FY26.
- FY25 expenses Rs 237 cr (standalone). Detailed expense split: data not found.
- Consolidated figures and loan-book size: data not found.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical-ish financial with a tech wrapper. Revenue fell ~25% in FY25 after the RBI's crackdown on unsecured digital lending hit the whole sector — proof this business lives at the mercy of regulators. Profitable and asset-light, which helps, but the FY25 reversal shows how fast the ground can shift. Fast grower only in friendly weather.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Loan disbursement volumes and take rates on the Kissht app.
2. Credit losses / loan write-offs on unsecured consumer credit.
3. Regulatory environment (RBI rules on digital lending and lending-service-provider models).

## Red flags

## Verdict
A profitable but regulator-exposed lender — buy only if you can stomach policy headlines.
