# Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency (IREDA)

## What it does
IREDA, a government-owned lender, gives loans for solar parks, wind farms, hydro and other green-energy projects. It earns the interest spread between what it charges borrowers and what it pays on its own borrowing.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY25 Rs 6,743 cr; FY26 Rs 8,310 cr (quarter sums, screener.in) — ~23% growth.
- PAT: FY25 Rs 1,699 cr; FY26 ~Rs 1,874 cr (Jun-25 quarter dipped to Rs 247 cr on a provision spike).
- Financing (NIM-type) margin ~29-35% of revenue; interest cost is the biggest expense (FY26 ~Rs 5,000+ cr).
- GNPA ~4.1% and NNPA ~2.1% (recent quarters, screener); Government of India holds ~majority; IPO November 2023.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with a state guarantee halo — the only listed pure-play green-energy lender, riding India's renewable capex boom. But it sells a commodity (money) with leverage: profit equals loan-book growth minus funding cost and bad loans. The Jun-25 provision spike shows the model's teeth. ## TAM
India needs ~US$30-35 billion+ annual renewable investment to hit 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030 (government/industry estimates) — IREDA's addressable lending pool runs into lakhs of crores.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Loan book growth (~20%+ p.a.) in solar/wind/hydro lending.
2. Cost of funds — government-backed borrowing spreads versus lending yields.
3. Asset quality — provision spikes (Jun-25 quarter) directly hit PAT.

## Red flags
Government-owned pricing/mandates; rising NPAs from stressed solar/manufacturing borrowers; capital raises dilute as the book grows; interest-rate sensitive.

## Verdict
Growth + green-mission story, but a leveraged lender — size the position to survive a bad-loan cycle.
