# JSW Cement Ltd (JSWCEMENT)

## What it does
JSW Cement makes green cement, using GGBS (granulated blast furnace slag) from group steel plants to produce blended cement. It ranks among India's top 10 cement companies by capacity and sales volume (FY25). Buyers: construction firms, infrastructure projects, home builders.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 5,810 crore (FY23), Rs 6,002 crore (FY24), Rs 5,785 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 6,849 crore — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 104 crore (FY23), Rs 62 crore (FY24), Rs -164 crore loss (FY25); TTM profit Rs 721 crore
- Operating margin: 11% (FY23), 14% (FY24), 11% (FY25), 18% TTM
- Expenses (FY25): total Rs 5,169 crore; interest Rs 450 crore, depreciation Rs 310 crore — heavy debt load from capacity buildout
- P/E ~21.6; ROE 14.7%, ROCE 11.1% (latest)

## Lynch lens
Cyclical through and through — a FY25 loss followed by a sharp TTM profit rebound tracks cement prices and utilisation. The green-cement/GGBS angle gives a niche cost-and-ESG edge: slag costs less than clinker. Capacity expansion adds the growth story. PEG: volatile earnings make PEG meaningless; the 21.6 P/E sits on recovering (not steady) profits.

## TAM
India cement market: the world's second-largest; JSW ranks top-10 by capacity (FY25). Verified absolute market-size figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Cement realisations per tonne — price recovery swung FY25 loss to TTM profit
2. GGBS-based blended mix — structural cost and margin edge vs clinker-heavy rivals
3. Interest burden (Rs 450 crore FY25) from expansion debt — the main profit drag

## Red flags
- Net loss in FY25 (Rs -164 crore) — earnings whipsaw
- High leverage: Rs 450 crore interest vs Rs 310 crore depreciation signals big debt
- Capacity wars in Indian cement keep pricing brutal
- Still subscale vs Ultratech/Adani-era consolidation

## Verdict
A leveraged cyclical with a green twist — for investors who track cement cycles, not buy-and-hold sleep.
