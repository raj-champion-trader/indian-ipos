# Laxmi India Finance Ltd (LAXMIINDIA)

## What it does
Laxmi India Finance lends money — a non-deposit-taking NBFC serving individuals, small businesses and institutions, with a focus on MSME borrowers, mainly in Rajasthan. It raises funds from banks, debentures and its own capital, and earns the spread between lending rates and borrowing costs.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 246 cr (FY25) → Rs 317 cr (FY26), up 29%.
- PAT: Rs 36 cr (FY25) → Rs 50 cr (FY26), up 39%.
- 5-year CAGR: revenue ~29%, profit ~36.5%.
- Gross NPA 2.40% (Jun 2026); ROE ~14%; P/E ~11.7x; market cap ~Rs 662 cr.
- Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026): revenue Rs 94 cr, PAT Rs 17 cr.
- AUM: data not found. Zero dividend payout.

## RevenueStreams
Interest income on MSME and other loans forms nearly all revenue (FY25/FY26); fee/other income minor. Detailed product split: data not found.

## Lynch lens
Small fast grower in a boring niche — small-town MSME lending, away from headline fintech. 36% profit CAGR at 11.7x P/E reads attractive (PEG well under 0.5) if asset quality holds. Regional concentration in Rajasthan cuts both ways. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
Indian MSME credit gap estimated in the tens of lakh crore rupees (industry estimates, mid-2020s). Precise figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Loan book growth (~29% revenue growth, FY26).
2. Net interest margin — lending yields of ~17-29% (quarterly financing margins per screener) against borrowing costs.
3. Credit costs / NPAs — gross NPA 2.40% (Jun 2026) the main swing factor.

## Expenses
Borrowing cost (interest on debt/bank lines) forms the largest head — exact % data not found; provisions for bad loans and employee cost follow. Figures: data not found.

## Red flags
- Small balance sheet; funding cost rises when rates rise.
- MSME unsecured/semi-secured lending carries default risk; NPA trend needs watching.
- Zero dividend, limited public float and disclosure depth.

## Verdict
A cheap, fast-growing small-town lender — interesting PEG story, but size and asset quality make it a small-position or watch-list stock.
