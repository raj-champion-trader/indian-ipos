# Sri Lotus Developers & Realty Ltd (LOTUSDEV)

## What it does
Sri Lotus Developers builds and redevelops homes and commercial properties in Mumbai's western suburbs, focusing on luxury and ultra-luxury projects. It works on an asset-light model — redeveloping existing buildings rather than buying expensive land.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 453 cr (FY24) → Rs 548 cr (FY25) → Rs 769 cr (FY26).
- PAT: Rs 120 cr (FY24) → Rs 228 cr (FY25) → Rs 243 cr (FY26) — ~32% net margin (FY26).
- OPM ~36% (FY26); ROCE ~21%; ROE ~17%.
- Borrowings Rs 131 cr (FY26) — nearly debt-free; interest just Rs 2 cr.
- Cash flow from operations -Rs 326 cr (FY26) — working capital (construction cycle) absorbs cash.

## TAM
Mumbai redevelopment pipeline runs into thousands of ageing societies; lakhs of units await rebuild (industry estimates, mid-2020s). Precise figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Margins on luxury/redevelopment projects (low land cost → ~32% net margin, FY26).
2. Pace of project completions booked as revenue.
3. Near-zero interest cost — almost all operating profit flows to the bottom line.

## Expenses
Construction/materials and operating expenses Rs 488 cr total (FY26, ~63% of revenue); interest Rs 2 cr; depreciation Rs 2 cr.

## Red flags
- Lumpy, project-timing-driven revenue.
- Negative operating cash flow (-Rs 326 cr, FY26) despite accounting profit.
- Concentration in one micro-market (Mumbai western suburbs); regulatory approvals can delay projects.

## Verdict
A high-margin, low-debt Mumbai redeveloper — attractive niche economics, but real estate cycles and lumpy bookings demand a margin of safety.
