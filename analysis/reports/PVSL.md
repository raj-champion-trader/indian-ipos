# Popular Vehicles & Services Ltd (PVSL)

## What it does
Company runs automobile dealerships. It sells Maruti Suzuki cars, services them, sells spare parts, and earns commission on car loans and insurance. One of India's oldest Maruti dealers (Kuttukaran Group, since 1983).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 5,616 cr (FY24); Rs 5,541 cr (FY25) — flat/declining.
- PAT: Rs 76 cr (FY24); loss of Rs 10 cr (FY25).
- OPM: 4.6% (FY24) to 2.9% (FY25). Thin dealer margins turning thinner.
- Borrowings: Rs 922 cr (FY25), rising to ~Rs 1,406 cr (FY26); interest Rs 89 cr (FY25); depreciation Rs 99 cr (FY25). Interest coverage flagged low; negative ROE in FY25/FY26 quarters.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical, and a weak one. Car retail follows demand cycles, and PVSL adds leverage on top. No niche dominance — dealership businesses lack pricing power against the manufacturer. PEG not meaningful with falling earnings.

## TAM
India passenger vehicle dealership and after-sales service market — large, but PVSL's slice depends on Kerala/Tamil Nadu demand. Specific TAM figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. New vehicle sales volumes and margins (Maruti demand cycle, discounting).
2. After-sales service and spare parts (higher-margin, cushions downturns).
3. Finance/insurance commission income and interest cost burden (Rs 89 cr FY25 interest vs Rs 159 cr FY25 operating profit).

## Red flags
- Swung to net loss in FY25; losses continuing into FY26 quarters.
- Debt climbing from Rs 922 cr (FY25) to ~Rs 1,406 cr (FY26) while profits shrink.
- Thin margins (OPM 2.9% FY25) leave no cushion.

## Verdict
Lynch would pass — a leveraged cyclical in a downturn, not a turnaround with visible catalysts yet.
