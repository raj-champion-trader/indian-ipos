# Aeroflex Industries Limited (AEROFLEX)

## What it does
Aeroflex makes stainless steel flexible hoses, assemblies and fittings that carry liquids and gases in factories, planes and ships. Sells mostly to exporters and industrial users — about 80% of revenue comes from outside India. A subsidiary of SAT Industries.

## The numbers
- Revenue FY25: Rs 376 crore; PAT FY25: Rs 52.5 crore (up ~26% YoY). EBITDA FY25 ~Rs 80 crore (~21% margin).
- Growth target: Rs 1,000 crore revenue by FY29 (management guidance, ~27% CAGR).
- New: metal bellows launched FY25; capacity expanded to 20 million metres. Export mix ~80% (FY25).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring niche. Nobody gets excited about flexible metal hoses, yet the company grows 25%+ with 20%+ EBITDA margins and a new data-centre liquid-cooling (SFN) line — a Q with Rs 32.4 crore SFN skid revenue. Niche dominance in India with global export reach. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: data not found, so PEG not computable.

## TAM
Global flexible metal hose market: data not found for exact size. India data-centre liquid-cooling TAM: data not found. Adjacent signal: management targets Rs 1,000 crore own revenue by FY29.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Stainless steel flexible hoses segment — roughly Rs 423 crore of ~Rs 442 crore annual run-rate revenue (dominant profit engine).
2. Exports (~80% of revenue) — dollar sales carry better margins than domestic.
3. New SFN liquid-cooling skids for data centres — fast-growing second engine.

## Red flags
- Customer concentration/dependence on oil & gas and industrial capex cycles.
- New businesses (bellows, SFN) still small; execution risk on 30%+ growth targets.
- Metal (steel) price swings hit raw material costs.

## Verdict
Boring hose-maker turned fast grower with a data-centre kicker — worth tracking margin delivery, not just guidance.
