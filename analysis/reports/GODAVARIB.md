# Godavari Biorefineries Ltd (GODAVARIB)

## What it does
Company runs an integrated biorefinery in Maharashtra (Somaiya Group, ~63% promoter stake). Turns sugarcane into sugar, ethanol, bio-based chemicals and power. Largest Indian maker of MPO by capacity and one of only two global makers of natural 1,3-Butylene Glycol; sells branded sugar under "Jivana".

## The numbers
- Revenue Rs 1,965 crore, PAT ~Rs 1 crore (FY26, latest).
- Operating profit ~Rs 124 crore, OPM ~6% (FY26).
- Q1 Jun 2026 standalone: sales Rs 542 crore, net loss Rs 21 crore, OPM -0.3%.
- Debt ~Rs 525 crore; ROE 4.1% last year, 2.1% over 3 years; ROCE 6.3%.
- 5-year sales CAGR ~5%; 3-year profit CAGR ~37% (off a low base).
- Segment-wise mix percentages: data not found in fetched sources.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with turnaround hopes. Sugar and ethanol realisations swing with government policy (ethanol blending prices, sugar export quotas) — classic commodity cycle. Low interest coverage and sub-6% ROCE mean the turnaround has not arrived yet. Speciality chemicals niche (natural 1,3-BG, MPO) gives a small moat, but that remains a minor slice of revenue. P/E: not meaningful on Rs 1 crore PAT.

## TAM
India ethanol blending program targets (E20) drive a multi-thousand-crore ethanol procurement market; India sugar industry size roughly Rs 1.5+ lakh crore annually (exact sourced figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Ethanol sales realisation and blending procurement price (government-set).
2. Sugar prices and cane cost (largest raw material).
3. Speciality chemical volumes (MPO, natural 1,3-BG) at higher margins.

## Red flags
- Near-zero FY26 PAT and a loss in Jun 2026 quarter.
- Rs 525 crore debt with low interest coverage.
- ROE ~2-4%; policy dependence on ethanol/sugar pricing.

## Verdict
Policy-dependent cyclical in a weak stretch — watch, don't buy, until ethanol economics and ROCE recover.
