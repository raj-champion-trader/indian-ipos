# Aditya Infotech Limited (CPPLUS)

## What it does
Aditya Infotech owns the CP PLUS brand, India's biggest homegrown video-surveillance (CCTV camera) maker and seller. Sells cameras, recorders and security gear through a huge dealer network, plus monitoring and software services. Listed on NSE in 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue from operations: Rs 1,661 crore (FY22) to Rs 3,123 crore (FY25).
- Profit FY25: ~Rs 351 crore (~11% net margin). FY26: ~Rs 368 crore; TTM ~Rs 477 crore on TTM revenue ~Rs 4,883 crore.
- Market cap ~Rs 42,311 crore; trades ~22x book.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with niche dominance. Market share in Indian video surveillance: 20.2% by revenue FY24, 43.3% FY26 per Frost & Sullivan — share doubling while growing the top line ~23% a year. Lynch loves a local champion taking share from foreign brands (Hikvision, Dahua). Insider buying: data not found. P/E on TTM earnings ~90x+ (42,311/477) — extremely rich; PEG roughly 3-4 even on fast growth.

## TAM
Indian video surveillance industry: company holds 43.3% share (FY26, Frost & Sullivan); absolute market size figure: data not found in sources gathered.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Camera and recorder hardware volumes through the CP PLUS dealer network — the profit engine.
2. Market-share gains vs multinational/Chinese brands — pricing plus local service.
3. Higher-margin software/monitoring and solutions attach — growing layer.

## Red flags
- Valuation: ~90x TTM earnings, 22x book — perfection already priced.
- Competition from Chinese giants with deeper component pipelines.
- Component (chip, lens) import dependence and duty changes.

## Verdict
Great business, dangerous price — Lynch would admire the moat but wait for a pullback.
