# Canara HSBC Life Insurance Company Ltd (CANHLIFE)

## What it does
Canara HSBC Life sells life insurance — savings plans, term protection, retirement, ULIPs, and annuities — mostly through its bank shareholders' branches (Canara Bank and HSBC). Banks distribute the policies; the insurer invests the premiums and pays claims. Listed October 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 11,841 crore (FY24) → Rs 10,710 crore (FY25, screener view — insurance revenue varies with accounting/ULIP marking).
- PAT: Rs 113 crore (FY24) → Rs 117 crore (FY25). 3-year ROE ~8%.
- Borrowings: zero (FY25). Market cap Rs 14,648 crore; P/E ~112; dividend payout ~32% (Aug 2026).
- Expense split: claims/benefit payouts plus commissions dominate; exact % data not found.

## Lynch lens
Bancassurance stalwart. Bank branches sell the policies cheaply — no costly agent force — a structural cost edge. But P/E ~112 and ~9x book against 8% ROE prices it like a fast grower while it grows slowly. Lynch pass.

## TAM
Indian life insurance industry: annual premium (APE) market in the Rs 3-4+ lakh crore range with low penetration (exact size data not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Premium growth via Canara Bank's branch network (bancassurance productivity).
2. Investment/actuarial experience — surplus on liabilities drives profit.
3. Product mix shift to protection/high-margin plans from ULIPs.

## Red flags
- Extreme valuation (P/E ~112) for single-digit growth.
- Revenue declined FY24→FY25 on screener view; ULIP market sensitivity.
- Dependence on promoter bank distribution; regulator caps on bank-led selling.

## Verdict
Solid insurer, silly price — wait for a valuation reset before touching it.
