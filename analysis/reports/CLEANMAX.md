# Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited (CLEANMAX)

## What it does
CleanMax builds and runs solar, wind, and hybrid power plants and sells that electricity to factories, offices, hospitals, and colleges under long-term contracts. It also earns from energy services and carbon credits. It holds the #1 spot in India's commercial & industrial (C&I) clean power market with ~8% share (2025) and 2.80 GW operational capacity.

## The numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 1,425 cr (FY24), Rs 1,610 cr (FY25); screener FY26 shows Rs 6,003 cr (standalone basis — jump likely reflects project/other revenue recognition; consolidated FY26: data not found).
- PAT: loss of Rs 37.6 cr (FY24), Rs 19.4 cr (FY25) consolidated; screener standalone PAT Rs 298 cr (FY25), Rs 767 cr (FY26).
- OPM ~19-21% (screener, FY24-FY26); FY26 standalone interest Rs 369 cr, depreciation Rs 50 cr.
- Debt heavy: borrowings Rs 7,974 cr consolidated (Mar 2025); 3-year RoE only 0.71%.
- IPO Feb 2026; listed at ~9% discount.

## Lynch lens
A fast grower in a hot industry — the trap category. Capacity grew fast, but shareholders earned almost nothing (RoE 0.71% over 3 years) because debt eats the returns. Renewable power to businesses sounds great; sub-1% RoE tells the real story. P/E near 377x (IPO review) prices in perfection.

## TAM
India C&I renewable/open-access market: company holds ~8% share of it (2025, IPO documents); absolute market size figure: data not found. India targets ~500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030, giving a long runway.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Sale of power from open-access solar/wind/hybrid farms to C&I customers — core margin engine.
2. Interest cost on Rs ~8,000 cr debt — the biggest profit killer.
3. EPC and O&M services plus carbon credit income — secondary contributors.

## Red flags
- Contingent liabilities Rs 7,230 cr (screener).
- 78.8% revenue from just Karnataka and Gujarat — policy-concentration risk.
- IPO money goes to debt repayment and unnamed acquisitions, not growth assets.
- Other income Rs 464 cr flatters earnings (FY26 screener).

## Verdict
Glamour-industry fast grower with debt-drowned returns — avoid until RoE proves itself.
