# Central Mine Planning & Design Institute Limited (CMPDI)

## What it does
CMPDI, a Coal India subsidiary, advises governments and miners on how to find, plan, and run mines — exploration drilling, mine design, environmental clearance studies, geomatics (mapping), and coal beneficiation advice. It covers the whole mining lifecycle from first survey to mine closure and holds ~61% share of India's mining consultancy market.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,733 cr (FY24), Rs 2,103-2,178 cr (FY25, screener standalone vs IPO review consolidated), Rs 2,317 cr (FY26 screener standalone).
- PAT: Rs 503 cr (FY24), Rs 667 cr (FY25), Rs 613 cr (FY26); PAT margin 29-32%.
- OPM: 42% (FY24), 40% (FY25), 34% (FY26) — softening.
- Virtually debt free (borrowings Rs 1 cr); CFO Rs 716 cr (FY26); RoE 28.4%.
- IPO Mar 2026 at 18.5x FY25 P/E; screener P/E now ~25x.

## Lynch lens
An asset play wrapped in a stalwart: a fat-margin, debt-free consultancy with a 61% market share and ~30% PAT margins — a toll booth on India's coal plans. Consulting scales without capital. Risk reads like a family business with one parent: Coal India and its units supply 66% of revenue, and the top-10 client list supplies 98%.

## TAM
India mining consultancy market: CMPDI holds ~61% share (IPO documents); absolute market size: data not found. Growth driver: Coal India's expansion to 1 billion tonnes output plus rising mineral exploration spend.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Consultancy fees from Coal India and subsidiaries — 66% of revenue, the profit core.
2. Exploration and drilling services for government mineral programs.
3. Employee costs and project execution margins — OPM slipped from 42% to 34% (FY24 to FY26), the drag to watch.

## Red flags
- 98% revenue from top 10 clients, mostly government.
- OPM fell 8 points over two years (FY24-FY26).
- Government policy/budget cycles decide its income; parent controls strategy.

## Verdict
Dominant, debt-free, high-margin monopoly consultancy — a keeper if valuation stays sane.
