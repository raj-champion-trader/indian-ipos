# HDB Financial Services Limited (HDBFS)

## What it does
HDB Financial Services, a subsidiary of HDFC Bank, lends money to individuals, micro-enterprises and businesses. It runs three lines — consumer finance (personal, gold, used-vehicle loans), enterprise lending (MSME loans) and asset finance. Listed July 2025 IPO.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 14,173 crore (FY24); Rs 16,300 crore (FY25); Rs 18,431 crore (FY26).
- PAT: Rs 2,461 crore (FY24); Rs 2,176 crore (FY25); Rs 2,544 crore (FY26).
- Total expenses: Rs 5,817 crore (FY24); Rs 6,745 crore (FY25); Rs 7,980 crore (FY26).
- RBI-classified Upper-Layer NBFC (NBFC-UL) — brings scale and tighter regulation.
- Employee/financing cost split data not found; financing cost + credit provisions dominate an NBFC's expense line.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart. A bank-backed lender with big, predictable earnings — the bluest of boring financials, growing revenue ~13-15% a year. Lynch liked financiers that grow loans steadily without blowing up credit quality; parent HDFC Bank gives funding cost and collection muscle. PAT dipped in FY25 (credit cost pressure) before recovering in FY26 — watch the asset-quality cycle rather than growth.

## TAM
India retail and MSME credit market — NBFC segment AUMs run into tens of lakh crore rupees; precise sourced TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Net interest margin — lending yields minus borrowing cost (HDFC Bank parentage lowers funding cost).
2. Credit costs — provisioning on MSME/consumer loans (drove the FY25 PAT dip).
3. Loan book growth across consumer, enterprise and asset finance segments.

## Red flags
- FY25 profit fell ~12% on credit-cost stress before FY26 recovery.
- Unsecured/MSME lending sensitive to economic slowdowns.
- Valuation shadowed by HDFC Bank — parent owns the majority, limiting float and upside independence.

## Verdict
Bank-backed stalwart lender — steady compounder for patient money, not a ten-bagger.
