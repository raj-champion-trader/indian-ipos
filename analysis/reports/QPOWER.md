# Quality Power Electrical Equipments Limited (QPOWER)

## What it does
Quality Power makes high-voltage electrical equipment — busbars/busducts, transformers, switchgear and grid-connection gear — that links power plants, data centres and grids. Most sales go to global clients across ~80 countries (per IPO materials); it listed in February 2025.

## The numbers
- Standalone (screener): sales Rs 95 crore FY23, Rs 130 crore FY24, Rs 152 crore FY25; net profit Rs 13 / 18 / 30 crore; OPM 18/18/26%.
- Consolidated IPO figures: FY25 revenue ~Rs 311–337 crore with PAT ~Rs 100 crore; FY26 consolidated revenue crossed ~Rs 1,007 crore (company/Tickertape).
- Almost debt-free; ROCE ~20%; P/E ~162 at Rs 1,431 (market cap ~Rs 11,084 crore).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — revenue up ~3x in FY26 consolidated on data-centre and grid capex, margins expanding (26% OPM FY25 standalone). Niche angle: critical high-voltage connectivity for data centres fits the AI-power buildout theme. But P/E ~162 prices in years of flawless execution; PEG unattractive unless 60%+ growth persists several years. Insiders: data not found.

## TAM
Global busbar/energy-transition equipment market: multi-billion USD annually (company DRHP cites large global T&D and energy-transition equipment opportunity); exact sourced TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Data-centre and grid capex orders in export markets (US/Europe/Middle East).
2. Busduct/busbar mix and margin expansion (OPM 18%→26% FY24→FY25 standalone).
3. Execution of new capacity funded by the 2025 IPO.

## Red flags
Extreme valuation (P/E ~162, ~20x book); growth concentrated in one capex cycle; standalone vs consolidated gaps need reading; short listed history.

## Verdict
Genuine fast grower riding the power-hungry data-centre boom, but the price already celebrates several years of future growth.
