# Samhi Hotels (SAMHI)

## What it does
Samhi owns hotels across India and rents the brand names — Marriott, Hyatt, IHG — to run them. It earns from guests' room nights, food and beverage, and banquets.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY25 Rs 1,123 cr; FY26 Rs 1,248 cr (sum of quarters, screener.in). FY24 ~Rs 957 cr.
- PAT: FY25 +Rs 86 cr; FY26 +Rs 566 cr, but Mar-26 quarter alone contributed Rs 399 cr — a one-off (tax/Ind AS 116 adjustment type gain), not operating.
- EBITDA margin: ~34-38% quarterly through FY25-FY26.
- Interest cost: FY26 ~Rs 151 cr (FY24 was ~Rs 346 cr — big deleveraging after IPO and AC Hotels portfolio buy).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical riding India's hotel upcycle — asset-heavy operator in a boring, capital-intensive trade. Niche: largest owner of Marriott/Hyatt-branded rooms in India. Leverage falling and RevPAR rising = earnings torque. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: data not found.

## TAM
India branded hotel room supply — a few hundred thousand rooms in organised segment; industry RevPAR growing low-teens (company presentations FY25). Exact TAM figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Room revenue (occupancy + rate, RevPAR up ~12-16% FY25 presentations).
2. Finance costs — every rate cut/refinance drops straight to profit.
3. AC Hotels portfolio acquisition (962 rooms) — integration and ramp-up of new keys.

## Red flags
High debt despite reduction; one-off-heavy FY26 PAT; asset-heavy model needs capex; brand partners (Marriott/Hyatt) hold the customer relationship.

## Verdict
Cyclical with leverage-torque — attractive while the hotel cycle and rate cuts run, not a compounder yet.
