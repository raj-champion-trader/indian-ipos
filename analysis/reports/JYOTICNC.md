# Jyoti CNC Automation Ltd (JYOTICNC)

## What it does
India's largest maker of CNC machine tools — the computer-controlled machines that factories use to cut and shape metal parts. Sells 200+ machine variants (turning centres, machining centres, 5-axis machines) to aerospace, defence, auto components and general engineering buyers. Holds roughly 10% share in India's simultaneous 5-axis CNC machine segment.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 1,338 cr (FY24) → Rs 1,818 cr (FY25), +36%; TTM FY26 Rs 2,191 cr
- PAT: Rs 151 cr (FY24) → Rs 316 cr (FY25), +109%
- OPM: 22% (FY24), 27% (FY25), 24% (FY26 TTM)
- Debt: Rs 853 cr (Mar 2026); ROCE 21.3%, ROE 18.2%
- Executed 4,072 machines (FY25) vs 5,550 (FY26), avg realisation ~Rs 35.62 lakh per machine (FY26 standalone)
- P/E ~73.5; 5-yr sales CAGR 32%, profit CAGR 45%
- Promoter holding 62.55% (no dividend yet)

## Lynch lens
Fast grower. High import-substitution play in machine tools (India imports most high-end CNC machines), 5-axis niche with 10% share, and profit doubling on 36% sales growth shows operating leverage. Boring-ish capital-goods business with a moat in aerospace/defence qualifications. But P/E 73 vs ~25-30% growth gives PEG near 2.5 — expensive. Cash conversion cycle of 359 days (working-capital heavy) deserves watching.

## TAM
India machine tools market estimated at roughly USD 4-5 bn annually, with imports dominating the high end (industry estimates; exact figure data not found). Global CNC machine market far larger — data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Aerospace & defence machine orders (highest margin 5-axis machines)
2. Auto components/general engineering capex cycle in India
3. Operating leverage — profit grew 109% (FY25) on 36% sales growth

## Red flags
- 359-day cash conversion cycle; heavy working capital
- Rich valuation (P/E ~73) prices in flawless execution
- No dividend; promoter pledge data not found
- Cyclical order flow if industrial capex slows

## Verdict
Great fast grower in import-substitution machine tools — but at 73x earnings, Lynch would wait for a better price.
