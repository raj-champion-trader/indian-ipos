# Shankesh Jewellers Ltd (SHANKESH)

## What it does
Ahmedabad-based (est. 2005) B2B wholesale maker of handcrafted gold jewellery, with three decades of family operating history. Sells customised gold jewellery and job-work services to jewellery retailers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 909 Cr; FY24 Rs 1,062 Cr; FY25 Rs 1,404 Cr; FY26 Rs 1,631 Cr (screener). 3-yr sales CAGR ~22%.
- PAT: FY23 Rs 10 Cr; FY24 Rs 13 Cr; FY25 Rs 40 Cr; FY26 Rs 107 Cr. 3-yr PAT CAGR ~117%.
- OPM: 2.5% (FY23) → 10% (FY26); PAT margin 1.1% → 6.6%.
- Debt: borrowings Rs 98 Cr (FY23) to Rs 168 Cr (FY26); interest Rs 8-13 Cr.
- ROCE ~50%, ROE 3-yr avg ~55%.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround/margin-expansion story inside a boring wholesale gold business. Nothing duller than B2B gold job-work — exactly the Lynch hunting ground. Profit grew 8x FY24→FY26 while revenue grew ~50%: massive operating leverage plus better mix (making charges on handcrafted pieces). Razor-thin starting margins (2.5%) mean small pricing changes swing profits wildly — both ways.

## TAM
India gold jewellery market ~US$ 40-50 bn, world's second-largest consumer (industry estimates); organised/B2B share rising post-GST and hallmarking. Company-specific TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Making-charge margin on handcrafted gold (OPM 2.5%→10%, FY23→FY26).
2. Gold price pass-through inflating revenue (FY25-FY26 rally).
3. Volume growth with retailers — 22% sales CAGR.

## Red flags
- Profit surge rides gold prices; a correction reverses the mix benefit.
- Debt rose to Rs 168 Cr (FY26) — working-capital funding of gold inventory.
- Small-cap, family-run, thin disclosure; 117% PAT CAGR comes off tiny base.

## Verdict
Boring-business turnaround with real operating leverage — hold with eyes on gold price and debt.
