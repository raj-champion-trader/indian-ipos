# Dev Accelerator Limited (DEVX)

## What it does
Company runs managed offices and co-working (flex) spaces, with a focus on Tier-2 cities — among the largest flex operators there. Also earns from design and fit-out work (Neddle & Thread Designs), payroll/facility services, and IT services (Saasjoy).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 61 crore (FY23) → Rs 99 crore (FY24) → Rs 127 crore (FY25) → Rs 171 crore (FY26).
- Net profit: -13 (FY23) → +1 (FY24) → +1 (FY25) → +3 crore (FY26).
- Operating margin ~61% (FY26) — typical for flex operators who report lease cost below the op-profit line.
- Finance cost ~26% of sales (FY26); borrowings Rs 226 crore (Mar 2026).
- ROE ~2.2%; market cap ~Rs 340 crore; P/E ~66 (Aug 2026).
- 3-year sales CAGR ~41%.

## Lynch lens
Possible fast grower, but the financial shape smells like an asset play gone leveraged. Revenue compounds fast (41% over 3 years), yet profit stays tiny (Rs 3 crore on Rs 171 crore sales) because lease and finance costs eat the operating profit. P/E of 66 on near-zero earnings makes PEG meaningless. Lynch would want profits to scale with revenue before paying up.

## TAM
India flex office space market estimated at roughly 60-70 million sq ft and growing 15-20% annually; Tier-2 flex remains underpenetrated (exact sourced figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Occupancy and rental spreads on managed seats (core gross margin).
2. Lease rentals on leased centres — largest expense, directly offsets revenue.
3. Finance costs (~26% of sales) on Rs 226 crore borrowings.

## Red flags
- Finance cost ~26% of sales; heavy leverage.
- Profit rounding-error thin: Rs 3 crore on Rs 171 crore revenue.
- P/E 66 for a business earning ~2% ROE.

## Verdict
Fast-growing but capital-hungry flex operator — the growth story costs real money; wait for profit proof.
