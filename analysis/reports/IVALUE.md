# Ivalue Infosolutions Ltd (IVALUE)

## What it does
Distributes and integrates enterprise technology — cybersecurity, networking and data-centre infrastructure — for mid-size Indian companies, working through channel partners and OEMs (like Fortinet, and similar brands). Acts as the bridge between global tech makers and Indian business buyers in hybrid-cloud environments.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 780 cr (FY24) → Rs 923 cr (FY25), +18%; TTM Rs 1,007 cr
- PAT: Rs 71 cr (FY24) → Rs 85 cr (FY25), +20%; TTM Rs 98 cr
- OPM: 13% (FY24) → 12% (FY25) → 14% (TTM)
- Interest Rs 11 cr (TTM); Depreciation Rs 7 cr (TTM)
- Borrowings Rs 69 cr (Mar 2026) — low
- ROE 19.8%, ROCE 25.4%; P/E ~13.5; market cap ~Rs 1,449 cr
- Exact segment split (cybersecurity vs data-centre infrastructure) data not found

## Lynch lens
Boring-but-profitable distributor — Lynch's favourite hunting ground. Value-added distribution of cyber/data-centre gear in India rides two durable waves (security spending, data-centre buildout). ROCE of 25% with modest debt shows capital-light economics unusual for a distribution business. At P/E 13.5 with ~18-20% growth, PEG under 1 — the cheapest quality name in this batch. Risk sits in OEM concentration and gross-margin thinness typical of distribution.

## TAM
India cybersecurity market estimated at USD 3-4 bn and data-centre investments booming; combined addressable spend for enterprise distribution data not found precisely.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Cybersecurity product sales (largest, fastest-moving segment per company disclosure)
2. Data-centre infrastructure demand as Indian enterprises expand cloud
3. Gross-margin mix (services/OEM rebates vs hardware pass-through)

## Red flags
- Dependent on a few global OEM relationships for product rights
- Thin gross margins typical of distribution; working-capital swings
- Small cap — liquidity risk
- Segment mix undisclosed publicly

## Verdict
Quiet 25%-ROCE tech distributor at 13.5x earnings — a genuine Lynch-style boring bargain if OEM ties hold.
