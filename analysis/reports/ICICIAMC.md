# ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Ltd (ICICIAMC)

## What it does
ICICI Prudential AMC runs mutual funds, portfolio management services (PMS) and alternative investment funds for Indian investors. It earns fees as a percentage of assets under management — India's largest active mutual fund manager by quarterly average AUM. Joint venture between ICICI Bank and Prudential.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,837 crore (FY23), Rs 3,758 crore (FY24), Rs 4,977 crore (FY25), Rs 6,040 crore TTM — standalone, per screener.in
- Net profit: Rs 1,516 crore (FY23), Rs 2,050 crore (FY24), Rs 2,651 crore (FY25), Rs 3,486 crore TTM
- Operating margin: 73-74% across years — extraordinary
- Zero borrowings; ROE 3-year average 83%; ROCE 115% (Mar 2026)
- Free cash flow Rs 3,167 crore (FY26)
- Market cap Rs 1,55,177 crore; P/E 44.5; dividend yield 0.87%

## Lynch lens
Stalwart of the highest quality — a fee machine tied to India's SIP culture. ~28% sales and ~30% profit 3-year CAGR actually puts it in fast-grower territory, unusual for a stalwart. PEG roughly 44/25 ≈ 1.8 — you pay up for the franchise. Lynch would note promoter Prudential selling ~9.9 million shares in Aug 2026 (stake cut 87.6% to 85.6% for MPS compliance) — regulatory, not a vote of no confidence.

## TAM
India mutual fund industry AUM — one of the fastest-growing pools globally, SIP-driven; exact figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Management fees on active equity mutual fund AUM — largest and highest-yielding pool
2. Mix shift — equity/hybrid fund flows carry higher fee rates than debt funds
3. Operating leverage — 73%+ margins on a largely fixed cost base; every rupee of AUM growth drops to profit

## Red flags
- P/E 44.5 and P/B 37.2 price in years of flawless market-linked growth
- Equity market corrections directly shrink AUM and revenue
- Passive/index funds keep eating active fund market share

## Verdict
Best-in-class fee compounder — great business, full price; accumulate on market panic.
