# BLS E-Services Ltd (BLSE)

## What it does
Company runs "assisted digital services" for rural and semi-urban India through a network of customer service points (CSPs): e-governance services (government documents, certificates), business correspondent banking (deposits, withdrawals, account opening for banks), and allied financial services. It earns fees per transaction from governments and banks. Listed on NSE/BSE via IPO in February 2024.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: ~Rs 545 crore (approx; derived from FY26 total income of Rs 1,143 crore, +109.7% YoY).
- Q4 FY25: revenue Rs 239 crore (+224.8% YoY); PAT ~Rs 17 crore (+59% YoY).
- TTM (mid-2026): revenue ~Rs 1,178 crore, profit ~Rs 70 crore (Screener/Yahoo).
- CSP network grew from 41,500+ (Q3 FY25) to 45,800+ (Q3 FY26).
- Market cap ~Rs 2,960 crore at ~Rs 326/share.

## Lynch lens
Boring business, high barriers — rare listed pure-play on India's digital-public infrastructure rails (BLS also runs visa application centers via a separate parent arm). Steady fee income, asset-light, scalable per-CSP economics. Trades around 40x+ TTM earnings — pricing in continued hyper-growth. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
India assisted financial/e-governance services market — hundreds of millions of under-banked citizens; market size figure: data not found in searched sources.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Business correspondent banking transaction fees (largest segment).
2. E-governance services (state government contracts).
3. Allied digital services (insurance, Bharat BillPay, travel) riding CSP network.

## Red flags
- Government/bank contract concentration and renewal risk.
- Commission/fee regulation risk (BC commissions cut in the past).
- Revenue recognition of one-time items boosted FY25/FY26 growth rates — quality of earnings needs checking.

## Verdict
Quality boring compounder on digital India rails — hold-quality business, but valuation demands growth persistence.
