# Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited (CORDELIA)

## What it does
Cordelia Cruises runs India's only ocean cruise line. Its single ship, MV Empress (796 cabins), carries passengers from Mumbai, Goa, Kochi, and Chennai to Lakshadweep, Visakhapatnam, and Sri Lanka. Money comes from ticket sales, onboard spending, and charters.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 452.15 cr (FY24), Rs 597.68 cr (FY25), Rs 586.99 cr (FY26) — flat then down.
- PAT: loss Rs 122.73 cr (FY24), profit Rs 168.19 cr (FY25, 28.2% margin), Rs 52.14 cr (FY26, 8.9%) — a 69% profit drop.
- Screener standalone: sales Rs 583 cr / PAT Rs 186 cr (FY25), Rs 580 cr / PAT Rs 60 cr (FY26).
- Debt-equity 1.27 (FY26); FY26 interest Rs 14 cr, depreciation Rs 22 cr (screener).
- IPO June 2026 (Rs 585 cr fresh issue); stock P/E ~151 post-listing.

## Lynch lens
A story stock, not a Lynch buy. One ship, glamorous brand, extreme valuation (P/E 151), profits falling 69% in FY26, and 82% of IPO proceeds going to lease payments to its own subsidiary. First-mover in Indian cruising gives the niche angle, but the numbers do not back the price.

## TAM
India cruise tourism market size: data not found in fetched sources. Government port-led cruise tourism push and a tiny base (549,051 passengers carried since launch) imply a long runway — but TAM numbers remain unverified.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Ticket revenue and cabin occupancy on MV Empress — the entire business.
2. Onboard spending (food, drinks, retail) and charter income — the margin layer.
3. Fuel, port, and ship operating costs — FY26 cost rise crushed PAT despite flat sales.

## Red flags
- One vessel = single point of failure.
- PAT down 69% in FY26; ROCE 1.14%.
- Promoter held 99.3% pre-IPO; exchange sought clarification on price movement (Aug 2026).

## Verdict
Glamour single-asset cruise story at 151x earnings — admire from the shore.
