# Atlanta Electricals Ltd (ATLANTAELE)

## What it does
Company makes power transformers (up to 200 MVA), auto transformers, inverter-duty and furnace/special transformers, and executes turnkey electrical EPC projects. It sells to power utilities and industrial customers from Anand, Gujarat, with 4,500+ transformers installed over four decades. It listed on NSE/BSE in September 2025 (Rs 687 crore IPO; listed at Rs 857, ~14% premium).

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: ~Rs 471 crore (+48.2% YoY); net profit ~Rs 53 crore (+70.5% YoY).
- EBITDA ~Rs 82 crore; net margin ~11%.
- Post-listing momentum: Q3 FY26 revenue Rs 472 crore (+80% YoY), PAT Rs 43 crore; trailing revenue ~Rs 1,851 crore.
- Debt/ROE details: data not found in searched sources.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with a boring-product core — transformers benefit from India's grid upgrade, renewables transmission and capex cycle. EPC mix adds execution risk. No P/E or insider-buying data found post the FY26 earnings surge.

## TAM
India power transformer / T&D equipment market — multi-billion-dollar and growing with grid expansion and renewable integration (exact TAM figure: data not found in searched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Power transformer sales to utilities (core revenue).
2. EPC/turnkey electrical projects (fast-growing, execution-heavy).
3. Special-duty transformers (furnace, inverter-duty) for industry.

## Red flags
- EPC projects carry working-capital and margin risk.
- Order-book concentration and receivables from government utilities (data not found).
- Stock re-rated massively post listing — expectations already high.

## Verdict
Grid-capex fast grower with a real product moat — watch order book and receivables before chasing the re-rating.
