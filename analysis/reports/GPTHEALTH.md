# GPT Healthcare Ltd (GPTHEALTH)

## What it does
GPT Healthcare runs ILS Hospitals — five multi-specialty hospitals with 719 beds in Eastern India (Kolkata region and nearby). Patients pay for beds, surgery, doctors and diagnostics, supported by 91 full-time and 630 visiting consultants.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 361 cr (FY23), Rs 400 cr (FY24), Rs 407 cr (FY25), Rs 473 cr (FY26, standalone)
- Net profit: Rs 39 cr (FY23), Rs 48 cr (FY24), Rs 50 cr (FY25), Rs 42 cr (FY26) — dipped despite higher sales
- OPM eased from 22% (FY24) to 18% (FY26)
- FY26 expenses Rs 387 cr (interest Rs 8 cr, depreciation Rs 27 cr); P/E ~26; ROE ~17%; market cap ~Rs 1,245 cr

## Lynch lens
A small regional stalwart — the "local champion" Lynch liked: dominant ILS brand in its eastern-India catchment, steady demand, no glamour. Growth runs mid-single-digit on revenue, so P/E ~26 still looks full unless new beds lift it. The FY26 profit dip (50 to 42 cr) suggests cost pressure or ramp-up drag — a watch item, not yet a trend. Insider activity data not found.

## TAM
Eastern-India private hospital market — under-served relative to the west/north; exact size data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Occupancy and ARPOB across the 719 beds
2. Doctor/employee and medical-consumable costs (compressing OPM in FY26)
3. New capacity/expansion ramp (adds depreciation before revenue matures)

## Red flags
- Profit fell in FY26 despite 16% revenue growth
- OPM fell 4 points over two years
- Small scale (Rs 473 cr revenue) limits bargaining power

## Verdict
Steady regional hospital operator at a fair-ish price — worth holding if FY27 restores profit growth.
