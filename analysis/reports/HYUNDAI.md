# Hyundai Motor India (HYUNDAI)

## What it does
The company makes and sells cars in India — 13 models spanning hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs and EVs, plus parts and service. It belongs to Korea's Hyundai Motor Group, the world's third-largest carmaker, and ranks as India's second-largest vehicle exporter (April 2021-June 2024).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 69,829 crore (FY24); Rs 69,193 crore (FY25) — flat; Rs 70,763 crore (FY26)
- PAT: Rs 6,060 crore (FY24); Rs 5,640 crore (FY25); Rs 5,432 crore (FY26) — declining
- OPM: 13% (FY24/FY25) to 12% (FY26); ROE ~29.9%, ROCE ~38.4%
- Low debt: borrowings ~Rs 1,098 crore; dividend payout ~26.5%
- Market cap ~Rs 1.84 lakh crore; P/E ~37x (screener snapshot)

## RevenueStreams
- SUVs — dominant share of India volumes (Creta, Venue, Exter etc.) (FY25)
- Hatchbacks/entry cars (Grand i10, i20) (FY25)
- Exports, spare parts and service — India's #2 vehicle exporter (FY25)

## Lynch lens
Stalwart — a mature, cash-rich market leader with ~29-30% domestic passenger-vehicle share alongside Maruti. But the P/E (~37x) prices it like a fast grower while PAT has slipped three years straight (FY24-FY26). Lynch would frown at that mismatch. The franchise, dealer network and export engine give durability; growth needs a new compact-SUV/UV cycle and EV traction.

## TAM
India passenger vehicle market ~4.3 million units/year (FY24-FY25, SIAM); SUVs now over half of domestic sales.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. SUV mix and pricing — Creta/venue-class margins drive profitability
2. Commodity and freight costs — raw material swings move the 12-13% OPM
3. Volume leverage across the Sriperumbudur and Talegaon plants

## Red flags
- PAT declining three years running while P/E stays at ~37x
- Rising competition from Maruti, Tata, Mahindra and new EV entrants
- EV transition costs; CNG/price-sensitive segment pressure

## Verdict
Great stalwart franchise at a growth-stock price — wait for a better entry point.
