# JNK India Limited (JNKINDIA)

## What it does
JNK India makes heating equipment for oil refineries, petrochemical plants and fertiliser units — fired heaters, cracking furnaces, flares and incinerators. It sells these as made-to-order projects to large industrial clients like Reliance and ADNOC.

## The numbers
- FY25 consolidated revenue: Rs 838 crore, up ~68% YoY from Rs 498.7 crore in FY24 (one earlier release cited standalone FY25 revenue of ~Rs 495 crore, +2.5% YoY — consolidated vs standalone basis differs across sources).
- FY25 consolidated net profit: ~Rs 30 crore (implied from FY26 PAT of Rs 64.82 crore, +114.6% YoY).
- Order book ~Rs 1,220 crore at end-March 2025; bid pipeline ~Rs 4,000 crore; Reliance cracking furnace order won; first export order from ADNOC (Rs 100-300 crore range).
- Segment mix (FY25 order inflow): heating equipment ~76%, process plants ~15%, flares/incinerators ~9%.
- Expense heads detail: data not found (project business; key costs: raw materials i.e. steel, fabrication and labour).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical / small fast grower hybrid — a boring, niche capital-goods business (nobody gets excited about furnace heaters) with order-book-driven lumpy revenue. Growth depends on refinery and petrochemical capex cycles. P/E data not found; treat as a cyclical that needs the capex cycle, not a steady compounder.

## TAM
Global heat-exchanger and process-fired-heater equipment market — exact India TAM data not found; served market driven by refinery, petrochemical and fertiliser capex in India and the Middle East.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Order book execution and milestone billing on heating-equipment projects (~76% of FY25 order inflow).
2. Raw material (steel) costs and project execution delays.
3. Export orders (ADNOC) and large domestic orders (Reliance) adding margin and scale.

## Red flags
- Lumpy, order-driven revenue; Q4 FY25 profit fell ~20.5% YoY.
- Customer concentration in cyclical refinery capex.
- Process equipment segment occasionally loss-making while ramping.

## Verdict
A niche cyclical that does well only when refinery capex flows — buy the downcycle, not the headlines.
