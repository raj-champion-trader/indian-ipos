# AWFIS Space Solutions Ltd (AWFIS)

## What it does
Company provides flexible office space in India — from single desks to full custom offices for startups, SMEs, and large corporates. As of March 2025 it ran 208 centers with 134,000+ seats across 18 cities, using an asset-light model (mostly managed seats in partnered buildings).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 545 cr (FY23), Rs 847 cr (FY24), Rs 1,203 cr (FY25) — standalone.
- PAT: Rs -46 cr (FY23), Rs -18 cr (FY24), Rs +66 cr (FY25) — first full-year profit.
- OPM: 29% (FY23/FY24), 34% (FY25); ROCE 12.1%, ROE 10.2% (FY25).
- Expenses FY25: Rs 799 cr, including interest Rs 136 cr and depreciation Rs 276 cr.
- Borrowings: Rs 1,413 cr (Mar FY25).

## Lynch lens
Turnaround — losses in FY23/FY24 flipped to Rs 66 cr profit in FY25 on 42% revenue growth and seat-utilization gains. Flex-space rides the return-to-office and corporate cost-flex trend. Watch debt (Rs 1,413 cr) and lease obligations; execution in filling seats decides everything.

## TAM
India flexible workspace market estimated at roughly 55-65 million sq ft and growing double-digit annually (CBRE/JLL India flex-space reports, 2024).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Seat occupancy/utilization across 208 centers — direct margin lever.
2. Rental and CAM payments to building partners — largest operating cost.
3. Depreciation (Rs 276 cr FY25) and interest (Rs 136 cr FY25) on leased fit-outs — the swing factors between loss and profit.

## Red flags
- Rs 1,413 cr borrowings plus large off-balance-sheet lease commitments.
- Profit only one year old; earlier losses show fragility.
- Competition from WeWork India, Smartworks, and landlord-run flex brands.

## Verdict
Real turnaround with scale and 34% OPM, but debt-heavy — buy only if occupancy keeps climbing.
