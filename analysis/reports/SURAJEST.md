# Suraj Estate Developers (SURAJEST)

## What it does
Suraj builds and sells homes in South Mumbai and runs redevelopment projects of old buildings. It also develops business parks on the city's periphery.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 411 cr; FY25 Rs 549 cr; FY26 Rs 556 cr (quarter sums, screener.in).
- PAT: FY24 ~Rs 68 cr; FY25 ~Rs 100 cr; FY26 ~Rs 90 cr — net margin ~15-18%.
- OPM ranges 22-66% by quarter (real-estate revenue books in slabs).
- Interest: ~Rs 90-100 cr FY24, falling to ~Rs 20-30 cr/quarter — still meaningful for a developer.
- IPO December 2023.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical, micro-cap property developer with a genuine niche — South Mumbai land scarcity and redevelopment mandates. Real estate books revenue on completion, so quarters look random; judge by launches and collections. Boring-business angle: nobody in Mumbai lacks a developer, but few own the island-city land bank. ## TAM
Mumbai residential market — among India's deepest by value; redevelopment pipeline of ageing island-city buildings runs into tens of thousands of crores (industry estimates). Exact sourced TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. South Mumbai project completions — revenue lands in lumps (Dec quarters peak).
2. Redevelopment and SRA-type projects — margin and land-bank accretion without upfront land cost.
3. Finance costs — deleveraging lifted FY25 PAT.

## Red flags
Lumpy revenue; small scale; Mumbai approval/regulatory delays; rising construction cost; Mar-26 quarter PAT dropped to Rs 11 cr (project timing).

## Verdict
Decent niche developer — hold only if land bank and launch pipeline stay healthy; wait for cheaper entry.
