# Lohia Corp Limited (LCL)

## What it does
Lohia Corp builds the machines that make woven plastic sacks — circular looms, extrusion lines, winders, and laminating equipment sold worldwide. It also supplies spares and field service for the machines it installs. The current entity took its name in 2024 after an NCLT restructuring of the Lohia Group.

## The what-you-see numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 1,811 cr; FY25 Rs 1,386 cr; FY26 Rs 1,738 cr (per IPO documents).
- PAT: FY23 Rs 82 cr; FY25 Rs 118 cr; FY26 Rs 193 cr.
- FY26 EBITDA margin 19.5%; PAT margin 11.1%; ROE 36.8%; ROCE 40.9%.
- OPM improved from 10% (FY23) to 20% (FY26 TTM, Screener).
- Debt cut from Rs 313 cr (FY23) to Rs 119 cr (FY26). IPO (2026) a pure offer-for-sale.
- P/E ~21.7; market cap ~Rs 5,970 cr.

## Lynch lens
Boring business, beautiful economics. Machinery for FIBC/woven-sack producers sounds gloriously dull — exactly Lynch's hunting ground. ROCE near 41%, margins doubling in three years, debt shrinking, and spares/service revenue giving razor-blade stickiness. FY24 shows zero because of the group restructuring, which muddies the trend line and deserves a check of the scheme documents. At ~22x for a cyclical capital-goods maker, price looks reasonable, not silly.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Export order book for circular looms and extrusion lines.
2. Aftermarket spares and service (high-margin annuity on installed base).
3. Operating leverage — margins expanded as revenue recovered post-FY25 trough.

## Red flags

## Verdict
A quiet machinery champion with 40% ROCE — one of the more Lynch-worthy names in this batch.
