# Milky Mist Dairy Food Ltd (MILKYMIST)

## What it does
Milky Mist processes milk and makes value-added dairy products — paneer, curd, ghee, butter, cheese. It sells premium packaged dairy through retail channels across India, mostly in the south. Unlike typical co-operatives, it focuses on higher-margin products rather than liquid milk.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,328 crore FY25 vs Rs 1,803 crore FY24 (+29%); 3-year CAGR 32%.
- PAT: Rs 44 crore FY25 vs Rs 18 crore FY24. OPM 13% FY25.
- Borrowings: Rs 1,375 crore FY25; interest cost Rs 86 crore FY25 — meaningful leverage.
- Post-listing P/E about 377 (screener.in); ROE 14%, ROCE 11% FY25.

## Lynch lens
A boring-business consumer staple — Lynch loves dull staples with repeat purchase. Paneer and curd get bought weekly. But raw-milk pricing is cyclical and margins stay thin (13% OPM, ~2% PAT margin FY25). At a P/E near 377 the price already discounts years of growth; PEG badly stretched. Watch, not buy.

## TAM
India dairy market: widely cited at roughly Rs 13-14 lakh crore, with value-added dairy products (Milky Mist's segment) growing in double digits (industry estimates, FY24-25).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Value-added products (paneer, curd, ghee) with better margins than liquid milk.
2. Raw milk procurement cost swings (biggest expense head).
3. Interest burden on Rs 1,375 crore FY25 debt and new-capacity ramp.

## Red flags
- Very high P/E (about 377) vs single-digit net margin.
- Heavy debt; interest coverage thin.
- Milk inflation squeezes margins quickly.

## Verdict
Good boring staple business, terrible current price — wait for a pullback.
