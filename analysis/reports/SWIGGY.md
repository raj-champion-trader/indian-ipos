# Swiggy Ltd (SWIGGY)

## What it does
Company runs the Swiggy app — food delivery from restaurants, Instamart quick-commerce groceries in minutes, and Dineout restaurant bookings. It earns fees and commissions on every order plus advertising income.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 6,372 cr (FY24), Rs 6,667 cr (FY25), Rs 8,258 cr (FY26).
- Net loss: Rs -1,888 cr (FY24), Rs -2,542 cr (FY25), Rs -3,419 cr (FY26).
- Losses widened as Instamart expansion (dark stores, quick commerce) ate cash.
- Market cap ~Rs 77,427 cr; price ~Rs 280; 3.7x book. ROE -20.7%.
- Nearly debt free (Rs 185 cr borrowings); IPO Nov 2024 built the cash pile.
- Latest quarter (Jun 2026): positive operating profit Rs 80 cr, OPM 3.2%.

## Lynch lens
Lynch rarely bought story stocks losing money — Swiggy sits outside his classic buckets; closest fit: turnaround-in-progress with a network-effect moat in food delivery, burning cash to win quick commerce against Blinkit (Zomato/Eternal). Q1 FY27 turning operating-profit-positive hints the model can work. Valuation rests on faith, not earnings — no P/E, no PEG.

## TAM
Indian food delivery (~USD 15-20 bn and growing) plus quick commerce (fastest-growing e-commerce segment, projected USD 25-40+ bn by 2030) — figures approximate; exact sourced TAM data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Food delivery take rates and ad income (profit pool funding everything else).
2. Instamart dark-store expansion — biggest loss driver, then contribution-margin inflection.
3. Delivery-partner cost and promotional spend per order.

## Red flags
- Losses widening every year to Rs -3,419 cr (FY26).
- Brutal duopoly battle vs Eternal/Zomato; price wars can re-erupt.
- Valuation (~Rs 77,000 cr m-cap) assumes flawless quick-commerce execution.

## Verdict
Category duopolist nearing profitability — a speculation, size it accordingly.
