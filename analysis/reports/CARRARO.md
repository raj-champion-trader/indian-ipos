# Carraro India Ltd (CARRARO)

## What it does
Carraro India makes axles, transmission systems, gears, and driveline components for tractors and construction equipment as an independent Tier-1 supplier to off-highway vehicle OEMs. The parent, Italy's Carraro Group, listed the Indian arm via IPO in December 2024.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,770 cr (FY24), Rs 1,792 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 2,290 cr, TTM growth 26%.
- Operating profit: Rs 128 cr / 7% OPM (FY24), Rs 166 cr / 9% (FY25); TTM Rs 209 cr.
- PAT: Rs 61 cr (FY24), Rs 86 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 130 cr, TTM profit growth 56%.
- Debt reduced from Rs 214 cr (Mar 2024) to Rs 155 cr (Mar 2026). ROE 25.6%, ROCE 28.2%; dividend payout 20%.

## Lynch lens
A classic cyclical tied to tractor and construction-equipment demand, but with an engineering niche (precision gears/axles) that protects margins — ROCE of 28% proves the moat. FY25 revenue was flat (tractor downcycle) while profits still grew: sign of operating leverage and better mix. P/E data not found; PEG not computable.

## TAM
India tractor market (~9 lakh units/yr, world's largest) plus construction equipment and exports via the global Carraro group. Exact off-highway driveline market size data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Indian tractor OEM volumes (M&M, TAFE-class customers; exact client list data not found).
2. Operating leverage — OPM recovery from 7% (FY24) to 9-10% drove 40%+ PAT growth.
3. Exports/gear sales to the global Carraro network (mix data not found).

## Red flags
- Customer concentration inherent to Tier-1 supplier status (few large OEM buyers).
- Dependence on parent group for technology and export channels.
- Tractor demand cyclicality: FY25 flat revenue shows the downcycle bite.

## Verdict
Quality cyclical with 28% ROCE — buy on tractor-cycle troughs, trim at peaks.
