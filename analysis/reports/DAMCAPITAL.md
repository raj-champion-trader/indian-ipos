# Dam Capital Advisors Ltd (DAMCAPITAL)

## What it does
Dam Capital advises companies on IPOs, fund raises and M&A — a boutique investment bank, formerly Investec Capital India. It earns fees when deals close, and grows client money in asset management and wealth services. Listed December 2024.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Revenue: FY22 94; FY23 181; FY24 250; FY25 237 (flat)
- Net profit: FY22 22; FY23 71; FY24 104; FY25 73 (FY25 dipped on fewer big IPOs)
- Operating margin ~50%+ (FY25 expenses 115 on revenue 237); ROE 24.4%
- P/E ~14 at price Rs 146; market cap Rs 1,034 cr; nearly debt-free (borrowings ~3)

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with asset-light economics. Investment banking fees swing with the IPO cycle — FY24's boom delivered PAT of 104, FY25's quiet market gave 73. No recurring-revenue moat yet; the AMC/wealth arm could add stickier income over time. Employee bonuses and deal flow, not assets, drive the model. P/E ~14 looks cheap only if the IPO cycle returns; earnings could halve again in a drought.

## TAM
India ECM fees: record years (2024) saw US$1 bn+ in investment banking fees across the street (industry league tables), with IPO pipeline staying deep into 2025-26. AMC TAM: India mutual fund AUM above Rs 70 lakh crore (AMFI, 2025).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Equity capital markets fees — IPOs/QIPs mandates (fees recognised on deal closing)
2. M&A and advisory mandates
3. Employee cost and bonus pool — the biggest expense head, swings directly with fee income

## Red flags
- Lumpy, cycle-dependent revenue; FY25 already showed the downside
- Key-person and talent retention risk typical of boutiques
- Small float/market cap (Rs ~1,000 cr) — volatile stock (286 high vs 146 now)

## Verdict
Cheap, profitable fee machine — but a cyclical bet on the IPO cycle, not a steady compounder.
