# Orient Technologies Limited (ORIENTTECH)

## What it does
Company sells and services IT gear — servers, storage, networking, security — runs managed services and IT facility management, and shifts client workloads to the cloud. Mumbai-based systems integrator since 1997.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 603 cr (FY24), Rs 840 cr (FY25), TTM Rs 852 cr — 5-yr sales CAGR 28%
- Net profit: Rs 41 cr (FY24), Rs 50 cr (FY25), TTM Rs -3 cr — swung to loss
- OPM: 9% (FY24), 8% (FY25), 4.6% (TTM)
- Interest: Rs 1 cr (FY25), Rs 6 cr (TTM); depreciation jumped Rs 5 cr to Rs 18 cr (TTM)
- Borrowings just Rs 6 cr (Mar 2025); P/E 53.6; ROE 7.1%

## Lynch lens
Was a fast grower, now stumbling. Revenue still climbing but TTM shows a small loss — thin-margin hardware distribution eats any services mix gain, and depreciation on new data-centre/cloud assets tripled. P/E 53.6 against falling earnings makes no PEG sense. Watch whether cloud/ITeS (higher-margin) share of revenue rises.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. IT infrastructure (hardware resale) — biggest revenue, thinnest margin; mix decides profitability
2. Cloud & data management services — the margin lever and reason for the depreciation jump
3. Depreciation and interest on new capacity (Rs 18 cr + Rs 6 cr TTM) — pushed the company into loss

## Red flags
- TTM net loss (Rs -3 cr) after Rs 50 cr FY25 PAT
- OPM halved to 4.6%
- P/E 53.6 with negative TTM earnings
- Working-capital-heavy reseller model

## Verdict
Growth story hit a speed bump at 4.6% margins — wait for services mix to show up in profits before paying 50x.
