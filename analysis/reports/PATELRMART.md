# Patel Retail Ltd (PATELRMART)

## What it does
Patel Retail runs "Patel's R Mart" supermarkets — 43 value-focused grocery stores (as of May 2025) selling food, FMCG, general merchandise and apparel in tier-III towns of Thane and Raigad districts, Maharashtra. Shoppers get low prices close to home.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 821 cr (FY25) → Rs 1,048 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 1,175 cr, growing ~42% (TTM).
- PAT: Rs 25 cr (FY25) → Rs 39 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 42 cr, up ~60%.
- OPM ~7%; ROE ~16%; ROCE ~15%; P/E ~18.7x; market cap ~Rs 777 cr.
- 5-year sales CAGR only ~5% — recent growth reflects new-store scale-up.
- Total retail space ~1.79 lakh sq ft (May 2025).

## Lynch lens
Boring business, Lynch favourite territory — groceries in small towns nobody covers. But thin 7% operating margins and competition from DMart, Jio Mart and local kiranas leave little pricing power. Profit growing faster than sales hints at operating leverage as stores mature. Insider buying: data not found. PEG roughly 18.7x P/E vs ~60% TTM profit growth — flattering but from a small base.

## TAM
Indian organised grocery retail runs into lakhs of crore rupees annually, with tier-II/III India under-penetrated (industry estimates, mid-2020s). Precise figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Same-store sales and maturing of new stores (profit +60% TTM on +42% sales).
2. Purchase-cost negotiation on grocery/FMCG inventory.
3. Store additions in Thane/Raigad — each new store adds fixed-cost leverage.

## Expenses
Cost of goods (merchandise purchases) dominates at ~90%+ of revenue (typical; exact % data not found); store rent, staff and power form the rest — figures: data not found.

## Red flags
- Razor-thin ~6-7% margins; one price war erases profit.
- Tiny scale (43 stores) versus national giants.
- Regional concentration in two Maharashtra districts.

## Verdict
A humble small-town grocer with decent operating leverage — fine as a small speculative position, but DMart-scale economics look far away.
