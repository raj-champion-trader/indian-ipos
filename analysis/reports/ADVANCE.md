# Advance Agrolife Ltd (ADVANCE)

## What it does
Advance Agrolife makes agrochemicals — pesticides, insecticides and fungicide formulations and technicals — from its Gujarat plants. It sells to Indian farmers through distributors and also exports. Listed August 2025.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in — limited history)
- Revenue: FY24 451; FY25 496 (+10%)
- Net profit: FY24 25; FY25 26; ROE 29.1%, ROCE 28.0%
- Operating margin ~10% (FY25 expenses 447 on sales 496)
- P/E ~29 at price Rs 117; market cap Rs 752 cr; borrowings FY25 80
- Depreciation rising (3 → 8) as new capacity commissioned; interest cost Rs 7 cr FY25

## Lynch lens
Small-cap cyclical. Agrochemical earnings ride farm incomes, monsoon cycles and Chinese generic prices — FY margins near 5% net confirm a commodity-ish formulation business. ROE near 29% looks strong, but with only two years of listed history and a Rs 752 cr market cap at P/E 29, the price already assumes healthy growth. No dominant niche disclosed; distribution reach appears its main asset.

## TAM
Indian agrochemical market estimated around Rs 60,000-70,000 crore and growing mid-to-high single digits (industry/CRISIL-type estimates); low per-hectare pesticide use versus global peers leaves headroom.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Formulation volumes sold through the domestic distributor network (rural demand, monsoon-linked)
2. Raw material costs (imported technicals/intermediates dominate expenses — bulk of the Rs 447 cr FY25 expense base)
3. Export realisations and new technical-grade capacity utilisation

## Red flags
- Only two years of consolidated data available — short track record
- Thin ~5% net margin; commodity pricing risk
- Small market cap / low float; stock already off highs (154 → 117)

## Verdict
Decent ROE small-cap in a monsoon-dependent commodity trade — too small and too priced (P/E 29) to chase; keep on watch.
