# Jupiter Life Line Hospitals Limited (JLHL)

## What it does
Jupiter runs multi-specialty tertiary hospitals in the Mumbai region and western India — Thane, Pune, Indore and Dombivli — with about 1,248 operational beds. Patients pay for surgery, beds, doctors and diagnostics under one roof.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 787 cr (FY23), Rs 915 cr (FY24), Rs 1,060 cr (FY25), Rs 1,198 cr (FY26, standalone)
- Net profit: Rs 107 cr (FY23), Rs 152 cr (FY24), Rs 185 cr (FY25), Rs 191 cr (FY26)
- OPM steady at 25-26%; 5-year sales CAGR ~21%, profit CAGR ~62%
- Interest Rs 13 cr, depreciation Rs 69 cr (FY26); ROE 12.1%, P/E ~52, market cap ~Rs 9,881 cr

## Lynch lens
A regional stalwart-to-fast-grower hybrid: hospital chains compound steadily on bed additions and case mix (more complex surgeries = higher realisation). The "boring, sticky" angle — people always fall sick, and Mumbai-region brand loyalty runs deep. P/E ~52 vs ~15% profit growth gives PEG > 3, so growth looks already paid for. Insider activity data not found.

## TAM
Indian private hospital / healthcare delivery market — large and growing with insurance penetration; exact figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Inpatient occupancy and average revenue per occupied bed (ARPOB) at the Thane flagship
2. Case mix shift toward tertiary/quaternary specialities (oncology, cardiology, neuro)
3. New-bed ramp-up (Indore, Dombivli) adding revenue with lagging costs

## Red flags
- Valuation rich at P/E ~52
- Profit growth slowed to ~3% in FY26 (185 to 191 cr) while sales grew 13%
- Capex-heavy expansion phase keeps depreciation rising

## Verdict
Quality compounder in a defensive niche — buy the dips, not the P/E of 52.
