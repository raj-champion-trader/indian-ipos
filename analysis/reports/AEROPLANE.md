# Amir Chand Jagdish Kumar (Exports) Ltd (AEROPLANE)

Note: screener.in carries this company under the slug "AEROPLANE" (after its Aeroplane basmati brand); NSE symbol for the March 2026 SME IPO shows as AMIRCHAND. Both batch lines refer to the same company.

## What it does
Company processes and exports basmati and other rice plus FMCG staples (aata, maida, sooji, besan, salt, sugar) under registered brands, including the "Aeroplane" basmati label. Fully integrated chain: procurement, storage, processing, packaging, distribution. Exports to 37+ countries with 100 trademarks.

## The numbers
- FY26 revenue Rs 2,030 crore vs Rs 1,709 crore FY25 vs Rs 1,341 crore FY24; PAT Rs 76 crore (FY26) vs Rs 40 crore (FY25) vs Rs 22 crore (FY24) (screener).
- IPO filings showed FY25 revenue Rs 2,004 crore / PAT Rs 60.8 crore (period differences vs screener).
- OPM 8-9%; interest cost Rs 86 crore (FY26); borrowings Rs 758 crore.
- ROCE 13.8%; P/E ~20.4; market cap ~Rs 1,810 crore. IPO (Mar 2026) listed at ~5.7% discount.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring staple — profit CAGR ~40% over 5 years on 13% sales CAGR, riding record basmati exports. Cyclical element (basmati prices), heavy working capital. PEG roughly 0.5 at P/E 20 vs ~40% profit growth, but growth may normalize.

## TAM
India basmati exports roughly USD 5-6 billion a year (recent crop years; approximate trade figures); company ranks 3rd among listed basmati peers by revenue. Global basmati demand centered on Middle East, US, EU, Iran.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Basmati rice export volumes and price spreads (dominant profit pool).
2. Raw paddy procurement cost and inventory gains/losses.
3. Interest burden (Rs 86 crore FY26) on Rs 758 crore working-capital borrowings.

## Red flags
- Thin 8-9% operating margins; commodity price risk both ways.
- High leverage and interest drag typical of rice millers.
- SME listing liquidity; SME IPO listed below issue price.

## Verdict
Cheap-ish basmati exporter compounding fast — cyclical, leveraged, but the boring-staple niche does the work.
