# GNG Electronics Limited (EBGNG)

## What it does
GNG Electronics, which runs the "Electronics Bazaar" brand, refurbishes used laptops and desktops — it buys old IT equipment, repairs and certifies it, then resells the machines in India. It ranks as India's largest refurbisher of laptops and desktops.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: Rs 1,411 crore; PAT Rs 69 crore; EBITDA margin improved from 4.60% in FY24 (level after improvement data not found).
- FY25 revenue mix: refurbished laptops ~76% of sales; other products ~24%.
- Latest annual (FY26 context): revenue ~Rs 1,991 crore, profit ~Rs 142 crore; market cap ~Rs 7,241 crore at one point.
- IPO: SME issue of Rs 460.44 crore (fresh Rs 400 crore + OFS) at Rs 225-237 per share; listed July 30, 2025 at Rs 355.
- Expense heads detail: data not found; cost of procuring used IT devices dominates, plus refurbishment labour and logistics.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a dusty corner — second-hand laptops lack glamour, yet digitisation plus tight budgets feed a booming refurb market, and this company leads it. Profits roughly doubled the next year (Rs 69 to Rs 142 crore). Market cap of ~Rs 7,200 crore against ~Rs 142 crore profit implies ~50x earnings — the market pays up front. SME-listed and newly public, so volatility runs high.

## TAM
India refurbished electronics market — exact size data not found; demand fed by affordable-computing push, education digitisation and corporate IT refresh cycles.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Refurbished laptop volumes (~76% of FY25 sales) — sourcing cheap used stock and reselling at a spread.
2. Device procurement costs for used IT inventory — the key margin lever.
3. Expansion into other refurbished IT products (~24% of sales) and scale economies in refurbishment.

## Red flags
- Thin FY25 margins (EBITDA ~4.6% in FY24 improving).
- SME listing, short operating history as a public company.
- Supply of used devices and e-waste regulation changes could disrupt the model.
- Valuation already embeds sustained hyper-growth.

## Verdict
A leader in unglamorous refurbishment growing fast — the business excites, the ~50x price tag demands caution.
