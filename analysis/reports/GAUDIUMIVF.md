# Gaudium IVF and Women Health Ltd (GAUDIUMIVF)

## What it does
Gaudium runs fertility clinics — it helps couples have babies through IVF and other treatments. Hub-and-spoke model with 30+ locations, anchored by a 15-bed day-care hospital in Delhi NCR, plus an in-house pharmacy selling medicines to patients.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 44 crore (FY23), Rs 46 crore (FY24), Rs 59 crore (FY25) — standalone, per screener.in
- Net profit: Rs 13 crore (FY23), Rs 10 crore (FY24), Rs 19 crore (FY25)
- Operating margin: 45% (FY23), 41% (FY24), 48% (FY25) — very high for healthcare services
- Finance cost: Rs 2 crore (FY25); borrowings Rs 21 crore
- Market cap Rs 886 crore; P/E 41

## Lynch lens
Classic Lynch territory — a boring, emotionally-sticky consumer healthcare niche (fertility) with fat 48% operating margins. Growth of 28% in FY25 revenue makes it a small fast grower. PEG roughly 41/28 ≈ 1.5 — not cheap, not silly. The hub-and-spoke model replicating clinics across India gives the repeatable-expansion story Lynch loved.

## TAM
India IVF/fertility treatment market — under-penetrated and growing; exact TAM figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. IVF/ART treatment cycles at clinics — core revenue
2. Pharmacy sales to own patients — in-house margin capture
3. Occupancy/doctor utilisation at new spoke clinics ramping to full volumes

## Red flags
- Doctor dependence — key specialists leaving hurts a hub
- FY24 profit dipped before FY25 recovery — small base volatility
- Competition from national chains (Nova, Indira IVF) on pricing

## Verdict
High-margin niche healthcare fast grower at a fair-ish price — worth tracking clinic additions each quarter.
