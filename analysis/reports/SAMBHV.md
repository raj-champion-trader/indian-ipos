# Sambhv Steel Tubes Ltd (SAMBHV)

## What it does
Sambhv makes steel pipes and tubes using electric resistance welding (ERW) — black pipes, galvanised pipes, pre-galvanised tubes and hollow structural sections — plus galvanised and cold-rolled coils. Buyers include construction firms, infrastructure projects and OEMs.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,286 cr (FY24) → Rs 1,511 cr (FY25) → Rs 2,413 cr (FY26), +60% in FY26.
- PAT: Rs 82 cr (FY24) → Rs 57 cr (FY25) → Rs 142 cr (FY26) — FY25 dipped, FY26 rebounded past earlier highs.
- OPM: 12% (FY24) → 10% (FY25) → 11% (FY26).
- Borrowings Rs 373 cr (Mar 2026); ROE ~18.7%; ROCE ~19.4%; market cap ~Rs 3,754 cr.

## RevenueStreams
ERW pipes and tubes (black, GI, pre-galvanised, CRFH) form the dominant stream; GP/CR coils and stainless products add the rest (FY25/FY26). Exact % split: data not found.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with growth — steel tube pricing follows steel cycles (see the FY25 margin dip and FY26 recovery). Backward integration into coils gives a cost edge versus plain pipe-sellers, a quiet niche advantage in a boring industry. At ~Rs 3,754 cr market cap vs Rs 142 cr PAT, P/E ~26x (2026) — not cheap for a cyclical unless integration keeps lifting margins. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
Indian steel tube/pipe consumption runs in the tens of lakh tonnes annually, driven by construction, water infra and solar structures (industry estimates, mid-2020s). Precise figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Spread between HR coil input cost and finished pipe prices — the FY25 squeeze and FY26 relief show this clearly.
2. Volume growth from expanded integrated capacity (revenue +60%, FY26).
3. Product mix shift toward higher-margin value-added (GP/CRFH) tubes.

## Expenses
Raw material (steel coils) dominates — total expenses Rs 2,137 cr of Rs 2,413 cr revenue (FY26, ~89%); interest and depreciation modest (figures embedded in total; split data not found).

## Red flags
- Steel price cyclicality — margins halved in FY25 without warning.
- Capex-heavy integration raises execution risk.
- Working capital swings typical of steel businesses.

## Verdict
A decent cyclical with an integration edge — ride it when steel spreads widen, but it earns its place only at a cyclical-cheap price.
