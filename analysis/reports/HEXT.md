# Hexaware Technologies Ltd (HEXT)

## What it does
Company builds software and AI systems for global businesses. It sells AI-based technology services through its own platforms and tools, running client IT operations since 1992. Relisted via IPO in February 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 11,974 cr (FY24); Rs 13,430 cr (FY25) — ~12% growth; TTM Rs 14,420 cr.
- PAT: Rs 1,174 cr (FY24); Rs 1,368 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 1,343 cr.
- OPM: 15% (FY24); 14% (FY25).
- Interest Rs 106 cr; depreciation Rs 361 cr (FY25); borrowings Rs 681 cr (Dec 2025) — low.
- ROE 24.9%, ROCE 30.1%; P/E 24.0; 10-year sales CAGR 16%, 5-year profit CAGR 18%.

## Lynch lens
Steady grower in a competitive, unexciting corner — IT services. Lynch's angle here: boring business, consistent compounding (16%/18% CAGRs), high ROCE (30.1%). The AI-first repositioning adds a possible re-rating kicker. P/E 24 vs ~12-18% growth gives PEG above 1 — not a bargain, a fair-price grower.

## TAM
Global IT services / AI services market — hundreds of billions of dollars; specific sourced figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Headcount-billed delivery revenue in North America/Europe — volumes drive the top line.
2. Utilisation and pricing — the 1-point OPM slip (15% to 14%, FY24 to FY25) moved ~Rs 130 cr of profit.
3. AI platform-led deals — higher-margin outcome pricing versus time-and-materials.

## Red flags
- Margin dipped in FY25 (OPM 15% to 14%) while peers defended theirs.
- IT services offers little differentiation; AI pricing pressure could compress everyone.
- Recent IPO means limited public-market track record.

## Verdict
Decent Lynch-style consistent grower at a full price — buy on dips, expect mid-teens compounding, not fireworks.
