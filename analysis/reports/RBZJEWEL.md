# RBZ Jewellers (RBZJEWEL)

## What it does
RBZ manufactures antique-style gold jewellery — Jadau, Meena, Kundan and Polki designs — at its Ahmedabad factory and wholesales it to big jewellers. Titan, Malabar Gold, Senco Gold and PNG Jewellers number among its ~190 retailer clients across 20 states. Capacity exceeds 2 tonnes of jewellery a year with 250 artisans.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 327 cr; FY25 Rs 530 cr (+62%); FY26 Rs 636 cr (+20%) — stockanalysis.com (S&P data)
- PAT: FY24 Rs 21.6 cr; FY25 Rs 38.8 cr (+80%); FY26 Rs 54.8 cr (+41%)
- TTM (Jun 2026): revenue Rs 682 cr, PAT Rs 56.8 cr — margins ~8-9%
- Promoters hold 75%; FIIs/DIIs under 0.05% (Jun 2026)
- Expense structure: gold metal dominates costs (typical 80-90% of sales for antique-jewellery makers; exact split not found in fetched sources)

## Lynch lens
Boring-business niche play in a hot sector. Making fiddly antique jewellery that big chains cannot mass-produce gives a real niche, and the client list (Titan, Malabar) validates quality. 62% and 41% PAT growth reads like a fast grower, but small-cap jewellers carry promoter-governance and working-capital risks — gold inventory gobbles cash. No institutional ownership at all signals low discovery — both opportunity and warning.

## TAM
Indian gold jewellery market — one of the world's largest, worth multiple lakh crores of rupees annually (precise figure not found in fetched sources); antique/heritage segment a premium sliver of it.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Wholesale antique-jewellery orders from large retail chains (Titan, Malabar, Senco) — the whole business
2. Gold price movement — swings revenue and inventory gains/losses
3. Artisan productivity and metal-loss control — the thin-margin maker's real profit lever

## Red flags
- ~8-9% net margin on a commodity-priced product
- Tiny PAT base (Rs 55 cr) — one lost client dents earnings
- Near-zero institutional holding; low liquidity
- Working capital intensity of gold inventory (detailed balance-sheet data not found)

## Verdict
Interesting small niche grower — position size must stay small; needs balance-sheet verification.
