# Oswal Pumps Ltd (OSWALPUMPS)

## What it does
Company makes submersible and monoblock pumps — both solar-powered and grid-connected — and installs turnkey solar pumping systems for farmers under the PM-Kusum scheme. It sells pumps across India and exports too.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 774 cr (FY24), Rs 1,272 cr (FY25), Rs 1,796 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 1,697 cr.
- PAT: Rs 89 cr (FY24), Rs 231 cr (FY25), Rs 282 cr (FY26).
- OPM: 28% (FY25), 22% (FY26).
- P/E ~14.6x; market cap ~Rs 3,369 cr. ROE 29.2% (3-yr avg 43.8%).
- Debt Rs 210 cr (Mar 2026). IPO May 2025.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower riding a government subsidy wave — PM-Kusum solar pumps turned a modest pump maker into a 2.3x revenue machine in two years. 14.6x P/E against 40%+ profit growth = very attractive PEG. The catch: the "niche" depends on policy continuity and heavy receivables from subsidised government-linked sales.

## TAM
Indian solar pump market under PM-Kusum (target of millions of solar pumps; component subsidy scheme) plus agricultural pump replacement demand — exact TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. PM-Kusum solar pump orders (high-margin turnkey systems).
2. Regular submersible/monoblock pump sales (base business).
3. Raw material costs (metals, solar panels) vs OPM — margin eased from 28% to 22% in FY26.

## Red flags
- Policy dependence: PM-Kusum scheme changes or subsidy delays hit revenue.
- Debtor days 224; working capital days 185 — government receivables pile up.
- FY26 TTM revenue below FY26 full-year — momentum cooling; IPO-priced expectations high.

## Verdict
Fast, cheap, subsidy-powered — hold only while PM-Kusum flows.
