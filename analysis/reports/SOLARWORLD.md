# Solarworld Energy Solutions Ltd (SOLARWORLD)

## What it does
Solarworld builds solar power plants for government utilities (PSUs) and industrial customers — engineering, procurement and construction (EPC). It now also makes battery energy storage systems (BESS) and solar modules/cells, and has started 5 GW junction-box production. Recent IPO (2025) funded capacity expansion.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 501 cr (FY24), Rs 546 cr (FY25), Rs 1,202 cr (FY26) — revenue more than doubled.
- Net profit: Rs 49 cr (FY24), Rs 88 cr (FY25), Rs 133 cr (FY26).
- OPM 12% (FY26); other income Rs 51 cr (FY26) — a big chunk of pre-tax profit.
- ROE ~22.5%, stock P/E ~9.7, borrowings Rs 120 cr (FY26). SJVN dispute pending (disclosed Aug 2026).

## Revenue streams
Solar EPC (turnkey project construction for PSUs/C&I) the dominant stream; BESS supply ramping (facility ready, Aug 2026); module manufacturing and order book of ongoing capacity — exact FY split: data not found on free screener.

## Expenses
Solar modules, inverters and construction materials dominate (detail: data not found). FY26: interest Rs 14 cr, depreciation near zero (asset-light EPC).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in solar EPC — but EPC contracting carries commodity-thin margins and PSU payment risk. Revenue doubled in FY26 while OPM slipped (20% to 12%) — growth via lower-margin big jobs. P/E 9.7 with 50%+ growth gives a very low PEG, plus BESS adds a second act. Promoter 65.8%; DIIs hold ~9%.

## TAM
India targets ~500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030; annual solar additions 30-40 GW and BESS market scaling to multi-GW — domestic TAM in tens of billions of dollars (FY25-FY30 estimates).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. EPC project execution and order-book conversion (FY26 doubling of revenue).
2. BESS supply ramp from FY27 — new margin pool.
3. Other income Rs 51 cr (FY26) — sizable, needs quality check.

## Red flags
Other income forms ~29% of FY26 pre-tax profit. OPM fell despite doubling sales. SJVN dispute pending. Receivables 84 days from PSUs. Little operating history as a listed firm.

## Verdict
Cheap fast grower on the solar+BESS build-out — hold through execution, but discount the other income when sizing the margin of safety.
