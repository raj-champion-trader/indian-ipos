# Amagi Media Labs Ltd (AMAGI)

## What it does
Company runs a global SaaS platform for TV and streaming: cloud-native, AI-enabled tools covering live production, content preparation, global distribution, and advertising monetization. Connects content providers, distributors and advertisers. Founded 2008.

## The numbers
- FY26 revenue Rs 949 crore vs Rs 667 crore FY25 (+42%); FY26 PAT Rs 26 crore vs FY25 loss of Rs 122 crore.
- TTM revenue Rs 1,018 crore; PAT Rs 55 crore.
- Nearly debt-free (borrowings Rs 19 crore vs reserves Rs 1,522 crore); depreciation Rs 15 crore; interest Rs 3 crore.
- ROCE 2.8%; ROE 4.4%; P/E ~229; P/B 7.6x; promoter holding just 14.9%.
- Working capital days ballooned from 54 to 179; debtor days ~78.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround — swung from a Rs 122 crore loss (FY25) to profit as revenue scaled 42%. But P/E of 229 with 2-3% return on capital means the market already priced the recovery many years out. Real niche: cloud playout/ad-tech for global TV. Low promoter stake a governance watch item; no insider buying data found.

## TAM
Global cloud playout, channel distribution and TV/streaming ad-tech market; exact size data not found. Shift of TV broadcast workflows to cloud underpins the runway.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. SaaS platform revenue scale (distribution + monetization modules) — operating leverage.
2. Streaming revenue-share costs ("Streaming Unification Revenue Share" line).
3. Other income (Rs 73.9 crore) currently cushioning thin operating margins (OPM near zero).

## Red flags
- Operating margin barely positive/negative; profits lean on other income.
- P/E ~229 and 7.6x book for 2-3% ROE.
- Promoters under 15%; working capital days tripled.

## Verdict
Genuine SaaS turnaround, priced like it already won — the operating margin, not the story, must do the catching up.
