# Sudeep Pharma Ltd (SUDEEPPHRM)

## What it does
Manufacturer (est. 1989) of pharmaceutical excipients and specialty mineral-based ingredients — calcium, zinc, iron and magnesium salts — for pharma, food and nutrition customers. Portfolio of 100+ products sold to 1,100+ customers in 100+ countries.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 404 Cr; FY24 Rs 344 Cr; FY25 Rs 357 Cr; FY26 Rs 339 Cr (standalone, screener) — declining top line.
- PAT: FY23 Rs 67 Cr; FY24 Rs 98 Cr; FY25 Rs 96 Cr; FY26 Rs 108 Cr — profits up despite falling sales. PAT margin 16.6% → 31.9%.
- OPM: 22% (FY23) → 36-39% (FY24-FY26).
- Borrowings: Rs 60 Cr (FY24) → Rs 127 Cr (FY26).

## The numbers anomaly: revenue fell ~16% FY23→FY26 while PAT rose 61% and OPM jumped from 22% to 36%. Mix shift toward high-margin specialty excipients or price discipline explains it; verify sustainability before extrapolating.

## Lynch lens
Boring-business niche player — pharma excipients (the "inactive" ingredients) generate steady, price-inelastic demand and few domestic competitors make specialty grades. Profit resilience on a shrinking top line shows pricing power. Lynch concern: a company whose sales shrink for three straight years, and rising debt (Rs 127 Cr FY26), keeps ROE middling. Asset-play/niche angle real but needs growth to return.

## TAM
Global pharma excipients market estimated ~US$ 9-10 bn, growing mid single digits; India import-substitution opportunity sizable (industry estimates). Company-specific TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Specialty excipient margins — OPM 36% (FY26) vs 22% (FY23) drove PAT to Rs 108 Cr.
2. Export mix across 100+ countries (1,100+ customers) supporting realisations.
3. Input costs of mineral salts (calcium/zinc/iron/magnesium) — key swing factor.

## Red flags
- Three consecutive years of falling revenue (FY24-FY26).
- Debt doubled to Rs 127 Cr (FY26) while profits stayed flat-ish.
- Margin jump (22%→36%) unexplained by public disclosure — check one-offs/mix.

## Verdict
Profitable niche excipient play with a shrinking top line — buy only when revenue growth returns.
