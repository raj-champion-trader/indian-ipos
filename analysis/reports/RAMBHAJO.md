# Advit Jewels Ltd (RAMBHAJO)

## What it does
Company makes handcrafted fine jewellery — Kundan, Polki, diamond and studded pieces in 14k/18k gold — under the "Rambhajo" brand. Sells mainly B2B to retailers, with 21 product categories and 2,000+ base designs. Blends old-school craftsmanship with CAD and 3D printing.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 69 Cr; FY25 Rs 125 Cr; FY26 Rs 167 Cr (screener standalone). Sales growth ~34% TTM.
- PAT: FY24 Rs 15 Cr; FY25 Rs 25 Cr; FY26 Rs 34 Cr. PAT margin FY25 ~20%.
- OPM: ~27% (FY24/FY25), ~29% (FY26). ROE ~46%, ROCE ~32% (FY26, screener).
- Debt: borrowings Rs 73 Cr (FY26); IPO (Rs 165 Cr, June 2026) used Rs 65 Cr to repay debt. P/E ~31.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — revenue nearly doubled FY24→FY25 and profit compounding ~35%+. Jewellery making sounds boring but branded handcrafted gold jewellery rides a real niche (wedding/premium demand). P/E ~31 vs ~35% growth gives PEG near 1 — tolerable for a fast grower, but tiny base inflates growth rates. Fresh listing, no insider-buying record yet.

## TAM
India gems & jewellery market roughly US$ 80-100 bn (industry estimates); branded/studded segment growing faster than plain gold. Precise company TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gold jewellery sales volume and gold price pass-through (dominant revenue line).
2. Gross margin on studded/Kundan-Polki handcrafted pieces vs plain gold.
3. Finance costs — fell after IPO-funded Rs 65 Cr debt repayment (FY26).

## Red flags
- Very small base (FY25 revenue Rs 125 Cr) — growth rates flatter easily.
- Working-capital-hungry business; gold price swings hit margins.
- SME-grade listing history, promoter concentration, limited segment disclosure.

## Verdict
Fast grower at PEG ~1 — interesting watcher, position size small until it proves scale beyond FY26.
