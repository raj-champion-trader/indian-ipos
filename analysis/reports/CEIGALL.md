# Ceigall India Limited (CEIGALL)

## What it does
Ceigall builds roads and bridges — elevated roads, flyovers, bridges, railway overpasses, runways and highways — mainly under government contracts. It bids for EPC projects and gets paid as construction milestones complete.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,065 cr (FY23), Rs 2,955 cr (FY24), Rs 3,385 cr (FY25), Rs 3,869 cr (FY26, standalone)
- Net profit: Rs 185 cr (FY23), Rs 277 cr (FY24), Rs 270 cr (FY25), Rs 305 cr (FY26)
- OPM steady ~13-15%; 5-year sales CAGR ~35%
- FY26 expenses Rs 3,382 cr including interest Rs 78 cr; ROE ~16%; debt reducing
- Order book value: data not found (paywalled)

## Lynch lens
A cyclical growth-at-a-reasonable-price EPC name riding India's roads capex cycle. Not boring enough for stalwart status — order inflow lumpiness governs everything — but debt reduction and 35% sales CAGR give it fast-grower credentials within the cycle. Margin stuck at ~13% means execution, not pricing, drives profit. P/E not retrieved; insider activity data not found.

## TAM
Indian road/highway EPC market — government capex programmes (Bharatmala-style) worth lakhs of crores annually; exact figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Order-book execution pace on elevated-structure projects (higher-margin speciality)
2. Raw-material costs (bitumen, steel, cement) vs contract prices
3. Receivables cycle — debtor days rose to 101 (FY26), hurting cash profit

## Red flags
- Working capital stretch: debtor days up to 101, WC days to ~71
- OPM slipped from 15% (FY24) to 13% (FY26)
- Government payment delays and bid competition

## Verdict
Solid roads-cycle compounder — cash-flow discipline decides whether it compounds your money or the government's.
