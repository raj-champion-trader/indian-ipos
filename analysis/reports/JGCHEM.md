# J.G. Chemicals Limited (JGCHEM)

## What it does
J.G. Chemicals makes zinc oxide, a white powder used in tyres, ceramics, cosmetics and medicines. It ranks as India's largest zinc-oxide maker by volume and revenue, running plants including one in Dahej.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: ~Rs 848 crore (Rs 8,479 million, per Q4 FY25 investor presentation); net profit ~Rs 67 crore (Rs 668 million).
- Q1 FY25 revenue: Rs 202.5 crore.
- FY26 (context): record revenue Rs 972.9 crore, PAT Rs 68.6 crore; new Dahej plant slated to commission H1 FY27.
- Revenue mix: rubber/tyre grades dominate; non-rubber (ceramics, cosmetics, pharma, agrochemicals) is the growth push via import substitution.
- Expense heads detail: data not found; zinc metal/hind (raw material) dominates cost of a zinc-oxide maker's cost base.

## Lynch lens
Boring stalwart — nobody dreams about zinc oxide, yet tyres and a hundred other products need it, and this company leads India. Growth stays modest and profits track zinc prices. P/E around 7.7x book-value-multiple context (market cap ~Rs 2,550 crore at one 2026 point). A classic dull compounder unless commodity margins crack.

## TAM
India zinc oxide market — exact size data not found; management sees significant import-substitution headroom in non-rubber grades (per NSE-filed investor presentation).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Tyre-industry demand for rubber-grade zinc oxide (largest revenue slice).
2. Zinc (hind) raw-material prices setting the margin spread.
3. Volume growth from new Dahej capacity and non-rubber grade expansion.

## Red flags
- Commodity economics: profit hostage to zinc prices.
- Customer concentration in tyre industry.
- Single-product dependence — zinc oxide laws/regulations (e.g., zinc content limits) could shift demand.

## Verdict
A dull market leader in a powder nobody thinks about — fine stalwart holding, watch zinc spreads.
