# M & B Engineering Ltd (MBEL)

## What it does
Company makes pre-engineered buildings (PEBs) — factory sheds, warehouses and industrial structures fabricated from steel, delivered as design-led solutions. Two divisions cover design/engineering and manufacture/erection.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 859 crore (FY23) → Rs 787 crore (FY24) → Rs 909 crore (FY25) → Rs 1,085 crore (FY26).
- Net profit: Rs 40 crore (FY23) → Rs 48 crore (FY24) → Rs 74 crore (FY25) → Rs 79 crore (FY26).
- OPM: 8% (FY23) → 10% (FY24) → 13% (FY25) → 11% (FY26).
- Borrowings cut to Rs 84 crore (FY26) from Rs 204 crore (FY24).
- ROE ~16.4%; ROCE ~19.9%; market cap ~Rs 1,551 crore; P/E ~19.7 (Aug 2026); stock -37% over 1 year.
- Debtor days 63, inventory days 161, cash cycle 125 days.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with stalwart habits. PEB demand tracks industrial and warehouse construction — cyclical — but steady margin repair (OPM 8%→11-13%) and deleveraging (debt halved since FY24) show management quality. After a 37% one-year drop, ~20x earnings for ~26% 3-year profit CAGR gives PEG near 0.75. Steel price swings run the margin cycle.

## TAM
Indian PEB market estimated at roughly Rs 15,000-20,000 crore growing 10-15% annually with warehouses, factories and data centres driving demand (exact sourced figure: data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Steel/raw material cost vs contract prices — steel dominates the expense base (exact % data not found).
2. Order-book execution and factory utilisation.
3. Operating leverage — OPM recovery from 8% (FY23) to 11-13% (FY25-FY26).

## Red flags
- Steel price volatility compresses margins quickly.
- Inventory days high at 161 — working capital locked in steel stock.
- Stock fell 37% in a year; check for order-book slowdown before buying the dip.

## Verdict
Deleveraging, decent-ROCE PEB maker at a fair price after a big drawdown — a cyclical worth accumulating on weakness.
