# HMA Agro Industries Ltd (HMAAGRO)

## What it does
Company processes and exports frozen boneless buffalo meat, plus basmati rice, seafood and pet food. It buys buffalo from Indian markets, processes the meat at its plants, and ships mainly to the Middle East, Southeast Asia and Africa. Exports make up the bulk of sales; the Government of India recognises it as a Star Export House.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 4,665 cr (FY24), Rs 4,862 cr (FY25), Rs 7,753 cr (FY26) — 59% jump in FY26.
- Net profit: Rs 111 cr (FY24), Rs 60 cr (FY25), Rs 152 cr (FY26).
- Thin OPM: 2.7% (FY24), 0.8% (FY25), 0.8% (FY26) — classic commodity trader margins.
- Stock P/E ~6.9, ROE 15%, dividend yield ~1.4%. Borrowings jumped to Rs 834 cr (FY26) from Rs 504 cr (FY25) to fund working capital.

## Revenue streams
Frozen buffalo meat ~majority of sales (~10-11% share of India's buffalo-meat exports); rice, seafood, pet food and leather the rest; exact FY split — data not found on free screener. Other income (Rs 184 cr FY26, likely duty drawbacks/exports incentives) props up profit.

## Expenses
Raw material (livestock purchase) the dominant head, ~95%+ of sales; interest Rs 38 cr, depreciation Rs 5 cr (FY26).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical commodity exporter. Boring business with a real niche (#4-ish player, 10-11% market share) but no pricing power — margins swing with buffalo prices and export demand. At ~7x earnings and 1.2x book it already prices in modesty. Other income forms a third of pre-tax profit — quality of earnings low.

## TAM
India buffalo-meat exports ~USD 3.5-4 bn annually (FY24-FY25, APEDA data); global halal meat demand growing.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Buffalo raw-material cost vs export realisation spread (OPM 0.8-5% range).
2. Export incentives/other income — Rs 184 cr FY26 vs Rs 123 cr FY25.
3. Volume growth into Vietnam/Middle East markets driving the FY26 59% sales jump.

## Red flags
OPM collapsed from 4.9% (FY23) to 0.8%. Profit depends on other income. Debt doubled in FY26. Inventory days rose to 77. Promoter stake diluted 83.6% to 75% (2026).

## Verdict
Cheap cyclical riding a strong FY26 export year — a trading stock, not a compounder; margins decide everything.
