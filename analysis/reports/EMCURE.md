# Emcure Pharmaceuticals Ltd (EMCURE)

## What it does
Emcure makes medicines — tablets, injections and capsules — across gynaecology, cardiology, oncology, respiratory and HIV. It sells these under its own brands in India and exports generics to 70+ countries. It also makes drugs for other pharma companies under contract (CDMO-like work through its arm).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 6,658 cr (FY24) → Rs 7,896 cr (FY25) → Rs 9,204 cr (FY26). ~18% growth in FY26.
- PAT: Rs 528 cr (FY24) → Rs 707 cr (FY25) → Rs 941 cr (FY26).
- OPM: 18% (FY24) to 20% (FY26). Net margin ~10% (FY26).
- Finance cost ~Rs 148 cr (FY25, ~1.5% of sales); borrowings ~Rs 1,558 cr (Mar 2026). ROCE ~24%.

## RevenueStreams
India branded business forms the largest chunk (~55-60%); Europe and rest-of-world generics next; US business smaller but growing; contract research/manufacturing adds the balance. Exact FY26 split: data not found.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart drifting toward fast grower — mid-teens revenue growth with a home-market branded base that rivals cannot easily copy. The gynaecology niche in India gives it a quiet dominance angle. ## TAM
Indian pharmaceutical market ~US$ 50 billion+ and global generics market several hundred billion dollars (industry estimates, mid-2020s). Precise FY26 figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. India branded portfolio (gynaecology, cardiology) — high margin, drives bulk of profit.
2. Europe generics business — steady export revenue.
3. US launches and contract manufacturing — growing profit contributors.

## Expenses
Cost of materials forms the largest head (data not found for exact %); finance cost ~1.5% of sales (FY25); employee cost data not found.

## Red flags
- US generics price erosion can drag margins.
- Regulator (US FDA) inspection risk at plants.
- Rising receivables in export markets possible.

## Verdict
A steady pharma stalwart with a strong India-branded core — worth buying on dips, not a ten-bagger story.
