# Shiprocket Ltd (SHIPROCKET)

## What it does
Merchant-first e-commerce enablement platform for Indian MSMEs and large retailers. API-led stack covering shipping (domestic + cross-border), fulfilment, checkout, marketing tech, omnichannel commerce and hyperlocal delivery; also facilitates financing for sellers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 1,067 Cr; FY24 Rs 1,283 Cr (screener). FY25 standalone: data not found on screener page.
- PAT: FY23 -Rs 287 Cr; FY24 -Rs 599 Cr — heavy losses driven by ESOPs and acquisition-related costs. FY25/FY26: data not found (listed Aug 2026 at IPO price Rs 97/share).
- OPM: -19% (FY23 and FY24). Total expenses FY24 Rs 1,528 Cr vs Rs 1,283 Cr revenue.
- Borrowings Rs 287 Cr (Mar 2024); interest coverage low.

## Lynch lens
Classic VC-style growth platform still hunting for its Lynch moment — losses widening FY23→FY24 even as revenue grew 20%. The aggregation model (aggregating courier partners, adding software layers) creates switching costs for merchants, but the core shipping business earns thin spreads and the growth came from acquired companies (Wigzo, Pickrr, Omuni etc.). Wait for the profitable quarter to print before treating it as a fast grower rather than a story stock.

## TAM
India e-commerce enablement/D2C logistics: e-commerce logistics market estimated US$ 8-12 bn, D2C segment growing 25%+ CAGR (industry estimates); precise TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Take-rate/spread on shipping volumes across courier partners.
2. ESOP and amortisation costs — the main loss drivers FY23-FY24.
3. Cross-sell of software (checkout, marketing) at higher margins than logistics.

## Red flags
- Losses widened to Rs 599 Cr (FY24) — cash burn ongoing.
- Growth partly acquisition-bought; integration risk.
- Hyper-competitive: Delhivery, Amazon/Flipkart in-house, Shadowfax all fight the same merchant.

## Verdict
Story stock with real network value but unproven profits — watch, don't chase post-IPO.
