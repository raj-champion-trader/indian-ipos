# ASK Automotive Ltd (ASKAUTOLTD)

## What it does
Company makes advanced braking systems for two-wheelers — brake shoes, brake panels, disc-brake parts — plus aluminium die-cast (lightweighting) parts and safety control cables. Sells mainly to India's big two-wheeler makers; holds roughly 50% share in 2W advanced braking systems.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 2,926 Cr (FY24), Rs 3,220 Cr (FY25), Rs 3,305 Cr (FY26). 5-yr CAGR 16%.
- Net profit: Rs 176 Cr (FY24), Rs 213 Cr (FY25), Rs 230 Cr (FY26). 5-yr profit CAGR 18%.
- ROE 20%; borrowings only Rs 154 Cr; market cap Rs 12,481 Cr; price Rs 633.
- Volumes FY26: 20.5 Cr brake shoes, 1.5 Cr brake panel assemblies.
- Finance costs Rs 14 Cr (FY26) — trivial.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with a niche. Half the 2W braking market in India, tied to steady two-wheeler volumes, low debt, 20% ROE — classic dependable auto-ancillary. Growth ~10-12% now; at Rs 12,481 Cr cap vs Rs 230 Cr PAT, P/E near 54x — very rich for a stalwart (PEG ~4-5). Lynch would love the business, not the price.

## TAM
India two-wheeler braking systems / auto components — data not found (company cites ~50% share of 2W advanced braking; industry size not verified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Advanced braking systems segment (core volume driver).
2. Two-wheeler industry demand (customer schedule volumes).
3. Aluminium/steel input costs (raw materials dominate its Rs 2,971 Cr FY26 expense base).

## Red flags
- Valuation: ~54x earnings for mid-teens profit growth.
- Customer concentration in 2W OEMs (Hero MotoCorp and peers — exact mix data not found).
- FY26 sales growth slowed to ~2.6%.

## Verdict
Great niche business at a stretched price — wait for a correction.
