# Rubicon Research Limited (RUBICON)

## What it does
Rubicon makes hard-to-copy medicines — specialty formulations and tricky drug-delivery systems — and sells them mostly in the US market. An R&D-driven pharma company: ~98.5% of FY25 revenue came from outside India. Listed via IPO (raised ~Rs 161 crore).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 394 crore (FY23) to Rs 854 crore (FY24) to Rs 1,284 crore (FY25) — ~76% CAGR.
- PAT: loss of ~Rs 16.8 crore (FY23) to Rs 91.1 crore (FY24) to Rs 134.3 crore (FY25). EBITDA margin ~20.7% (FY25); RoE 29% (FY25).
- R&D spend: 10.4% of income (FY25). Annualised FY26: revenue ~Rs 1,410 crore, PAT ~Rs 173 crore.
- Holds >25% US market share by value in 9 products (FY25).

## Lynch lens
Turnaround turned fast grower. From loss (FY23) to 29% RoE (FY25) in two years — Lynch loved exactly this inflection. Complex generics create a temporary niche moat; niche share (>25% in 9 products) shows real specialisation. Insider buying: data not found. IPO priced ~55x earnings vs industry ~24x — steep, so PEG depends on sustaining 30%+ growth (Motilal bull case: 32% revenue, 50% PAT CAGR FY25-28).

## TAM
US complex generics / specialty formulations TAM: data not found as a single figure. Proxy: revenue tripled FY23-FY25 in the addressed segments.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. US sales of specialty/complex formulations — nearly the entire profit pool.
2. R&D pipeline conversions — new niche launches add chunky, high-margin revenue.
3. Margin expansion — EBITDA up ~6x FY23-FY25 as scale spread fixed R&D costs.

## Red flags
- US market concentration (~98.5% export revenue FY25) — one channel, one regulator (USFDA).
- Valuation: IPO at ~55x vs peers ~24x.
- Price erosion generic to US markets; any regulatory observation hits hard.

## Verdict
Genuine turnaround with a niche moat, priced for continued perfection — size the position accordingly.
