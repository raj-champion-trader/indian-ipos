# Behari Lal Engineering Limited (BLEL)

## What it does
BLEL makes iron and steel products — alloy steel bars, metal rolls for rolling mills, heavy engineering castings, and forged shafts and ingots. Customers include steel plants, auto and aerospace part makers, and machinery builders; it exports to 21 countries. Per CRISIL, it ranks among India's largest metal rolls producers, meeting 10-11.5% of domestic demand in FY26.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 449.96 cr (FY24), Rs 516.30 cr (FY25), Rs 546.52 cr (FY26) — steady ~10% annual growth.
- PAT: Rs 35.79 cr (FY24, 8.0% margin), Rs 52.95 cr (FY25, 10.4%), Rs 64.64 cr (FY26, 12.1%).
- RoCE: 22.0% (FY24), 28.2% (FY25), 27.1% (FY26); RoNW ~20.9%.
- Borrowings Rs 41 cr (Mar 2024, screener standalone) — nearly debt free.
- Expenses (FY25 screener): interest Rs 2 cr, depreciation Rs 9 cr; raw material (scrap, steel) dominates costs — detailed % split: data not found.
- IPO Aug 2026 priced at 18.65x FY26 P/E; listed ~63% up.

## Lynch lens
A boring, niche steel products maker — exactly the kind Lynch likes: metal rolls (a dull, technically sticky product), rising margins, minimal debt, RoCE above 25%. Margins improving through a soft steel cycle shows pricing power. Cyclical by industry, but behaves more like a niche stalwart so far.

## TAM
Metal rolls domestic demand share 10-11.5% (FY26, CRISIL via IPO review); broader alloy steel bar and castings market size: data not found. Serves 1,825+ customers with no single-name dependency disclosed.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Alloy steel bars — ~46% of FY26 revenue, riding steel demand.
2. Metal rolls — ~26% of FY26 revenue, highest-niche product with 10-11.5% domestic share.
3. Engineering castings — ~20% of FY26 revenue for steel, mining, power, sugar plants.

## Red flags
- Deep commodity linkage: scrap and steel input prices squeeze or free margins.
- Small scale (Rs 546 cr revenue) vs large listed steel names.
- Contingent liabilities Rs 5.49 cr (small); no dividend history pre-2026.

## Verdict
Quality niche smallcap growing steadily — a Lynch-style watchlist candidate on dips.
