# Alpine Texworld Ltd (ALPINETEX)

## What it does
Company dyes and processes fabrics (job-work and own production) and trades grey fabric and yarn; also provides yarn sizing services for weavers. Incorporated 2016, operates in the textile processing segment of the value chain.

## The numbers
- FY26 revenue Rs 240 crore vs Rs 177 crore FY25 (+36%); PAT Rs 21 crore vs Rs 9 crore (+133%).
- OPM 15% (FY26) vs 12% (FY25); interest Rs 12 crore; depreciation Rs 10 crore; tax rate 15%.
- Borrowings Rs 122 crore (FY26); ROE 42%; ROCE 21.4%.
- Market cap ~Rs 211 crore; P/E ~9.8; promoters 68.6%.
- TTM profit growth ~150%; 3-year profit CAGR 142%.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower at single-digit P/E — boring fabric-processing job work that nobody follows, exactly the Lynch sweet spot. PEG well under 1 if growth sustains. Watch debt-funded capex and whether 42% ROE survives competition. No insider buying data found.

## TAM
Indian textile processing/dyeing industry (fabric processing capacity, especially Gujarat/Tamil Nadu clusters); exact size data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Dyeing/processing job-work volumes and capacity utilization — core profit driver.
2. Grey fabric/yarn trading spreads.
3. Interest (Rs 12 crore FY26) and depreciation on processing capacity.

## Red flags
- Rs 122 crore debt on a Rs 211 crore market cap.
- Profit CAGR of 142% off a small base normalizes fast.
- Small, promoter-heavy microcap; commodity processing margins can erode.

## Verdict
Classic cheap microcap grower — high risk, but P/E ~10 for 30%+ growth deserves a watchlist spot.
