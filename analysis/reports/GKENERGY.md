# GK Energy (GKENERGY)

## What it does
GK Energy installs solar-powered water pumps for farmers under the government's PM-KUSUM scheme. It builds the whole system — panels, pump, wiring — as an EPC contractor empanelled in Maharashtra, Haryana, Rajasthan, UP and MP. It has installed over 1,00,000 solar pumps and recently won a Rs 454.5 cr rooftop-solar order (100 MW, 1,00,000 households, Aug 2026).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,742 cr (Mar 2026 FY), TTM sales growth +41% — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 224 cr (FY26), TTM profit growth +46%
- OPM ~20%; ROE 36.8%; ROCE 40.9%
- 3-yr sales CAGR 75%; 3-yr profit CAGR 171%
- Borrowings Rs 203 cr; equity Rs 885 cr — modest leverage

## Lynch lens
Cyclical fast grower — but the cycle's driver sits in Delhi. Essentially all revenue flows from government schemes (PM-KUSUM and state programmes): subsidy delays, scheme extensions or policy changes whipsaw the P&L. While schemes run, the economics look spectacular (37% ROE, 171% 3-yr profit CAGR). Lynch would call this a "government-scheme play" — fine while funded, dangerous the day budgets shift. Recent rooftop-solar win diversifies the bet slightly.

## TAM
PM-KUSUM alone targets tens of lakh solar pumps for Indian farmers (component B/C of the scheme), plus state schemes and rooftop solar — aggregate opportunity well above Rs 1 lakh cr over the scheme horizon (approximate; precise current TAM not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. PM-KUSUM component-B solar pump EPC contracts — nearly all revenue and profit
2. Government subsidy payment timing — receivables make or break cash and reported margins
3. New rooftop-solar (PM Surya Ghar) orders — the Rs 454.5 cr win points to the second leg

## Red flags
- Single-customer risk in effect: government schemes
- Subsidy receivables can bloat working capital
- Competition from other MNRE-empanelled EPC firms compresses margins over time
- 75% CAGR base effect — growth must normalise down

## Verdict
Ride the solar-subsidy wave with a stop-loss — policy risk, not competition, decides the ending.
