# Manoj Vaibhav Gems 'N' Jewellers (MVGJL)

## What it does
The company sells jewellery through its own retail chain, "Vaibhav Jewellers," across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. It focuses on affordable daily-wear gold jewellery plus wedding jewellery for regional buyers. It listed via IPO in 2023 and operates roughly a few dozen showrooms.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,025 crore (FY23); Rs 2,148 crore (FY24); Rs 2,382 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 2,909 crore
- PAT: Rs 72 crore (FY23); Rs 81 crore (FY24); Rs 100 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 122 crore
- OPM steady at ~7% (FY23-FY26); net margin 4.2% (FY25)
- Interest Rs 41 crore (FY25); depreciation Rs 8 crore (FY25); total expenses Rs 2,217 crore (FY25)
- 5-year sales CAGR ~14%; 5-year profit CAGR ~32%; ROE ~15%; P/E ~6.3x (screener snapshot)

## RevenueStreams
- Gold jewellery — dominant share of sales (FY25)
- Studded/diamond jewellery and silver — smaller share (FY25)

## Expenses
- Cost of gold/raw materials — roughly 80%+ of sales for a jewellery retailer (FY25); exact %: data not found

## Lynch lens
A slow grower at a deep-value price — P/E ~6x with 14% sales CAGR, 32% profit CAGR and ROE ~15%. Lynch liked consumer franchises quietly compounding in one region; the Andhra/Telangana wedding-gold niche provides loyalty and pricing trust. Thin margins (7% OPM) mean gold prices drive revenue more than effort. Region-concentrated retailers (think Titan's early regional rivals) can compound well if store additions continue without leverage creeping up.

## TAM
India jewellery market estimated at ~US$ 80-100 billion annually (FY24-FY25, industry/IBEF estimates); organised players gaining share from family jewellers.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gold price and volume — revenue scales with gold rates, margin roughly fixed
2. New showroom additions across AP/Telangana — the volume growth engine
3. Studded-jewellery mix — higher margin than plain gold

## Red flags
- Single-region concentration — cyclones, local economics and competition (Titan, local family jewellers)
- Heavy working-capital borrowing (interest Rs 41 crore, FY25) tied to gold inventory
- Thin 7% OPM — little cushion for error
- Regulatory shifts on gold hallmarking/imports

## Verdict
A cheap regional consumer franchise — a slow grower priced for gloom, worth a small position.
