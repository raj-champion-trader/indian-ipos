# Jain Resource Recycling Ltd (JAINREC)

## What it does
Company recycles non-ferrous metal scrap into lead, copper and aluminium ingots and alloys. Its lead ingots carry London Metal Exchange registration — a quality badge few Indian recyclers hold. It also trades non-ferrous metals, and via subsidiary Jain Ikon (UAE, 99.7% held) buys scrap globally.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 4,157 cr (FY24), Rs 6,143 cr (FY25), Rs 9,231 cr (FY26) — 50%+ CAGR over three years.
- Net profit: Rs 160 cr (FY24), Rs 211 cr (FY25), Rs 347 cr (FY26).
- OPM ~5-6%. ROE ~31% (FY26), ROCE 26%. Stock P/E ~27.6, market cap ~Rs 9,937 cr.
- Borrowings Rs 1,253 cr (FY26); operating cash flow -570 cr FY26 (working-capital gulp as scrap prices/volumes rose).

## Revenue streams
Lead ingots/alloys the historical core; copper and aluminium recycling plus trading expanding fast; UAE subsidiary adds overseas scrap sourcing. Exact FY segment split — data not found on free screener (lead vs copper vs aluminium shares premium-gated).

## Expenses
Scrap purchase (raw material) ~93-94% of sales; interest Rs 96 cr, depreciation Rs 11 cr (FY26).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring, dirty business Lynch loves — metal scrap recycling. LME-registered lead gives a niche moat; CRISIL upgraded it to AA- (Aug 2026), a good sign. 3-yr profit CAGR ~79% vs P/E 27.6 gives a PEG well under 0.5 — if growth holds. Promoters 73.6%; DIIs entered (9.7%).

## TAM
Global non-ferrous scrap recycling market >USD 100 bn; India secondary lead/copper/aluminium recycling a multi-billion-dollar, underpenetrated market (industry estimates, FY25).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Recycling spread: scrap cost vs LME-linked metal prices (OPM 5-6%).
2. Volume scale-up — sales up 50% CAGR FY24-FY26.
3. Copper/aluminium mix and UAE trading entity boosting turnover and margins.

## Red flags
Negative operating cash flow (-570 cr FY26) while profit grows — working capital eats cash. Thin commodity margins. Big related-party loans to subsidiaries. Small listed history (2025 main-board listing).

## Verdict
Textbook Lynch fast grower in an unglamorous niche at a PEG under 0.5 — main risk sits in cash flow, not earnings.
