# Highway Infrastructure Ltd (HILINFRA)

## What it does
Company collects toll money on highways, builds road and infrastructure projects under EPC contracts, and sells real estate. It runs toll operations across 11 states and 1 union territory in India.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 266 cr (FY23), Rs 401 cr (FY24), Rs 418 cr (FY25) — standalone.
- PAT: Rs 12 cr (FY23), Rs 19 cr (FY24), Rs 20 cr (FY25).
- OPM fell from 13% (FY18) to 5% (FY25); net margin ~4.8% (FY25).
- Borrowings: Rs 61 cr (Mar FY25). Total expenses Rs 398 cr (FY25).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical. Toll collection resembles a steady annuity, but EPC contracting drags margins and depends on government orders. Boring business with predictable toll cash flows — the interesting half of the story. Detailed segment split: data not found.

## TAM
India road sector: government capex on roads/highways ~Rs 2.6 lakh cr per year (FY24, NHAI/MoRTH budget estimates); toll collection industry several Rs 40,000+ cr per year (NHAI figures). Period/source: FY24 government budget documents.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Toll collection revenue across its tollways — main stable earner.
2. EPC project execution — margin pressure dragged OPM from 7% (FY24) to 5% (FY25).
3. Real estate sales — small, lumpy contributor.

## Red flags
- Margin erosion over seven years (13% to 5%).
- Thin ~4.8% net margin; small PAT base of Rs 20 cr (FY25).
- Dependence on government contracts and re-awards of toll plazas.

## Verdict
Boring toll collector with steady cash but shrinking margins — watch, not buy, until EPC drag fades.
