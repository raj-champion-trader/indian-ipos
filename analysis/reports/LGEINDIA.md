# LG Electronics India Ltd (LGEINDIA)

## What it does
Company makes and sells home appliances and consumer electronics — washing machines, refrigerators, TVs, air conditioners, microwaves. Indian arm of Korea's LG, operating since 1997. Holds #1 market share in washing machines, refrigerators, panel TVs, inverter ACs, and microwaves in the offline channel. Listed January 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 24,367 cr (FY25).
- PAT: Rs 2,203 cr (FY25) — ~9% net margin; ROE 25%.
- Operating profit Rs 3,115 cr; OPM 13% (FY25), up from ~10% a year earlier.
- Total expenses Rs 21,252 cr; interest just Rs 35 cr; depreciation Rs 380 cr (FY25); tax rate 26%.
- Borrowings only Rs 428 cr (FY25) — effectively debt-free.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart. A dominant, boring appliance maker — exactly the kind of consumer franchise Lynch liked: #1 share across five categories, 25% ROE, no debt. Consumer durables carry mild cyclicality (discretionary demand), but brand and distribution make this a steady-Eddie compounder. Segment P/E data: data not found for PEG math.

## TAM
Indian home appliances and consumer electronics market — large and premiumising; specific sourced figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Air conditioner and refrigerator sales — seasonal summer demand drives the biggest volume swings.
2. Margin mix from premium models — OPM jumped from ~10% to 13% (FY25) as premium share rose.
3. Operating leverage on its distribution network — nearly zero interest cost (Rs 35 cr FY25) keeps profits clean.

## Red flags
- Parent LG holds 85% — minority shareholders get little say; related-party pricing (royalties, imports) needs watching.
- Offline-channel dominance could erode as e-commerce grows.
- Premium-cycle margin gains (13% OPM FY25) may mean-revert in a demand slowdown.

## Verdict
Textbook Lynch stalwart — dominant, debt-free, high-ROE appliance leader; a hold-forever candidate if the entry multiple behaves.
