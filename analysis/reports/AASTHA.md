# Aastha Spintex Ltd (AASTHA)

## What it does
Gujarat-based spinner that makes and trades 100% cotton yarn and cotton bales from an integrated ginning-and-spinning facility. Sells B2B to textile manufacturers and yarn exporters. Incorporated 2013.

## The numbers
- FY26 revenue Rs 443 crore vs Rs 351 crore in FY25 (+26%); PAT flat at Rs 24 crore both years.
- OPM 12% (FY26) vs 14% (FY25); interest Rs 12 crore; depreciation Rs 8 crore.
- Borrowings Rs 107 crore (FY26); ROE 16.8%; ROCE 19%.
- 5-year sales CAGR 19%; 5-year profit CAGR 46%. Promoters hold 53.2%.
- Board approved 1:1 bonus and dividend (Aug 2026).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical — cotton yarn spreads swing with raw cotton prices and export demand. Growth decent, returns respectable (ROCE ~19%), but FY26 margin slip shows cycle risk. Bonus issue suggests promoter confidence; no formal insider-buying data found.

## TAM
Indian cotton spun yarn industry; exact size data not found. Demand tied to domestic textile offtake and yarn exports (Bangladesh, China, EU).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Cotton yarn volumes and price spreads (raw cotton vs yarn realizations) — dominant driver.
2. Raw cotton cost movements (ginning economics, cotton bales).
3. Interest cost (Rs 12 crore FY26) on Rs 107 crore working-capital-heavy borrowings.

## Red flags
- PAT flat despite 26% revenue growth — margin squeeze.
- Cyclical, commodity product with no pricing power.
- Small cap; working-capital-intensive business.

## Verdict
Fairly-priced cotton cyclical riding volume growth — profits need the yarn cycle to cooperate.
