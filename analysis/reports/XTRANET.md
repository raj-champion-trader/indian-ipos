# Xtranet Technologies Ltd (XTRANET)

## What it does
Xtranet Technologies builds and runs enterprise IT systems — custom applications, digital transformation projects, managed services and its own platforms called Synergy and XtraTrust. Founded 2002, it serves a heavy share of government and PSU clients through fixed-price projects, time-and-materials contracts and recurring service agreements, delivered onsite and offshore.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 233 crore FY24, Rs 277 crore FY25, Rs 366 crore FY26 (RHP figures; screener standalone shows Rs 254 crore FY25 — presentation differences).
- PAT: Rs 11 crore FY24, Rs 30 crore FY25, Rs 41 crore FY26; PAT margin ~11% FY26.
- OPM improved 5% (FY23) to 17% (FY25, screener); ROE ~35% FY26.
- Borrowings Rs 38 crore FY25 with Rs 7 crore interest; debtor days ~236 — slow-paying government clients.
- IPO July 2026: Rs 167 crore fresh issue; Rs 102 crore for working capital, Rs 22 crore debt repayment.

## Lynch lens
Boring-business fast grower — unglamorous government IT plumbing with 25%+ revenue growth, margins expanding, and ROE of ~35%. Lynch liked dull IT service niches with sticky government clients. P/E ~25x on FY26 earnings gives a PEG near 1 — reasonable if growth holds. The catch: government receivables (236 debtor days) tie up cash.

## TAM
Data not found (India e-governance / enterprise IT services market size not stated in sources reviewed).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Government/PSU project deliveries (fixed-price and T&M contracts) — volume driver.
2. Recurring managed-services and platform revenue (Synergy, XtraTrust) — margin driver.
3. Receivables cycle — 236 debtor days swing working-capital and finance costs.

## Red flags
- Government client concentration and very long receivable days (236).
- Revenue presentation differences (screener vs RHP) cloud comparability.
- Small scale (Rs 366 crore FY26) in a crowded IT services market.
- Working-capital-hungry model — IPO money mainly funds receivables.

## Verdict
A quietly compounding government-IT niche player at a fair ~25x — decent, but watch the receivables.
