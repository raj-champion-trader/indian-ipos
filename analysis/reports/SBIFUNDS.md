# SBI Funds Management Ltd (SBIFUNDS)

## What it does
Company runs SBI Mutual Fund, India's largest mutual fund house. Earns management fees on assets investors park in its schemes. State Bank of India holds the promoter stake; Amundi the minority partner historically.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 2,404 Cr; FY24 Rs 3,423 Cr; FY25 Rs 4,232 Cr; FY26 Rs 4,970 Cr (standalone, screener). 5-yr CAGR ~25%.
- PAT: FY23 Rs 1,331 Cr; FY24 Rs 2,063 Cr; FY25 Rs 2,531 Cr; FY26 Rs 3,051 Cr. 5-yr CAGR ~29%.
- Operating margin ~82% (FY26) — fee machine.
- Zero debt. ROE ~43%, ROCE ~57% (FY26).
- Market cap Rs 1,14,836 Cr; P/E ~37.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart-plus — a toll booth on India's savings flows. Every SIP flowing into SBI MF pays this company a clip with near-zero marginal cost, hence 82% margins. Dominant franchise (#1 fund house) with a parent (SBI) distribution moat. Lynch loves boring businesses collecting tolls. P/E 37 vs ~29% profit growth gives PEG ~1.3 — not cheap, but quality compensates partly. Watch fee compression from regulator/competition.

## TAM
Indian mutual fund industry AUM crossed ~Rs 70 lakh crore in FY25 (AMFI); SIP flows running ~Rs 26,000+ Cr/month (FY25). Penetration stays low vs GDP, so the pool grows structurally.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Average AUM under management — equity scheme mix drives effective fee rate.
2. Equity market levels (mark-to-market lifts AUM and thus fees).
3. Operating leverage — expenses (FY26 total ~Rs 914 Cr, mostly employee/distribution) grow far slower than fee income.

## Red flags
- Valuation prices in continued bull-market AUM growth; a market correction cuts fees twice (flows + MTM).
- Regulatory risk on expense ratios (TER cuts directly hit revenue).
- 82% margins invite competition and scrutiny.

## Verdict
Classic Lynch toll-road stalwart — accumulate on market-driven dips.
