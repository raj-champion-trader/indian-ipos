# Wakefit Innovations Ltd (WAKEFIT)

## What it does
Wakefit sells mattresses, pillows, beds, sofas and other home furniture directly to consumers online and through its own stores, cutting out middlemen to keep prices low. Founded 2016, it built its brand on memory-foam mattresses sold over the internet, then expanded into whole-home furniture and decor. It runs a direct-to-consumer (D2C) model with company-owned stores.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 813 crore FY23, Rs 986 crore FY24, Rs 1,274 crore FY25, Rs 1,489 crore FY26.
- PAT: -Rs 146 crore FY23, -Rs 15 crore FY24, -Rs 35 crore FY25, +Rs 189 crore FY26 — first full-year profit.
- OPM: -10% FY23 to 12% FY26; finance cost Rs 29 crore FY26; borrowings Rs 272 crore FY26.
- P/E ~24x post listing; ROE 23% FY26; ROCE 10.9% FY26.
- IPO (Oct 2025): Rs 1,289 crore (Rs 377 crore fresh + OFS); funds for 117 new company-owned stores; listed flat.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround that just turned — six loss-making years, then a Rs 189 crore FY26 profit as scale and store economics kicked in. The mattress business is boring and repetitive (people replace bedding every 8-10 years), which Lynch likes; the D2C brand with ~1,500 crore revenue and 24x P/E on fresh earnings looks reasonable IF the profit holds. Watch whether FY26 profit came from operations or one-offs/other income.

## TAM
Data not found precisely; context — India mattress and home-furnishing market large and shifting from unorganised to organised/online players.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Sleep category (mattresses, pillows) — core margin engine.
2. Furniture category scale (sofas, beds, storage) — margin diluter historically, improving.
3. Store-network economics and advertising spend efficiency — 117 new stores funded by IPO.

## Red flags
- Only one profitable year; FY26 profit needs verification for sustainability.
- Rs 272 crore borrowings and Rs 29 crore interest while expanding offline stores.
- Competition from Sleepwell, Kurlon, Duroflex and marketplace brands.
- ROCE (10.9%) still well below ROE — capital-hungry model.

## Verdict
A real brand at a fair price if FY26 profits stick — a watchlist turnaround, buy the dip not the story.
