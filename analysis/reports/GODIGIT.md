# Go Digit General Insurance Ltd (GODIGIT)

## What it does
Company sells general insurance — motor, health, travel, property, marine, and liability cover — fully online through a digital model. It designs products, sells them through its platform and partners, and handles claims itself.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 5,885 cr (FY23), Rs 8,147 cr (FY24), Rs 9,370 cr (FY25).
- PAT: Rs 36 cr (FY23), Rs 182 cr (FY24), Rs 425 cr (FY25) — profit growing fast.
- Operating profit slim: Rs 50 cr (FY23), Rs -269 cr (FY24), Rs +120 cr (FY25); earnings include other income of ~Rs 468-472 cr.
- Nearly debt free (borrowings ~Rs 350 cr); ROE 12.2%; 5-yr sales CAGR 35%, profit CAGR 45%.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a dull industry — insurance earns attention, but underwriting discipline and investment income drive the compounding. PAT up from Rs 36 cr to Rs 425 cr in two years. Caveat: underwriting result (Rs 120 cr FY25 operating profit) still trails investment/other income, so profits lean on float returns and interest rates. Segment GWP mix detail: data not found.

## TAM
Indian non-life insurance industry gross written premium ~Rs 2.5-2.9 lakh cr annually (FY24, IRDAI), growing low-to-mid teens; among the most underpenetrated insurance markets globally.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Motor insurance — the largest premium line and main underwriting contributor.
2. Investment/other income (~Rs 468-472 cr) — bridges underwriting to final profit.
3. Health insurance growth — margin and scale lever as the book diversifies.

## Red flags
- FY24 underwriting loss (Rs -269 cr operating) — profitability leans on investment income.
- Competition from Digit's larger rivals (ICICI Lombard, Bajaj, new digital insurers).
- Catastrophe/motor claims can swing results sharply.

## Verdict
Digitally-led fast grower with real profit momentum — worth holding if underwriting stays clean.
