# Powerica Ltd (POWERICA)

## What it does
Powerica makes diesel generator sets and medium-speed large generators (a 40+ year OEM partnership with Cummins) and builds wind power projects as an independent power producer, including EPC and O&M services. Customers span industries needing backup and captive power.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,496 crore FY25 vs Rs 2,195 crore FY24 (+14%).
- PAT: Rs 142 crore FY25 vs Rs 213 crore FY24 (-33%). OPM fell 17% to 12% FY25.
- Depreciation Rs 116 crore and interest Rs 32 crore FY25; borrowings Rs 308 crore Mar 2025.
- 5-year sales CAGR 24%, 5-year profit CAGR 73% (screener.in).

## Lynch lens
A cyclical in both engines and wind — data-centre and industrial backup-power demand could drive the next upcycle, while the wind IPP book gives some annuity income. FY25 margin compression (12% OPM, PAT down a third) shows the cyclical bite. The Cummins OEM tie is a real niche asset. Reasonable candidate on a bad-year P/E; check current multiple.

## TAM
India DG set market plus wind IPP pipeline: DG market commonly estimated at Rs 15,000-20,000 crore and India targets ~100 GW wind capacity by 2030 (industry/MNRE estimates); exact split data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. DG set and MSLG volumes (industrial/data-centre demand) and Cummins OEM economics.
2. Wind IPP generation income and project EPC margins.
3. Raw material costs (engines, steel, wind turbine components) squeezing or releasing margins.

## Red flags
- PAT down 33% FY25 on rising sales — margin squeeze.
- Heavy depreciation (Rs 116 crore FY25) from capex-heavy wind assets.
- Wind-IPP execution and offtake risk; DG demand tied to industrial capex cycles.

## Verdict
Cyclical power play with a strong Cummins moat — buy the bad year, not the good one.
