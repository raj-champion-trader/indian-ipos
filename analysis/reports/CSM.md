# CSM Technologies Limited (CSM)

## What it does
CSM Technologies builds software for governments — e-governance platforms for mining, agriculture, trade, education, and healthcare departments. It helps public departments move paperwork and services online, and sells these systems in 14 countries including India, Kenya, Ethiopia, Rwanda, and Malawi.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 161.50 cr (FY23), Rs 198.65-193 cr (FY24), Rs 200.63-198 cr (FY25), Rs 213 cr (FY26 screener standalone) — low single-digit growth lately.
- PAT: Rs 15.82 cr (FY23), Rs 12.55 cr (FY24), Rs 14.09 cr (FY25), Rs 20 cr (FY26 screener) — flat-to-bouncy.
- OPM: 14% (FY24), 16% (FY25), 18% (FY26); RoNW ~21.5% average.
- Borrowings Rs 72 cr (Mar 2026); interest Rs 7 cr, depreciation Rs 6 cr (FY26); employee costs dominate an IT shop (split %: data not found).
- Order book Rs 357.63 cr (Mar 2026) — ~1.7x annual revenue.
- IPO June 2026 at 41x FY25 P/E; listed flat at Rs 113.

## Lynch lens
Neither fast grower nor stalwart — a slow-growing small-cap IT firm. 5-year sales growth ~23% came mostly early; FY24-FY26 revenue crawled. GovTech sounds exciting, but tiny contracts (a Rs 3.21 cr Malawi deal made news) show the scale problem. At 41x FY25 earnings with flat profits, the price ran ahead of the business.

## TAM
Global GovTech market size: data not found in fetched sources. India digital governance spend and Africa digital-identity programs form the demand base; the company cites DPI (digital public infrastructure) exports as its growth theme.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Government software contracts in mining and allied sectors (India) — the core revenue.
2. African government deals (Ethiopia, Kenya, Rwanda, Malawi) — the growth layer, lumpy and currency-risky.
3. Employee cost control — margin lever for a 1,700-person firm; OPM improved 14% to 18% (FY24-FY26).

## Red flags
- Debtor days 154 — governments pay slowly; cash gets stuck.
- Contingent liability Rs 41.66 cr (Dec 2025) on a small balance sheet.
- Inconsistent bottom line; revenue growth stalled FY24-FY26.

## Verdict
Small GovTech player priced for growth it has yet to deliver — skip for now.
