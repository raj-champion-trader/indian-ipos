# Belrise Industries Ltd (BELRISE)

## What it does
Belrise makes parts for vehicles — sheet metal and casting components, polymer parts, suspension systems and mirror systems — for two-wheelers, cars and farm equipment. It supplies safety-critical parts in bulk to major automakers (IPO coverage highlights Bajaj Auto among its largest customers).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 7,484 cr (FY24) → Rs 8,291 cr (FY25) → Rs 9,509 cr (FY26).
- PAT: Rs 314 cr (FY24) → Rs 355 cr (FY25) → Rs 497 cr (FY26).
- OPM steady at ~12%; net margin ~5.2% (FY26).
- Borrowings cut to Rs 1,521 cr (Mar 2026) from Rs 2,964 cr (Mar 2025) — IPO proceeds used for debt reduction.
- P/E ~43.6x; ROCE ~14.5%; market cap ~Rs 22,232 cr (2026).

## RevenueStreams
Sheet metal and casting parts form the largest product line; polymer components, suspension and mirror systems follow (FY25/FY26). Exact % split: data not found. Two-wheeler segment dominates end-market mix.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical/stalwart hybrid — auto component supply moves with two-wheeler and car demand cycles. Pros: customer stickiness, safety-critical niche, debt halved post-IPO. Cons: 43.6x P/E for a 12%-margin, ~11% profit-growth business gives a poor PEG (~4) — the market already paid for the story. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
Indian auto components industry ~US$ 70-80 billion (ACMA estimates, mid-2020s), with two-wheeler volume among the world's largest. Precise FY26 figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Two-wheeler production volumes at anchor customers (volume-linked pricing).
2. Raw material (steel, polymers) cost pass-through — margins swing with input prices.
3. Interest cost savings after debt reduction (Rs 232 cr interest, FY26, down as borrowings halved).

## Expenses
Materials and other operating expenses ~Rs 7,762 cr (~82% of sales, FY26); depreciation Rs 361 cr; interest Rs 232 cr.

## Red flags
- Customer concentration (Bajaj Auto a very large share per IPO coverage).
- Thin 12% OPM; steel price spikes squeeze margins.
- Valuation rich versus growth.

## Verdict
A solid parts maker that fixed its balance sheet — business earns a hold; at ~44x earnings, the price outruns the story.
