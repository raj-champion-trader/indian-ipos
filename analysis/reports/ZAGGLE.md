# Zaggle Prepaid Ocean Services Ltd (ZAGGLE)

## What it does
Company runs a digital platform that helps businesses control spending — corporate prepaid cards, expense automation, reward and gift card programs, and SaaS spend-management tools. It plugs into payment networks, banks, and company ERP systems to automate and reconcile expenses.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 553 cr (FY23), Rs 776 cr (FY24), Rs 1,303 cr (FY25) — standalone.
- PAT: Rs 23 cr (FY23), Rs 44 cr (FY24), Rs 87 cr (FY25); net margin 6.7% (FY25).
- OPM steady ~9% (FY23-FY25).
- Nearly debt free: borrowings Rs 16 cr (Mar FY25), down from Rs 141 cr (FY23).
- 5-yr sales CAGR 51%; 5-yr profit CAGR 47%; ROE ~10%.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — revenue up 68% in FY25 with PAT nearly doubling. Business model resembles a fintech tollbooth on corporate spending. Low margin (6.7%) but capital-light and debt-free. Consistency needs watching: growth must hold for a few more years before Lynch-style conviction. Segment split detail: data not found.

## TAM
India corporate expense management / prepaid card market — spend on employee rewards, taxi/food benefits, and B2B payments runs into tens of thousands of crores annually; industry estimates (FY24) size the corporate cards + employee benefits market at Rs 50,000+ cr.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Payment/transaction volumes on corporate prepaid cards — bulk of revenue.
2. Tax benefits and employee benefits programs (taxi, food, gift cards).
3. SaaS subscription revenue from spend-management software — high-margin kicker.

## Red flags
- Thin 6.7% net margin; OPM stuck at 9% while revenue scales.
- Revenue growth partly driven by low-margin pass-through payment volume.
- Low ROE (~10%) despite growth.

## Verdict
Genuine fast grower in boring corporate-spending plumbing — hold and watch margins scale with volume.
