# Kalpataru Limited (KALPATARU)

## What it does
Company develops real estate — finds land, designs, builds and sells residential and commercial projects, mainly in Mumbai. One of the older Mumbai developers, recently listed via IPO.

## The numbers (standalone)
- Sales: Rs 293 cr (FY24), Rs 283 cr (FY25), Rs 210 cr (FY26) — shrinking
- Net profit: Rs 82 cr (FY24), Rs 23 cr (FY25), Rs -16 cr (FY26) — into losses
- OPM: 31% (FY24), 20% (FY25), 8% (FY26)
- Borrowings: Rs 2,128 cr (Mar 2026), down from Rs 2,981 cr (Mar 2024)
- Finance cost: Rs 184 cr (FY26) — massive vs Rs 210 cr sales; depreciation Rs 21 cr
- ROE -0.6%, ROCE 2.6%; 3-yr sales -15%; stock -27% in 1 year

## Lynch lens
Turnaround — if any. Real estate with Rs 2,128 cr debt, interest of Rs 184 cr eating an Rs 210 cr standalone revenue base, and FY26 losses. The IPO proceeds went largely to debt paydown. No PEG possible with negative earnings. Mumbai real estate as an asset play angle exists, but standalone numbers look grim; consolidated reality (projects in JVs/SPs) may differ — data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Finance cost (Rs 184 cr FY26) — the single biggest destroyer; every rupee of deleveraging drops to profit
2. Project completions and revenue recognition timing — swings OPM 31% to 8%
3. Mumbai property demand/pricing — recovery lifts both sales and margins

## Red flags
- FY26 standalone net loss (Rs -16 cr)
- Interest cost nearly equals revenue
- Five-year sales decline of 19%
- Heavy reliance on project-SPV structure — limited standalone visibility

## Verdict
Leveraged Mumbai property turnaround bet — only for buyers who trust the land bank more than the P&L.
