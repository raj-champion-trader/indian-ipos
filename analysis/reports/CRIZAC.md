# Crizac Ltd (CRIZAC)

## What it does
Crizac runs a B2B platform that connects education agents with universities abroad. When an agent places an international student at a partner university in the UK, Canada, Ireland, Australia or New Zealand, the university pays Crizac a commission. It also sells marketing and admission-office services to those institutions.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 344 cr (FY24), Rs 187 cr (FY25, UK commission-rule disruption), Rs 267 cr (FY26, recovering).
- Net profit: Rs 188 cr (FY24), Rs 109 cr (FY25), Rs 167 cr (FY26).
- Stunning OPM: 41% (FY24), 82% (FY25-FY26) — asset-light commission model.
- Debt free. ROE ~31%, ROCE 41%, stock P/E ~16.9, dividend yield 4.6% (84% payout FY26).

## Revenue streams
Commissions from partner universities on placed students, dominated by UK institutions (UK share of revenue — premium-gated, data not found); plus brand-management/admission-office services.

## Expenses
Minimal — employee and tech costs only (FY26 total expenses Rs 44 cr on Rs 267 cr sales). Interest zero, depreciation Rs 26 cr (FY25, includes intangible amortisation).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with a fat-margin niche: it monetises Indian students going abroad — a structural growth story — without owning classrooms or planes. FY25 dip (UK visa tightening) shows policy risk, but FY26 rebounded. PEG ~1 or better (16.9 P/E vs 53% 5-yr profit CAGR). Promoter 79.9%; big 4.6% dividend yield rewards patience.

## TAM
~1.3 million Indian students study abroad (FY24-FY25 estimates); global agent-recruitment commission pool estimated USD 3-5 bn annually.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. UK university placement volumes and commission rates (largest revenue chunk).
2. Diversification into Canada/Ireland/Australia recovering after FY25 UK shock.
3. Zero debt + near-zero capex means almost all operating profit drops to PAT.

## Red flags
Heavy UK concentration exposed by the FY25 revenue crash (-46%). Policy/visa-rule risk in every destination country. Debtor days up to 109 (FY26). Agent-network model reputational risk.

## Verdict
High-margin toll-taker on the study-abroad wave at a fair price — a fast grower worth holding, UK policy the thing to watch.
