# Regaal Resources Ltd (REGAAL)

## What it does
Company grinds maize into starch, speciality starches and starch derivatives at a 750 TPD plant. Sells to food, paper, textile and pharma makers that use starch as an input. Ranks among India's top 10 maize milling companies.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 600 Cr (FY24), Rs 915 Cr (FY25), Rs 1,134 Cr (FY26). 5-yr profit CAGR 37.2%.
- Net profit: Rs 22 Cr (FY24), Rs 48 Cr (FY25), Rs 56 Cr (FY26).
- OPM 11% (FY26); ROE 16.5%; ROCE 12%; borrowings Rs 614 Cr vs reserves Rs 438 Cr (FY26) — debt-heavy.
- Market cap Rs 953 Cr; P/E 14.7 at Rs 92.5/share.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical / commodity processor. Maize starch margins swing with maize prices and starch realisations — no pricing power visible. Growth looks fast (sales +53% FY25), but FY26 growth slowed to ~24% and interest costs of Rs 31 Cr eat a quarter of operating profit. P/E 14.7 with slowing growth gives PEG near 0.6 on fading numbers — cheap for a reason. Niche angle: top-10 position in an boring, fragmented agro-processing industry, the kind Lynch liked — but leverage spoils it.

## TAM
India maize starch / maize milling industry — data not found (fragmented, unlisted peers; company ranks top-10 by capacity).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Maize price vs starch/derivative realisations (gross margin).
2. Capacity utilisation of the 750 TPD plant.
3. Interest burden (Rs 31 Cr FY26) on Rs 614 Cr borrowings.

## Red flags
- Debt nearly 1.4x equity; interest coverage thin.
- FY26 profit growth (+17%) lagged sales growth (+24%).
- Commodity business, working-capital hungry.

## Verdict
Cheap commodity grinder with real debt — watch, don't chase.
