# Blue Jet Healthcare Limited (BLUEJET)

## What it does
Company makes pharmaceutical intermediates, APIs, and contrast media agents (iodinated and barium-based dyes used in medical scans like CT and X-ray). It sells to global pharma and imaging companies. Listed October 2023.

## The numbers
- FY24 revenue ~Rs 750+ crore; management guided ~20% revenue CAGR FY24-26.
- FY25 revenue ~Rs 886 crore with PAT ~Rs 235 crore (~26% PAT margin); Q4 FY25 revenue Rs 340 crore, up 13% ahead of estimates.
- EBITDA margins 40%+; ROE ~24%; debt-free.
- 3-year profit growth ~15.7%; market cap ~Rs 10,900 crore.
- Expense heads: raw materials (iodine and barium compounds — iodine prices swing) and employee costs dominate — exact percentages data not found.

## Lynch lens
A perfect Lynch niche: contrast media dyes for scans — boring, medically essential, repeat-purchase, with 40%+ EBITDA margins and zero debt. Category: niche fast grower / high-quality stalwart. Iodine raw-material price cycles give it mild commodity exposure. P/E on ~Rs 10,900 crore market cap vs ~Rs 235 crore PAT ≈ 46x — rich, PEG ~2 if 20% growth holds. Insider buying: data not found.

## TAM
Global contrast media market estimated at ~$5-6 billion, growing mid-to-high single digits (period/source: industry estimates — exact figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Contrast media agents (iodinated/barium) — specialty niche with 40%+ margins and limited global competition.
2. Pharmaceutical intermediates and APIs — capacity-driven volume growth (Q4 FY25 beat came from these lines).
3. Backward integration into iodine derivatives — protects margin from raw-material swings.

## Red flags
- Iodine price volatility squeezes margins in bad years.
- Stock down ~13.5% over past year — de-rating despite good results.
- Valuation near 46x earnings leaves no room for margin misses.

## Verdict
Elite niche compounder — buy when the P/E compresses toward growth.
