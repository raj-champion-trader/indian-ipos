# Kusumgar Limited (KUSUMGAR)

## What it does
Kusumgar weaves, coats, and laminates technical fabrics from nylon and polyester — think parachute cloth, military stealth material, and gear for outdoor and industrial uses. It has developed over 100 fabric types since 1990 and serves aerospace, defence, automotive, and lifestyle brands.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 475 cr; FY25 Rs 790 cr; FY26 Rs 712 cr (per IPO documents / Screener).
- PAT: FY24 Rs 84 cr; FY25 Rs 112 cr; FY26 Rs 98 cr.
- OPM: 27% (FY24), 23% (FY25), 25% (FY26) — far above typical textile mills.
- 5-year sales CAGR 29%, profit CAGR 37%.
- Borrowings: Rs 118 cr (FY24) → Rs 301 cr (FY25) → Rs 290 cr (FY26).
- Stock P/E ~47x; market cap ~Rs 5,912 cr. IPO (2026) a pure Rs 650 cr offer-for-sale.

## Lynch lens
Niche dominance meets rich price. Technical textiles for defence and aerospace earn 25% margins where commodity garment makers earn 8% — a genuine Lynch-style boring-business moat. But FY26 revenue slipped ~10% and the P/E sits at 47x for a mid-cap fabric maker; the market has already paid for the moat. A pure OFS IPO also means promoters cashing out, not funding growth.

1. Defence/aerospace order flow (parachute systems, stealth fabrics).
2. Nylon/polyester yarn prices — key raw material.

## Red flags

## Verdict
Great fabrics, expensive stock — wait for a boring-price entry into this niche business.
