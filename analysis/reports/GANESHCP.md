# Ganesh Consumer Products Limited (GANESHCP)

## What it does
Ganesh Consumer Products makes packaged food for Indian kitchens — wheat flour (atta), maida, sooji, dalia, besan and sattu — under the Ganesh brand, strongest in East India. It sells through grocery shops and distributors. Recent IPO (2025), Kolkata-headquartered.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 759 crore (FY24); Rs 850 crore (FY25) — ~12% growth.
- PAT: Rs 27 crore (FY24); Rs 35 crore (FY25) — ~30% growth.
- OPM: 8% (FY24) → 9% (FY25); TTM OPM 10%, TTM PAT Rs 45 crore.
- ROE 14%, ROCE 19.4%; nearly debt-free; dividend payout 42% (FY25).
- Expense heads: raw material (wheat/grain) cost dominates an ~9% OPM business; exact split data not found.

## Lynch lens
Slow-and-steady fast grower in the making. Boring staples business — grinding wheat into flour — with regional dominance: 3rd-largest packaged atta brand in East India (FY25), largest wheat derivatives player in East India, 43.4% share in packaged sattu. Boring, regional, cash-generative, near debt-free, pays dividends — very Lynch. Watch whether it compounds outside East India.

## TAM
India packaged staples/atta market runs into thousands of crores (large and under-penetrated in branded formats); precise TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Wheat procurement cost vs atta realisation (gross margin on grain).
2. Volume growth and premiumisation in East India core markets.
3. Distribution expansion into new states and export channels.

## Red flags
- Thin OPM (~9%) — commodity grain price spikes squeeze profit.
- Regional concentration (East India).
- Competition from national giants (ITC, Patanjali, Fortune).

## Verdict
Boring regional staples compounder at reasonable economics — the kind Lynch liked; worth accumulating on dips.
