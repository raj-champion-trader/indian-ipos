# Inox India Ltd (INOXINDIA)

## What it does
Company makes cryogenic equipment — vacuum-insulated tanks and transport tankers (1,000 litres to 1 million litres) that store and move liquefied gases like oxygen, nitrogen, LNG and hydrogen at very low temperatures. Customers include industrial gas companies, hospitals, space and defence programs, and EPC contractors. Listed via IPO in December 2023; part of the INOX group.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: Rs 1,354 crore; PAT Rs 224 crore (+15.4% YoY).
- Segment mix FY25: industrial & medical gases bulk tanks ~61% of revenue; Cryo Scientific ~17% (Rs 228 crore).
- Q3 FY26: revenue Rs 436 crore (+27.4% YoY); adjusted PAT Rs 68 crore (+32.4%).
- Net margin ~16.5% (FY25); company historically runs debt-light with strong return ratios (exact ROE/DE figures: data not found in searched sources).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with a niche moat — India's largest cryogenic tank maker, a boring, technical product with few competitors and sticky customers (first Indian firm with IATF 16949 certification for cryogenic fuel tanks). Green-energy angle (LNG truck fuel tanks, hydrogen storage, space program) gives optionality. Steady mid-teens profit growth, low drama. P/E and insider activity: data not found.

## TAM
Global cryogenic equipment market in the multi-billion-dollar range and growing on LNG, hydrogen, space and medical-gas demand (exact TAM figure: data not found in searched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Bulk cryogenic storage tanks and tankers for industrial/medical gas customers (~61% of revenue, FY25).
2. Cryo Scientific division — LNG/hydrogen fuel tanks, space and turnkey projects (~17%).
3. Export growth and green-fuel (LNG/H2) mobility programs.

## Red flags
- Order-book lumpiness in turnkey/scientific projects.
- Government space/defence program timing affects Cryo Scientific.
- Cyclical capex of industrial gas majors.

## Verdict
Classic Lynch stalwart — dominant niche, boring product, clean growth; accumulate on dips.
