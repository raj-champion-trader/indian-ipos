# Plaza Wires Ltd (PLAZACABLE)

## What it does
Company makes electrical wires and cables, and sells fast-moving electrical goods — fans, water heaters, switches — under Plaza Cables, Action Wires and PCG brands. Sells to electricians, dealers and households across India.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 199 Cr (FY24), Rs 218 Cr (FY25), Rs 318 Cr (FY26) — FY26 jump from FMEG push; TTM Rs 343 Cr.
- Net profit: Rs 4 Cr (FY24), Rs 3 Cr (FY25), Rs 7 Cr (FY26); TTM Rs 11 Cr.
- ROE 5.8%; P/E 22; borrowings Rs 32 Cr (Mar 2026); interest ~Rs 4 Cr TTM.
- Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026): sales Rs 90 Cr, PAT Rs 4.5 Cr, OPM 8.7% — improving.

## Lynch lens
Boring business in a dull industry — the good news. Wires and FMEG sell year after year with housing and electrification. But Plaza sits in a brutally fragmented market ruled by Polycab, Havells and anchors of scale; ROE under 6% shows the brand lacks pricing muscle. Small-cap FMEG expansion (fans, geysers, switches) gives a fast-grower option at Rs 236 Cr cap. P/E 22 vs TTM PAT Rs 11 Cr with margins rising — borderline; growth must continue to justify.

## TAM
India wires & cables + FMEG market — data not found (industry large,Polycab-led; exact size not verified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Wires & cables volumes (core business, copper-price linked).
2. New FMEG products (fans, water heaters, switches) margin ramp.
3. Copper/aluminium/PVC input costs (raw materials dominate expenses).

## Red flags
- Thin margins: 3-yr ROE ~4.1%.
- Working capital and competition against giant brands.
- Small scale (TTM revenue Rs 343 Cr).

## Verdict
Honest small-cap wire maker turning the corner — track FMEG margin proof before buying.
