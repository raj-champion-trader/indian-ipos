# Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited (LALITHAA)

## What it does
Lalithaa sells gold, silver, and diamond jewellery through 61 stores across South India, mostly in smaller towns. It courts value-conscious shoppers with low markup, fixed-price gold and customer savings schemes.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 16,801 cr; FY25 Rs 16,908 cr; FY26 Rs 25,040 cr (consolidated, IPO documents).
- PAT: FY24 Rs 360 cr; FY25 Rs 365 cr; FY26 Rs 1,010 cr.
- PAT margin: ~2.1% (FY24, FY25) → 4.0% (FY26).
- Revenue per store Rs 410 cr (FY26) — highest among organised peers.
- Customer advances Rs 5,043 cr (FY26); borrowings Rs 2,045 cr (Mar 2026).
- ROE 40.2%, P/E ~14.7. IPO objects: Rs 1,033 cr to open 10 new stores.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart retailer in a low-margin, high-velocity trade. Two straight flat years (FY24-FY25) then a FY26 explosion — PAT nearly tripled as revenue jumped 48%, showing operating leverage on store scale. At ~15x earnings with 40% ROE, it prices cheaper than big-city peers; the mass-market, Tier-II/III niche (45 of 61 stores, 60% of revenue) gives the Lynch "dull but dominant corner" feel. Jewellery remains cyclical with gold prices though.

## TAM
India organised jewellery retail market size: data not found in sources fetched (company operates in the larger India gems & jewellery market).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gold price levels (inventory gains/losses drive the FY26 PAT jump).
2. New store openings and same-store revenue per store.
3. Customer advance schemes (Rs 5,043 cr float, financing the working capital).

## Red flags
2% net margin leaves little room for error; huge gold-price exposure; Rs 5,043 cr customer advances invite regulatory scrutiny (gold-scheme rules); Rs 2,045 cr borrowings; FY26 profit surge may not repeat if gold plateaus.

## Verdict
A high-turnover, low-margin gold retailer at a fair price — decent, if gold cooperates.
