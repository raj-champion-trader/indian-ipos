# Netweb Technologies India Limited (NETWEB)

## What it does
Company builds high-end computing systems — AI servers and workstations, supercomputing (HPC) clusters, enterprise servers and storage — for research labs, government and large Indian companies. It partners with NVIDIA, AMD and Intel. Listed July 2023.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue from operations Rs 1,149 crore, up 58.7% YoY; FY25 PAT Rs 114.5 crore, up 50.8% YoY (IIFL result review).
- Q4 FY25: revenue Rs 414.7 crore (+55.9% YoY), PAT Rs 43 crore (+45% YoY), EBITDA margin 14.4%.
- AI systems vertical: Rs 169.4 crore in FY25, +112% YoY (~14.8% of revenue); by Q1 FY27 AI systems hit Rs 510.5 crore, 62.3% of quarterly revenue.
- Q1 FY27 record quarterly revenue Rs 819.7 crore, up 172% YoY.
- IIFL forecasts ~42% sales / ~44% PAT CAGR over FY25-28.
- Expense heads: hardware/components procurement dominates cost base (trading-like margins ~10-14% EBITDA); exact percentages data not found.

## Lynch lens
India's closest listed pure-play on the AI server buildout — a genuine fast grower (58.7% revenue growth FY25, accelerating). But the business resembles high-end systems integration: margins stay modest (14% EBITDA) and growth depends on GPU supply and government/enterprise IT budgets — a hot-sector premium with execution risk. P/E: data not found; PEG not computable.

## TAM
India AI servers/HPC market projected in the billions of dollars as data-centre and GPU capacity scales; global AI infrastructure spend in the hundreds of billions (source: industry/brokerage estimates — exact figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. AI systems and GPU servers — fastest-growing line, now the dominant revenue share.
2. HPC/supercomputing clusters — government and defence research orders.
3. Enterprise servers, storage and services — steady base margin business.

## Red flags
- Thin margins for the growth rate — profit rides component costs and GPU allocation.
- Customer concentration in government/institutional tenders.
- Valuation likely prices in years of hypergrowth already.

## Verdict
Real AI fast grower — great story, but buy the dip, not the hype.
