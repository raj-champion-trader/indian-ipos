# ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited (ESAFSFB)

## What it does
ESAF Small Finance Bank lends small amounts to rural and low-income customers, with microfinance loans as its historic core and a growing gold-loan book. It also takes deposits and offers standard banking products.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,854 crore FY23; Rs 3,819 crore FY24; Rs 3,858 crore FY25 (screener).
- Net profit: Rs 302 crore FY23; Rs 426 crore FY24; net loss Rs 521 crore FY25 (screener; other reports cite ~Rs 544 crore loss).
- GNPA 5.40%, NNPA 0.83% (Mar 2026, after peaking at 7.48%/3.77%); ROE -8.86%; book value Rs 34.6; market cap ~Rs 2,307 crore at Rs 44.7/share.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround. The FY25 loss came from the sector-wide microfinance credit stress plus elevated provisions. Recovery signs exist: gold loans +106% YoY, retail back to profit, Q1 FY-next PAT ~Rs 80 crore. Banking maths: price of ~Rs 45 vs book Rs 34.6 (~1.3x book) — cheap for a bank only if asset quality holds. Not a fast grower until credit costs normalise.

## TAM
Indian microfinance / SFB addressable market: industry MFI gross loan portfolio ~Rs 4–5 lakh crore (FY25 period, industry estimates); gold loan market ~Rs 6+ lakh crore. Exact sourced figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Credit costs / provisioning on the microfinance book (the whole FY25 loss story).
2. Net interest margin on the lending book (gold + microfinance mix shift).
3. Deposit cost and branch overhead ratio as the bank restructures.

## Red flags
Two loss-making / crisis years; microfinance book still healing; repeated capital-raising needs; regulatory scrutiny history on small finance banks.

## Verdict
A deep-value turnaround ticket priced near book — buy only with conviction that the worst provisions sit behind it.
