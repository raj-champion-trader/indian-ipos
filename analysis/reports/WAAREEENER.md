# Waaree Energies Ltd (WAAREEENER)

## What it does
Waaree makes solar PV modules at multi-GW scale, builds solar plants, and runs a growing US business (Waaree Solar Americas). India's largest module maker by capacity; listed October 2024.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Sales: FY23 6,751; FY24 11,398; FY25 14,444; FY26 26,537 (FY26 +84% YoY)
- Net profit: FY23 500; FY24 1,274; FY25 1,928; FY26 3,884 (net margin ~14.6% FY26)
- ROE 32.8%, ROCE 38.8%; P/E ~19 at price Rs 2,640; market cap Rs 75,940 cr
- Borrowings rising with expansion: FY25 1,199 → FY26 3,213; free cash flow negative FY26 (-3,209) on heavy capex
- 5.4 GW+ module capacity going to ~11 GW with ingot-wafer-cell backward integration (company statements)

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — sales up ~4x in three years, profits up ~7x, ROE above 30%. Lynch would like the backward integration into cells/wafers (margin control) and the US business (second engine). P/E ~19 on this growth gives a PEG well under 0.5 — the market already de-rated the stock from Rs 3,865 highs on solar tariff fears.

## TAM
India solar module demand tied to the ~500 GW renewables-by-2030 target; US market adds a second large TAM (US utility-scale solar, tens of GW/year). Global solar equipment market runs into hundreds of billions of dollars (industry estimates).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Module volumes and prices in India (ALPM-protected domestic market) — core profit pool
2. US module sales (higher realisations, tariff-advantaged) via Waaree Solar Americas
3. Backward integration (cells, wafers) that lifts margins; raw materials (cells, glass, EVA) historically ~70%+ of revenue per DRHP

## Red flags
- US trade/tariff policy can whack the Americas business overnight
- Negative FY26 free cash flow and rising debt from aggressive capex
- Customer concentration with large developers; solar pricing cycles (FY21 OPM was under 5%)

## Verdict
India's solar champion compounding fast at a mid-teens P/E — a genuine fast grower, sized down for policy risk.
