# Azad Engineering Limited (AZAD)

## What it does
Azad Engineering machines tiny, super-precise metal parts — blades, nozzles and fasteners — for aeroplanes, missiles, gas turbines and nuclear plants. It sells mostly to global aerospace and energy giants, exporting to 12 countries with a zero-defect delivery record.

## The numbers
- FY25 consolidated revenue: ~Rs 590-638 crore (Screener shows Rs 638 crore; some analyses cite ~Rs 590 crore), up sharply from ~Rs 440 crore in FY24.
- FY25 PAT: ~Rs 139 crore (per Screener); Q1 FY25 revenue jumped ~134% YoY to Rs 122.9 crore with net profit up 448%.
- Order book: ~Rs 6,000-6,080 crore as of mid-2025 (~3,500 crore energy, ~1,720 crore+ others) — about 10x annual revenue.
- Aerospace & defence: ~19% of revenue in Q1 FY25 (up from ~9% in FY23); exports ~90%+ of revenue; supplies six key aerospace & defence OEMs; 3 million+ parts delivered at zero PPM defects.
- Expense heads detail: data not found; key costs: exotic super-alloy raw materials and skilled machining labour.
- Valuation context: stock traded around 100x trailing earnings at one point in 2026 coverage.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a niche fortress — mission-critical, zero-defect precision parts that only a handful of firms worldwide can machine, certified into decades-long customer relationships. Classic Lynch "boring-to-glamour" trap risk: at ~100x trailing P/E, the market already pays for perfection. Any execution slip or de-rating hurts badly despite great fundamentals.

## TAM
Global aerospace & defence and gas-turbine precision-component supply chains — exact TAM data not found; India's defence-localisation push adds a domestic tailwind.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Aerospace & defence revenue ramp (~19% of Q1 FY25 revenue, growing ~26% YoY in later quarters) with richer margins.
2. Order-book execution (~Rs 6,000 crore, ~10x revenue) across energy turbines and defence.
3. Design-led manufacturing (DLM) growth — drove the 448% Q1 FY25 profit jump.

## Red flags
- Extreme valuation (~100x trailing earnings per 2026 coverage) — priced for perfection.
- Customer concentration: a handful of global OEMs dominate revenue.
- Small revenue base vs order book — execution and working-capital strain risk.

## Verdict
Wonderful niche business, scary price — Lynch would admire the moat and refuse to pay 100x for it.
