# Indo-MIM Ltd (INDOMIM)

## What it does
Indo-MIM makes small, complex metal parts using metal injection moulding (MIM) — a process that moulds metal powder like plastic to produce precision components at scale. It sells these parts to industries such as firearms, automotive, aerospace, consumer electronics and healthcare. One of the world's largest MIM producers.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 2,317 crore (FY23), Rs 2,412 crore (FY24), Rs 2,795 crore (FY25) — standalone, per screener.in; TTM growth 27%
- Net profit: Rs 416 crore (FY23), Rs 288 crore (FY24), Rs 404 crore (FY25); TTM profit growth 29%
- Operating margin: 31% (FY23), 28% (FY24), 31% (FY25) — strong for a manufacturer
- Finance cost: Rs 51 crore (FY23), Rs 78 crore (FY24), Rs 87 crore (FY25)
- Borrowings: Rs 1,266 crore (Mar 2025)
- ROE 34.5%, ROCE 32.2%
- Market cap Rs 42,923 crore; P/E 64.8
