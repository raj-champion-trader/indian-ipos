# Muthoot Microfin (MUTHOOTMF)

## What it does
Muthoot Microfin gives small loans, mostly to women in villages, so they can run tiny businesses — cattle, shops, tailoring. Groups of women guarantee each other's loans. It belongs to the Muthoot Pappachan Group and ranks among India's largest NBFC-MFIs (2nd by gross loan portfolio in FY23).

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 2,249 cr; FY25 Rs 2,563 cr; FY26 Rs 2,375 cr — screener.in
- PAT: FY24 Rs 450 cr; FY25 minus Rs 223 cr (loss); FY26 Rs 170 cr
- Financing margin: FY24 27% → FY25 negative → FY26 10% (TTM 14%)
- Q1 FY27 (company IR): AUM Rs 14,457 cr (+18% YoY); PAT Rs 81.3 cr (12x YoY); GNPA 3.70% (down 115 bps); collection efficiency 97.97%; disbursements Rs 2,645 cr (record Q1); credit cost 2.6%
- Strong regional share: ~16% of Tamil Nadu MFI market; largest in Kerala

## Lynch lens
Turnaround in progress. FY25's loss came from the industry-wide microfinance repayment crisis (Karnataka/assam-style stress plus regulatory tightening on pricing and collections). Q1 FY27's 12x profit jump, 98% collections and falling GNPA show the classic Lynch turnaround pattern: bad year priced in, operations repairing. If normalised earnings (FY24's Rs 450 cr) return, the stock re-rates hard. Confirmation still partial — one strong quarter.

## TAM
Indian microfinance — the industry's gross loan portfolio runs into lakhs of crores serving tens of millions of women borrowers (precise current TAM figure not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Net interest income on the Rs 14,000+ cr women's joint-liability loan book — the core engine
2. Credit costs / provisions — the single factor that flipped FY24 profit to FY25 loss and back
3. Cost of borrowings (bank-led funding lines) — margin lever as rates fall

## Red flags
- FY25 loss of Rs 223 cr proves the model's fragility to collection shocks
- Regulatory intervention in MFI pricing/collection practices
- Heavy south-India concentration (Karnataka events have hurt before)
- Thin capitalisation vs 2.6% credit costs

## Verdict
Textbook turnaround candidate — buy small, add as quarterly proof stacks up.
