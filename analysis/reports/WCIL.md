# Western Carriers (India) Ltd (WCIL)

## What it does
Western Carriers moves freight by rail, road and river — a multi-modal, asset-light logistics company. It runs 4PL supply-chain solutions for large industrial customers: freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing and port handling, using containers and trains rather than owning trucks heavily. Clients include coal, steel and FMCG majors.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 1,686 cr (FY24), Rs 1,726 cr (FY25), Rs 1,829 cr (FY26) — slow ~5% growth.
- Net profit: Rs 80 cr (FY24), Rs 65 cr (FY25), Rs 39 cr (FY26) — falling hard.
- OPM: 9% (FY24) down to 4.7% (FY26). ROCE down from 26% (FY21) to 7% (FY26).
- Stock P/E ~24 despite falling earnings; book value Rs 85/share, price Rs 86.

## Revenue streams
Multi-modal freight (rail-focused container movement) plus road and river transport; customs house agency, warehousing/C&F add fees. Exact FY segment split — data not found on free screener.

## Expenses
Freight/hire charges (rail and road) the biggest head (detail: data not found). FY26: interest Rs 18 cr, depreciation Rs 28 cr (rising after terminal investments).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart gone sour — a dull logistics business Lynch normally likes (boring, essential, 50 years old), but earnings declined 3 of the last 3 years. OPM halved as rail costs rose and clients negotiated hard. At 24x falling earnings it looks expensive; at ~1x book it looks like a potential turnaround if rail margins recover. Promoter raised stake to 73% — a mild positive.

## TAM
Indian logistics market ~USD 200+ bn (FY24 estimates); rail freight and container segments growing with dedicated freight corridors.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Rail freight cost pass-through and margin per TEU (OPM 9% to 4.7% FY24-FY26 crushed profit).
2. New cargo-terminal capex (Kolkata terminal approved Aug 2026) — depreciation drag now, profit later.
3. Working capital cycle — 139 debtor days ties up cash.

## Red flags
Profit falling for three straight years. No dividend. FIIs fully exited (4.2% to 0%). Operating cash flow weak (-22 cr FY26). Fixed assets tripled (Rs 71 to 206 cr FY19-FY26) while returns collapsed.

## Verdict
Boring-business turnaround bet at 1x book — wait for OPM to stop falling before buying a turnaround without a trigger.
