# Shreeji Shipping Global Limited (SHREEJISPG)

## What it does
Shreeji Shipping Global moves dry bulk cargo — coal, grain, fertilizer-type commodities — by sea for clients at ports in India and Sri Lanka, focusing on non-major ports and jetties on India's west coast. It earns freight by shipping and port-side logistics. The company listed in August 2025 via a Rs 411 crore fresh-issue IPO.

## The numbers
- Standalone (screener): sales Rs 731 crore FY24; Rs 584 crore FY25; Rs 705 crore FY26.
- Net profit: Rs 125 crore FY24; Rs 144 crore FY25; Rs 156 crore FY26 (IPO documents cite PAT Rs 118.9 crore FY23 → Rs 124.5 crore FY24 → Rs 141.2 crore FY25).
- OPM 26–33%; ROE ~30%; ROCE ~28%; market cap ~Rs 10,630 crore; P/E ~65; price/book ~13.8x at Rs 652.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical dressed as a fast grower. Shipping freights swing with commodity trade, and FY25 revenue fell ~17% even as volumes rose 14% (15.7 MMT) — classic freight-rate sensitivity, not volume weakness. ROE ~30% impresses, but 13.8x book for an asset-heavy shipping firm signals cycle-peak pricing. Boring-and-niche angle: west-coast non-major-port logistics gives some moat via jetty relationships.

## TAM
Indian dry bulk coastal and short-sea shipping market: multi-million-tonne annual trade at west-coast ports (company DRHP anchors on west-coast bulk volumes); exact sourced TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Freight/charter rates on dry bulk routes (the single biggest profit swing).
2. Vessel utilisation and fleet size (IPO proceeds fund fleet expansion).
3. Fuel (bunker) costs and port/jetty operating costs.

## Red flags
P/E ~65 and 13.8x book at a cyclical high; one-year stock return +158%; freight-rate downturn would compress both; short listed history.

## Verdict
Well-run niche shipper at cycle-euphoria pricing — Lynch would admire the ROE and refuse the multiple.
