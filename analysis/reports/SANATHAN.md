# Sanathan Textiles Ltd (SANATHAN)

## What it does
Sanathan makes yarn — polyester filament yarn, cotton yarn, and technical-textile yarn — at large factories in India. It sells this yarn to weavers and fabric makers who turn it into clothes, home furnishings, and industrial products. It listed on the NSE in December 2024.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,957 crore (FY24) → Rs 2,997 crore (FY25), flat. 5-year sales CAGR ~10%.
- PAT: Rs 141 crore (FY24) → Rs 174 crore (FY25); TTM profit growth ~24%.
- Expenses (FY25): total Rs 2,725 crore (~91% of revenue); finance cost Rs 25 crore; raw-material share (PET/cotton chips dominates cost, exact % data not found).
- Borrowings cut to Rs 129 crore (Mar 2025). ROE ~10.5%. Market cap Rs 3,782 crore; P/E ~18 (Aug 2026).

## Lynch lens
Cyclical. Yarn prices move with crude (PET feedstock) and cotton cycles, so earnings swing even when volumes grow. Debt reduction post-IPO and profit growth despite flat sales show margin recovery — the classic early-upcycle signal. Promoters hold 78.6%; no insider buying data found. P/E 18 with modest growth = no obvious bargain, no obvious trap.

## TAM
Indian polyester and technical textiles industry: India's technical textiles market ~USD 25-40 billion estimates exist in government/industry reports (exact figure data not found in fetched sources); company sells largely domestically plus exports.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Raw material prices (PX/PTA, cotton) — pass-through speed decides margin.
2. Capacity utilisation and specialty/technical-yarn mix (higher margin).
3. Finance costs — down from deleveraging post-IPO.

## Red flags
- Commodity business: pricing power near zero.
- Flat FY25 revenue; growth depends on cycle.
- Related-party/exports concentration data not found.

## Verdict
A leveraged play on the yarn cycle turning — cheap-ish, but only for investors who can track PET spreads.
