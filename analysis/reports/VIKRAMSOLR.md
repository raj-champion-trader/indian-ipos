# Vikram Solar Ltd (VIKRAMSOLR)

## What it does
Vikram Solar makes solar photovoltaic modules in India and also builds solar plants as an EPC contractor. Customers include NTPC, Adani Green and Tata Power. It listed in September 2025 after a large IPO.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Sales: FY24 2,511; FY25 3,415; FY26 4,803 (+41% YoY)
- Net profit: FY24 80; FY25 139; FY26 469 (FY26 profit jumped ~3.4x)
- ROE 21.4%, ROCE 30.6%; P/E ~17 at price Rs 173; market cap Rs 6,258 cr
- Borrowings modest: FY26 617. Operating cash flow positive (FY26: 667)
- Capacity expanding from ~4.5 GW toward 6 GW+ of modules with new cell lines

## Lynch lens
Fast grower riding India's solar build-out. FY26 numbers (sales +41%, PAT up 3.4x) fit the Lynch template of a 20%+ grower in a boring-to-glamour spectrum industry. P/E ~17 against that growth implies a PEG under 0.5 — attractive, if the growth holds. The catch: module makers earn cyclical, policy-dependent margins, so treat it as a fast grower with cyclical genes.

## TAM
India targets ~500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030 (per government statements) with solar the biggest slice; module demand runs into tens of GW a year. Global module market exceeds US$100 bn annually (industry estimates).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Module sales volume and utilisation of expanded TOPCon capacity
2. Module selling prices versus cell/raw material costs ( Imported cells and materials dominate costs; raw materials run roughly 60-70% of revenue per DRHP)
3. Entry into cell manufacturing and solar developer (IPP) business for higher-margin revenue

## Red flags
- Stock fell from Rs 408 listing-area high to ~Rs 173 — de-rating despite good FY26
- FY22 loss (PAT -63) shows how fast module economics can turn
- Competition from Chinese module pricing and ALMM policy shifts; interest cost Rs 160 cr FY26 rising with capex

## Verdict
Fast grower at a growth-stock price that already corrected — worth a watchlist spot if FY27 keeps the profit momentum.
