# Park Medi World Ltd — Park Hospitals (PARKHOSPS)

## What it does
Park Hospitals runs a chain of multi-specialty hospitals across North India — about 14-17 NABH-accredited hospitals with 4,000+ beds in Haryana, Delhi, Punjab, Rajasthan, UP and Uttarakhand. It earns from surgeries, inpatient stays, outpatient consults, diagnostics and pharmacy sales, led by cardiac, neuro, renal and cancer care.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 92 crore FY25 vs Rs 90 crore FY24 (standalone, screener.in; FY26 Rs 129 crore).
- PAT: Rs 7 crore FY25 vs Rs 8 crore FY24 (FY26 jumped to Rs 37 crore).
- OPM 26% FY25; FY26 borrowings down to Rs 19 crore from Rs 158 crore Mar 2025 — post-IPO deleveraging.
- P/E about 383 at market cap Rs 12,550 crore — extreme.

## The numbers caveat: consolidated figures (the chain's full revenue across hospitals) differ from the standalone numbers above; consolidated mix data not found.

## Lynch lens
A regional hospital stalwart — steady demand, pricing power in tertiary care, and post-IPO the balance sheet got cleaned (debt down Rs 158 crore to Rs 19 crore). But the market cap prices years of bed-ramp perfection at P/E ~383. Good business, silly price. Lynch would wait for the growth to catch up.

## TAM
North India tertiary healthcare: India's private hospital market runs into lakhs of crore (industry estimates, FY25); exact North India TAM data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Inpatient surgeries and occupancy (cardiac, neuro, renal, oncology specialties).
2. Doctor-led case mix shift toward high-ARPOB procedures.
3. New-hospital ramp costs and interest/depreciation from expansion (FY25 depressed PAT).

## Red flags
- Standalone P/E near 383 — valuation leaves no margin of safety.
- PAT lumpy (Rs 7 crore FY25 to Rs 37 crore FY26) — hard to value reliably.
- Promoter/consolidation structure complexity across hospital entities.

## Verdict
Solid regional hospital chain priced for perfection — admire, do not buy at these levels.
