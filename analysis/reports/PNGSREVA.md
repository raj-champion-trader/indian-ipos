# PNGS Reva Diamond Jewellery Ltd (PNGSREVA)

## What it does
PNGS Reva sells diamond and gemstone-studded gold and platinum jewellery under the Reva brand through retail stores and online. It makes its money on the spread between jewellery retail prices and the cost of gold, diamonds and stones.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 64 crore FY25 vs Rs 196 crore FY24 — a steep fall (-67%).
- PAT: Rs 7 crore FY25 vs Rs 42 crore FY24. OPM fell from 29% (FY24) to 15% (FY25).
- Borrowings jumped from Rs 0 (Mar 2024) to Rs 91 crore (Mar 2025).

## The numbers caveat: revenue collapse may reflect changed accounting (net vs gross recognition) or loss of wholesale/B2B volumes — reason data not found.

## Lynch lens
Jewellery retail can be a decent consumer franchise, but this one just shrank two-thirds in a year and took on Rs 91 crore of fresh debt. That smells like either an accounting change or genuine business trouble — both demand answers before any money goes in. Cyclical (gold-price linked) and unproven at current scale. Not investable on available data.

## TAM
India branded jewellery market: roughly USD 80-100 billion industry overall, with organised/gems-studded jewellery growing faster than the market (industry estimates FY25); exact studded-jewellery TAM data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Studded-jewellery retail margins (making charges on diamonds/gemstones).
2. Gold and diamond price swings on inventory.
3. Interest cost on the new Rs 91 crore FY25 debt load vs shrunken revenue base.

## Red flags
- Revenue down 67% FY25 with no clear explanation found.
- New debt (Rs 91 crore FY25) against Rs 7 crore profit.
- Small scale, single brand, competitive market vs Titan and regional giants.

## Verdict
Collapsing revenue plus rising debt — avoid until the FY25 drop gets explained.
