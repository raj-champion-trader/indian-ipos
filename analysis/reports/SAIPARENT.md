# Sai Parenterals Ltd (SAIPARENT)

## What it does
Company makes pharma formulations — injectables (parenterals), general orals, and cephalosporin orals/injectables. Sells in India and abroad, including an OTC supply deal with a large Australian pharmacy chain through subsidiary Noumed.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 97 Cr; FY24 Rs 114 Cr; FY25 Rs 127 Cr; FY26 Rs 166 Cr (standalone, screener). 3-yr sales CAGR ~20%.
- PAT: FY23 Rs 5 Cr; FY24 Rs 7 Cr; FY25 Rs 10 Cr; FY26 Rs 17 Cr. 3-yr profit CAGR ~55%.
- OPM: 18-21% (FY23-FY25), 17% (FY26). ROE only ~5.8%, ROCE ~7% (FY26).
- Debt rising: borrowings Rs 69 Cr (FY23) to Rs 133 Cr (FY26); interest Rs 9 Cr.
- Market cap Rs 2,303 Cr; P/E ~93.

## Lynch lens
Growth stock priced like a superstar: P/E ~93 against 20% revenue growth and single-digit ROE. Sales grow but returns on capital stay poor — capital-hungry expansion funded by doubling debt. Lynch would pass: good story, wrong price, and low ROE means growth consumes cash rather than minting it.

## TAM
Indian formulations market ~US$ 25-30 bn; global injectables/parenterals segment growing mid-to-high single digits (industry estimates). Company-specific TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Domestic formulations volume growth.
2. Noumed Australia OTC contract — renewed 3-year deal worth AUD 30 mn (~Rs 204 Cr) — export revenue kicker.
3. Interest costs (Rs 5→9 Cr, FY23→FY26) dragging an otherwise improving operating line.

## Red flags
- P/E ~93 with ROE ~6% — valuation disconnected from capital returns.
- Debt nearly doubled FY23→FY26 while profits grew modestly.
- Margin dip in FY26 (OPM 17%) despite scale.

## Verdict
Overpriced pharma story — wait for either earnings catch-up or a valuation reset.
