# Akme Fintrade (India) Ltd (AFIL)

## What it does
Akme Fintrade lends money. This RBI-registered NBFC finances commercial vehicles and gives MSME/business loans, mainly to rural and semi-urban customers in Rajasthan and nearby states who banks avoid. It serves over 2.25 lakh customers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 73 crore (FY25), Rs 159 crore (FY26, standalone) — nearly doubled as a weak FY24 base (Rs 68 crore) recovered.
- Net profit: Rs 15 crore (FY25), Rs 44 crore (FY26). Financing margin ~36% (FY26).
- Market cap ~Rs 413 crore; P/E ~9.3; trades around 0.95x book (Aug 2026).
- ROE low: ~10% over 3 years. Promoter holding ~39% and slipping.
- Interest expense Rs 63 crore vs revenue Rs 159 crore (FY26) — borrowings eat ~40% of income.

## Lynch lens
A cheap small-cap financial (P/E ~9, ~book value) — Lynch rarely favored tiny NBFCs because credit quality hides in the loan book until it bites. Growth recovery from FY24's washout gives it a mild turnaround flavor, but low ROE and thin interest coverage make it a low-quality turnaround at best.

## TAM
Data not found (rural vehicle-finance and MSME lending TAM not reliably published).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Vehicle financing book (largest revenue stream; AUM data behind screener paywall).
2. SME/business (LAP) loans — higher-yield book driving the FY26 revenue jump.
3. Cost of funds — interest expense of Rs 63 crore (FY26) is the single biggest drag on profit.

## Red flags
- Low interest coverage ratio (flagged by screener).
- Promoter holding low (~39%) and fell again last quarter.
- ROE ~10%, below cost of equity for an NBFC.
- Working capital days rose from ~67 to ~95.
- Lumpy earnings: PAT collapsed to Rs 4 crore in FY24 before rebounding.

## Verdict
Statistically cheap NBFC with shaky economics — a lottery ticket on rural credit cycles, not a Lynch-style buy.
