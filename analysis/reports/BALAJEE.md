# Shree Tirupati Balajee Agro Trading Co. Ltd (BALAJEE)

## What it does
Company makes FIBC bulk bags (big industrial sacks) plus woven sacks, fabric and tapes. Sells packaging to agro and industrial customers. Simple polypropylene weaving — capital-hungry, commodity-like.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 359 cr (FY24), Rs 358 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 384 cr — flat
- Net profit: Rs 17 cr (FY24), Rs 15 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 8 cr — halving
- OPM: 9% (FY24), 9% (FY25), 5% (TTM)
- Debt: Rs 142 cr (FY24), Rs 109 cr (FY25) — coming down
- Interest: Rs 12 cr (~3.4% of sales, FY25); ROE 3% (FY25); market cap ~Rs 216 cr

## Lynch lens
Cyclical commodity converter at the wrong point in the cycle — profit falling, margins thin. ROE of 3% barely covers cost of capital. Debt reduction helps, but a 5% OPM packaging business earns no premium. No PEG case: no growth, shrinking earnings.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Polypropylene raw material prices vs FIBC selling prices — spread drives the 9%-to-5% OPM collapse
2. Export FIBC volumes (main product line)
3. Interest cost (Rs 12 cr FY25) — debt paydown releases profit

## Red flags
- TTM profit halved to Rs 8 cr
- ROE 3% — value destroyer at current spreads
- Flat revenue for two years

## Verdict
Cheap-looking sack maker destroying value at 3% ROE — avoid until spreads recover.
