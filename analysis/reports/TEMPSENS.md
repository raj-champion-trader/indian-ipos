# Tempsens Instruments (India) Ltd (TEMPSENS)

## What it does
Tempsens makes temperature sensors, electrical heaters and specialised mineral-insulated cables for factories, power plants and other industries. It claims the position of India's largest maker of contact and non-contact temperature sensors, with about 10.5% market share. Roughly 30% of revenue comes from exports, and customers include over 1,000 buyers across project/OEM and recurring MRO replacement business.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 240 crore FY23, Rs 278 crore FY24, Rs 382 crore FY25, Rs 456 crore FY26 (RHP figures; screener standalone shows Rs 367 crore FY25).
- PAT: Rs 32 crore FY23, Rs 41 crore FY24, Rs 63 crore FY25, Rs 71 crore FY26; PAT margin ~16% FY25/FY26.
- RoCE ~22-23% across FY24-FY26; 5-year profit CAGR ~29%.
- Borrowings rose to Rs 70 crore Mar 2025 (screener); IPO Aug 2026: Rs 650 crore (Rs 95 crore fresh + Rs 555 crore OFS), Rs 55 crore of fresh for debt repayment.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart/fast grower — a boring, niche industrial (thermocouples and heaters nobody talks about) growing revenue ~20%+ with mid-teens margins and 20%+ RoCE. Lynch loves niche leaders in dull products. IPO priced at ~35x FY26 EPS — PEG near 1.7 on 18% profit growth, not a bargain but not absurd. Well diversified: top 10 customers under 25% of revenue.

## TAM
Data not found in absolute terms; proxy — company holds 10.5% share of India contact & non-contact temperature sensor market (RHP, FY25 basis), implying an India market of roughly Rs 600-700 crore per year for its core sensor line plus heating and cable adjacencies.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Temperature sensing solutions (core, largest product line).
2. Electrical heating solutions (process heaters).
3. Specialised/mineral-insulated cables and export mix (~30% of revenue).

## Red flags
- Heavy OFS (Rs 555 crore of Rs 650 crore issue) — existing holders cashing out.
- Borrowings doubled to Rs 70 crore in FY25 before IPO.
- Single-country currency/concentration in industrial capex cycles.

## Verdict
A classic Lynch-style boring niche leader growing nicely — decent business, but you pay a full price at 35x.
