# Kronox Lab Sciences Limited (KRONOX)

## What it does
Company makes high-purity specialty fine chemicals used in medicines, biotech, agrochemicals and laboratory research. It sells mostly to export markets — the US, UK, Australia and Europe. Listed June 2024 via a Rs 130 crore offer-for-sale IPO.

## The numbers
- FY24 (annualised) revenue ~Rs 90.25 crore, PAT ~Rs 20.63 crore (9M FY24: revenue Rs 68.44 crore, PAT Rs 15.5 crore).
- FY21-FY23 revenue CAGR 23.7%; PAT CAGR ~30.7%.
- FY24 saw slight revenue degrowth in some reporting, with operating margins improving YoY.
- Market cap at IPO upper band ~Rs 505 crore.
- Expense heads: raw materials dominate for a fine-chemicals maker; exact percentages data not found.

## Lynch lens
Classic Lynch small-cap niche: purifying niche chemicals for pharma labs — dull, specialised, and 70%+ exported at presumably better realisations. Profit pool small (~Rs 20 crore) but margins fat (~23% PAT margin annualised FY24). Category: small fast grower. P/E: data not found; PEG not computable. Liquidity stays thin for a company this size.

## TAM
Global high-purity/specialty fine chemicals market serves pharma R&D and production — multi-billion dollar (exact TAM figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. High-purity fine chemicals for pharma/biotech — core margin engine.
2. Exports (70%+ of revenue) to US/UK/Europe/Australia — currency and pricing tailwind.
3. Operating leverage — margins improved even in a flat-revenue FY24.

## Red flags
- Very small revenue base (~Rs 90 crore) — a single client or product shift moves numbers a lot.
- FY24 revenue roughly flat — growth stalled post-IPO.
- Thin trading liquidity; promoter selling via OFS IPO.

## Verdict
Tiny high-margin niche exporter — watch for growth restart before committing.
