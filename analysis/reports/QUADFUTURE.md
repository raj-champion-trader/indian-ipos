# Quadrant Future Tek Ltd (QUADFUTURE)

## What it does
Company makes speciality cables (electron-beam irradiated) for railways, naval defence, renewables and EVs, and builds train control and signalling electronics — KAVACH anti-collision systems, electronic interlocking, digital axle counters. Sells to Indian Railways and defence buyers.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 151 Cr (FY24), Rs 150 Cr (FY25), Rs 153 Cr (FY26) — flat for three years.
- Net result: +Rs 12 Cr (FY24), -Rs 20 Cr (FY25), -Rs 43 Cr (FY26) — losses deepening.
- FY26: total expenses Rs 192 Cr; depreciation Rs 19 Cr; interest Rs 3 Cr.
- Specialty Cables revenue Rs 153 Cr (FY26 segment figure as disclosed); cable order book Rs 40.7 Cr.
- Debt cut to Rs 24 Cr (FY26) from Rs 85 Cr (FY25); book value Rs 64.5; market cap Rs 1,717 Cr; ROE -15.4%.

## Lynch lens
Story stock, not a numbers stock. KAVACH and railways modernisation sound exciting — which Lynch warned about ("if it's got a sexy story, the numbers still must pay"). Losses widen while sales stay flat; working capital ballooned (debtor days 160; cycle 183 → 392 days) as capacity ramped. Market cap of Rs 1,717 Cr against a loss-making Rs 153 Cr revenue business prices in execution that has not happened yet.

## TAM
Indian Railways signalling/KAVACH and speciality cable market — data not found (Railways capex programs large; exact TAM not verified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Specialty Cables division utilisation (Rs 153 Cr FY26 segment revenue).
2. KAVACH / signalling order conversions and execution timing.
3. Depreciation + working-capital drag (receivables from railway customers).

## Red flags
- Two straight years of widening losses.
- Debtor days 160; working-capital cycle more than doubled.
- Flat revenue despite capacity build-out.

## Verdict
Railways story without earnings — speculative until orders convert to cash.
