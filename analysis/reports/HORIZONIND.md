# Horizon Industrial Parks Ltd (HORIZONIND)

## What it does
Horizon Industrial Parks owns and rents out modern warehouses and industrial parks across India — big sheds used by e-commerce, third-party logistics and manufacturing companies. It earns rent from tenants on long leases. Part of the Nifty IPO index; Blackstone-backed platform.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 131 crore (FY25), Rs 137 crore (FY26) — standalone, per screener.in
- Operating profit: Rs 24 crore (FY25, OPM 18%), Rs 2 crore (FY26, OPM 1.4%)
- Other income: Rs 76 crore (FY25), Rs 167 crore (FY26) — dominates the P&L
- Net profit: Rs -9 crore (FY25), Rs 66 crore (FY26)
- Interest: Rs 82 crore (FY25), Rs 73 crore (FY26) — interest exceeds operating profit; low interest coverage flagged by screener
- Market cap Rs 16,735 crore; P/E 255

## Lynch lens
Asset play — the value sits in land and leased warehouses, not reported earnings. Classic real-estate accounting buries rent income under other income while interest eats operating profit. At P/E 255, earnings-based valuation says nothing; NAV, occupancy and lease escalations matter instead. Lynch would demand the asset value per share before paying this price.

## TAM
Indian warehousing/industrial real estate market — large, driven by GST-driven consolidation, e-commerce and 3PL; exact TAM figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Rental income from leased warehouse portfolio — core engine
2. Other income (Rs 167 crore FY26) — includes items beyond straight rent; drove the FY26 profit swing
3. Interest cost (Rs 73 crore FY26) — the biggest drag; refinancing at lower rates directly lifts bottom line

## Red flags
- P/E 255; FY26 profit rests on Rs 167 crore other income
- Interest (Rs 73-82 crore) exceeds operating profit — low interest coverage
- OPM collapsed from 18% (FY25) to 1.4% (FY26) — check what ran through expenses

## Verdict
Asset play on Indian logistics real estate — fine idea, terrifying headline valuation; buy only on a hard NAV discount.
