# Indogulf Cropsciences (IGCL)

## What it does
Indogulf makes crop protection chemicals — insecticides, fungicides, herbicides — plus plant nutrients and biological products for farmers. It sells both to Indian farmers (its own brands) and to global agrochem companies (contract manufacturing), exporting to 34+ countries. Recent IPO; promoters hold 69%.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 676 cr (FY26, Mar 2026), +14% TTM growth — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 39 cr (FY26), +24% TTM growth
- OPM ~10%; ROE ~10-12%
- P/E ~12x; market cap Rs 454 cr
- Borrowings Rs 177 cr; book value Rs 72.6/share
- Weak long-term record: 5-yr sales growth only 8.8%; no dividends

## Lynch lens
Cyclical in a dull commodity-ish industry. Agrochemicals swing with monsoons, crop prices and the China inventory cycle — FY26's 14% growth follows years of single-digit drift (8.8% 5-yr CAGR). At 12x P/E and 10% ROE this looks like a low-expectations cyclical rather than a grower; the biologicals/plant-nutrient push offers a small option on the cleaner-agriculture trend. Small-cap illiquidity adds risk.

## TAM
Indian agrochemical market — roughly Rs 60,000+ cr domestically with global export demand on top (approximate industry estimates; precise sourced figure not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Domestic crop-protection formulation sales — volume x price of insecticides/fungicides
2. Export/contract-manufacturing orders from overseas agrochem majors
3. Raw-material (China-imported intermediates) cost swings — with 10% OPM, input costs decide most of the profit line

## Red flags
- Thin 10% operating margin, commodity economics
- 5-yr sales growth under 9% — no compounding engine
- Rs 177 cr debt against Rs 454 cr market cap
- Micro-cap liquidity; zero dividend history

## Verdict
Cheap cyclical, no visible moat — trade the agrochem cycle, don't marry the stock.
