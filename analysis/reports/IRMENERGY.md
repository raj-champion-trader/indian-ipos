# IRM Energy Ltd (IRMENERGY)

## What it does
City-gas distributor — pipes natural gas (CNG for vehicles and PNG for homes, factories) to households, vehicles and industry under exclusive licences. Holds city-gas rights for Banaskantha (Gujarat) and Diu & Gir Somnath, with long infrastructure exclusivity (25 years).

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 891 cr (FY24) → Rs 975 cr (FY25), +9%
- PAT: Rs 86 cr (FY24) → Rs 45 cr (FY25), -48%
- OPM: 17% (FY24) → 10% (FY25)
- Interest: Rs 27 cr (FY24) → Rs 22 cr (FY25); Depreciation up to Rs 35 cr (FY25)
- Borrowings cut from Rs 251 cr (FY24) to Rs 152 cr (FY25) — near debt-free
- ROE 5.49%, ROCE 8.63%; P/E ~15.9; dividend yield 0.53%

## Lynch lens
Cyclical-turned-turnaround. CGD economics hinge on gas sourcing cost vs administered selling price — margin crashed when input costs/industrial PNG spreads compressed (OPM 17%→10%, PAT halved in FY25). Licences give a local monopoly (Lynch loves exclusive franchises), and balance sheet now nearly clean. Current P/E 15.9 on trough earnings; if margins mean-revert, this fits the turnaround bucket.

## TAM
India CGD: ~10% of gas demand via city networks, government targets pushing gas to 15% of energy mix; India natural gas market estimated ~USD 40-50 bn annually (industry estimates; precise figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Industrial PNG volumes and realisation spreads (biggest margin driver)
2. CNG volume growth tied to vehicle conversions in licence areas
3. Capex tapering — depreciation and interest now trending down, aiding PAT

## Red flags
- PAT halved (FY25); OPM collapse from 17% to 10%
- Small ROE (5.5%) — heavy pipeline capex still dragging returns
- Geographic concentration in two Gujarat-region licences
- Administered-pricing risk on gas allocation

## Verdict
Licensed local monopoly at trough margins — a genuine turnaround candidate, but only for investors who believe gas spreads recover.
