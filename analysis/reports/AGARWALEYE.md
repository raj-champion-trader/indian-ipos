# Dr. Agarwals Health Care (AGARWALEYE)

## What it does
Dr. Agarwals runs a chain of eye hospitals — India's largest — doing cataract and laser (refractive) surgeries, plus eye check-ups, glasses and eye drops. It holds roughly a quarter of the organised eye-care market and keeps expanding into smaller cities.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY26 Rs 2,080 cr, +23% (TTM growth) — screener.in
- PAT: FY26 Rs 168 cr, +43% (TTM growth); operating margin ~27%
- 5-yr sales CAGR 35%; 5-yr profit CAGR 34%
- ROE only 6-7%; ROCE 11.1% — hospital build-outs consume capital
- Borrowings Rs 1,066 cr
- One-year stock return ~17%

## Lynch lens
Fast grower, weak returns so far. Hospital chains grow revenue easily (new beds fill up) but each hospital needs heavy capex, so ROE stays low until mature — the classic "growth before returns" trap. 35% revenue CAGR with 26-27% market share in organised eye care gives a genuine niche-dominance story (Lynch would like the specialisation). Watch ROE trajectory; if it climbs toward 15% as hospitals season, earnings compound fast.

## TAM
Indian eye-care market — cataract backlog alone runs into millions of surgeries a year with low organised-chain penetration in tier-2/3 cities; precise market size not found in fetched sources.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Cataract surgeries — highest-volume, steady cash-generating procedure
2. Refractive (LASIK/smile) surgeries — premium-priced, higher-margin growth
3. New hospital maturation — each centre moves from loss-making ramp-up to profit, driving the 43% PAT growth

## Red flags
- ROE of 6-7% despite fast growth — capital-hungry model
- Rs 1,066 cr debt funding expansion
- Doctor attrition/competition from LV Prasad, Aravind, Narayana Nethralaya
- High-growth stocks punish any quarterly slip

## Verdict
Dominant niche fast grower — attractive if ROE turns up, watch two more years.
