# Indo Farm Equipment Limited (INDOFARM)

## What it does
Indo Farm Equipment builds tractors (16-110 HP) and pick & carry cranes (9-30 tonne) at its factory in Baddi, Himachal Pradesh. It sells through dealers across India and exports to 30+ countries. Recent IPO (Dec 2024).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 338 crore (FY24); Rs 348 crore (FY25) — flat.
- PAT: Rs 14 crore (FY24); Rs 23 crore (FY25) — ~64% jump.
- Total expenses Rs 300 crore (FY25), ~86% of sales; raw material/employee split data not found.
- Crane segment carries the profit: cranes earn much richer margins than tractors.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with a niche hidden inside. The pick & carry crane business gives it a specialised, less-crowded niche (few organised players, strong replacement demand), while tractors tie it to farm cycles and monsoon. Profit jump in FY25 on flat sales shows operating leverage. Small-cap though — treat as cyclical plus niche, not a pure growth story.

## TAM
India tractor industry ~7-9 lakh units annually (FY25, industry estimates); India pick & carry crane market estimated in the low-thousand-crore range — exact sourced figures data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Pick & carry crane volumes and pricing (high-margin niche).
2. Tractor demand cycle (monsoon, farm incomes, government schemes).
3. Raw material costs (steel) and operating leverage at the Baddi plant.

## Red flags
- Revenue flat FY24→FY25.
- Fragmented, promotion-heavy tractor market against M&M, TAFE, Sonalika.
- Small scale; detailed expense and segment profit data not found.

## Verdict
Niche-crane profits pulling a cyclical tractor body — interesting small-cap, buy only on cyclical downturns.
