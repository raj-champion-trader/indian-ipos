# R K Swamy Ltd (RKSWAMY)

## What it does
R K Swamy sells advertising and marketing services — media planning, creative campaigns, customer data analytics and market research. Clients span consumer, financial and industrial companies. Listed March 2024.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Revenue: FY23 144; FY24 162; FY25 120 (FY25 dipped — client spending and associate-entity mix); FY26 151
- Net profit: FY24 27; FY25 14; FY26 20 — margins ~13%, still below FY24 peak
- ROE 7.7%; P/E ~23 at price Rs 93.8; market cap only Rs 474 cr; dividend yield ~2.1%
- Debt low (FY26 borrowings 27); other income Rs 15 cr FY26 is a large slice of Rs 24 cr PBT

## Lynch lens
Stalwart of the small-cap variety — a decades-old, boring ad agency growing slowly. FY25's revenue drop (162 → 120) shows client-spend cyclicality. At Rs 474 cr market cap it sits in neglected small-cap territory Lynch loved hunting, but the fundamentals show modest growth and single-digit ROE, so it qualifies as a middling stalwart, not a fast grower. P/E 23 for this profile looks full.

## TAM
Indian advertising industry roughly Rs 1 lakh crore+ and growing high-single/double digits annually (industry body FICCI-EY estimates); data analytics and martech grow faster.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Client media budgets — gross media billing pass-through and fee income
2. Employee costs — the dominant expense for any services firm; utilisation drives margin
3. Analytics/research (customer data) arm margins versus traditional ad-buying margins

## Red flags
- Revenue fell ~26% in FY25; recovery still partial in FY26
- PBT leans on other income (Rs 15 cr of Rs 24 cr FY26)
- Tiny market cap and low liquidity; clients can cut budgets quickly

## Verdict
Boring ad-services stalwart at a small size — cheap on yield, but no visible growth engine yet; watch for FY27 budget recovery.
