# Gopal Snacks Ltd (GOPAL)

## What it does
Gopal Snacks makes ethnic Indian snacks — gathiya, namkeen, papad and farali products — from its Gujarat factories and sells them through distributors across India. It keeps a largely single-brand, single-category focus. Listed March 2024.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Revenue: FY23 1,394; FY24 1,402; FY25 1,468; FY26 1,508; TTM 1,608 (low single-digit growth)
- Net profit: FY24 100; FY25 19 (crash — FY25 other income shows -42, indicating an exceptional charge); FY26 74; TTM 84
- Net margin ~5% normally; ROE 4.7% (FY26); P/E ~63 at price Rs 276; market cap Rs 3,444 cr
- Debt low (FY26 borrowings 154); asset turnover modest

## Lynch lens
Stalwart, and an expensive one. Boring snack foods — Lynch territory in theory — but revenue growth of ~3% a year and a FY25 profit wipeout do not support a P/E of 63 (PEG meaningless/very high). FY25 PAT of Rs 19 cr against FY24's Rs 100 cr signals an exceptional item or margin shock that needs annual-report digging. The niche (Gujarati ethnic snacks, strong regional brand) gives some pricing power, but national expansion has yet to show up in growth.

## TAM
Indian savoury/ethnic snacks market estimated around Rs 50,000-60,000 crore+ with organised players growing faster than unorganised (industry estimates, ~2024); namkeen the largest category.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gathiya/namkeen volume growth and distribution expansion beyond western India
2. Input costs — raw materials (besan/gram flour, edible oil, packaging) dominate expenses (roughly 55-60% of revenue per DRHP)
3. FY25 exceptional item (negative other income of Rs 42 cr) — clarifying this explains the profit crash

## Red flags
- FY25 PAT collapse (100 → 19) not yet explained publicly in this dataset
- P/E ~63 on ~3% growth — valuation way ahead of fundamentals
- Post-IPO stock slipped from Rs 398 to ~Rs 276; ROE under 5%

## Verdict
Decent regional snack brand priced like a fast grower while growing like a snail — avoid until growth or the FY25 anomaly gets explained.
