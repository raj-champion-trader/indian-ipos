# Ecos (India) Mobility & Hospitality Ltd (ECOSMOBLTY)

## What it does
Company runs chauffeur-driven car rentals and employee transportation for corporates across 131 Indian cities and 30+ countries. It keeps an asset-light model — 20,000+ vehicles, 90%+ owned by vendors. Clients include 1,750+ organizations, among them 70+ Fortune 500 companies.

## The numbers
- FY24 revenue Rs 568 crore; PAT Rs 62.5 crore (IPO filings).
- TTM (to ~Jun 2026) revenue Rs 809 crore; PAT Rs 58 crore; OPM ~11%; ROE 24%; ROCE 30.7% (screener).
- Revenue CAGR: 24% over 3 years, 51% over 5 years.
- Nearly debt-free: borrowings Rs 8 crore vs reserves Rs 248 crore. FCF Rs 39 crore (FY26).
- Market cap Rs 680 crore; P/E ~11.7.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower at a stalwart price — mid-20s revenue growth with P/E under 12 (PEG well under 1). Boring, unglamorous business (corporate cabs) with high ROCE and an asset-light niche in managed employee transportation. No insider-activity data found.

## TAM
India corporate car rental and employee transportation market; precise size data not found. Large, fragmented, and formalizing — room for organized players to take share.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Corporate employee transportation contracts (recurring, bulk of revenue).
2. Chauffeur-driven rentals (airport/outstation) margins.
3. Vendor cost control — 90% of fleet vendor-owned keeps capex and depreciation low.

## Red flags
- TTM PAT flat vs FY24 despite 42% revenue growth; margin compression.
- Tiny market cap / low liquidity; promoter-heavy small cap.
- Dependence on vendor supply chain for fleet.

## Verdict
Cheap, high-ROCE niche operator — watch whether profit growth catches up with revenue before buying hard.
