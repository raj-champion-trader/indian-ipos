# NTPC Green Energy (NTPCGREEN)

## What it does
The company builds and runs solar and wind power plants across India. It sells electricity under long-term contracts, mostly to government-linked buyers. NTPC, the state power giant, owns it, and it held the title of largest renewable public-sector enterprise in India (excluding hydro) as of September 2024.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 1,963 crore (FY23); Rs 2,210 crore (FY24); Rs 2,858 crore (FY25)
- PAT: Rs 343 crore (FY23); Rs 474 crore (FY24); Rs 521 crore (FY25)
- OPM: 89% (FY23) to 86% (FY25) — typical for utility-scale generation where depreciation and interest hit below the operating line
- Interest: Rs 694 crore (FY24), rising toward ~Rs 898 crore (FY26E per screener); depreciation Rs 643 crore (FY24) rising toward ~Rs 1,102 crore (FY26E)
- 3-year sales growth ~156%

## RevenueStreams
- Solar power sales under PPAs — majority of capacity (FY25)
- Wind power sales under PPAs (FY25)
- Exact solar/wind revenue split: data not found

## Lynch lens
Fast grower with a stalwart's contract book — capacity growth drives 25-30% revenue CAGR while 25-year PPAs give utility-like predictability. The catch: heavy capex means big debt, big depreciation and big interest, so PAT growth (Rs 343 to Rs 521 crore, FY23-FY25) lags revenue growth. A government-backed "boring business with a tailwind" — India's energy transition guarantees demand.

## TAM
India targets ~500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030 (NDC/National Infrastructure Pipeline); NTPC group targets ~60 GW renewables by 2032 for this subsidiary.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. New capacity commissioning — each GW added adds contracted revenue
2. Depreciation and interest burden from capex — the main profit drags
3. PDA/PPA tariffs and curtailment risk on solar plants

## Red flags
- Debt-funded capex: interest costs rising fast (Rs 694 crore FY24 → ~Rs 898 crore FY26E)
- Depreciation load nearly doubling as plants commission
- Government ownership caps returns; equity dilution risk for growth funding
- Solar eviction/curtailment issues have hit PSU renewable peers

## Verdict
A long-duration fast grower at utility returns — fine for patient capital, but profit growth will trail capacity growth for years.
