# Anthem Biosciences Ltd (ANTHEM)

## What it does
Anthem Biosciences does contract research, development, and manufacturing (CRDMO) — it cooks up and makes drugs and specialty ingredients for global pharma companies. It offers both new-chemical-entity and new-biological-entity capabilities, plus platforms like RNAi, antibody-drug conjugates, peptides, and oligonucleotides.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 1,419 crore (FY24), Rs 1,845 crore (FY25), Rs 2,124 crore (FY26) — 26% 3-year sales CAGR.
- Net profit: Rs 367 crore (FY24), Rs 451 crore (FY25), Rs 592 crore (FY26). OPM ~39%.
- Market cap ~Rs 50,695 crore; P/E ~85 (Aug 2026). ROCE 30.4%, ROE 22.3% — excellent.
- Almost debt free; first dividend paid (19% payout FY26). Working capital days cut from 118 to 92.
- Recent IPO (listed 2025); promoter holding data not in extract.

## Lynch lens
A high-quality niche compounder — ROCE of 30% with 20%+ growth marks a genuine fast grower with a moat (few Indian CRDMOs run both NCE and NBE platforms). But P/E ~85 against ~21% 3-year profit growth gives PEG ~4: Lynch would call that paying tomorrow's price today. Boring it is not, and the market knows it.

## TAM
Data not found (global CRDMO market size not reliably published here; industry commentary puts it well above USD 100 bn globally — unverified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. CRDMO services revenue — integrated drug discovery-to-manufacturing contracts for global pharma (core of Rs 2,124 crore FY26 sales).
2. Specialty ingredients manufacturing — second, steadier stream.
3. Margin expansion and other income — OPM 36-39% plus Rs 131 crore other income (FY26) lifted PAT 31% in FY26.

## Red flags
- P/E 85 and 16.6x book — very expensive.
- Recent quarterly lumpiness (Dec 2025 quarter sales dipped to Rs 423 crore).
- TTM sales trend slightly negative (-2% per screener TTM note) even as annual FY26 grew — watch closely.
- Young listing; lock-in expiries can pressure the price.

## Verdict
World-class returns on capital wearing a stratospheric multiple — great company, punishing price; buy only on a big drawdown.
