# All Time Plastics Ltd (ALLTIME)

## What it does
Company makes plastic houseware products and exports them worldwide. Sells on a B2B white-label basis — big retailers market the goods under their own brands. Boring plastic houseware, exactly the kind of business Lynch liked.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 513 cr (FY24), Rs 558 cr (FY25), Rs 610 cr (FY26)
- PAT: Rs 45 cr (FY24), Rs 47 cr (FY25), Rs 36 cr (FY26) — profit fell in FY26
- OPM: 19% (FY24), 18% (FY25), 15% (FY26) — margin compression
- Borrowings: Rs 86 cr (FY26), down from Rs 223 cr (FY25); finance cost Rs 15 cr (FY26)
- ROE ~9%, ROCE 11.6%, P/E ~35 (post-listing)

## Lynch lens
Boring-business stalwart: plastic houseware for export brands. But growth is single-digit revenue and PAT declined in FY26. P/E 35 against falling earnings gives no PEG comfort — expensive for a slow grower. Asset/debt-reduction story (Rs 223 cr to Rs 86 cr) offers some turnaround flavour.

## TAM
Data not found (global plastic houseware market; export white-label demand).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Export demand from white-label retail clients (US/Europe) — main revenue engine
2. Polymer (raw material) prices — biggest cost, drives the OPM slide from 19% to 15%
3. Finance cost reduction from debt paydown (Rs 15 cr FY26) — supports bottom line

## Red flags
- PAT drop FY25 to FY26 (Rs 47 cr to Rs 36 cr)
- OPM compressed 19% (FY24) to 15% (FY26)
- P/E ~35 with shrinking profits
- Customer concentration risk typical of white-label export models

## Verdict
Decent exporter with real debt reduction, but at P/E 35 with falling profits the price runs ahead of the business — watch, don't chase.
