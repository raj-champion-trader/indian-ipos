# Concord Enviro Systems Ltd (CEWATER)

## What it does
Company builds water and wastewater treatment plants — including zero-liquid-discharge (ZLD) systems and compressed biogas plants. Also sells spare parts and runs maintenance contracts on the plants it installs. Dirty water in, clean water out — a needed, unglamorous service.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 38.97 cr (FY24), Rs 56.58 cr (FY25), Rs 55.87 cr (FY26) — standalone
- Net profit: Rs 2.32 cr (FY24), Rs 3.13 cr (FY25), Rs 9.59 cr (FY26)
- OPM: -3.80% (FY24), 1.29% (FY25), -3.49% (FY26) — negative operating margins, yet positive net profit (other income/one-offs)
- Borrowings: Rs 2.59 cr (FY26) — effectively debt-free
- TTM: revenue Rs 57.17 cr, PAT Rs 5.17 cr

## Lynch lens
Cyclical/turnaround candidate in water infrastructure — a structural growth theme (industrial ZLD mandates, CBG push). Negative OPM with positive PAT means earnings quality looks weak. Small revenue base means lumpy project wins swing results violently. No meaningful PEG possible given unstable margins.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Systems & plants segment (ZLD, CBG projects) — main revenue and margin driver, lumpy order flow
2. O&M and consumables/spares — recurring, steadier income stream
3. Other income / project cost overruns — explains negative OPM with positive net profit

## Red flags
- Negative operating margin in 2 of last 3 years
- Profit quality: net profit positive while operations lose money
- Very small revenue base (under Rs 60 cr) — one project makes or breaks the year

## Verdict
Right theme (water, ZLD, biogas), shaky execution — a lottery ticket, not an investment yet.
