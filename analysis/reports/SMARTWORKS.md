# Smartworks Coworking Spaces Limited (SMARTWORKS)

## What it does
Smartworks operates managed office campuses for companies. It leases buildings, fits them out, and rents desks, offices and services to enterprises. India's largest player in this space by seats.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 711 cr (FY23), Rs 1,039 cr (FY24), Rs 1,374 cr (FY25), Rs 1,796 cr (FY26)
- Net profit: Rs -101 cr (FY23), Rs -50 cr (FY24), Rs -63 cr (FY25), Rs +11 cr (FY26) — first full-year profit
- OPM ~60-64% (before lease charge); the killer below the line: interest Rs 381 cr + depreciation Rs 901 cr (TTM)
- Market cap ~Rs 6,162 cr; P/E ~221; ROCE 8.3%

## Lynch lens
A turnaround story: heavy upfront capex and lease costs crushed earlier years, but scale (1,900+ seats added across 45+ centers) finally pushed FY26 into the black. The boring-asset angle — long-duration enterprise leases on fitted campuses — gives some annuity character. P/E of 221 on nascent profits makes PEG meaningless; watch EBITDA-minus-lease vs debt paydown instead. IPO (Jul 2025) proceeds went to debt repayment.

## TAM
India managed/flexible workspace market — several crore sq ft of enterprise office demand across top-7 cities; exact market size data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Occupancy and pricing on ~190,000 seats (core rental revenue)
2. Finance costs on lease/other borrowings (interest Rs 381 cr TTM)
3. Depreciation on fit-outs (Rs 901 cr TTM) — capex intensity decides reported profit

## Red flags
- Thin FY26 profit (Rs 11 cr) against Rs 6,100 cr market cap
- Interest + depreciation together exceed Rs 1,200 cr per year
- Lease-linked balance sheet risk if occupancy dips

## Verdict
Real turnaround, but the stock already celebrates it — only for buyers who trust the occupancy story through a full cycle.
