# Rossell Techsys Limited (ROSSTECH)

## What it does
Rossell Techsys builds wire harnesses, electrical interconnects and sheet-metal parts for military and commercial aircraft. It sells engineering-and-manufacturing services to global aerospace OEMs such as Boeing, Lockheed Martin and Bell.

## The numbers
- Sales (standalone, screener): Rs 217 crore FY24; Rs 259 crore FY25; Rs 485 crore FY26 (+87%). TTM ~Rs 553 crore.
- Net profit: Rs 11 crore FY24; Rs 7 crore FY25; Rs 21 crore FY26 (+177%). TTM ~Rs 25 crore.
- OPM steady ~13%; ROE ~15%; market cap ~Rs 4,164 crore; P/E ~163 at Rs 1,105.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a defence niche. Boring-product angle: nobody writes poems about wire harnesses, yet every fighter jet and helicopter needs them, and qualification locks in suppliers. Order momentum strong: ~Rs 400 crore space contract plus ~Rs 3,000 crore strategic agreements (company commentary, FY26). But P/E ~163 against ~70% TTM profit growth still leaves a stretched PEG; margins (13% OPM) cap compounding.

## TAM
Indian aerospace & defence manufacturing market projected to grow several-fold over the decade under Make-in-India offsets (government/industry estimates); precise sourced TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Aerospace & defence engineering segment (wire harnesses) — dominant revenue and profit source.
2. New verticals: space systems (~Rs 400 crore contract) and semiconductor entries.
3. Operating leverage on the Bengaluru facility as OEM volumes ramp.

## Red flags
P/E ~163 prices in perfection; FY25 profit actually dipped before FY26 recovery; customer concentration among a few global OEMs; working-capital-heavy harness business.

## Verdict
Quality niche defence fast grower — great story, punishing price; wait for pullbacks or margin proof.
