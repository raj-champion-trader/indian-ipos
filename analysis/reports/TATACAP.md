# Tata Capital Ltd (TATACAP)

## What it does
Company lends money. The Tata Group's flagship NBFC gives business loans (commercial finance), consumer loans, manages wealth, and distributes Tata credit cards. India's 3rd largest diversified NBFC by loan book — Rs 2.77 lakh crore (FY26) — serving 7.3 million customers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 28,322 cr (FY25); Rs 31,566 cr (FY26) — ~12% growth.
- PAT: Rs 3,655 cr (FY25); Rs 4,891 cr (FY26) — ~34% growth.
- Financing margin: 19% (FY25) to 23% (FY26); 26% in Jun 2026 quarter.
- Interest expense Rs 15,985 cr; opex Rs 8,458 cr; depreciation Rs 538 cr (FY26).
- Borrowings Rs 2,36,376 cr (FY26) — funds the loan book. Gross NPA 0.80%, Net NPA 0.40% (FY26).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with fast-grower earnings. A lender grows as fast as India's credit market, and PAT jumped 34% in FY26. The Tata brand lowers funding costs — a genuine moat in lending. Quality of book looks strong (sub-1% gross NPA). Lynch avoided banks/lenders he couldn't read, but the growth-plus-brand combination fits a stalwart core holding.

## TAM
Indian NBFC / retail + MSME credit market — multi-lakh-crore and underpenetrated; specific sourced figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Net interest margin — financing margin widened 19% (FY25) to 23% (FY26); every spread point compounds.
2. Commercial finance and consumer loan book growth (Rs 2.77 lakh cr book, FY26).
3. Credit costs — gross NPA of just 0.80% (FY26) keeps provisions low; any slip hits PAT hard.

## Red flags
- Rs 2.36 lakh cr of borrowings (FY26): asset-liability mismatch and rate-cycle risk inherent to the model.
- Margin expansion from 19% to 23-26% in one year — question mark on sustainability.
- Lenders' earnings quality only shows up across full credit cycles.

## Verdict
High-quality financial stalwart compounding at 30%+ — Lynch would call it a bank-like grower worth owning at a reasonable multiple.
