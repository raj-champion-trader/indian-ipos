# Credo Brands Marketing Ltd (MUFTI)

## What it does
Company sells men's casual fashion under the MUFTI brand — shirts, jeans, t-shirts and accessories. It designs the clothes and outsources manufacturing, then sells through large-format stores, multi-brand outlets, own stores and e-commerce.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 567 cr (FY24), Rs 618 cr (FY25), Rs 592 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 597 cr.
- PAT: Rs 59 cr (FY24), Rs 68 cr (FY25), Rs 47 cr (FY26); TTM down 32%.
- OPM: 28% (FY24) to 27% (FY26) — high for apparel.
- P/E ~11x; market cap ~Rs 496 cr; dividend yield 2.63%. ROE 12%; ROCE 14.4%.
- Total debt Rs 228 cr (Mar 2026).

## Lynch lens
Small-cap brand play — a niche men's casualwear label with fat 27-28% operating margins, asset-light via outsourced making. Trading at ~11x with a dividend. But growth has stalled: TTM sales -3%, profit -32%. Lynch would call it a stalled fast grower — needs a demand revival story; if it re-accelerates, the P/E re-rates hard.

## TAM
Indian men's casual wear market — large and growing; specific TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. MUFTI brand full-price sell-through and discounts discipline (margin engine).
2. Distribution expansion — EBOs/LFS presence driving volume.
3. Cotton/input costs and ad spend levels.

## Red flags
- TTM profit down 32%, sales shrinking — brand losing momentum to competitors.
- Debt rose to Rs 228 cr.
- Single-brand concentration — everything rides on the MUFTI label.

## Verdict
Cheap brand with stalled engine — turnaround bet, small position only.
