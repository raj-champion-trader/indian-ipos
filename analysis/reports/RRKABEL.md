# R R Kabel Limited (RRKABEL)

## What it does
RR Kabel makes wires and cables that carry electricity through homes and factories, sold under its own brands. Also sells fans, lights, switches and home appliances (bought the Luminous Home Electrical business from Schneider in 2022). Listed in 2023.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: ~Rs 7,620 crore (implied by FY26 revenue Rs 9,722 crore, +27.6% YoY). FY25 PAT: ~Rs 311 crore (implied by FY26 PAT Rs 492 crore, +58% YoY).
- Q4 FY25: record revenue Rs 2,218 crore (+26.4% YoY), net profit ~Rs 129 crore (+64% YoY).
- Wires & cables: ~88-90% of revenue; FMEG/consumer segment ~10-12%, which grew ~28% YoY and hit operational breakeven for the first time (Q4 FY25 basis).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with a fast-grower limb. Wire and cable looks like the dullest corner of the market — exactly what Lynch bought. Brand-led house-wiring carries better margins than commodity cable. The FMEG (fans, lights) arm just crossed breakeven; each year it scales, group margins rise. TPG held ~5% and has sold via block deals (~Rs 1,000 crore) — insider selling, not buying. P/E: data not found in sources gathered; PEG N/A.

## TAM
India wires & cables and consumer electricals (FMEG) market: very large, growing with housing electrification; exact TAM figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. House-wiring brand volumes (RR Kabel brand) — premium pricing over unbranded cable.
2. Copper cost pass-through — margins hinge on copper price timing.
3. FMEG segment scaling past breakeven — newly additive to profit.

## Red flags
- Copper price swings dominate raw material cost (~majority of cost base).
- Competition from Polycab, Havells, Anchor — crowded brandscape.
- TPG block-decel selling overhang; FMEG still thin-margin.

## Verdict
Dependable brand stalwart compounding through the housing cycle — own it for boring consistency, not fireworks.
