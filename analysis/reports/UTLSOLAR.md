# Fujiyama Power Systems Ltd (UTLSOLAR)

## What it does
Company sells rooftop solar gear under the "UTL Solar" and "Fujiyama Solar" brands: solar panels, solar inverters, solar batteries and UPS systems. It sells through dealers and installers to homes, shops and small factories across India. It runs a 29+ year old business that listed via IPO in November 2025 (Rs 828 crore issue).

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: above Rs 1,550 crore; PAT ~Rs 156 crore (per IPO coverage).
- H1 FY26 revenue: Rs 1,166 crore (+61.5% YoY); net profit Rs 130 crore.
- Q4 FY26 revenue: Rs 901 crore (+87.5% YoY); EBITDA up 116.9% to Rs 172 crore.
- Cumulative 5-year deliveries: ~0.9 GW panels, ~2.0 GW inverters, ~2.56 GWh batteries; ~9.6% of India's rooftop inverter capacity.
- Market cap ~Rs 13,700 crore; P/E (TTM) ~44 (as of mid-2026).

## Lynch lens
Fast grower — revenue compounding 60-90% YoY on India's rooftop solar boom (PM Surya Ghar subsidy push). Niche angle: ~15.5-16% share of India's solar battery market and a top rooftop inverter brand. PEG looks stretched (44 P/E vs ~60-80% growth → PEG under 1 if growth holds, but growth can deflate with subsidy changes). No insider-buying data found.

## TAM
India rooftop solar market — GW-scale and growing fast on government subsidy programs; solar equipment (panels, inverters, batteries) TAM in tens of billions of dollars globally (exact India TAM figure: data not found in searched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Solar panel sales (~43% of revenue mix, FY25).
2. Solar inverters/UPS (string inverters, PCUs).
3. Solar batteries (~15.5% India market share).

## Red flags
- Valuation rich (~44x TTM P/E at listing pop prices).
- Crowded, price-competitive market (Luminous, Microtek, Waaree, Chinese imports).
- Subsidy-dependent demand; policy changes can hit growth.
- Thin IPO history — one year of listed track record.

## Verdict
Fast grower riding the rooftop solar wave — good story, but buy only on meaningful dips given the crowded field and rich multiple.
