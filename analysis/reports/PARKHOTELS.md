# Apeejay Surrendra Park Hotels Ltd (PARKHOTELS)

## What it does
Company runs upscale hotels ("The Park" and other brands) plus restaurants, bars and nightclubs, and the Flurys bakery retail chain. It owns some hotels, leases some, and manages others for fees.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 555 cr (FY24), Rs 605 cr (FY25), Rs 669 cr (FY26).
- PAT: Rs 66 cr (FY24), Rs 85 cr (FY25), Rs 70 cr (FY26).
- OPM ~31-33%. 5-year sales CAGR 31%, profit CAGR 26%.
- P/E ~35x; market cap ~Rs 2,397 cr. ROE (3-yr avg) only 6%.
- Deleveraged: borrowings from Rs 610 cr (FY23) to Rs 93 cr (FY24), back up to Rs 263 cr (Mar 2026); interest cost fell to Rs 28 cr.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with an asset-play undertone — hotels ride the travel boom, and the company owns prime real estate in Kolkata, Delhi, Bangalore and other metros. Post-COVID revival delivered 31% sales CAGR. But 35x P/E for a 6% ROE business prices the boom fully. Managed-hotel growth would improve return quality.

## TAM
Indian upscale hotel industry — strong room-rate and occupancy upcycle; exact TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Room revenue — ARR (average room rates) and occupancy at owned hotels.
2. F&B — restaurants, banquets, and the Flurys retail chain.
3. Interest cost — deleveraging added meaningfully to bottom line FY23→FY26.

## Red flags
- FY26 profit fell from Rs 85 cr to Rs 70 cr despite higher sales.
- Capital-hungry owned-hotel model; ROE historically weak (6% avg).
- Cyclical peak risk if travel demand or room rates cool.

## Verdict
Quality cyclical at peak-cycle pricing — the hidden land value tempts, the P/E doesn't.
