# Om Power Transmission Ltd (OMPOWER)

## What it does
Om Power Transmission builds power transmission infrastructure on turnkey contracts — HV/EHV transmission lines, substations and underground cabling. It handles design, procurement, erection, testing, commissioning and O&M for utilities.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 279 crore FY25 vs Rs 183 crore FY24 (+52%).
- PAT: Rs 22 crore FY25 vs Rs 7 crore FY24 (tripled). OPM 13% FY25 (8% FY24); PAT margin 7.9% FY25.
- Borrowings: Rs 19 crore FY25 (rising to Rs 48 crore FY26); interest Rs 6 crore FY25. ROCE 42.7%, P/E about 14 (screener.in).

## Lynch lens
A cyclical riding India's grid buildout. Order-book-driven EPC means lumpy revenue, but the numbers show a sharp margin and profit inflection. At P/E ~14 with 52% growth, PEG looks under 0.3 — cheap if growth holds, classic cyclical trap if the order pipeline dries up. Small size adds execution risk.

## TAM
India transmission capex: utilities plan several lakh crore of transmission investment over FY25-32 (National Electricity Plan / CEA projections); company-specific share data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Turnkey transmission-line and substation project execution margins.
2. Order-book conversion pace (revenue recognition milestones).
3. Commodity (steel/conductor/copper) price moves on fixed-price contracts.

## Red flags
- Small scale (Rs 279 crore FY25 revenue) — one bad project hurts badly.
- Rising FY26 borrowings; working capital swings in EPC.
- Cyclical, tender-dependent revenue; margins can mean-revert.

## Verdict
Cheap cyclical with a strong FY25 inflection — worth tracking order wins, size the position small.
