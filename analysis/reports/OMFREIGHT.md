# Om Freight Forwarders Ltd (OMFREIGHT)

## What it does
Om Freight Forwarders moves cargo for exporters and importers across sea, air, road and rail as a third-party logistics (3PL) provider. Running since 1995 from Mumbai, it handles multimodal transport, freight forwarding and customs clearance end-to-end.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 471 crore (FY23), Rs 410 crore (FY24), Rs 489 crore (FY25) — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 27 crore (FY23), Rs 10 crore (FY24), Rs 22 crore (FY25)
- Operating margin: 7% (FY23), 3% (FY24), 8% (FY25) — freight forwarding margins swing with freight rates
- Market cap ~Rs 303 crore; P/E ~15. Promoter holding ~74.85% (June 2026).
- Expense heads: purchased freight/transportation charges dominate; detailed split: data not found.

## Lynch lens
Boring business, decent angle — nobody gets excited about customs clearance, and that keeps competition rational. But this behaves like a cyclical tied to global freight rates and trade volumes: FY24 profit crashed to Rs 10 crore when rates fell, then doubled back in FY25. Freight forwarding revenue share %: data not found. A cyclical in a dull package.

## TAM

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Global sea/air freight rate cycles — rates crushed FY24 profit, lifted FY25
2. India export-import cargo volume growth
3. Operating leverage on the 3PL network (customs clearance, multimodal handling)

## Red flags
- Profit halved then doubled in two years — freight-rate cyclicality
- Thin margins (3-8% OPM)
- Small cap, low float, SME-listing liquidity risk
- Revenue still below FY23 level in FY25

## Verdict
A rate-cycle proxy, not a compounder — buy only when freight rates sit at a cyclical bottom.
