# Sai Life Sciences Limited (SAILIFE)

## What it does
Company does contract research, development and manufacturing of medicines (a CRDMO) for global pharma and biotech clients from Hyderabad. It handles discovery chemistry, process R&D, and cGMP making of APIs, intermediates and formulations, selling mostly to US and Europe customers. Listed December 2024 (IPO was pure offer-for-sale, ~Rs 3,042 crore).

## The numbers
- FY23 revenue ~Rs 873 crore, PAT ~Rs 64 crore.
- FY24 revenue ~Rs 1,221 crore, PAT ~Rs 244 crore (per DRHP/coverage).
- H1 FY25 (Apr-Sep 2024): revenue ~Rs 741 crore, PAT ~Rs 156 crore (~21% PAT margin); full FY25 revenue ~Rs 1,300+ crore, PAT data not found in verified sources (search limited).
- Margins rank among the best of Indian CRDMOs; customer base concentrated in US/Europe.
- Expense heads: raw materials/chemicals, employee (scientist-heavy) costs — exact percentages data not found.

## Lynch lens
A fast grower in a specialised, sticky niche — once a client's molecule enters Sai's plants, the work repeats for years (Lynch's "repeat-purchase" service). High PAT margin (~20%) signals pricing power. Risks: pharma outsourcing cycles and client concentration. P/E: data not found; PEG not computable.

## TAM
Global pharma CDMO/CRDMO market estimated at $100+ billion and growing high single digits (period/source: industry estimates — exact figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Commercial API/intermediate manufacturing — long-duration contracts at high margins.
2. Discovery and process R&D services — feeds molecules into its own plants.
3. US/Europe client base — dollar revenue with margin premium.

## Red flags
- Client and geography concentration (US/Europe).
- FY24 PAT (Rs 244 crore) may include one-offs versus Rs 64 crore in FY23 — verify quality of earnings.
- IPO was OFS-only; pre-IPO investors may keep selling.

## Verdict
High-margin niche fast grower — worth owning if entry P/E stays sane.
