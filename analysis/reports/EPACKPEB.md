# EPack Prefab Technologies Ltd (EPACKPEB)

## What it does
Makes pre-engineered buildings (PEB) — factory-built steel structures that workers bolt together on site as warehouses, factories and commercial buildings. Handles design, manufacture, supply and erection turnkey. Sells to industrial, commercial, infrastructure and institutional customers in India and abroad.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 905 cr (FY24) → Rs 1,134 cr (FY25) → Rs 1,525 cr (FY26)
- PAT: Rs 43 cr (FY24) → Rs 59 cr (FY25) → Rs 93 cr (FY26)
- OPM steady ~10%; net margin ~6% (FY26)
- Borrowings cut from Rs 216 cr (FY25) to Rs 115 cr (FY26)
- ROCE 21.7%, ROE 17% (FY26); P/E ~24; FCF only Rs 3 cr (FY26)
- 5-yr sales CAGR 45%, profit CAGR 58%; promoters hold 64.95%

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a genuinely boring business — steel sheds. One of India's top PEB players alongside Tata BlueScope, Kirby and Jindal Steel. P/E 24 vs ~34% sales growth gives PEG under 1 on the E side — attractive. Debt falling, ROCE solid. Watch the capex-heavy cash flow (FCF Rs 3 cr despite Rs 93 cr PAT).

## TAM
India PEB industry estimated in the range of Rs 15,000-20,000 cr and growing double-digits on warehouse/logistics/industrial capex (industry estimates; precise figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Steel prices — raw material (steel) dominates cost; margin swings with steel price
2. Industrial/warehouse capex cycle (e-commerce, logistics parks)
3. Operating leverage from higher volumes — PAT up 58% (FY26) on 34% sales growth

## Red flags
- Thin ~6% net margins; commodity steel pass-through risk
- FCF nearly zero (FY26) due to heavy working capital and capex
- Competitive industry with large rivals
- No dividend

## Verdict
Decent fast-growing PEB play at reasonable P/E — margins thin, so steel-price discipline decides everything.
