# Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd (JSFB)

## What it does
Bank takes deposits and lends to small borrowers — microfinance-style small business loans plus affordable-housing and other retail loans — across 816 outlets in 23 states. Serves 4.3 million active customers with about 26,000 employees.

## The numbers
- Total income: Rs 4,014 Cr (FY24), Rs 4,710 Cr (FY25), Rs 5,353 Cr (FY26).
- Net profit: Rs 670 Cr (FY24), Rs 501 Cr (FY25), Rs 326 Cr (FY26) — falling for two years (sector-wide microfinance stress).
- Deposits: Rs 22,571 Cr (FY24) → Rs 35,784 Cr (FY26).
- Gross NPA 2.39%, Net NPA ~0.9% (Jun 2026); ROE 7.6%; book value Rs 421/share.
- Interest expenses Rs 2,760 Cr (FY26) — the dominant cost. Market cap Rs 5,721 Cr.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical, not broken. Small-finance banks ride the microfinance credit cycle; FY25-26 industry-wide delinquency squeeze crushed profits, and JSFB's Rs 5,721 Cr cap against Rs 326 Cr PAT (~17x P/E, under 0.5x book on Rs 421 book value) prices in pain. Lynch's financials rule: buy when the cycle bottoms and P/B sits well below 1 — watch for NPA peaking. Promoter (Jana Holdings ~16.9%) trimmed 4.9% last quarter — negative signal.

## TAM
India microfinance / small business lending market — data not found (MFI sector serves tens of millions of borrowers; exact size not verified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Credit costs / provisioning on microfinance loans (the FY25-26 profit killer).
2. Net interest margin — lending yields vs cost of deposits (interest expense Rs 2,760 Cr FY26).
3. Deposit growth (Rs 35,784 Cr FY26) funding cheaper lending.

## Red flags
- Profit down 51% over two years.
- Microfinance asset-quality cycle not yet clearly turned.
- Promoter selling; ROE only 7.6%.

## Verdict
Deep-value bank if the microfinance cycle turns — deep loss risk if it doesn't.
