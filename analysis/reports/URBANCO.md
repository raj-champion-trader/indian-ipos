# Urban Company Ltd (URBANCO)

## What it does
Company runs an online marketplace connecting homes with service professionals — cleaning, repairs, appliance service, painting, beauty and wellness at home. Makes money taking a cut from each job plus selling own-brand products. Operates in India and abroad (UAE, Singapore, Saudi and other markets).

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 828 Cr (FY24), Rs 1,144 Cr (FY25), Rs 1,556 Cr (FY26). 5-yr sales CAGR 44%.
- Net result: -Rs 93 Cr (FY24), +Rs 240 Cr (FY25), -Rs 235 Cr (FY26). TTM net -Rs 334 Cr.
- OPM: -18% (FY24), -3.5% (FY25), -16% (FY26).
- Almost debt free (borrowings Rs 136 Cr, FY26); market cap Rs 25,972 Cr; ROE -12%.
- Shareholding: promoters 19%, FIIs 55%, DIIs 11% (Jun 2026).

## The numbers caveat
FY25 profit vs FY26 loss swings likely reflect ESOP/other one-offs — but the pattern shows the business has not yet locked in steady profitability.

## Lynch lens
Not a classic Lynch pick — a venture-backed platform still burning cash at a Rs 26,000 Cr market cap. Fast grower in revenue, yes; in earnings, no. No PEG math possible with negative earnings. The boring-business angle Lynch loved (home cleaning) sits inside an exciting, money-losing wrapper.

## TAM
India home & personal services market — data not found (DRHP cited multi-billion-dollar India services TAM; exact figure not verified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Take rate / contribution margin per service order.
2. Beauty & wellness vertical and international (Gulf) markets — highest-margin businesses.
3. Employee/ESOP and advertising spend (heavy costs); finance costs minor (Rs 12 Cr FY26).

## Red flags
- TTM loss widening (-Rs 334 Cr); interest coverage flagged weak.
- Promoter stake under 20%; heavy FII ownership = momentum-driven stock.
- P/B of 12x with negative ROE.

## Verdict
Growth story without profits — Lynch would pass until the model prints money consistently.
