# Afcons Infrastructure Ltd (AFCONS)

## What it does
Afcons builds big infrastructure — metro lines, bridges, roads, ports and jetties, tunnels and dams. It sells EPC construction services mainly to government clients like Indian Railways, NHAI and port authorities. The Shapoorji Pallonji Group owns and controls it; the company listed in November 2024.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Sales: FY23 12,637; FY24 13,268; FY25 12,548; FY26 11,948 (declining two years straight)
- Net profit: FY23 411; FY24 450; FY25 487; FY26 251 (FY26 PAT nearly halved)
- Net margin: ~3.7% (FY25), ~2.1% (FY26). P/E ~51 at price Rs 287; market cap Rs 10,543 cr
- Borrowings: FY25 2,343; FY26 3,627 (rising). Operating cash flow negative in FY25 and FY26
- Order book roughly Rs 40,000+ cr (FY25, per company presentations) — about 3x annual sales

## Lynch lens
Cyclical. Construction lives and dies with the government capex cycle, execution speed and working capital. Thin margins plus long payment cycles make this a "watch the cycle" stock, not a compounding machine. No niche monopoly — competes with L&T, NCC, KEC and dozens of mid-size EPC players, though marine works (ports/jetties) give it a genuine specialisation edge. P/E of ~51 against falling earnings gives a meaningless, unattractive PEG.

## TAM
India infrastructure pipeline runs into lakhs of crores (National Infrastructure Pipeline ~Rs 100+ lakh crore over 2020-25, per government estimates). Plenty of work to bid on — the constraint for Afcons sits in execution and cash, not demand.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Execution pace and margin on the transportation/metro order book (largest revenue chunk)
2. Marine and underground (tunnel/hydro) projects, where specialised skills carry better margins
3. Finance costs (Rs 629 cr FY25, Rs 674 cr FY26) and receivable days (105 TTM) that eat the thin profit

## Red flags
- FY26 PAT collapse (487 → 251) despite sizeable order book
- Negative operating cash flow in FY25 and FY26; borrowings climbing
- Stock down from Rs 479 high to ~Rs 287; promoters' parent group carries heavy group debt

## Verdict
Cheap-looking cyclical that turned expensive on crashed earnings — wait for execution and cash flow to mend before touching it.
