# Laxmi Dental Ltd (LAXMIDENTL)

## What it does
Laxmi Dental makes dental products: crowns and bridges via dental lab services, clear aligners (Bizdent), aligner materials and machines, and paediatric dental products (Kids-e-Dental). Dentists send it impressions; it returns finished dental devices. It ranks as the second-largest domestic dental lab player and the largest Indian export dental laboratory.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 162 crore (FY23), Rs 194 crore (FY24), Rs 239 crore (FY25) — screener.in
- PAT: Rs -4 crore loss (FY23), Rs 25 crore (FY24), Rs 32 crore (FY25)
- Operating margin: 6% (FY23), 13% (FY24), 18% (FY25) — a real turnaround in margins
- Market cap ~Rs 1,099 crore; ROE 14.8%, ROCE 14.9%; 5-year profit CAGR ~103%
- Expenses (FY25): total Rs 196 crore including interest Rs 6 crore; head-wise split (materials, employees): data not found.
