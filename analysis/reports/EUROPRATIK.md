# Euro Pratik Sales Ltd (EUROPRATIK)

## What it does
Euro Pratik Sales makes and sells decorative wall panels and laminates under the "Euro Pratik" and "Gloirio" brands. Founded 2010, it runs an asset-light model: design, branding and distribution in-house, manufacturing outsourced to contract partners. Buyers: interior designers, retailers and homeowners upgrading walls.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 221 crore (FY24), Rs 284 crore (FY25), Rs 335 crore (FY26, screener latest year) — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 63 crore (FY24), Rs 76 crore (FY25), Rs 77 crore (FY26); TTM Rs 88 crore
- Operating margin: 36% (FY24, FY25), 34% (FY26); TTM 31% — exceptionally fat for a building-materials brand
- ROE 3-year: 36%. P/E ~30.5; market cap ~Rs 2,631 crore
- Expense heads: manufacturing costs (outsourced), distribution and marketing; head-wise % split: data not found.

## Lynch lens
Classic Lynch fast grower candidate — 20%+ revenue growth with 30%+ operating margins in a boring, decorative-panels niche. Asset-light + brand = high ROE without capital bloat. Margin drift (36% → 31% TTM) deserves watching. Insider buying: data not found. PEG roughly 30.5 P/E vs ~18-20% profit growth ≈ 1.5+ — not obviously cheap.

## Red flags
- Margin compression trend (36% FY25 → 31% TTM)
- Growth flattening: PAT Rs 76 crore (FY25) to Rs 77 crore (FY26) — near-zero profit growth
- Outsourced manufacturing = limited control over quality/cost
- P/E 30 with stalling profit growth

## Verdict
Great niche, fat margins, but growth has paused — wait for earnings to re-accelerate before paying 30x.
