# Knack Packaging Limited (KNACK)

## What it does
Knack Packaging makes printed and laminated woven polypropylene bags — the tough sacks that hold rice, pet food, fertilizer, and chemicals. It sells to big food and agri brands in India and abroad.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 659 cr; FY25 Rs 747 cr; FY26 Rs 844 cr (per IPO documents).
- PAT: FY24 Rs 46 cr; FY25 Rs 74 cr; FY26 Rs 93 cr.
- PAT margin climbed from ~7% (FY24) to ~11% (FY26).
- OPM (standalone, Screener): 11% FY23 → 18% FY25.
- Borrowings Rs 188 cr (FY25, Screener standalone); IPO raised Rs 320 cr for a new factory.

## Lynch lens
Boring-business speciality. Nobody at a dinner party talks about woven PP sacks, yet customers include Cargill, KRBL, Drools, and Ebro Foods — sticky, repeat buyers who need certified food-grade packaging. PAT grew ~24% CAGR FY24→FY26 with rising margins: a quiet fast grower hiding in a dull package. Watch capacity utilisation at the new plant.

## TAM
India industrial woven-PP packaging market size: data not found in sources fetched.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Volume growth from the new manufacturing facility (Rs 320 cr capex).
2. Raw material (polypropylene resin) prices — the dominant cost input.
3. Export mix and value-added (pinch-bottom, laminated) bag pricing.

## Red flags
Commodity raw-material exposure (PP prices swing with crude); competitive fragmented industry; customer concentration among large agri-food brands; detailed segment mix not disclosed.

## Verdict
Dull sacks, decent numbers — a boringly good compounder worth holding while margins keep climbing.
