# Cello World Ltd (CELLO)

## What it does
Company makes everyday consumer products — pens and stationery, moulded plastic furniture, and kitchen housewares — under the Cello brand. Sells through shops across India; a household name in pens and plastic chairs.

## The numbers (standalone)
- Revenue: Rs 1,016 cr (FY24), Rs 1,113 cr (FY25), Rs 1,116 cr (FY26) — growth stalled
- Net profit: Rs 62 cr (FY24), Rs 112 cr (FY25), Rs 80 cr (FY26) — falling again
- OPM: 8% (FY24), 10% (FY25), 3.2% (FY26) — collapsed
- Almost debt-free (borrowings Rs 3 cr FY26); P/E 98.8; ROE 2.4%; dividend payout 40.7%
- Note: standalone figures; consolidated (incl. glassware/PLGW) will differ — data not found

## Lynch lens
A brand stalwart that has stopped growing and lost margin. Consumer products with a trusted brand should earn 15%+ margins; standalone OPM of 3.2% in FY26 says input costs or pricing power broke down. P/E 98.8 against a shrinking Rs 80 cr profit gives an absurd PEG. The brand remains the asset; the numbers do not justify the price.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Polymer/plastic raw material costs — crushed OPM from 10% to 3.2%
2. Pen & stationery volumes (core heritage line) — stable but low-growth
3. Moulded furniture and houseware demand — consumer cycle lever

## Red flags
- Standalone OPM 3.2% (FY26) — abnormally low for a branded consumer company
- P/E 98.8 with declining profit
- Revenue flat: Rs 1,113 cr to Rs 1,116 cr (FY25 to FY26)

## Verdict
Great brand, wrong price — 98x earnings for a no-growth, margin-squeezed consumer play makes no sense.
