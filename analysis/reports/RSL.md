# Rajputana Stainless Ltd (RSL)

## What it does
Company makes iron and steel products — stainless-steel long products for industrial users. Sells steel into construction and manufacturing supply chains as a B2B producer.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 952 Cr; FY24 Rs 910 Cr; FY25 Rs 932 Cr; FY26 Rs 1,007 Cr (screener). Flat-to-modest top line.
- PAT: FY23 Rs 24 Cr; FY24 Rs 32 Cr; FY25 Rs 40 Cr; FY26 Rs 50 Cr. TTM PAT Rs 59 Cr.
- OPM improving: 4.6% (FY23) to 9% (FY26). ROE ~25% (FY25), 19% (FY26).
- Debt: borrowings Rs 69 Cr (Mar 2026); company reduced debt. Interest Rs 20 Cr, depreciation Rs 9 Cr (FY26).
- Market cap Rs 1,483 Cr; P/E ~25.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical — steel prices and spreads drive everything; margins swing with nickel/chrome and scrap prices. The quiet positive: margins expanded five years straight on flat sales, meaning mix or efficiency improved. P/E 25 for a 9% OPM steel cyclical looks rich unless the earnings rebound continues. No niche dominance visible; commodity player.

## TAM
India stainless steel market estimated ~4-5 mt consumption, growing high single digits (industry estimates); global stainless market ~US$ 100+ bn. Precise company TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Stainless steel price spreads (raw material/scrap cost vs realisation).
2. Operating leverage — OPM expansion from 4.6% to 9% (FY23→FY26) drove PAT doubling.
3. Lower interest burden as debt reduced (FY26).

## Red flags
- Commodity margins can reverse as fast as they came.
- Flat revenue FY23-FY25 — growth only appeared FY26.
- Thin disclosure on segment mix.

## Verdict
Cyclical riding a margin upcycle — fine to hold while spreads hold, not a compounder.
