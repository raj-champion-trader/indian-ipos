# Shanti Gold International Ltd (SHANTIGOLD)

## What it does
Company makes 22KT gold jewellery — bangles, rings, necklaces, and full sets — across daily-wear, festive, and bridal collections. It uses CZ casting techniques. Incorporated 2003.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 2,019 cr (FY26); FY25 figure: data not found (recently listed).
- PAT: Rs 140 cr (FY26); ~7% net margin. Q1 FY27 (Jun 2026): sales Rs 716 cr, PAT Rs 50 cr.
- OPM: 10% (FY26); interest Rs 18 cr, depreciation Rs 6 cr (FY26).
- Market cap Rs 1,859 cr; P/E 11.9 (as of Mar 2026 data). Promoters hold 74.89%.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with a commodity twist — profits ride gold prices and wedding-season demand. Low P/E (11.9) fits Lynch's "boring industry, cheap multiple" screen, but jewellers carry working-capital and hedging risks Lynch distrusted. No niche dominance versus Titan or regional giants.

## TAM
Indian gold jewellery market — one of the world's largest (Rs lakh-crore scale); specific sourced figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Gold price levels — inventory gains/losses flow straight through margins.
2. Bridal and festive jewellery volumes (wedding-season demand).
3. Making charges / design premium over raw gold cost, and modest interest cost (Rs 18 cr FY26) on inventory funding.

## Red flags
- Thin 10% OPM (FY26) — typical for jewellery, but leaves little room for error if gold corrects.
- Short listed history; FY25 comparatives not available.
- High promoter holding (74.89%) with minimal institutional ownership beyond ~2% FII/DII.

## Verdict
A cheap gold cyclical (P/E ~12) — only attractive if you hold a view on gold and weddings; not a Lynch-style compounder.
