# Mamata Machinery (MAMATA)

## What it does
Mamata builds packaging machines — vertical and horizontal form-fill-seal bag-making lines plus spares and service — sold in India and exported worldwide under the Mamata brand.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY25 ~Rs 255 cr (quarter sums, screener.in); FY26 ~Rs 233 cr.
- PAT: FY25 ~Rs 41 cr (boosted by a strong Mar-25 quarter, PAT Rs 27 cr); FY26 ~Rs 15 cr — big earnings swing.
- OPM: 33% in Mar-25 quarter but only 5-13% in most FY26 quarters — lumpy, project-based revenue recognition.
- Nearly debt-free (interest under Rs 1 cr/yr). IPO December 2024.

## Lynch lens
Would-be fast grower in a genuinely boring niche — bag-making machines — with high export reach (a majority of revenue historically from exports per DRHP coverage; exact FY25 split data not found). But quarterly numbers whipsaw: one fat quarter (Mar-25) then near-zero (Mar-26, tax-impacted). Machine-makers earn machine-like economics only when order books smooth out. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: data not found.

## TAM
Global packaging machinery market — US$50+ billion (industry estimates); Indian packaging machinery segment thousands of crores. Exact sourced TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Machine sales timing — revenue booked on dispatch/acceptance, so one quarter makes the year.
2. Exports (higher margin geographies) versus domestic competitive orders.
3. Spares and service revenue — steady annuity on installed base.

## Red flags
Extreme quarter-to-quarter volatility; FY26 profit collapse despite similar revenue; small scale (~Rs 250 cr); post-IPO lock-up/expiry dynamics unknown.

## Verdict
Interesting niche exporter, but earnings too lumpy to trust yet — watch order book and export mix for two more years.
