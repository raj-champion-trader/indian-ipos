# Indegene Ltd (INDGN)

## What it does
Serves the world's top 20 biopharma companies as a strategic operating partner — running their drug discovery support, regulatory work, launch operations, medical content and patient programmes using AI-enabled platforms. Earns mostly in dollars from global life-sciences clients.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 2,590 cr (FY24) → Rs 2,839 cr (FY25), +10%
- PAT: Rs 337 cr (FY24) → Rs 407 cr (FY25), +21%
- OPM: 20% (FY24), 19% (FY25)
- Interest: Rs 49 cr (FY24) → Rs 22 cr (FY25); Depreciation Rs 80 cr (FY25)
- Borrowings just Rs 102 cr (FY25) — near debt-free
- ROE 14.4%, ROCE 18.8%; P/E ~33.5; dividend yield 0.39%
- 5-yr sales CAGR 29%, profit CAGR 23%

## Lynch lens
Stalwart-to-slow-fast-grower hybrid. Business model resembles IT services applied to pharma — sticky clients (top 20 biopharma), recurring outsourced spend. Growth decelerated to ~10% (FY25), below its 29% five-year pace, so it has slipped from classic fast-grower status. PEG on current growth looks stretched (33.5 P/E vs ~10-15% growth). Employee-cost-heavy P&L (people business); margin lever sits in utilisation and AI-driven productivity.

## TAM
Global life-sciences commercialisation/services spend runs into tens of billions of dollars; India-specific TAM not meaningful (exports). Exact figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Outsourcing budgets of top-20 biopharma clients (client concentration)
2. Employee costs/utilisation — largest expense head for this people business
3. AI platform leverage improving margins (PAT +21% on +10% sales, FY25)

## Red flags
- Heavy client concentration in top 20 pharma
- Growth slowdown to ~10% (FY25) vs 29% 5-yr CAGR
- US pharma pricing/regulatory pressure can crimp client budgets
- P/E 33.5 demands re-acceleration

## Verdict
Quality pharma-services stalwart with clean books — but growth has cooled and the price hasn't.
