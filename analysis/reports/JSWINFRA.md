# JSW Infrastructure Ltd (JSWINFRA)

## What it does
JSW Infrastructure operates ports and terminals — ships berth at its facilities, and it charges for loading, unloading, and storing cargo. India's second-largest commercial port operator by cargo capacity (part of the JSW Group), it also runs two O&M terminals in the UAE handling 41 MTPA.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 3,763 crore (FY24), Rs 4,476 crore (FY25), Rs 5,361 crore (FY26) — 40% 5-year profit CAGR.
- Net profit: Rs 1,161 crore (FY24), Rs 1,521 crore (FY25), Rs 1,547 crore (FY26). OPM ~49-51%.
- Market cap ~Rs 79,907 crore; P/E ~51.5 (Aug 2026). ROCE 13.6%, ROE 15.3%.
- Capex-heavy: FY26 depreciation Rs 614 crore, interest Rs 383 crore. Second-largest port operator in India.

## Lynch lens
A infrastructure stalwart with fast-grower numbers — toll-booth economics on water (50% operating margins, cargo volume like traffic). Lynch liked businesses that collect rent on unavoidable flows; ports qualify. But 51x earnings for a ~10-15% profit grower gives PEG well above 2 — the market already paid for the next decade of capacity additions. Group backing (JSW) brings captive cargo, plus related-party questions.

## TAM
Data not found (Indian port cargo TAM not cited here; company handles a growing share of India's ~400+ MTPA-plus annual maritime cargo per public commentary).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Cargo handling volumes and tariffs at its ports (core ~50%-margin revenue).
2. New capacity and acquisitions ramping (sales nearly quintupled since FY17) — while depreciation (Rs 614 crore FY26) and interest (Rs 383 crore FY26) absorb much of the gross profit.
3. Other income (Rs 266-353 crore range FY24-FY26) steadying PBT.

## Red flags
- P/E ~51.5 with FY26 PAT growth of just ~2% — expensive vs growth.
- Interest capitalization flagged by screener; true interest burden may exceed the reported line.
- Related-party exposure: JSW Group companies supply a chunk of cargo.
- High capex cycle keeps free cash flow under pressure.

## Verdict
Wonderful toll-road-on-sea business at a rich toll — hold quality, but at 51x you buy the harbor and pay for the ocean too.
