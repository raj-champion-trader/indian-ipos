# Laser Power & Infra Limited (LASERPOWER)

## What it does
Laser Power makes power cables and conductors in Kolkata and also builds power lines for utilities — rural electrification, substations, and distribution work on turnkey contracts. It sells to state electricity boards and distribution companies.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 1,764 cr; FY25 Rs 2,593 cr (per IPO documents); FY26 Rs 2,326 cr (Screener standalone).
- PAT: FY24 Rs 40 cr; FY25 Rs 107 cr; FY26 Rs 119 cr.
- OPM: 9% (FY24) → 10% (FY25) → 13% (FY26); PAT margin 5.1% (FY26).
- Debt Rs 478 cr (Mar 2026); interest cost Rs 133 cr (FY26).
- IPO (Rs 742 cr: Rs 542 cr fresh + Rs 200 cr OFS) — Rs 490 cr earmarked to repay borrowings.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical riding the power-capex upcycle. India's grid buildout (distribution reforms, rural schemes) feeds both factories and EPC orders, and margins keep inching up. But EPC work brings payment-cycle risk, working-capital bloat, and government-counterparty delays — the interest bill (Rs 133 cr, bigger than half the operating profit) shows the strain. Not a stalwart; a cycle stock to ride and exit.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Order book and execution of power-distribution EPC projects.
2. Conductor/cable manufacturing volumes and margins (higher-margin than EPC).
3. Interest costs and receivable recovery from state utilities (Rs 490 cr IPO debt paydown helps).

## Red flags
Interest cost Rs 133 cr FY26 nearly swallows profit; FY26 revenue declined; government-counterparty receivables; thin 5% net margin; working-capital-heavy EPC mix.

## Verdict
A leveraged play on India's grid-spending cycle — profitable trend, but remember cycles end.
