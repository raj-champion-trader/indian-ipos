# Arisinfra Solutions Ltd (ARIS)

## What it does
Arisinfra buys cement, steel, aggregates and other construction materials in bulk and delivers them to real estate developers and infrastructure contractors. Its tech platform cuts out middlemen from construction procurement, earning a margin on materials supplied.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 746 crore (FY23), Rs 697 crore (FY24), Rs 768 crore (FY25), Rs 1,067 crore (FY26) — 39% growth in FY26.
- Net profit: first full-year profit of Rs 60 crore in FY26 (vs Rs 6 crore FY25; losses before). OPM climbed from ~0% to 9%.
- Market cap ~Rs 1,238 crore; P/E ~18.7 (Aug 2026). ROE ~11%.
- IPO in 2026 cleaned the balance sheet: borrowings fell from Rs 343 crore (FY25) to Rs 70 crore (FY26); reserves jumped to Rs 723 crore.
- Interest cost Rs 28 crore (FY26) — still meaningful but halved.

## Lynch lens
Textbook Lynch turnaround: a former money-loser (FY22-FY24 in the red) that flipped to scale and profit after cutting debt and financing costs. The niche — boring bulk-construction procurement digitized — has repeat purchase volume. P/E ~19 against explosive profit growth off a small base; PEG looks great but only because the base sits near zero.

## TAM
Data not found (Indian construction-materials B2B market size not reliably published; multi-lakh-crore industry per general commentary).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Spread on bulk materials (cement, steel, aggregates) sold to developers/contractors — volume drove FY26 sales +39%.
2. Interest expense collapse post-IPO (Rs 41 crore FY25 to Rs 28 crore FY26; borrowings down to Rs 70 crore) — directly widened profit.
3. Operating leverage: OPM from ~0% (FY23) to 9% (FY26) as the platform scaled on a fixed cost base.

## Red flags
- Thin margins (9% OPM) in a working-capital-heavy, commodity-price-linked trade.
- Track record of losses as recently as FY24; one good year so far.
- No dividend; promoter/RIFPSA-style pre-IPO investors may sell post lock-in.
- Receivables cycle of builders — real-estate slowdown hits hard and fast.

## Verdict
Genuine turnaround with a debt-free balance sheet now — interesting at ~19x, but it must prove FY26 repeats before it earns blind trust.
