# Vishal Mega Mart Ltd (VMM)

## What it does
Company runs a chain of hypermarkets selling apparel, groceries and home essentials to middle and lower-middle-income shoppers in India. It buys in bulk and sells value-priced goods under its own stores and brands.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 6,239 cr (FY24), Rs 5,947 cr (FY25), Rs 6,631 cr (FY26); TTM Rs 6,849 cr.
- PAT: Rs 318 cr (FY24), Rs 473 cr (FY25), Rs 656 cr (FY26). 5-year profit CAGR 76%.
- OPM improved from 9% (FY24) to 15% (FY26). Nearly debt free.
- ROE 10%; ROCE 13.2%. P/E ~77x; market cap ~Rs 50,127 cr; 7.1x book.

## Lynch lens
Fast grower on profits (76% CAGR) from a value-retail base — India's largest fashion-led hypermarket chain. Scale economics drive margin expansion. But P/E of ~77x prices in years of flawless execution; PEG looks unattractive unless profit growth sustains at very high rates. FY25 revenue dipped, showing demand sensitivity.

## TAM
Indian value retail (value fashion + FMCG) — a very large, growing market; specific TAM figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Private-label apparel margins (highest-margin category).
2. Store additions and same-store sales growth in FMCG/general merchandise.
3. Operating leverage — OPM expansion from 9% to 15% drove the FY24→FY26 profit surge.

## Red flags
- Valuation: 77x P/E, 7x book for a ~10% ROE business.
- FY25 standalone revenue declined 5%.
- Recent IPO (Dec 2024); lock-in expiries and insider sales can pressure the stock.

## Verdict
Good business, expensive price — Lynch would wait for a better entry.
