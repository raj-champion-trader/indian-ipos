# Bharti Hexacom (BHARTIHEXA)

## What it does
Bharti Hexacom, a Bharti Airtel subsidiary, runs mobile phone and home-broadband services in the Rajasthan and North-East telecom circles. Customers pay monthly tariffs for calls, data and internet.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY24 Rs 7,089 cr; FY25 Rs 8,549 cr; FY26 Rs 9,354 cr (quarter sums, screener.in) — steady ~10-15% growth.
- PAT: FY25 ~Rs 1,493 cr; FY26 ~Rs 1,734 cr (FY24 ~Rs 505 cr, distorted by a one-off Rs -184 cr quarter).
- EBITDA margin ~46-53% and rising; depreciation huge (Rs 560+ cr/quarter — spectrum/amortisation).
- Government (TCIL etc.) holds a minority stake after the April 2024 IPO.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with a tailwind. A duopoly telecom market, tariffs rising, and these two circles have lower competition and data headroom — Airtel's Rajasthan/North-East cash machine. Boring business, pricing power, 18%+ net margin. Insider buying: parent Bharti Airtel periodically buys stake — data not found for current year. P/E: data not found.

## TAM
India telecom services revenue — roughly Rs 2.5+ lakh crore annually (TRAI/industry estimates); circle-level TAM data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Mobile tariff hikes in a 2-3 player market — flow straight to EBITDA.
2. Subscriber mix-up (higher-ARPU data users) in Rajasthan and North-East.
3. Home broadband/Airtel Black cross-sell in these circles.

## Red flags
Depreciation and spectrum obligations dwarf reported EBITDA; controlled by Airtel (related-party network costs); regulated industry; North-East circle economics historically weaker.

## Verdict
Clean telecom stalwart compounding at low-teens — a hold-forever type if bought at a reasonable P/E.
