# Tenneco Clean Air India (TENNIND)

## What it does
Tenneco's Indian arm makes exhaust and clean-air systems, powertrain parts and ride/aftermarket products (ART segment) for cars, UVs and commercial vehicles. It supplies the big Indian vehicle makers and exports to Tenneco's global plants.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY25 Rs 4,891 cr; FY26 Rs 5,405 cr, +10.5% YoY (stockanalysis.com/S&P; matches screener quarterly sums).
- PAT: FY26 ~Rs 605 cr (168+151+119+167); FY25 ~Rs 552 cr — ~11% net margin, unusually rich for auto components.
- OPM steady ~16-18%; interest small (~Rs 30 cr/yr); depreciation ~Rs 108 cr FY26.
- Screener flags a very high dividend payout (~100% of profit).

## Lynch lens
Stalwart-quality growth inside a cyclical sector. Lynch loves a boring exhaust-pipe maker with pricing power nobody watches — record FY26 margins and market-share gains per company commentary. Export mix and new-technology content (emissions tightening) add a kicker. Analyst consensus ~Buy (4 analysts, stockanalysis.com). P/E/PEG: data not found.

## TAM
India auto components industry ~US$70-80 billion (ACMA estimates, mid-2020s); exhaust/clean-air slice grows with emission-norm tightening (BS-VI→future). Exact segment TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Clean Air & Powertrain OEM volumes (Maruti/M&M/Tata truck cycles).
2. ART aftermarket segment — margins and stability through cycles.
3. Exports + new-tech content per vehicle (record margins FY26 per company slides).

## Red flags
Parent-group ownership/structuring (global Tenneco control, royalty/related-party terms); auto-cycle dependence; high payout leaves little reinvestment; new listing → short public track record.

## Verdict
Best-in-class boring cyclical — double-digit growth, 11% net margin, fat dividends; buy on auto-cycle dips.
