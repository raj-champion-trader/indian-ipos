# MV Electrosystems Ltd (MVELECTRO)

## What it does
MV Electrosystems designs and builds electrical and power-electronics equipment for railways — IGBT-based propulsion systems for electric locomotives, switchgear panels and vehicle control systems. It supplies rolling-stock makers and Indian Railways-linked customers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 63 crore FY25 vs Rs 50 crore FY24.
- PAT: Rs 1 crore FY25 vs Rs 1 crore FY24. OPM 12% both years.
- Borrowings: Rs 32 crore FY25; interest Rs 4 crore FY25 — debt heavy for the profit level.
- TTM sales down ~21% with recent quarterly losses (screener.in).

## Lynch lens
A tiny supplier riding the railway capex cycle — cyclical, single-customer-ecosystem risk (Railways/PSU OEMs). Thin margins and Rs 32 crore debt against Rs 1 crore profit make it fragile. Too small and too cyclical for a Lynch core holding; possible turnaround bet only with evidence of order wins.

## TAM
Indian railway electrification and rolling-stock electricals: part of Indian Railways' multi-lakh-crore capex plan (FY24-30); exact segment TAM data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Railway electric-locomotive propulsion and control-system orders.
2. Execution pace and margin on turnkey sub-systems.
3. Interest cost on Rs 32 crore FY25 borrowings vs tiny profit base.

## Red flags
- Shrinking TTM sales; quarterly losses reported.
- Interest cost roughly equals PAT — leverage danger.
- Customer concentration in railway orders; lumpy tender flow.

## Verdict
Micro-cap railway cyclical with fragile finances — speculative only.
