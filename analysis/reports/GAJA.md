# Gaja Alternative Asset Management Ltd (GAJA)

## What it does
Gaja runs alternative investment funds — pooled investment vehicles for wealthy investors — focused on Indian real estate and special situations. It earns management fees on assets under management plus a share of profits (carry) when investments do well. Small, niche fund manager.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 39 crore (FY23), Rs 58 crore (FY24) — standalone, per screener.in; FY25: data not found on screener page
- Net profit: Rs 40 crore (FY23, inflated by Rs ~56 crore other income), Rs 11 crore (FY24)
- Operating margin: 5% (FY23), 35% (FY24)
- TTM profit growth: -73% per screener
- Finance cost: Rs 1 crore; borrowings Rs 4 crore — almost debt free
- Market cap Rs 2,237 crore; P/E 206

## Lynch lens
Neither stalwart nor classic fast grower — a small fund manager whose earnings swing with fees and carry. At P/E 206 and shrinking TTM profits, the market pays for AUM growth that has not yet reached the P&L. Lynch avoided stories priced like this unless growth visibility proved out. Asset-play angle exists only if AUM ramps sharply — data not found to confirm.

## TAM
Indian alternative investment funds (AIF) industry — large and growing; exact TAM figure: data not found in sources used.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Management fees on AIF assets under management
2. Performance fees / carry from real estate and special situation exits
3. Other income — Rs 56 crore in FY23 made profit exceed operating profit; lumpy item

## Red flags
- P/E 206 with profit declining 73% TTM
- FY23 profit quality poor — driven by other income, not core fees
- Small base; one bad exit can sink a year

## Verdict
Interesting niche, but valuation has run far ahead of earnings — watch from the sidelines.
