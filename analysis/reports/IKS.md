# Inventurus Knowledge Solutions (IKS)

## What it does
IKS helps doctors' groups in the US, Canada and Australia run their paperwork — billing, coding, patient records and care coordination — through its software-plus-services platform. Doctors pay it to handle the admin so they can treat patients. It earns under a "fee for value" model, classified as IT-enabled healthcare services.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 3,347 cr TTM, +21% YoY — screener.in
- PAT: Rs 764 cr TTM, +41% YoY; PAT margin ~23%; OPM 34%
- 5-yr sales CAGR 42%; 3-yr sales CAGR 46%
- ROE 40.3%; borrowings Rs 766 cr (Sep 2025), interest Rs 62 cr TTM
- P/E ~39.6x; market cap ~Rs 30,200 cr

## Lynch lens
Fast grower in a boring-but-rich niche — US healthcare administration. Lynch loved businesses riding another industry's pain: US doctors drown in paperwork and regulation, and IKS sells the mop. 40% ROE shows pricing power and sticky contracts. 39.6x P/E against 21-41% growth gives a PEG near 1-1.9 depending on the growth year you trust — fair-ish, not cheap. Currency (rupee appreciation) and US healthcare policy shifts swing results.

## TAM
US healthcare revenue-cycle-management and care-enablement market — a multi-billion-dollar annual outsourcing/software spend (precise TAM figure not found in fetched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Fee-for-value contracts with US physician enterprises — the entire profit base
2. Operating leverage on the tech-plus-services platform (OPM 34%, improving)
3. Client concentration and US healthcare reimbursement policy — biggest downside levers

## Red flags
- Near-total revenue dependence on the US/developed markets; currency risk
- Client concentration typical of healthcare BPO platforms
- 39.6x P/E — priced for continued 20%+ growth
- Interest costs on Rs 766 cr borrowings

## Verdict
High-quality high-ROE fast grower at fair-to-full price — accumulate on dips.
