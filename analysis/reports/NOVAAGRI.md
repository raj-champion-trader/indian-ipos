# Nova Agritech Ltd (NOVAAGRI)

## What it does
Company makes agri inputs — bio-fertilisers, bio-pesticides, organic fertilisers and micronutrients that improve soil health and crop yields. Sells to farmers through dealer networks. Riding the biological/organic farming theme.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 179.84 cr (FY24), Rs 165.86 cr (FY25) — declining
- Net profit: Rs 18.60 cr (FY24), Rs 16.52 cr (FY25)
- OPM: 15.37% (FY24), 13.29% (FY25); TTM OPM 7.83%, TTM PAT Rs 4.82 cr — deteriorating
- Finance cost: Rs 3.19 cr = 1.9% of sales (FY25); borrowings Rs 31.83 cr (FY25)
- Debtor days: 436; working capital days: 373 — very heavy
- ROE 2.2%, stock -52% in 1 year

## Lynch lens
Falling knife, not a category fit. TTM profit collapsed to Rs 4.82 cr with 7.8% OPM; 436 debtor days means cash comes more than a year after the sale. The bio-agri niche has a story, but the numbers show a company that grows receivables faster than profits. No PEG — earnings shrinking.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Dealer receivables cycle (436 debtor days) — working capital drag, finance and impairment risk
2. Raw material costs (nutrient/bio input inputs) — squeezed OPM from 15.4% to 7.8% TTM
3. Seasonal agri demand and monsoon — drives volume and pricing

## Red flags
- TTM PAT down to Rs 4.82 cr from Rs 16.52 cr (FY25)
- 436 debtor days — severe collection problem
- Stock down 52% in a year; ROE 2.2%

## Verdict
Working-capital disaster in a nice-sounding niche — avoid until cash conversion improves.
