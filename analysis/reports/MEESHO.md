# Meesho Ltd (MEESHO)

## What it does
Meesho runs an app-based shopping marketplace connecting small sellers to value-seeking buyers, mostly in smaller towns. Sellers list fashion, home goods, electronics and more; Meesho earns fees on services, not commissions (zero-commission model). It also owns the Valmo logistics brand.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 9,388 crore FY25 vs Rs 7,615 crore FY24 (+23%).
- PAT: Rs -3,883 crore FY25 vs Rs -318 crore FY24 (per screener.in; loss includes non-operating/one-off items — operating profit about Rs -555 crore FY25).
- OPM: about -6% FY25. Almost debt free (borrowings Rs 58 crore FY25). P/E 67 (post-listing).

## Lynch lens
A hoped-for fast grower that has not yet grown into profits — revenue grows 20%+ but the bottom line bleeds. No niche dominance moat: it fights Amazon, Flipkart and quick-commerce in low-ticket e-commerce. Zero-commission model is the differentiator, but it caps revenue per order. Loss-making, so no PEG. Not a classic Lynch buy.

## TAM
India e-commerce market: widely cited at roughly USD 120-150 billion GMV (2024, various industry reports). Meesho focuses on the tier-2+ value segment.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Advertising and seller service fees on rising order volumes.
2. Valmo logistics / delivery fee income.
3. Growth of grocery vertical in tier-2+ cities vs heavy marketing and logistics spend.

## Red flags
- Large reported FY25 net loss (Rs -3,883 crore, screener.in standalone; treatment of one-offs needs checking).
- Price-war economics; competitors with deeper pockets.
- Low-margin, capital-hungry model; profitability hinges on cost discipline.

## Verdict
Fast grower without profits yet — watch, do not chase at P/E 67.
