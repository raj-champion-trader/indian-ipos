# Juniper Hotels (JUNIPER)

## What it does
The company owns and runs luxury hotels — seven properties with 1,836 keys including 245 serviced apartments — across Indian cities. All hotels operate under the Hyatt brand, and the company ranks as India's largest owner of Hyatt-affiliated hotels.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 667 crore (FY23); Rs 752 crore (FY24); Rs 818 crore (FY25); Rs 916 crore (FY26)
- PAT: Rs -2 crore (FY23); Rs 9 crore (FY24); Rs 80 crore (FY25); Rs 147 crore (FY26)
- OPM: 41% (FY23) → 37% (FY25) → 42% (FY26)
- Interest: Rs 88 crore (FY26); depreciation Rs 91 crore (FY26) — both heavy, typical for hotel owners
- TTM revenue Rs 945 crore, PAT Rs 171 crore; 5-year sales CAGR ~41%

## RevenueStreams
- Room revenue from luxury/superior-upscale Hyatt hotels (majority) (FY25)
- Food & beverage — banquets and restaurants (FY25)
- Serviced apartments and other income (FY25)
- Exact % split: data not found

## Expenses
- Interest Rs 88 crore (FY26); depreciation Rs 91 crore (FY26); total expenses Rs 528 crore (FY26)
- Staff, energy and operating costs: data not found

## Lynch lens
Turnaround that already turned — from a Rs 2 crore loss (FY23) to Rs 147 crore PAT (FY26) in three years, riding India's post-Covid travel and wedding boom plus an asset-owner's operating leverage. Hotel companies swing hard: occupancy and room-rate moves drop straight to the bottom line once fixed costs get covered. Cyclical upside with brand (Hyatt) quality; watch leverage through the cycle.

## TAM
Indian hotel industry revenue estimated in the range of tens of billions of dollars mid-2020s (India Brand Equity Foundation, 2024); premium-segment supply lags demand in key cities post-Covid.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Occupancy and average room rate (ARPL) gains across the Hyatt portfolio
2. Banquets/F&B — weddings and corporate events, high-margin business
3. Operating leverage — interest (Rs 88 crore) and depreciation (Rs 91 crore) fixed against rising revenue (FY26)

## Red flags
- Highly cyclical — a travel downturn reverses the profit swing fast
- Big interest and depreciation load eats a third of revenue (FY26)
- Leasehold/ownership structure and related-party links to Hyatt and promoters

## Verdict
A cyclical turnaround still climbing — hold while India travels, but this one dances to the tourism cycle.
