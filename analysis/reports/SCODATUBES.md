# Scoda Tubes (SCODATUBES)

## What it does
The company makes stainless-steel pipes and tubes — both seamless and welded — at Mehsana in Gujarat. Customers come from oil and gas, chemicals, fertilizers, power, pharma, automotive and railways. It listed via IPO in 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 305 crore (FY23); Rs 400 crore (FY24); Rs 485 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 546 crore (FY26)
- PAT: Rs 10 crore (FY23); Rs 18 crore (FY24); Rs 32 crore (FY25); TTM Rs 39 crore (FY26)
- OPM: 11% (FY23) → 15% (FY24) → 16% (FY25) → 14% (FY26 TTM)
- Interest costs Rs 26 crore (FY26 TTM); total expenses Rs 468 crore (TTM)
- Revenue growing ~25% CAGR (FY23-FY25), PAT nearly tripling

## RevenueStreams
- SS seamless pipes and instrumentation tubes — high-pressure applications, main revenue source (FY26)
- SS seamless tubes and U-tubes — heat exchangers, boilers, condensers (FY26)
- SS welded tubes/U-tubes — low-pressure uses; expansion still in implementation (FY26)
- Exact % split: data not found

## Expenses
- Raw materials (stainless steel — nickel-sensitive): dominant cost; exact %: data not found
- Interest Rs 26 crore (FY26 TTM)
- Employee and power: data not found

## Lynch lens
A small, boring niche manufacturer — exactly the kind Lynch loved: stainless tubes nobody talks about, sold into pharma/oil/chemicals, growing 25% a year with margins climbing (11% → 16%, FY23-FY25). If the welded-tube expansion completes and import substitution continues, this could stay a fast grower in stalwart clothing. Small size cuts both ways — agility plus customer-concentration and working-capital risk.

## TAM
India stainless-steel tube/pipe market estimated in the low tens of thousands of crore rupees annually (industry estimates, early 2020s); global SS tube demand growing mid-single digits.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Nickel/SS raw-material price spread — inventory gains or margin squeeze
2. Seamless tube volumes to oil/gas, pharma and chemical customers
3. New welded-tube capacity — adds scale once commissioned (FY26)

## Red flags
- Raw-material (nickel) price swings can crush the ~15% OPM
- Interest cost Rs 26 crore on a small base — capex-funded-by-debt risk
- Customer concentration in industrial buyers; execution risk on expansion
- Tiny market cap — low liquidity, high volatility

## Verdict
A promising small-cap fast grower in a boring niche — worth tracking as capacity ramps.
