# Globe Civil Projects Ltd (GLOBECIVIL)

## What it does
Globe Civil builds roads and highways as an EPC contractor, mainly for government authorities like NHAI. It bids for projects, builds them, and gets paid as work completes. Listed August 2025.

## The numbers
(Consolidated, Rs crore, per Screener.in)
- Revenue: FY24 332; FY25 379; FY26 406 (steady mid-teens growth); TTM 431
- Net profit: FY24 15; FY25 24; FY26 23; net margin ~5-6%
- ROE 13.5%, ROCE 16.1%; P/E ~10.7 at price Rs 45.6; market cap Rs 272 cr
- Interest cost Rs 20 cr FY26 (large relative to Rs 31 cr PBT); borrowings modest but rising working-capital funding
- Operating cash flow negative in FY25 (-11) and FY26 (-54) — receivables build with government clients

## Lynch lens
Small-cap cyclical. Road EPC runs on government order flow, execution speed and working capital. P/E ~10.7 with ~13% ROE reads cheap, classic neglected-small-cap territory; but a Rs 272 cr market cap contractor depends heavily on winning bids and timely NHAI payments. Order book details: data not found in this pass — verify before investing.

## TAM
India road construction pipeline: government programmes (Bharatmala-style national highway builds) award tens of thousands of crore of EPC/HAM projects annually (NHAI/MoRTH data) — deep, but crowded with larger listed rivals.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. EPC project execution and margin on the road order book
2. Interest and working-capital costs (Rs 20 cr interest FY26 vs Rs 31 cr PBT — a big swing factor)
3. Order inflows/execution pace from NHAI and state authorities

## Red flags
- Negative operating cash flow two years running; FCF -75 FY26
- Interest eats ~2/3 of pre-tax profit
- Very small size versus giants like Afcons/NCC/G R Infraprojects — thin bid pipeline visibility

## Verdict
Cheap little road builder with cash-flow strain — only for buyers who dig into the order book first.
