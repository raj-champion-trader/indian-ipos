# Krystal Integrated Services Ltd (KRYSTAL)

## What it does
Krystal provides integrated facility management — housekeeping, private security, staffing and payroll, catering, technical and waste management — mainly to hospitals, schools, government bodies, and large corporates. It focuses on healthcare, education, and public-administration clients. Listed March 2024.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 981 cr (FY24), Rs 1,113 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 1,129 cr (-3% TTM growth). 5-yr sales CAGR 17%.
- Operating profit: Rs 67 cr / 7% (FY24), Rs 73 cr / 7% (FY25).
- PAT: Rs 48 cr (FY24), Rs 60 cr (FY25), TTM Rs 59 cr. 5-yr profit CAGR 21%.
- Total expenses ~93% of sales; interest Rs 16 cr, depreciation Rs 11 cr (TTM); borrowings Rs 102 cr (Mar 2026). ROCE 14.8%, ROE 12.7%.

## Lynch lens
A dull-as-dishwater (literally) business with sticky government and hospital clients — the Lynch "boring is beautiful" bucket. Thin 7% margins come with the manpower-services territory, but contracts renew for years and working capital is the main strain. Growth has stalled (TTM -3%), so it currently behaves like a stalwart, not a grower. P/E data not found.

## TAM
India facility management market — a large, fragmented market estimated at over Rs 1 lakh cr and growing high single digits with outsourcing penetration (approximate; exact figure data not found).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Long-cycle IFMS contracts with government, hospitals, and education clients (recurring revenue base; exact mix % data not found).
2. Manpower cost management — employee/subcontracting costs dominate the 93% expense ratio.
3. Cross-selling security, catering, and waste services into existing client sites.

## Red flags
- Revenue contracted 3% TTM — client losses or slower government spending.
- Razor-thin 7% OPM; wage inflation (minimum wages) squeezes directly.
- Receivables from government clients can stretch working capital; Rs 102 cr debt.

## Verdict
Steady boring compounder at best — the 7% margin ceiling caps the excitement.
