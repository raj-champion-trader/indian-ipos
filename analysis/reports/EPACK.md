# EPACK Durable Ltd (EPACK)

## What it does
Company designs and manufactures room air conditioners and components (indoor and outdoor units) for big brands on an ODM basis — the brand names on the box belong to clients; EPACK does the engineering and factory work. It also makes small appliances.

## The numbers
- Standalone revenue: Rs 1,520 cr (FY23), Rs 1,420 cr (FY24), Rs 2,171 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 2,117 cr.
- PAT: Rs 31 cr (FY23), Rs 36 cr (FY24), Rs 58 cr (FY25); TTM Rs 18 cr (-73% yoy).
- OPM: 6-8% (FY23-FY25), 6% (TTM).
- Borrowings rose from Rs 386 cr (Mar FY24) to Rs 676 cr (Mar FY26); interest Rs 61 cr.
- P/E ~117 at ~Rs 1,847 cr market cap; ROE 1.8%; 5-yr sales CAGR 21%.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical contract manufacturer. Room AC demand swings with summers and consumer credit; customers hold the brand and much of the bargaining power. FY25 revenue jumped 53% but TTM profit collapsed 73% — classic thin-margin, high-volume capacitor business. Raw material (copper, steel) costs and debt-funded capacity decide profit.

## TAM
Indian room AC market ~11-12 million units annually (FY24, industry estimates), expected to grow strongly with penetration below 10% of households; component/ODM market several thousand crores.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. RAC ODM volumes for large brand clients — the whole revenue engine.
2. Copper/steel/aluminum input prices — squeeze or release the 6-8% OPM.
3. Interest cost (Rs 61 cr) on Rs 676 cr borrowings funding new capacity — caps net profit.

## Red flags
- TTM PAT down 73% despite flat revenue; 3-yr profit CAGR -18%.
- Rising debt and weak interest coverage; ROE 1.8%.
- Promoter holding declined (-0.74% last quarter); P/E ~117 vs shrinking earnings.

## Verdict
Debt-heavy AC contract manufacturer with collapsing near-term profits — a cyclical to revisit when margins recover, not now.
