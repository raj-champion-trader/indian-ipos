# Aequs Ltd (AEQUS)

## What it does
Company makes high-precision, high-complexity components and assemblies for global aerospace OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers, running India's only fully vertically integrated aerospace ecosystem (forging, machining, surface treatment, assembly) inside a single SEZ. Also runs consumer goods and precision machining verticals. IPO listed December 2025 (Rs 921.81 crore issue).

## The numbers
- Consolidated FY25 revenue Rs 959 crore vs Rs 988 crore FY24; FY25 PAT loss Rs 102.35 crore vs FY24 profit Rs 14.24 crore (IPO filings). FY23: revenue Rs 840.5 crore, PAT Rs 109.5 crore.
- H1 FY26: revenue Rs 565.6 crore; PAT Rs 16.98 crore — back in the black.
- FY25 EBITDA margin 11.68%; debt-equity ~0.99 pre-IPO; fresh issue earmarked mainly for debt repayment.
- Standalone screener data (FY26): revenue Rs 124 crore; PAT Rs 50 crore. Market cap ~Rs 16,498 crore; P/E ~498; P/B 8.8x; ROE ~2%.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround story wrapped in a hot defence theme — losses in FY25, recovering via H1 FY26 profit and IPO-driven deleveraging. But P/E near 500 prices in years of flawless execution. Niche (only single-SEZ integrated aerospace maker in India) real; valuation dangerous. No insider buying data found.

## TAM
Global commercial aerospace precision-components market; India aerospace & defence manufacturing push (defence index member). Exact size data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Aerospace & defence orders (4,500+ products: engine, landing, structures) — core profit engine.
2. Interest and depreciation load — IPO debt repayment directly lifts PAT.
3. Consumer goods/toys vertical margins — swing factor between profit and loss.

## Red flags
- FY25 loss of Rs 102 crore; standalone OPM just 1% in FY26.
- Extreme valuation (P/E ~498, 8.8x book, ROE 2%).
- Earnings propped by other income (Rs 75.9 crore standalone).

## Verdict
Real aerospace niche, story-stock price — wait for the turnaround to show in full-year numbers.
