# Brigade Hotel Ventures Limited (BRIGHOTEL)

## What it does
Brigade Hotel Ventures owns and operates hotels, mainly in South Indian cities, run under international chains (Marriott-family brands). It earns money from room tariffs and from food, beverage and banquet business. Recent IPO (2025) from the Brigade Group stable.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 343 crore (FY24); Rs 404 crore (FY25).
- PAT: Rs 19 crore (FY24); Rs 17 crore (FY25) — flat despite higher sales.
- OPM ~33-34%; ROE ~8-11%; ROCE ~11-13%.
- Debt cut sharply: Rs 1,001 crore (Mar 2024) down to Rs 309 crore — IPO proceeds used to deleverage.
- Market cap ~Rs 2,260 crore; P/E ~33-35; no dividend.
- Expense heads: operating costs dominate (OPM implies ~66% of revenue); employee/lease split data not found.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical with asset-play undertones. Hotels ride the travel cycle, and this one owns hard assets (hotels on prime South India land) with debt now slashed post-IPO — that deleveraging plus 2nd-largest chain-affiliated owner status in South India gives the asset angle. But P/E 33+ for a single-digit-ROE business looks full. Not cheap enough for Lynch.

## TAM
Indian hospitality market — large and growing (industry estimates put Indian hotel industry in the multi-billion-dollar range); precise South India figure data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Occupancy and average room rate (ARR) cycle in key South India cities.
2. Food & beverage / banquet and MICE revenue.
3. Finance costs — debt fell ~70%, so interest drag shrinks.

## Red flags
- PAT flat FY24→FY25 despite 18% revenue growth (cost pressure).
- Stock down ~32% over the past year — market already sceptical.
- Related-party dependence on Brigade Group ecosystem.
- Thin public float, no dividends.

## Verdict
Decent asset-backed hotel play post-deleveraging; hold off until valuation meets the earnings.
