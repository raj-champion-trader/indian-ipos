# Saraswati Saree Depot Ltd (SSDL)

## What it does
Company runs a B2B wholesale business in sarees and women's apparel — kurtis, dress materials, blouse pieces, lehengas. It buys from 900+ weavers and suppliers across Surat, Varanasi, Kolkata and Bengaluru, and sells to retailers across India.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 602 cr (FY24), Rs 604 cr (FY25), Rs 631 cr (FY26). TTM Rs 634 cr.
- Net profit: Rs 30 cr (FY24), Rs 31 cr (FY25), Rs 23 cr (FY26).
- OPM: 7% (FY25), 5% (FY26). Almost debt free. ROE 12.6% (FY26).
- P/E ~9.6; market cap ~Rs 226 cr. Dividend yield 2.65%.
- Working capital days jumped from 68 to 104 — cash cycles through inventory and receivables.

## Lynch lens
Small-cap stalwart-cyclical hybrid: a boring, low-margin saree wholesaler that grinds out small profits. Thin margins (5-7%) mean tiny slips hurt. Cheap at ~10x earnings with a dividend, but profit fell in FY26. No niche moat beyond supplier network scale.

## TAM
Indian women's ethnic wear market — multi-lakh crore retail market; organized B2B saree wholesale TAM data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Saree wholesale volume and pricing spread (main profit engine).
2. Other women's apparel (kurtis, dress materials, lehengas) mix.
3. Working capital/interest efficiency — longer debtor days squeezed FY26 profit.

## Red flags
- FY26 profit fell ~26% despite higher sales; OPM halved.
- Working capital days ballooned to 104.
- Razor-thin margins with commodity-style apparel distribution; supplier concentration risk.

## Verdict
Cheap but shrinking-margin trader — watch, don't chase.
