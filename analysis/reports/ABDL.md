# Allied Blenders & Distillers Ltd (ABDL)

## What it does
Allied Blenders makes liquor — whisky mainly, plus brandy, rum, vodka and gin across 31 brands. Its Officer's Choice whisky ranks among the world's biggest whisky brands by volume, sold mostly to value-conscious Indian drinkers.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 3,147 cr (FY23), Rs 3,328 cr (FY24), Rs 3,520 cr (FY25), Rs 3,880 cr (FY26, standalone)
- Net profit: Rs 5 cr (FY23), Rs 7 cr (FY24), Rs 200 cr (FY25), Rs 268 cr (FY26)
- OPM: 6% (FY23) to 15% (FY26) — a genuine margin transformation
- FY26 expenses Rs 3,304 cr (interest Rs 130 cr, depreciation Rs 69 cr); P/E ~61; ROE 16.2%; debtor days 166

## Lynch lens
A turnaround that already worked: profits jumped from Rs 5 cr (FY23) to Rs 268 cr (FY26) on premiumisation (ICONiQ White Whisky entering the global top-20) and pricing. The boring, sin-stock angle helps — steady demand, no technology risk. But P/E ~61 on a staple that grows revenue ~7% a year gives an unattractive PEG; the easy margin repair looks done.

## TAM
Indian alcoholic-beverages market — one of the largest globally by volume; exact value data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Officer's Choice franchise volume and pricing (value whisky core)
2. Premiumisation mix (ICONiQ White) lifting blended margins
3. State-level excise/policy changes and raw-material (grain, ENA, packaging) costs

## Red flags
- Margin expansion from 6% to 15% may not repeat; FY25 jump partly base-driven
- High debtors (166 days) and Rs 130 cr interest (FY26)
- Regulated market — excise policy risk never goes away

## Verdict
Fine turnaround already harvested by the market; hold only if premiumisation keeps beating.
