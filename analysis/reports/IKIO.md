# IKIO Technologies Ltd (IKIO)

## What it does
Company designs and manufactures LED lighting products and other electronics for other brands (ODM/EMS model) — lighting, product displays, energy solutions and diversified electronics. Does the R&D and factory work; customers put their own labels on the goods.

## The numbers
- Sales: Rs 241 Cr (FY24), Rs 208 Cr (FY25), Rs 170 Cr (FY26) — shrinking.
- Net profit: Rs 29 Cr (FY24), Rs 24 Cr (FY25), Rs 15 Cr (FY26) — halved in two years.
- OPM fell from 12% (FY24) to 6% (FY26).
- Almost debt free (Rs 1 Cr borrowings); ROE 3%; market cap Rs 1,652 Cr; P/E ~103x.
- Promoters hold 72.6% (Jun 2026).

## Lynch lens
Turnaround candidate — or a melting ice cube. Revenue and margins have declined for two straight years; P/E of 103x against falling earnings inverts every Lynch rule (he wanted P/E below growth rate). The ODM niche with in-house R&D has merit, but Signify concentration means one customer steers the ship. No insider-buying data found.

## TAM
India LED lighting / EMS market — data not found (industry multi-billion-dollar; exact size not verified).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Order flow from Signify, the dominant lighting customer.
2. Factory utilisation (fixed costs vs volumes — OPM compression driver).
3. Component/LED chip costs and any new vertical wins (energy solutions, displays).

## Red flags
- Sales down 29% over two years; PAT down ~48%.
- Single-customer dependence (Signify).
- 103x P/E on declining earnings.

## Verdict
Small, shrinking, expensive — Lynch would walk away.
