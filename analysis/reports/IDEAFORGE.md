# ideaForge Technology Ltd (IDEAFORGE)

## What it does
Company designs and builds drones (UAVs) in India — mostly small mapping and surveillance quadcopters plus fixed-wing VTOLs — and sells them to Indian security forces (BSF, army, police) and enterprise survey customers. It also runs drone services (drone-as-a-service) and pilot training. Listed via IPO in July 2023.

## The numbers
- FY25: revenue fell sharply — roughly Rs 100-130 crore (approx; down from ~Rs 280+ crore in FY24); full-year net loss ~Rs 100+ crore (approx per coverage).
- Q4 FY25: net loss Rs 25.7 crore (vs Rs 10.3 crore profit in Q4 FY24); third straight loss quarter of FY25.
- Q1 FY26: revenue Rs 12.8 crore (-85% YoY); net loss Rs 23.5 crore.
- Q3 FY26: revenue Rs 31 crore (+75% YoY); loss widened 42% to Rs 34 crore.
- Q1 FY27 (Jun-2026 quarter): revenue Rs 68.6 crore (+437% YoY); EBITDA positive at Rs 4.3 crore — recovery underway.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround / speculative growth — dominant Indian drone maker (~70%+ historic share of security-force procurements) but lumpy government orders wrecked FY25-FY26. PLI scheme, import bans on foreign drones and border surveillance demand give a real tailwind. Losses burn cash; recovery hinges on order execution in FY27.

## TAM
India drone market projected to multiply several-fold by 2030 (government PLI support, drone rules liberalisation); global drone TAM tens of billions of dollars (exact figures: data not found in searched sources).

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Defence/security-force drone orders (lumpy tenders).
2. Enterprise mapping/survey drone sales and services.
3. New product ramps (VTOL hybrids, drone services) and operating-cost cuts.

## Red flags
- Deep, prolonged losses through FY25-FY26; cash burn.
- Order-book lumpiness — a single tender decides the year.
- Competition from Genrobotics/Tata/Adani-backed and Chinese-adjacent players.
- Revenue quality: receivables from government buyers.

## Verdict
Turnaround option on India's drone push — the FY27 EBITDA-positive quarter hints at recovery, but position size must stay small until orders land.
