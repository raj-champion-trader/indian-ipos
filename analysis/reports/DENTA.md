# Denta Water & Infra Solutions Ltd (DENTA)

## What it does
Company designs and builds water management projects. It installs large-scale groundwater recharge systems using recycled water, and also builds railway and highway infrastructure. Incorporated 2015; listed 2025.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 239 cr (FY24); Rs 203 cr (FY25) — declined ~15%.
- PAT: Rs 60 cr (FY24); Rs 53 cr (FY25).
- OPM: 33% (FY24); 34% (FY25) — unusually fat margins for a contractor.
- Nearly debt-free: borrowings Rs 1 cr (FY25). ROE 14% (FY24).

## Lynch lens
Boring-business angle at its best: unglamorous water recharge work, near-zero debt, 25%+ net margins (FY25 PAT margin ~26%). Lynch loved niche infrastructure plays that competitors ignore. The catch: revenue fell in FY25, so the "fast grower" case rests on order pipeline, not track record.

## TAM
India water infrastructure / groundwater recharge and recycled-water market — large and policy-driven (government Jal Jeevan / water conservation spend); specific TAM figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Execution of water management / groundwater recharge projects (core revenue, ~34% OPM FY25).
2. Government and municipal order flow — revenue depends on public spending cycles.
3. Interest costs negligible (Rs ~1 cr borrowings) — nearly all operating profit reaches PAT.

## Red flags
- Revenue declined from Rs 239 cr (FY24) to Rs 203 cr (FY25) — single-customer/project concentration risk typical of small contractors.
- Margins (33-34% OPM) look high versus civil-construction peers (10-15%) — worth verifying accounting and related-party work.
- Small base; lumpy order book.

## Verdict
Interesting Lynch-style niche with a clean balance sheet, but shrinking revenue and suspiciously fat margins demand diligence before buying.
