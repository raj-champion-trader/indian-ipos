# BMW Ventures (BMWVENTLTD)

## What it does
BMW Ventures sells steel products across Eastern India through its own stockyards. It also fabricates pre-engineered buildings and girders, distributes tractor engines and spare parts, and makes PVC pipes under the BMW Polytube brand.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY26 Rs 2,279 cr (up ~10% YoY from ~Rs 2,063 cr FY25; stockanalysis.com/S&P + screener quarterly sums).
- PAT: FY26 ~Rs 38 cr (8+7+12+11 across quarters, screener.in); FY25 ~Rs 33 cr.
- Margins: razor-thin — OPM ~3-4% every quarter FY25-FY26.
- Interest ~Rs 6-10 cr per quarter; working-capital heavy trade.

## Lynch lens
Cyclical, commodity trader at heart. Steel distribution earns a spread, not a moat — boring, but Eastern India/Bihar presence and value-added fabrication (PEB, girders) give a small niche. Lynch would note: low margin + high volume means tiny gross-profit slips sink the year. Insider buying: data not found. P/E: data not found.

## TAM
India steel consumption ~150+ million tonnes and growing high-single-digits; PEB market several thousand crores (industry estimates). Exact sourced TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Steel price spread — flat-product prices versus procurement cost.
2. Fabricated steel/PEB volumes — slightly better margin than plain trading.
3. Interest and working-capital cost — inventory cycles amplify both ways.

## Red flags
3-4% OPM leaves no cushion; steel price swings; customer/supplier concentration unknown (data not found); PVC and tractor-parts lines small relative to steel.

## Verdict
Thin-margin steel cyclical — trade it with the steel cycle, don't compound in it.
