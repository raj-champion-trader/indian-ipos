# TVS Supply Chain Solutions Ltd (TVSSCS)

## What it does
Company manages supply chains for big businesses and governments. It moves goods, runs warehouses and in-plant logistics, sources parts, and designs supply networks. Part of the TVS Group; listed August 2023.

## The numbers
- Revenue: Rs 9,200 cr (FY24); Rs 9,996 cr (FY25); Rs 11,003 cr (FY26) — steady ~8-10% growth.
- PAT: loss Rs 90 cr (FY24); loss Rs 10 cr (FY25); profit Rs 117 cr (FY26) — just turned profitable.
- OPM: 7.7% (FY24), 6.7% (FY25), 7.1% (FY26). Thin but typical for logistics.
- Interest Rs 165 cr and depreciation Rs 570 cr (FY26); borrowings Rs 2,755 cr (FY26). Cash from ops Rs 746 cr (FY26); FCF Rs 481 cr.

## Lynch lens
Turnaround in progress. Boring, asset-heavy logistics business that bled red ink post-IPO and crossed into profit in FY26. Segment mix improving: Integrated Supply Chain Solutions grew from 40% of revenue (FY22) to 55% (H1 FY25) — higher-value work replacing low-margin Network Solutions. TVS Group pedigree adds credibility.

## TAM
India logistics and contract logistics market — multi-billion-dollar and fragmented; specific TAM figure: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Shift in mix toward Integrated Supply Chain Solutions (55% H1 FY25 vs 40% FY22) — lifts margins.
2. Interest burden (Rs 165 cr FY26) on Rs 2,755 cr borrowings — debt paydown directly boosts PAT.
3. Depreciation load (Rs 570 cr FY26) from owned warehousing/fleet — capex intensity caps earnings.

## Red flags
- FY24 and FY25 losses; profitability only just established (Rs 117 cr FY26 on Rs 11,003 cr revenue — ~1% net margin).
- Heavy debt and heavy depreciation; accounting quality of the turnaround needs a few more quarters.

## Verdict
Classic Lynch turnaround candidate — profitable at last in FY26, worth watching for margin follow-through, but the proof remains young.
