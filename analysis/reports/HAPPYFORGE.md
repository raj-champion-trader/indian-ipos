# Happy Forgings Limited (HAPPYFORGE)

## What it does
Happy Forgings makes large forged and machined parts — crankshafts, front axle components and other engine parts — for trucks, tractors and industrial machines. It sells mainly to Indian and overseas vehicle makers and tier-1 suppliers.

## The numbers
- FY25 revenue: ~Rs 1,409 crore, up 3.7% YoY despite weak CV/farm/off-highway end markets.
- FY25 PAT growth: ~10.1% YoY; Q2 FY25 PAT Rs 71 crore (+6.1%), Q3 FY25 PAT ~Rs 65 crore with EBITDA margin at 28.6%.
- Capex ~Rs 1,200 crore over FY25-27 to expand capacity and enter heavy forgings.
- Segment mix: axle/engine components ~40% of revenue; entered passenger vehicles in FY24 (5-6% of revenue, targeted 7.5-8%); won multi-year e-axle export order starting FY25.
- Expense heads detail: data not found; steel (forging input) dominates cost base for such companies.

## Lynch lens
Stalwart with a fast-grower option — a North-India forging shop with fat ~28% EBITDA margins, riding the boring truck-parts trade while adding PV, e-axle and heavy forgings legs. Growth slowed to ~4% in FY25 because CV and tractor demand slumped, confirming cyclical end-markets. P/E data not found here.

## TAM
India forging industry serves automotive, farm equipment and industrial OEMs — exact TAM figure data not found; heavy forgings (wind, defence, capital goods) adds a large addressable pool to the company's entry plans.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Commercial-vehicle and tractor demand cycles driving volumes of crankshafts and axle components (~40% of revenue).
2. Margin discipline — EBITDA margin expanded to 28.6% (Q3 FY25) even in a slowdown.
3. New legs: passenger-vehicle parts, e-axle exports and heavy forgings capex (Rs 1,200 crore, FY25-27).

## Red flags
- End-market cyclicality: FY25 revenue grew only 3.7% amid CV/farm weakness.
- Large capex (Rs 1,200 crore) ahead of demand — returns risk if EV transition accelerates.
- EV powertrains use fewer forged engine/axle parts long term.

## Verdict
A high-margin stalwart-cyclical worth holding through the CV cycle, with heavy-forgings capex as the kicker.
