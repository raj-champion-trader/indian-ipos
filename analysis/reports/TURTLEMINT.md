# Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Ltd (TURTLEMINT)

## What it does
Turtlemint runs a technology platform that sells insurance — motor, health, life and term plans — through a large network of certified freelance advisors called PoSPs (Point of Sale Persons). The company trains these advisors, gives them digital tools, and earns commission from insurers when policies sell. Founded 2015, it also distributes mutual funds and loans.

## The numbers
- Revenue (consolidated, RHP figures): Rs 460 crore FY23, Rs 119 crore FY24, Rs 693 crore FY25, Rs 749 crore for 9M/period ended Dec 2025 — reported differently across periods (gross vs net presentation), so trends read oddly.
- Losses: Rs 288 crore FY23, Rs 193 crore FY24, Rs 194 crore FY25, Rs 187 crore (Dec 2025 period).
- Expenses: Rs 312 crore FY24, Rs 883 crore FY25 — commissions, advisor payouts, marketing and technology dominate; finance costs minimal (~Rs 1 crore).
- Debt low (~Rs 10 crore); ROE deeply negative (~-41% to -47%).

## Lynch lens
Turnaround candidate — actually, a hoped-for turnaround. Lynch avoided businesses with no visible path to profit; Turtlemint burns Rs 190 crore a year in a market (insurtech distribution) where gross margins on insurance commissions get eaten by advisor payouts and marketing. The PoSP network scale (India's largest certified advisor network) gives a moat-if-monetised story. No meaningful P/E or PEG since there are no earnings.

## TAM
Data not found precisely; context — India's insurtech and insurance distribution market spans a large underinsured population, but no sourced market-size figure available.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Commission income on general insurance (motor, health) — primary revenue engine.
2. Advisor (PoSP) payout and acquisition costs — biggest loss driver.
3. Life/term insurance and mutual fund distribution economics.

## Red flags
- Six-plus consecutive years of large losses (~Rs 190-290 crore annually).
- Revenue recognition swings make trends hard to read.
- 260 debtor days (screener) — money stuck with insurers/receivables.
- Valued at ~Rs 4,500 crore market cap with zero profits.

## Verdict
A venture-style bet on PoSP insurance distribution turning profitable — Lynch would pass until the losses actually stop.
