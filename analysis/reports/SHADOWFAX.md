# Shadowfax Technologies Ltd (SHADOWFAX)

## What it does
Technology-led third-party logistics (3PL) company (est. 2016) doing last-mile and mid-mile delivery for e-commerce, quick commerce, food delivery and mobility platforms. Asset-light model — crowdsourced riders and network software rather than owned fleets.

## The numbers
- Revenue: FY23 Rs 1,415 Cr; FY24 Rs 1,885 Cr; FY25 Rs 2,467 Cr; FY26 Rs 4,080 Cr (standalone, screener) — ~42% CAGR.
- PAT: FY23 -Rs 143 Cr; FY24 -Rs 12 Cr; FY25 Rs 6 Cr; FY26 Rs 115 Cr — first full-scale profit year.
- OPM: -8% (FY23) → 5% (FY26).
- Debt down: borrowings Rs 383 Cr (FY25) to Rs 234 Cr (FY26) post-IPO. Interest Rs 27 Cr, depreciation Rs 108 Cr (FY26).

## Lynch lens
Turnaround that already turned — losses to Rs 115 Cr profit in three years on the quick-commerce delivery boom. Asset-light network economics finally kicked in: fixed tech costs spread over exploding parcel volumes. Lynch likes businesses that cross into profitability and then compound. Risk: hyper-competitive gig delivery where platforms (Swiggy/Zomato/Zepto in-house fleets) squeeze 3PL rates.

## TAM
India e-commerce logistics market estimated ~US$ 8-12 bn and quick-commerce among fastest-growing segments (industry estimates); exact company TAM: data not found.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Quick-commerce/hyperlocal parcel volumes — FY26 revenue jump to Rs 4,080 Cr.
2. Network utilisation — rider cost per drop falling as density rises (OPM 5% FY26).
3. Finance cost reduction — debt cut Rs 383→234 Cr (FY25→FY26).

## Red flags
- Thin 5% OPM in a price-war industry; customer concentration among a few large apps.
- Depreciation Rs 108 Cr nearly matches PAT — capex heavy despite "asset-light" label.
- One good year (FY26) — needs confirmation.

## Verdict
Turnaround into fast grower — attractive if FY27 repeats, size accordingly.
