# Dhoot Transmission Limited (DHOOTTRANS)

## What it does
Dhoot Transmission makes wiring harnesses — the bundles of wires and cables that carry electricity inside vehicles — plus battery packs, switches, sensors, and controllers. It sells to two-wheeler and three-wheeler makers in India, and holds ~70% share of the electric 2W/3W wiring market (FY26). Around 95% of its auto products serve EVs or work across powertrains.

## The numbers
- Consolidated revenue: Rs 2,799.32 cr (FY24), Rs 3,472.24 cr (FY25), Rs 4,563.70 cr (FY26) — 63% growth over 3 years.
- PAT: Rs 298.75 cr (FY24, 10.7% margin), Rs 353.89 cr (FY25, 10.2%), Rs 396.84 cr (FY26, 8.7%).
- RoCE fell from 33.6% (FY24) to 19.1% (FY26); screener standalone borrowings Rs 482 cr, interest Rs 46 cr, depreciation Rs 56 cr (FY25).
- 23 factories (2 more under construction); 41% share of India 2W/3W wiring market by value (FY26).
- IPO Aug 2026 at 44.9x FY26 P/E.

## Lynch lens
A fast grower riding the EV two-wheeler wave, but decelerating quality: margins slipped from 10.7% to 8.7% and RoCE nearly halved as capacity multiplied. Niche dominance (~70% of EV 2W/3W harnesses) gives real moat, yet 45x earnings prices the perfection. Lynch would call this a good business at the wrong price.

## TAM
India 2W/3W wiring harness market: Dhoot holds 41% by value (FY26) — implying a roughly Rs 7,000+ cr market (derived); EV 2W/3W harness segment where it holds ~70%: data not found in absolute terms. Growth rides EV penetration and premiumization.

## Top 3 P/L drivers
1. Wiring harnesses for EV 2W/3W makers — ~70% segment share, the profit engine.
2. Battery packs and electronics (controllers, sensors) — the second growth curve.
3. Raw material costs (copper, connectors) and receivables — margin squeeze took PAT margin down 2 points (FY24-FY26).

## Red flags
- Customer concentration typical of auto tier-1s; contingent liabilities Rs 60.05 cr.
- Declining RoCE and margins through the growth spurt.
- Valuation: 44.9x FY26 at IPO; OFS component Rs 1,667 cr cashes out promoters.

## Verdict
EV-wiring niche leader growing fast but priced for perfection — wait for a cheaper gear.
